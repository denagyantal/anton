# Product Brief: FleetLocal — Local Service Fleet GPS

---

## Executive Summary

**FleetLocal** is a GPS fleet management platform purpose-built for HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, and cleaning businesses with 3–25 vehicles. It occupies a genuinely unoccupied market position: $15/vehicle/month, month-to-month, no ELD compliance overhead, with native Jobber and HousecallPro integration.

The opportunity is created by a systematic overcharging problem. Enterprise fleet platforms — Samsara ($27–$33/vehicle/month, 3-year contracts) and Motive ($25–$50/vehicle/month) — were built for over-the-road commercial trucking and have successfully sold their bloated, overpriced tooling to local service fleets who are legally exempt from ELD requirements. No product currently exists at $15/vehicle/month with real service-fleet-specific features and native FSM integration.

The target market comprises 50,000–100,000 US businesses generating $600M–$1.5B ARR at full penetration. At 1% penetration (500 customers × 8 vehicles average), FleetLocal reaches $720K ARR — achievable for a focused bootstrapped launch in 12–18 months.

The product concept is straightforward: real-time GPS tracking via OBD-II plug ($45 add-on) or phone-based app, auto-logged arrival/departure events synced to Jobber or HCP job records, mileage-based maintenance reminders, digital DVIR, and customer ETA SMS — all on month-to-month billing with no exit fees, ever.

**Verdict: BUILD — Tier 1 Opportunity (Score: 97/105)**

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Small local service businesses with 3–25 vehicles are systematically overcharged and over-featured by GPS fleet platforms designed for commercial trucking. Samsara and Motive — built for interstate haulers with ELD compliance mandates — have sold 3-year contracts at $27–$50/vehicle/month to HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, and cleaning fleets who:

1. **Don't need ELD compliance**: Local service fleets operating within 150 air miles of their home base are legally exempt from ELD requirements under 49 CFR 395.1(e)(1). They're paying for Hours of Service reporting, DOT dashboards, and compliance infrastructure they cannot legally use.

2. **Are trapped by predatory contracts**: Samsara's 3-year auto-renewing agreements — with each added vehicle starting its own separate 3-year clock — create a contract trap with early termination fees of $220/device or remaining contract balance. A 30-vehicle fleet exiting after year 1 faces $15,000–$25,000 in exit fees. BBB complaints document customers being charged for cancelled contracts 2 years after written cancellation.

3. **Have no FSM-integrated alternative at the right price**: Jobber and HousecallPro are the operational backbone of this customer segment. Samsara and Motive have no native Jobber or HCP integration — GPS data lives in a separate silo. The few FSM-integrated options (Force Fleet at $10–$20, Trackem at $21.95) either have refresh rate issues or are priced above the $15 sweet spot.

**The gap**: No product currently occupies $15/vehicle/month + month-to-month + Jobber + HCP + no ELD + maintenance alerts.

### Problem Impact

For a 10-truck HVAC fleet, the financial impact is visceral:
- **Samsara**: $9,450/year (at $27/vehicle + hardware amortization)
- **Spytec (basic GPS only)**: $1,611/year — but no FSM integration, no maintenance scheduling
- **FleetLocal target**: $1,800/year with full feature set + FSM integration

The emotional impact is equally acute. Samsara's BBB rating of 1.17/5 (24 reviews) and Motive's D- BBB rating are not coincidental — they reflect documented experiences of billing overcharges, contracts that auto-renew without notice, and inability to exit despite written cancellation requests. These customers are vocal, angry, and actively searching for alternatives. They represent a high-motivation, high-conversion buyer segment with a specific name: "Samsara refugees."

- **Enterprise vendors** (Samsara, Motive): Wrong price, wrong contract, wrong product for local service fleets
- **Budget GPS** (Spytec, One Step): Right price, no contract — but no FSM integration, no maintenance features; these are basic trackers, not fleet management tools
- **Force Fleet**: Closest competitor; dual Jobber+HCP integration exists but at $10–$20 with documented HCP refresh delays and limited maintenance features. Not positioned as a "service fleet" brand.
- **Trackem GPS**: Deep Jobber integration (launched Aug 2026) but 46% overpriced vs. FleetLocal target; no HCP integration

### Proposed Solution

FleetLocal is a GPS fleet management platform that combines:

1. **Plug-and-play hardware**: OBD-II device ($45 add-on) that self-installs in under 2 minutes in any post-1996 vehicle — no professional installation, no downtime
2. **Phone-based tracking option**: GPS app for drivers as an alternative to hardware, enabling Phase 1 launch with zero hardware logistics
3. **Real-time location tracking**: Live vehicle map updated every 30 seconds; arrival/departure auto-detected via geofencing
4. **Native FSM integration**: Jobber and HousecallPro integration — GPS arrival events auto-log to job records, eliminating manual cross-referencing
5. **Maintenance scheduling**: Mileage-based oil change, tire rotation, and inspection reminders with push notifications to the owner
6. **Digital DVIR**: Pre/post-trip inspection checklist from driver's phone with photo damage documentation
7. **Customer ETA SMS**: Automated text to customer when tech departs for job site, with estimated arrival time
8. **Month-to-month billing**: $15/vehicle/month, cancel anytime from the dashboard, no exit fees — ever

### Key Differentiators

1. **The only product combining $15 + month-to-month + Jobber + HCP + no ELD**: This quadrant is genuinely unoccupied. Cannot be replicated by enterprise vendors without destroying their pricing model; cannot be replicated by no-integration vendors without building FSM integrations.

2. **Legally correct product positioning**: ELD exemption is not a missing feature — it's the correct product for this customer segment. Calling it out directly resonates with operators who resent paying for compliance they don't need.

3. **Contract trauma as channel**: Every business owner who has been burned by Samsara's auto-renewal trap is a pre-sold buyer. The "no contracts, cancel anytime" positioning directly addresses the #1 complaint across every review platform.

4. **FSM integration creates stickiness without lock-in**: When GPS arrival data flows automatically into Jobber job records, the product becomes embedded in daily workflow. Switching costs become real through value creation, not contractual coercion — the opposite of Samsara's model.

5. **Market timing**: Samsara's legal troubles (documented auto-renewal lawsuits, BBB/FTC complaints) are peaking in late 2026, driving organic search traffic and community discussion about alternatives. Trackem's Jobber integration launch in August 2026 at $21.95 creates a price anchor that makes $15 look compelling.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

**Phase 1 MVP — Software-Only Launch (Weeks 1–8):**

The Phase 1 MVP uses phone-based GPS (no hardware) to eliminate supply chain logistics and enable pure software validation. Core focus is on the Jobber integration — the primary differentiator.

**1. Driver Mobile App (iOS + Android)**
- Driver installs app, logs in, starts shift
- GPS location reported every 30 seconds while shift is active
- Simple shift start/end controls
- Pre-trip DVIR: 5-item photo checklist (lights, tires, mirrors, fluid leak, general condition); max 2 minutes

**2. Owner/Manager Dashboard (Web)**
- Live map showing all vehicles with driver name and current job
- Fleet list view with last-seen location and current status
- 30-day GPS history per vehicle with job overlay
- Vehicle and driver management (add/remove vehicles and drivers)
- Geofence job-site detection: auto-detect arrival/departure within 300-foot radius of Jobber job address

**3. Jobber Integration (Phase 1 Priority)**
- OAuth authorization via Jobber App Marketplace
- Pull job list and addresses from Jobber
- Push GPS arrival timestamps to Jobber job records as a note/event
- Push departure timestamps to Jobber job records
- Real-time vehicle location overlay on Jobber dispatch map (if Jobber API permits)

**4. Maintenance Scheduling**
- Manual odometer entry per vehicle (Phase 1 — no OBD-II in Phase 1)
- Configurable mileage-based reminders (oil change, tire rotation, inspection)
- Push notification + email to owner when mileage threshold is hit
- Maintenance history log per vehicle

**5. Billing and Subscription**
- Stripe-based billing: $15/vehicle/month, monthly billing only
- Add/remove vehicles instantly; billing adjusts next cycle
- No contracts, no minimum vehicles, cancel from dashboard
- 14-day free trial (no credit card required)

---

**Phase 2 — OBD-II Hardware + HCP Integration (Months 4–6):**

**6. OBD-II Hardware Option**
- Plug-in OBD-II GPS device ($45 add-on per vehicle)
- Automatic odometer reading (eliminates manual entry for maintenance)
- Vehicle-level tracking independent of driver's phone
- Basic engine diagnostics: battery voltage, check engine light detection
- Self-install guide (90-second video); no professional installation needed

**7. HousecallPro Integration**
- OAuth authorization
- Same arrival/departure auto-logging as Jobber integration
- Vehicle location overlay on HCP dispatch board

**8. Customer ETA SMS**
- Triggered automatically when driver departs for job site
- Message: "Hi [Customer Name], [Tech Name] is on the way and should arrive by [estimated time]. — [Company Name]"
- Configurable: owner can enable/disable per job type
- Requires Jobber or HCP job to be active for the dispatch event

### Out of Scope for MVP

The following features are explicitly deferred to avoid scope creep and maintain launch timeline:

- **Route optimization / AI dispatch suggestions**: Significant ML complexity; not the core pain point for the target persona in Phase 1
- **Fuel card integration** (WEX, Comdata, EFS): High value for the fleet fuel intelligence angle but requires API partnerships and additional customer data model; Phase 3
- **Driver scoring / behavior monitoring** (harsh braking, speeding scores): Alert fatigue is a documented pain point; deliberately excluded from MVP to differentiate from Samsara's "nonstop safety alerts that cry wolf"
- **ELD or HOS compliance features**: Out of scope permanently for this product; target customers are legally exempt
- **ServiceTitan integration**: Larger enterprise FSM; outside the primary SMB segment for Phase 1
- **Insurance telematics / discount qualification**: Revenue opportunity but requires insurer partnerships; Year 2+
- **Multi-location / enterprise fleet management**: Target is 3–25 vehicle local fleets; enterprise features deferred indefinitely
- **Geofence-based time clock / payroll integration**: Adjacent to the core problem; creates feature creep into HR software territory
- **Two-way messaging (owner → driver in-app)**: Phone calls and SMS are sufficient for Phase 1; adds significant complexity to mobile app

- If <50% retain at 60 days → identify churn reason before Phase 2 investment; do not add hardware logistics without software validation

### Future Vision

**Year 1–2: Fleet Intelligence Layer**

Building on the GPS foundation, FleetLocal becomes the intelligence layer for local service fleets:
- **Fuel intelligence**: Integrate WEX/Comdata fuel cards; surface idle time + fuel card data to identify trucks/drivers leaking money
- **Driver performance signals**: Speed events and idle time (not "safety alerts") surfaced as monthly owner summary — not real-time noise
- **Predictive maintenance**: ML model that predicts maintenance needs from OBD-II data patterns across the fleet
- **Multi-vehicle dashboard**: Side-by-side monthly cost-per-vehicle comparison to identify outliers

**Year 2–3: Platform Ecosystem**

- **ServiceTitan integration**: Expand to mid-market service businesses using ServiceTitan ($350K ARR per customer)
- **Insurance partnership**: Telematics data licensed to fleet insurers for usage-based discount qualification — additional revenue stream
- **Expansion verticals**: Pest control, pool service, mobile vet, medical transport — all use Jobber/HCP, all need simple fleet GPS
- **White-label offering**: License platform to Jobber or HCP as their premium GPS solution — acquisition target pathway

**Long-Term Position (3–5 Years)**

FleetLocal's long-term ambition is to be the GPS layer natively embedded in the Jobber/HCP ecosystem — not a standalone GPS product but the fleet intelligence module of the FSM stack. This positions it either as the dominant independent GPS add-on for service businesses or as a compelling acquisition target for Jobber, HCP, or a larger FSM platform consolidator.

The exit scenario: at $5M ARR with 3,000+ customers, strong retention, and deep Jobber/HCP integration, FleetLocal is a natural tuck-in acquisition for any FSM platform looking to add fleet management without building it from scratch. Precedent: ServiceTitan's acquisition of GPS partners; Jobber's active integration marketplace investment.
