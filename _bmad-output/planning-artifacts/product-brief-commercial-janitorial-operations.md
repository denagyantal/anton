# Product Brief: Commercial Janitorial Operations Platform (JanOps)

---

## Executive Summary

Commercial janitorial companies — B2B operators cleaning office parks, industrial facilities, healthcare buildings, and schools — are running $100K–$5M businesses on spreadsheets and disconnected apps. The software market has failed them: enterprise platforms (Aspire, ServiceTitan) start at $15,000/year and require $5M+ revenue to justify. Lightweight tools (CleanBid, Clean Smarts) cover only one piece of the workflow. The category leader (Swept) has no invoicing. General FSM tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro) are residential-first and lack proof-of-service documentation.

**JanOps** is the first purpose-built, full-workflow operations platform designed exclusively for B2B commercial janitorial companies doing $100K–$5M in annual contract revenue. It covers the complete operational cycle — bid estimation → contract CRM → crew scheduling → QC inspection with proof-of-service → supply tracking — in a single platform priced at $99–149/mo or $149–199 LTD.

The core insight driving JanOps: commercial cleaning contracts are renewed (or lost) based on documented compliance. GPS-tagged proof-of-service — timestamped check-ins, photo documentation, client-shareable inspection reports — is migrating from competitive differentiator to contract requirement. No affordable platform makes this the centerpiece of its value proposition today. JanOps will own that position.

**Market Opportunity:** $2.4B janitorial software market growing at 13.3% CAGR; ~50,000 US target customers; no dominant mid-market platform. Path to $50K MRR within 12 months, $300K MRR within 36 months.

**Verdict:** BUILD — Score 93/105, Tier 1 Strong Opportunity.

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Commercial janitorial business owners operate sophisticated B2B service businesses — recurring contracts, multiple client sites, night-shift crews, and documented compliance requirements — using tools built for residential maid services or for enterprises with $5M+ revenue and a dedicated IT team.

The specific pain is multi-layered:

1. **Bid complexity is unsupported**: Pricing a 55,000 sq ft machine shop requires understanding soil level, building type, cleaning frequency, specialty floor care, and supply costs. No affordable platform implements the full commercial bid formula (sq ft ÷ production rate × burdened labor cost + supplies + overhead + margin). Operators underprice large bids or spend hours building custom Excel models.

2. **Multi-site contract management doesn't exist at SMB pricing**: One commercial client may have 12 buildings under a single contract. Every current tool — Jobber, CleanBid, even Swept — treats each building as a separate job, preventing contract-level reporting, billing aggregation, and scope-of-work management.

3. **Proof-of-service is the hidden contract retention lever**: Commercial facility managers increasingly demand documented evidence that cleaning occurred to standard. Without GPS-stamped, photo-documented inspection reports, operators have no defense when clients dispute service delivery — and contracts are lost. No current affordable platform makes proof-of-service its primary value proposition.

4. **The category leader (Swept) has zero invoicing**: Swept, the dominant commercial janitorial scheduling tool, requires every customer to maintain a completely separate billing workflow. This structural gap has persisted despite years of user requests.

5. **Night-shift crews work offline**: Night-shift commercial cleaners work in buildings without supervisors, often in basement areas with poor connectivity. Apps without offline mode fail for this workforce.

### Problem Impact

- Operators underprice commercial bids by 15–30%, leaving significant margin on the table
- Multi-site contract disputes result in client churn that a documented inspection trail would prevent
- 3–5 disconnected tools (CleanBid + Google Calendar + QuickBooks + WhatsApp + paper forms) create data re-entry, version chaos, and administrative overhead that scales as the business grows
- "Text + spreadsheet + phone notes = chaos" — direct quote from r/sweatystartup, validated across 6+ threads in a single IdeaFast scan
- The inability to produce professional compliance documentation locks operators out of higher-value healthcare and industrial contracts that require audit trails

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

| Solution | Core Gap |
|----------|----------|
| **Aspire (ServiceTitan)** | Starts at $15K–$100K+/yr. Built for $5M+ enterprises. Completely inaccessible for the target segment. |
| **Swept** | No invoicing (confirmed structural gap, years of user requests unaddressed). No bid calculator. No supply tracking. |
| **Janitorial Manager** | Desktop-heavy, outdated UI, limited mobile, slow feature velocity, opaque pricing that drives SMBs away. |

| **Clean Smarts** | Lightweight scheduling/time tracking only. No bid calculator. Terrible customer support (most-cited Capterra complaint). |
| **QuoteIQ** | Growing but primarily residential-positioned. Commercial contract billing (net-30, PO numbers) not properly supported. |
| **ZenMaid** | Explicitly residential-only. Payment processing failures are the #1 Capterra complaint. |

The gap is precise: no platform at $79–149/mo covers the complete commercial janitorial workflow for the $100K–$5M operator.

### Proposed Solution

**JanOps** — Purpose-built for B2B commercial janitorial companies at $99–149/mo (SaaS) or $149–199 (LTD).

Five integrated modules covering the complete operational workflow:

**Module 1 — Smart Bid Calculator**
Commercial-grade bid estimation: sq ft × soil level × building type × cleaning frequency → auto-calculated labor + supply cost + overhead + target margin. Export as professional PDF proposal. Supports specialty calculations (post-construction cleanup, machine shop soil loading, healthcare facility requirements). Eliminates the Excel model guesswork that causes operators to underprice large contracts.

**Module 2 — Contract CRM (Multi-Site)**
Client and contract management built for commercial reality: one client, multiple locations, one contract. Renewal date tracking, scope-of-work per site, contract value reporting across all sites. Net-30 invoicing with PO number support. The first affordable platform to model commercial cleaning contracts the way operators actually run them.

**Module 3 — Crew Scheduling (Night-Shift Aware)**
Night-shift optimized scheduling with mobile GPS check-in/out per location. Offline mode for basement/no-signal environments. Assignment by skill and certification (biohazard certified, healthcare facility trained). Automated no-show alerting when a scheduled crew hasn't checked in within the alert window — eliminating the daily risk of discovering a missed shift at 3 AM.

**Module 4 — QC Inspection (GPS Proof-of-Service)**
Per-cleaning-cycle inspection forms with GPS-tagged, timestamped photo documentation. Client-facing inspection reports auto-generated and emailed after each cycle. Client portal for 90-day inspection history. This is the category-defining feature: "the software that protects your contracts." Commercial clients renew based on documented compliance; JanOps makes that documentation automatic.

**Module 5 — Supply & Consumable Tracking**
Per-client supply usage tracking — chemicals, trash bags, paper products, specialty supplies. Low-stock alerts. Supply cost allocation to client contract for per-contract margin tracking. Closes the billing gap where supply pass-throughs are estimated rather than documented.

### Key Differentiators

1. **Commercial-only positioning** — Never mentions maid services or residential cleaning. Every marketing message, every UI label, every onboarding flow speaks the language of B2B commercial operators. Competitors blur this line; JanOps owns it.

2. **GPS proof-of-service as the primary value proposition** — Not a checkbox feature. The entire QC module is architected around making compliance documentation automatic and client-shareable. This is the contract renewal lever no competitor has made central.

3. **Integrated bid → invoice in one platform** — Swept's most persistent structural gap, unaddressed for years. JanOps covers the full revenue cycle from proposal to payment.

4. **Offline-first mobile crew app** — Built for the reality of night-shift work in basements and facilities with poor connectivity. Sync when online, function when not.

5. **Flat pricing by crew size** — $99/mo up to 15 cleaners, $149/mo up to 50 cleaners. Removes the per-user scaling fear that kills SMB adoption when crews grow.

6. **Multi-site contract model** — One client, multiple buildings, one contract. The only affordable platform that reflects how commercial cleaning contracts actually work.

**What motivates Marcus**: Winning larger contracts (he wants to break $1M), surviving two simultaneous labor shortages (post-COVID cleaner turnover, no-shows), and keeping his best clients — the ones paying $5K+/mo — from leaving over documentation disputes.

**Success with JanOps**: Marcus bids a new 40,000 sq ft pharmaceutical manufacturing facility using the smart calculator. He wins. The QC inspection reports auto-email the facility manager after every cycle. Six months later, when the contract comes up for renewal, he emails his account history — 180 inspection reports with GPS timestamps and photos. ### Secondary Users

**Crew Members (Mobile App Users)**

Night-shift cleaners are the critical secondary user — they determine whether JanOps survives its first 30 days. If the crew doesn't adopt the mobile app, the owner abandons the platform.

Key requirements for crew adoption:
- One-tap GPS check-in/out — no training required
- Offline mode mandatory (basements, poor signal, industrial facilities)
- Photo upload for inspection checklists must work even with slow connections
- SMS notification fallback for schedule updates when app notifications fail
- Language support (significant Spanish-speaking workforce in commercial cleaning)

The crew app UX must be simple enough for a non-tech-native night-shift worker to use on their first shift with zero training. This is the single largest product risk — crew adoption failure ends accounts in 30 days.

**Bookkeepers / Office Managers**

Some JanOps customers (primarily Scalers and Franchise Operators) have a part-time bookkeeper or office manager who handles invoicing and billing. These users need:
- Read access to contract billing data without full admin access
- QuickBooks/Xero export for existing accounting workflows
- Invoice approval workflow before sending to clients

**Commercial Facility Managers (Client-Facing Portal Users)**

The facility manager at the client company is the ultimate proof-of-service audience. They need:
- Read-only client portal with inspection history
- Downloadable compliance reports for audits
- Real-time access to scheduled cleaning dates and completion confirmation

This user doesn't pay for JanOps — they're the reason the cleaning company's clients renew contracts. The client portal is a retention mechanism for the primary user, not a separate product.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

**MVP Sprint 1 (Weeks 1–4): The Bid-Win Module**

The first shipped feature must solve the highest-frequency pain point and deliver standalone value — even before the rest of the platform exists. The Smart Bid Calculator + Contract CRM enables an operator to generate a professional bid proposal and manage the resulting contract without any other module.

*Smart Bid Calculator*
- Input: sq footage, building type (office/industrial/healthcare/post-construction), soil level (light/medium/heavy), cleaning frequency, number of floors, special areas (restrooms, kitchens, loading docks)
- Processing: built-in production rate table by building type + soil level; burdened labor cost input by market; supply cost estimation by facility type
- Output: line-item cost breakdown + total price + margin calculation + professional PDF proposal template
- Export: branded PDF proposal with company logo, scope of work, pricing, terms

*Contract CRM (Multi-Site)*
- Client record with primary contact, billing address, contract start/end, renewal date
- Multiple site locations under one client/contract
- Per-site scope-of-work documentation (what's included, frequency, specialty services)
- Contract value aggregation across all sites
- Renewal reminder notifications (90/60/30 days before expiration)
- Basic activity log (notes, calls, visits)

**MVP Sprint 2 (Weeks 5–8): The Crew Module**

*Crew Scheduling*
- Calendar view of all scheduled jobs by date, crew, and site
- Assignment of cleaners to specific sites/shifts
- Night-shift aware scheduling (displays in local time; handles PM → AM shifts without date confusion)
- Automated no-show alerting (configurable window: "alert me if Crew A hasn't checked in 30 min after scheduled start")

*Mobile Crew App (iOS + Android)*
- GPS check-in/out per job site (geofenced to client address)
- **Offline mode**: jobs download to device; check-ins queue and sync when signal returns
- Schedule display for the current day
- Simple messaging (owner → crew broadcast; crew → owner)
- SMS notification fallback for crews who don't open the app

**MVP Sprint 3 (Weeks 9–12): The Proof Module**

*QC Inspection with GPS Proof-of-Service*
- Per-site inspection checklist templates (customizable per client contract requirements)
- Photo capture per checklist item with automatic GPS tagging and timestamp
- Inspection completion triggers automatic client-facing PDF report generation
- Client report auto-email: "Your [Site Name] cleaning inspection report for [Date]"
- Inspection history dashboard (owner view: all sites, all dates)
- Client portal: read-only view of inspection reports for facility managers

**MVP Sprint 4 (Weeks 13–16): The Revenue Module**

*Invoicing & Billing*
- Invoice generation from completed contract/site (auto-populated from contract scope + completed inspections)
- Net-30 invoicing support with PO number field (commercial contract requirement)
- Invoice PDF export + email to client billing contact
- Basic payment status tracking (sent / paid / overdue)
- QuickBooks Online integration (export invoices; import payments)

*Supply Tracking (Lightweight)*
- Per-client supply log (add supply usage by type and quantity)
- Supply cost allocation to client contract
- Basic low-stock alert (manual threshold setting)

### Out of Scope for MVP

The following are explicitly deferred to post-MVP to maintain build discipline:

- **AI-assisted bid generation** (floor plan photo → auto-calculated bid): powerful future feature, requires ML infrastructure not needed at launch
- **Robotic cleaning equipment tracking**: 32% of BSCs plan robotic adoption in 2026, but this is a Phase 2 expansion
- **Healthcare compliance documentation module** (HIPAA-adjacent facility audit trails): high-value vertical expansion for post-MVP
- **Multi-company / franchise network view**: valuable for franchise operators at scale; deferred until franchise channel is validated
- **Zapier / API integration**: post-MVP; validate core value before building integration ecosystem
- **Advanced analytics dashboard** (labor efficiency, margin analysis by service type): Phase 2 after sufficient data accumulates
- **Native desktop app**: web + mobile covers the workflow; desktop is legacy
- **Payroll integration**: adjacent to crew management but out of scope for MVP

**Rationale for scope discipline**: The research confirms that the primary churn risk is crew adoption failure in the first 30 days. Every scope addition that delays getting the offline-capable mobile crew app into the hands of night-shift cleaners increases this risk. Launch with bid + contract + scheduling + QC + invoicing. Everything else is Phase 2.

### MVP Success Criteria

The MVP is validated and ready to scale when:

1. **Crew adoption**: 40% of active accounts have crews using the mobile app daily by Day 30 of activation
2. **Bid conversion**: 70%+ of accounts that open the bid calculator export at least one PDF proposal within their first 14 days
3. **Proof-of-service activation**: 50%+ of accounts have at least one QC inspection report generated within 60 days of activation
4. **Retention**: Month 6 MRR retention rate > 85% of Month 1 subscribers
5. **LTD demand signal**: 250 AppSumo LTD sales within 60 days of launch confirms segment appetite
6. **Unprompted advocacy**: 1+ organic mentions per week in r/sweatystartup, Facebook Janitorial Business Owners group, or BSCAI forums by Month 4

If crew adoption (criterion 1) is below 30% at Month 3, that is the primary product risk to address before any new feature development.
