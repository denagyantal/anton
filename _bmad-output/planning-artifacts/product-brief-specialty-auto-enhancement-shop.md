# Product Brief: ShopShield — Specialty Auto Enhancement Shop Software

---

## Executive Summary

ShopShield is a purpose-built shop management platform for PPF (paint protection film), window tint, ceramic coating, and vinyl wrap installers. It enters a market at a rare inflection point: Urable, the dominant CRM for this segment, was acquired by PE-backed Fullsteam in May 2025, triggering price increases to $70-$183/mo and community distrust — creating an 18-month churn window that is active right now.

No single affordable tool covers the full PPF+tint+ceramic+wrap workflow at under $70/month. ShopShield fills that gap at $49/month flat, with a one-time $249 LTD (Lifetime Deal) on AppSumo as the primary acquisition lever. The LTD channel for this vertical is completely uncrowded — no competitor has launched on AppSumo.

The core differentiators are: (1) price leadership at $49/mo vs. $99-$299/mo for purpose-built full-stack alternatives, (2) first-mover AppSumo LTD positioning, (3) auto-generated warranty certificates critical for PPF/ceramic warranty compliance, and (4) a roadmap-defined 3D PPF vehicle template library — the single most-cited unmet need across the entire market, unbuilt by any competitor.

This is a bootstrapped, boring-business SaaS play. The TAM is bounded (~20,000-30,000 US specialty shops), but highly accessible via organized Facebook communities and YouTube creator channels. With realistic 10% US penetration plus international expansion to UK/AU/CA, this is a $1.5-2.5M ARR business with a clear path to $10M ARR at 20%+ penetration.

**Launch window: Q4 2026 — Q1 2027. This is the peak of the Urable churn window.**

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Specialty auto enhancement shops — those offering PPF, window tint, ceramic coating, and vinyl wrap — have no affordable, purpose-built shop management platform. The incumbent (Urable) was acquired by PE-backed Fullsteam in May 2025, sending prices from accessible to $70-$183/month. The 8-10 new entrants spawned by this acquisition event all charge $99-$299/month. No full-stack solution exists under $70/month.

These are not general detailers. Their workflow is fundamentally different:
- Multi-day PPF installations (vehicle prep → template cutting → installation → cure → delivery/pickup)
- Manufacturer-backed warranty certificates required at delivery (STEK, Xpel, Gyeon, 3M)
- Film inventory tracked at roll level by width, brand, and remaining footage
- Before/after photo documentation per job for warranty evidence and social media
- High-ticket per-job revenue ($500-$5,000+) with low job volume (2-8 per week)

No existing tool handles this workflow at a price point accessible to 1-5 bay specialty shops — which represent the vast majority of the market.

### Problem Impact

**For shop owners:**
- $70-$220/month software costs have doubled since the Fullsteam acquisition on a service that many describe as getting worse, not better
- Manual warranty certificate generation in Word/PDF = liability risk in disputes and professional image damage
- Spreadsheet-based inventory management leads to PPF roll waste ($300-$800/roll) and surprise stockouts before major installs
- Job status chaos on multi-day installs: technicians don't know what phase a vehicle is in without chasing the shop owner
- Lost revenue from missed maintenance reminder opportunities (2-year ceramic coating renewal, annual PPF maintenance detailing)

**For the market:**
- A growing industry (PPF market CAGR 5-6.5%, heading toward $943M globally by 2034) is being forced to either overpay for software or use general tools that don't fit their workflow
- The organized community of PPF/tint/ceramic/wrap installers is already primed to find alternatives — they discuss Urable openly in Facebook groups with 10,000+ members

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

| Tool | Why It Falls Short |
|------|-------------------|
| **Urable** | PE-acquired; $70-$183/mo with ongoing hidden rate increase risk; community trust eroded |
| **SalesThumb** | Purpose-built but $99-$599/mo — out of reach for 1-5 bay shops |
| **Wraptor** | Wrap-first, $99-$199/mo; lacks ceramic-specific features; wrap shops only |
| **Wrapstart** | $199-$299/mo — 4-6x the right price for a 2-bay shop |
| **TintWiz** | Tint-only; does not support ceramic coating or vinyl wrap |
| **RINZ** | $9.99/mo but feature-limited; no warranty certs, no multi-day job tracking |
| **QuoteIQ** | CRM-focused; lacks PPF/ceramic specialty workflow depth |
| **DetailPilot** | Mobile detailing focus; not shop-based enhancement workflows |

**Root cause:** Every purpose-built tool either lacks feature depth (for the full PPF+tint+ceramic+wrap stack) or prices out the core market segment (1-5 bay specialty shops). No one has launched on AppSumo. No one has shipped 3D PPF vehicle templates. The Urable churn window is wide open.

### Proposed Solution

**ShopShield** — the Urable replacement that PE-owned Fullsteam won't build.

A flat-rate shop management platform built specifically for PPF, window tint, ceramic coating, and vinyl wrap shops. The core thesis is simple: provide feature parity with Urable at half the price, then build the differentiated features (3D PPF templates, multi-day bay view) that no competitor has shipped.

**Pricing:**
- $49/mo for 1-3 bays (beats 9 of 11 purpose-built competitors)
- $89/mo for 4-10 bays
- $249 LTD AppSumo (1-3 bays, lifetime updates) — first in the vertical

**MVP core (4-6 weeks):**
1. Multi-stage job card: deposit → material order → installation → QC → delivery
2. Before/after photo documentation per job (warranty evidence + social sharing)
3. Warranty certificate auto-generation: PDF with VIN, service, install date, warranty period
4. Customer vehicle history with warranty status dashboard
5. Basic inventory: PPF roll tracking, tint film by shade %
6. Online booking with deposit collection

### Key Differentiators

1. **Price leadership**: $49/mo flat undercuts every full-stack purpose-built competitor by 50%+. When a shop owner compares ShopShield directly against SalesThumb ($99+) or Wrapstart ($199+), the choice is obvious for a 1-3 bay operator.

2. **LTD first-mover on AppSumo**: No auto enhancement shop management tool exists on AppSumo. The AppSumo audience of "boring business" operators is exactly the demographic. A $249 LTD converts the ongoing pricing objection to a one-time purchase. Zero competition in this channel.

3. **Warranty certificate auto-generation in MVP**: This is table-stakes for PPF and ceramic coating shops that honor manufacturer warranties (STEK, Xpel, 3M, Gyeon). Currently managed via Word doc templates or not at all — liability risk on every job. Shipping this in MVP, when RINZ and most lower-tier tools lack it, creates an immediate functional moat.

4. **3D PPF vehicle template library (Phase 2)**: The #1 confirmed unmet need across the entire market. No competitor has shipped it. Full-vehicle PPF quoting currently takes 30+ minutes per job in Excel and Word. A visual vehicle template selector (front end, full hood, full car packages) with pre-set package pricing cuts quote time to under 5 minutes. Defensible Phase 2 moat that legacy codebases will struggle to replicate.

5. **Community positioning as the independent alternative**: The specialty shop community actively distrusts PE-backed software ("Urable sold out"). ShopShield enters as the independent, installer-first alternative. Authentic community presence in PPF Facebook groups and YouTube channels builds trust that advertising cannot buy.

## Target Users

**Persona 1: Marcus — Solo PPF Installer / 1-Bay Shop Owner**

Marcus is 32, runs a 1-bay PPF and ceramic coating shop out of a leased bay in a commercial auto center. He does 2-3 high-ticket installs per week ($800-$2,500 each), mostly luxury vehicle owners referred by a local dealership. He learned the trade through YouTube tutorials and a STEK certified installer training program. He still uses a combination of Google Sheets for scheduling, Square for payments, and Word for warranty certificates — he calls it his "three-app nightmare."

He joined the PPF Installers Network Facebook group after getting certified and that's where he heard about Urable. He tried it at $45/mo (old pricing) but after the Fullsteam acquisition his price jumped to $89/mo and he started shopping for alternatives. He's been in that Facebook group asking "what is everyone using now?" — and he got 47 replies.

**What Marcus needs:** Online booking that collects a deposit automatically, warranty certs that look professional without manual work, photo storage per job for disputes and Instagram, and a job card he can check on his phone between installs.

**His "aha!" moment:** The first time a customer dispute about their ceramic coating coverage is resolved instantly because ShopShield has the before/after photos, the warranty cert PDF, and the VIN on file — in under 30 seconds.

### Secondary Users

**Installer / Technician (non-owner)**

In multi-bay shops, technicians use the job card to see their assignments, update stage status (installation complete → QC ready), and attach before/after photos from the shop floor. They don't need admin access or reporting — they need a mobile-friendly job view that tells them what vehicle is next and what phase it's in.

**Customers (self-serve booking + status)**

Shop customers book appointments and pay deposits via the online booking flow. They receive warranty certificates via email at job completion and can view their vehicle's service history via a customer portal. High-end customers with $5,000+ PPF installs care deeply about documentation and warranty evidence — this is a retention and trust mechanism, not just a feature.

**Film/PPF Distributors (future)**

STEK, Xpel, 3M, and Gyeon distributors maintain networks of certified installers. A partnership integration where distributors can see which ShopShield shops are low on inventory (opt-in) creates a B2B referral loop. This is Phase 3+ but shapes the data model early.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

The MVP covers the absolute minimum needed to replace Urable's core workflow for a 1-5 bay specialty shop. Each feature directly addresses a confirmed pain point from the market research.

**Feature 1: Multi-Stage Job Card**
The heartbeat of the platform. Every job flows through defined stages: Deposit Collected → Materials Ordered → Installation Scheduled → Installation In Progress → Quality Check → Ready for Delivery/Pickup → Delivered. Each stage change is timestamped. Shop owners see all jobs on a board view (Kanban-style) grouped by stage. Technicians can update stages from mobile. Stage transitions can trigger automations (e.g., QC stage triggers a customer text: "Your vehicle is almost ready!").

*Why MVP-critical:* This is the core workflow replacement for Urable. Without it, there's no product.

**Feature 2: Before/After Photo Documentation**
Each job has an attached photo gallery. Photos can be added from mobile (on the shop floor) or desktop. Before photos are captured at intake; after photos at QC or delivery. Photos are stored per job, associated with vehicle VIN, and accessible for warranty disputes or social sharing. Storage per shop: 10GB on base plan.

*Why MVP-critical:* Warranty protection and social proof. Shop owners cite this as a primary need in every community discussion about Urable.

**Feature 3: Warranty Certificate Auto-Generation**
One-click PDF generation at job completion. Certificate includes: shop logo, customer name, vehicle (year/make/model/VIN), service type (PPF brand/grade, ceramic brand/tier, tint brand/shade), installation date, warranty terms, and installer certification. PDF is emailed to customer automatically and stored on the job record. Shop owner can customize their standard warranty language per service type.

*Why MVP-critical:* This is the differentiator vs. RINZ and lower-tier tools. It's also a compliance necessity for shops offering manufacturer-backed warranties. It generates an immediately visible "aha!" moment in the trial period.

**Feature 4: Customer Vehicle History**
Customer profiles linked to vehicle records (VIN-based). Each vehicle shows all historical jobs, warranty status, photo galleries, and upcoming maintenance reminders. Shop owners can search by vehicle owner, VIN, or license plate. This data becomes the switching cost moat — once a shop has 6 months of job history in ShopShield, they won't leave.

*Why MVP-critical:* Switching cost and retention foundation. Builds over time — but needs to be in place from day one to accumulate.

**Feature 5: Basic Inventory Tracking**
PPF rolls tracked by brand, type (TPU/PVC), width, and remaining footage. Tint film tracked by brand, shade %, and roll quantity. Low-stock alerts when a roll drops below a user-defined threshold. Usage is logged per job (how much film was consumed). No automatic purchase order generation in MVP — just visibility.

*Why MVP-critical:* Waste reduction and stockout prevention. High-ticket PPF rolls ($400-$700 each) make this financially meaningful within the first month.

**Feature 6: Online Booking + Deposit Collection**
Embeddable booking widget for shop website or standalone booking page (ShopShield-hosted). Customer selects service type, preferred dates, provides vehicle info, and pays a deposit (configurable: flat $X or % of estimated job value). Deposit is collected via Stripe. Job is automatically created in ShopShield from the booking. Confirmation email sent to customer; notification sent to shop owner.

*Why MVP-critical:* This is the revenue-generating moment for shop owners. First deposit collected via ShopShield is the proof-of-ROI moment that converts trial-to-paid.

**The boundary rule for MVP**: If a feature doesn't directly (1) replace a workflow Marcus or Dana currently does in Urable, or (2) provide something Urable explicitly failed to deliver (warranty certs, affordable pricing), it waits.

### MVP Success Criteria

The MVP is validated and ready to scale past the LTD launch when:

1. **Trial conversion ≥ 25%** — 1 in 4 trial shops converts to paid within 14 days. Indicates the product solves the core pain point without requiring heavy hand-holding.

2. **Warranty cert adoption ≥ 70%** among active shops — The core differentiator is in use. If shops aren't generating certs, the value proposition is not landing.

3. **Online booking deposit capture** — At least 50% of active shops have used the booking widget to collect at least one deposit. This validates the "pays for itself" ROI story.

4. **NPS ≥ 50 from first 50 customers** — Community word-of-mouth is the primary growth lever. NPS ≥ 50 means enough promoters to drive organic PPF Facebook group mentions.

5. **Zero critical data loss incidents** — Job records, photos, warranty certs are mission-critical. Any data loss in MVP would destroy the community reputation the go-to-market strategy depends on.

**Go/no-go for AppSumo launch**: All 5 criteria met with ≥30 active paying shops before submitting AppSumo application. AppSumo requires demonstrated product-market fit.

### Future Vision

**Phase 2 (Months 3-6): The PPF Quoting Revolution**

The 3D PPF vehicle template library is the product's long-term defensible moat. Using partnerships with existing template providers (DAP Templates, STEK Pattern Portal), ShopShield surfaces a visual vehicle selection interface where shop owners select: vehicle make/model/year → coverage zones (front end only, full hood, full front, full vehicle) → film grade → instantly generates a quoted price from pre-configured rate cards.

This cuts PPF quote time from 30+ minutes to under 5 minutes. It becomes the reason a shop that's happy with SalesThumb switches to ShopShield. No competitor has shipped this. It becomes the product's headline feature at SEMA 2027.

Also in Phase 2: multi-day bay scheduling view (vehicles shown across a weekly calendar by installation phase), and automated maintenance reminder sequences (triggered by install date + warranty period).

**Phase 3 (Months 6-12): The Network Effects Play**

Multi-location support unlocks franchise and chain shop customers (higher ARPU). Commission tracking makes ShopShield the HR system for installer shops. Advanced analytics (margin by service, installer efficiency, revenue trends) gives owners the business intelligence they can't get from a whiteboard.

Supplier integration (Xpel, STEK, 3M distributor order connections) turns ShopShield from a CRM into a supply chain node — creating B2B referral loops with distributors who have direct relationships with every certified installer in their network.

**Phase 4 (Months 9-18): International + Platform**

UK, AU, and CA markets have extremely sparse specialty shop software options. Local Stripe/Square integration + VAT/GST tax support opens these markets with minimal incremental build. Partnering with STEK UK, Xpel Australia, and 3M Canada distributor networks provides direct installer access.

Longer-term (Years 2-3): a manufacturer warranty registry — where STEK, Xpel, and Gyeon warranty claims are managed through ShopShield as the authorized installer portal. This creates a defensible platform play where film manufacturers have direct incentive to recommend ShopShield to their installer networks. At scale, this becomes the Google Maps of the PPF industry: installer directory, warranty registry, certified shop search.

**The 5-year vision**: ShopShield is the operating system for the specialty auto enhancement industry — not just a CRM, but the layer connecting shop owners, film manufacturers, distributors, and vehicle owners. The warranty cert database becomes the authoritative record of every PPF/ceramic installation in North America and beyond.

## Technical Direction (Informing Architecture)

This section is non-binding for the product brief but captures the key technical constraints and preferences identified in market research to inform subsequent architecture work.

**Stack considerations:**
- React frontend (mobile-first for on-floor technician use)
- Node.js or similar backend
- PostgreSQL for relational data (job history, inventory, customer-vehicle relationships)
- S3-compatible storage for photo assets
- Stripe for payment processing (booking deposits, subscription billing)
- pdf-lib or similar for warranty certificate PDF generation
- Resend or similar for transactional email (warranty certs, booking confirmations)

**Key architectural decisions:**
- VIN as the central entity linking customer + vehicle + jobs + warranties + photos
- Job stage model must be extensible (shops will want to add custom stages in Phase 2)
- Photo storage must be cost-contained from day one (10GB/shop base limit)
- Booking widget must be embeddable (iframe) for shop websites

**Non-negotiable from day one:**
- Data portability: shops can export all their data (jobs, photos, warranty certs, customer records) at any time — this is an explicit anti-Urable/Fullsteam trust signal
- Mobile-responsive job board: technicians update stages from phones on the shop floor
- SSL/security: shops store VINs, customer names, payment records — basic compliance is table-stakes
