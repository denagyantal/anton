# Product Brief: MHPDesk — Mobile Home Park RUBS Compliance Platform

---

## Executive Summary

MHPDesk is the first affordable cloud SaaS platform designed specifically for small and mid-size mobile home park (MHP) operators — the only tool that combines MH-native property management with a built-in RUBS Compliance Shield at $49–$149/month.

**The Problem:** Six states (Minnesota, California, Ohio, Colorado, Arizona, Washington) enacted or enforced major RUBS (Ratio Utility Billing System) restrictions in 2025–2026. Operators who have used RUBS for years now face fines of $50,000–$500,000, class-action lawsuits, and lender audits — yet no affordable software exists to help them document compliance. The only compliant tools (Yardi MH Manager, ManageAmerica) are enterprise-only platforms costing $1,500–$5,000/month. The only affordable MH-native tool (LotSidekick, $49–$149/month) explicitly excludes RUBS compliance.

**The Solution:** MHPDesk closes this gap with a flat-rate, compliance-first SaaS platform offering: lot management, recurring billing, state-specific RUBS compliance documentation, one-click compliance reports, and tenant disclosure generation — all designed for non-technical operators who need to be up and running in under 30 minutes.

**The Opportunity:** 44,000 MHPs in the US; 76% of single-park owners using no software; $700M–$2.1B software TAM; a hard "before 2027" compliance window creates purchase urgency that typical SaaS products never achieve. MHPDesk enters as a Tier 1 opportunity with first-mover compliance positioning, a clear community-led go-to-market path, and defensible data moat via audit trail accumulation.

**Business Model:** Flat-rate SaaS at $49/month (up to 50 lots), $99/month (up to 100 lots), $149/month (up to 150 lots). Optional hybrid LTD launch via AppSumo ($99–$299 one-time for core PM features, $19–$29/month compliance subscription). Target: 50 customers at month 3 ($3,500 MRR), 200 customers at month 6 ($15,000 MRR), 500 customers at month 12 ($40,000+ MRR).

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Mobile home park operators managing 10–150 lots face an acute compliance crisis that no affordable software addresses. State legislatures are moving aggressively on utility billing practices:

- **Minnesota** banned RUBS for electricity billing (January 2025) and mandated specific allocation formulas for water and gas
- **Ohio Supreme Court** ruled that submetering companies in manufactured housing communities are public utilities subject to rate oversight (April 2026)
- **California** property management company paid a $495,000 settlement for using RUBS to disguise rent increases (October 2025); Los Angeles is considering a full RUBS ban
- **Colorado, Arizona, Washington** enacted new restrictions in 2025; Florida, New York, New Jersey, and Michigan have active pending legislation

For MHP operators who have relied on RUBS for utility cost recovery — often for decades — this regulatory wave means the exposure is not theoretical. It is live, material, and immediate. The "before 2027" compliance window cited by industry analysts is a genuine forcing function, not a soft suggestion.

The operators most at risk are the approximately 33,400 small parks (under 150 lots) currently running on spreadsheets, paper records, or general property management tools. None of these approaches generate the state-mandated tenant disclosure notices, billing methodology documentation, or audit-ready compliance trail that regulators now require.

### Problem Impact

**For small/mid-size MHP operators:**
- Financial exposure: One RUBS compliance fine = $50,000–$500,000 — dwarfing years of software costs
- Operational risk: Class-action lawsuits from tenants challenging billing practices; lenders pulling credit for compliance gaps
- Competitive disadvantage: Institutional buyers (PE-backed, REITs) entering at +47.1% YoY transaction volume already have compliance infrastructure; independent operators do not
- Data liability: Years of undocumented RUBS billing creates retroactive exposure that grows worse with each billing cycle

**For the market:**
- 76% of single-park owners (est. 33,400 operators) use no property management platform
- 15,000+ operators are in active high-risk RUBS states with no compliance tools available at their price point
- Spreadsheets cannot generate the audit-ready documentation trails that state compliance now requires

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

| Category | Vendor | Price | RUBS Compliance | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Yardi MH Manager | ~$1,500–$5,000/mo | Partial | 10–30x too expensive; 8–20 week implementation; requires IT staff |
| Enterprise | ManageAmerica | Quote-only | Yes | Quote-only pricing friction; complex implementation; minimum ~$1,000+/mo |
| Mid-market | Rent Manager | ~$200–$450/mo | No | No RUBS compliance features; going quote-only in 2026 |
| General PM | DoorLoop, AppFolio, Buildium | $59–$400/mo | No | Not MH-native; compliance documentation nonexistent; billing rules break when state law changes |
| Small operator | LotSidekick | $49–$149/mo | No | Closest competitor at matching price point; explicitly excludes RUBS compliance |
| Utility billing only | Vitality IO, Sentinel | $0.50–$9.95/unit/mo | Partial | Multifamily-focused; not MH-specific; requires separate PM tool; no MH compliance nuances |

The fundamental gap: **No product in the $49–$199/month range combines MH-native property management with state-specific RUBS compliance documentation.** This is not a feature gap — it is a product category gap.

### Proposed Solution

**MHPDesk** — A compliance-first, MH-native SaaS platform for operators of 10–150 lots.

The core value proposition is framed as insurance, not software: "One RUBS fine costs $50,000–$500,000. MHPDesk costs $99/month." This reframes the purchase decision from "do I need better property management software?" to "can I afford NOT to have compliance documentation?"

**Core platform capabilities (MVP):**
1. **Lot Management** — Tenant records, lot assignments, lease dates, contact info, dual lot+home asset tracking
2. **Billing Engine** — Recurring lot rent invoices, automatic late fees, cash/check/money order recording (matches actual MHP tenant payment behavior)
3. **RUBS Compliance Mode** — State-specific allocation calculators (sq ft, occupancy, bedroom count), compliance threshold alerts, audit trail storage
4. **Compliance Documentation Generator** — State-mandated tenant disclosure notices auto-generated; billing methodology documentation; one-click monthly compliance report PDF
5. **Spreadsheet Import** — CSV import of existing tenant/payment data; sub-30-minute onboarding target

**The RUBS Compliance Shield** is the defining differentiator: a continuously updated state rules engine that monitors an operator's billing practices against current law, alerts before approaching legal thresholds, and generates the specific documentation formats each state requires. No other affordable tool does this.

### Key Differentiators

1. **First and only affordable RUBS-compliant MH platform** — The market gap is structurally defined; no affordable tool occupies this position
2. **Compliance-first framing** — Buying MHPDesk is a risk management decision (avoid a $50K–$500K fine), not a software upgrade decision — dramatically higher purchase urgency and conversion
3. **State-specific rules engine** — Not generic RUBS math, but the specific allocation formulas, disclosure language, and documentation formats required by each high-risk state
4. **Audit-trail data moat** — Once an operator has 6–12 months of compliance audit trail data in MHPDesk, switching costs become enormous — their compliance history is the product
5. **Sub-30-minute onboarding** — Explicitly designed for non-technical, older operators who need to import from a spreadsheet and start billing on day one
6. **Flat-rate pricing** — Not per-unit pricing; operators with variable occupancy prefer predictable monthly costs; removes the "is it worth it this month?" calculation
7. **Community trust pathway** — BiggerPockets, MobileHomeUniversity, and state MHA associations provide direct acquisition channels that enterprise tools have never invested in

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

The MVP is defined by a single constraint: an operator must be able to sign up, import their existing data, and complete their first compliant billing cycle in under 30 minutes. Every feature either serves that journey or is deferred.

**Feature 1: Lot Management**
- Tenant record creation: name, contact info, move-in date, lease expiration
- Lot assignment: lot number, lot rent amount, lot size (sq ft) for RUBS allocation
- Multi-park support: operators with 2–3 parks manage all lots from one account
- Status tracking: active, delinquent, vacant, notice-to-vacate
- CSV import: upload spreadsheet of existing tenant data; system maps columns

**Feature 2: Billing Engine**
- Recurring lot rent invoices with configurable billing date and grace period
- Automatic late fee application (flat or percentage; configurable per park)
- Manual payment recording: cash, check, money order (critical for MHP tenant base)
- Basic payment history per tenant
- Month-end summary report: lots billed, collected, outstanding

**Feature 3: RUBS Compliance Mode (Core Differentiator)**
- Utility cost entry: operator enters master meter total for water, gas, and/or electricity each month
- Allocation method selection: sq ft, occupancy count, bedroom count, or hybrid
- State rules engine: system validates allocation method against selected state's current requirements
- Compliance threshold alerts: if billing approach approaches a legal threshold (e.g., CA cap on utility % of rent), system flags before the billing cycle runs
- Audit trail: every billing cycle's utility allocation calculation is stored with timestamp and methodology documentation

**Feature 4: Compliance Documentation Generator**
- State-specific tenant disclosure notice: auto-generated with each billing cycle in the exact format/language required by state law (where mandated)
- Billing methodology document: PDF that explains the operator's RUBS allocation approach, suitable for lender, insurance, or regulatory submission
- Monthly compliance report: one-click PDF summarizing the billing cycle, allocation calculations, compliance status, and any threshold alerts — the "show this to your regulator" document
- Document archive: all generated compliance documents stored and accessible by date

**Feature 5: Spreadsheet Import and Quick Start**
- CSV import for tenants (name, lot number, rent amount, move-in date, lot size)
- Template download: pre-formatted spreadsheet so operators know what columns to use
- Setup wizard: 5-step guided flow (add park → import tenants → configure billing → set up RUBS → run first cycle)
- Target: operator completes setup in under 30 minutes

**Feature 6: Basic Tenant Communication**
- Invoice delivery: email invoices to tenants with PDF attachments
- Disclosure notice delivery: compliance notices auto-attached to billing emails
- Late notice generation: standard late payment notice with correct state-required language
- Manual announcement: operator can send park-wide email to all tenants

### Out of Scope for MVP

The following features are explicitly deferred to keep MVP build time to 4–6 weeks and onboarding simple for non-technical operators:

- **Maintenance work orders / service requests** — Deferred to Month 2–4; operators can use email/phone for now
- **Online tenant payment portal** (ACH, credit card) — Deferred; MHP tenants predominantly pay by cash/check; adding payment processing adds complexity and compliance overhead; prioritized for Month 3–6
- **QuickBooks integration** — Deferred to Month 4–6; operators can export CSV and import to QuickBooks manually
- **Submetering hardware integration** — Deferred; some parks are moving to individual meters; IoT integration is a Month 12+ feature
- **Advanced financial reporting / lender packages** — Deferred to Month 6; basic rent roll export covers most lender needs in MVP
- **Home sales pipeline / chattel loan tracking** — Deferred; out of scope for initial compliance-focused positioning
- **Tenant background check / screening integration** — Deferred; not relevant to compliance-first MVP
- **Mobile app (iOS/Android)** — Deferred; responsive web app is sufficient for MVP; operators typically manage from desktop
- **Compliance rules for all 50 states** — MVP launches with 6 highest-risk states (MN, CA, OH, CO, AZ, WA); remaining states added incrementally
- **Automated utility meter reading / smart meter integration** — Deferred to Year 2; operators manually enter master meter readings for MVP
