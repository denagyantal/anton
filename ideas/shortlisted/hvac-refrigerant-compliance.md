## One-Line Pitch

Android-first offline-capable refrigerant log app for HVAC techs — "pay once, never worry about EPA audits again."

## Problem

Effective January 1, 2026, the EPA lowered the HFC refrigerant compliance threshold from 50 lbs to 15 lbs (Subpart C of the AIM Act), placing hundreds of thousands of additional systems under federal record-keeping requirements for the first time. Records must be kept for 3 years. A solo tech doing 8 calls/week generates 1,248+ compliance records over the retention window.

This is a direct forcing function for paper-to-digital transitions among HVAC techs who previously got away with clipboards:
- FieldPad (the leading solo HVAC compliance app) is **iPhone-only** — Android users (majority of HVAC techs) are unserved
- Refritrak focuses on refrigerant tracking but is limited in scope
- Zentid FSM has compliance modules but is a full FSM platform
- No tool combines offline-first mobile logging + pre-loaded EPA reg# database + auto-generated audit PDF at an indie price point

A solo tech doing 8 service calls/week with the 15-lb threshold could be on the hook for every call. Manual paper logs = compliance risk. One EPA audit = fines up to $44,000/day/violation for Section 608 violations.

## Product Concept: "CoolLog" (or "RefriLog" / "HVACCompliance")

**Core MVP (2-3 weeks)**:
1. **Offline-first job log** — create service record: date, location, equipment type, refrigerant type, amount added/removed, EPA reg#, tech EPA certification number
2. **Pre-loaded EPA database** — searchable refrigerant names → auto-populates EPA reg#, GWP, AIM Act classification
3. **3-year retention** — records stored locally + sync to cloud; auto-export to PDF for EPA audit
4. **Compliance summary** — monthly usage summary by refrigerant type; flags if approaching reporting thresholds
5. **Android-first** — offline-capable (works in basements, rooftop HVAC rooms with no cell signal)

**Phase 2**:
- Multi-tech shop: shared compliance dashboard, per-tech certification tracking
- Automated reminder for tech certification renewals (EPA Section 608 certification expiry)
- Customer-facing service record export (leaves behind compliance certificate for commercial customers)
- Integration with FieldPad, Jobber for HVAC-specific customers

**Pricing**:
- $19/mo solo tech (unlimited logs)
- $49/mo small shop (up to 5 techs)
- $49-79 LTD AppSumo launch — "Pass your EPA audit"

## Key Differentiators
1. **Android-first** — FieldPad is iPhone-only; 65%+ of HVAC techs use Android
2. **Offline-capable** — works in mechanical rooms, basements, rooftops with no cell signal
3. **Pre-loaded EPA database** — refrigerant → EPA reg# → AIM Act classification pre-filled; no manual lookup
4. **One-tap audit export** — auto-generates state-compliant EPA audit PDF in one tap
