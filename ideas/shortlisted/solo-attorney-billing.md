## One-Line Pitch
AI-assisted billing for solo attorneys doing PI, family law, estate, or criminal defense — auto-generate time entries from calendar events and email threads, produce invoices in one click, at $39/mo vs. Clio's $79–200+/user/mo.

## Problem
Solo attorneys and 2-attorney firms are the largest segment of the US legal market by firm count, yet every legal practice management tool is optimized for the 10+ attorney firm. The specific pain:

1. **Clio Essentials at $79/user/mo** doubles to $150–200/mo with add-ons (document automation, advanced reporting, marketing suite) — all gated at higher tiers
2. **Time tracking leakage** — attorneys doing PI/family/estate/criminal defense lose 20–40% of billable time by not recording it contemporaneously; billing from memory = undercharging
3. **Overcomplexity** — "Many practitioners wonder whether they're paying for features they'll never touch" (CaseTempo.com)
4. **MyCase/PracticePanther** at $39–49/user/mo are better but still require significant onboarding; feature-heavy enough to intimidate new solo practices

The specific opportunity: a tool that learns from the attorney's calendar (Google Calendar/Outlook) and email threads, auto-drafts billable time entries, and asks "did you do this?" at end of day — reducing billing leakage without requiring attorneys to remember to log every call.

## Market Evidence
- 500,000+ solo attorneys in the US (by far the largest segment)
- Clio's $1B+ valuation + extensive G2 reviews prove massive market
- CaseTempo ("built for solos; 10-minute onboarding") positioned exactly at this gap

- Solo attorneys doing simple practice areas (PI, family, estate) don't need full case management

## Key Differentiators
1. **AI time-entry capture** — reads Google Calendar and email metadata; auto-drafts time entries ("Reviewed settlement agreement with Client X — 1.2 hrs? [Approve / Edit]")
2. **15-minute onboarding** — designed to be operational in one session without an IT consultant
3. **Flat pricing, no per-matter fees** — $39/mo regardless of case count
4. **Simple PI/family/estate focus** — pre-built matter templates for common solo practice areas
5. **No bundled marketing suite** — no Clio Grow, no add-on CRM; just billing + matter tracking

## Product Concept
**MVP (4–6 weeks):**
- Matter management (create matter → client → billing type: hourly/flat/contingency)
- Time entry: manual + AI suggestions from Google Calendar sync
- Invoice generation (ABA-format time statements)
- Trust accounting (IOLTA basics — deposit/withdraw against client funds)
- Stripe payments on invoices
- Basic document storage per matter

**AI time entry (v2):**
- Google Calendar / Outlook integration
- End-of-day: "Today you had a 45-min call with [client], a 30-min court appearance, and drafted a 12-email thread. Here are your suggested time entries: [approve / edit]"

**Pricing:**
- $39/mo: up to 20 active matters; AI time entry; IOLTA basics
- $79/mo: unlimited matters + state court e-filing integration
- $79 LTD: 20 active matters, no AI time entry (AppSumo entry tier)
