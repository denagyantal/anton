## One-Line Pitch

Real-time performance pay dashboard for HVAC/plumbing/pest control technicians on GorillaDesk, PestPac, and simpler FSM tools — the ShareWillow for the shops ShareWillow won't touch.

## Problem

The trades have a structural labor shortage: 1.7 million skilled trade openings per year through 2035, only 55 trained workers per 100 needed. Shop owners can't grow without technicians, and they lose techs to competitors who offer better pay transparency.

ShareWillow ($8.5M seed, 8x revenue growth in 18 months) proved this market emphatically: connect to ServiceTitan/Jobber/HCP, calculate performance pay per job in real-time, let techs see their earnings job-by-job on a mobile app. The result: average technician earns $1,334/more per month on a performance plan.

**But ShareWillow requires ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro.** The 50,000+ pest control companies using GorillaDesk or PestPac can't use it. Neither can HVAC shops on FieldPulse, FieldEdge, or even the 75,000+ shops still on spreadsheets.

DASHP ($100K ARR, 1 founder) built exactly this for pest control commission tracking — and hit $100K ARR in a narrow niche. That's the validation pattern: vertical-specific commission tracking for field service = strong product-market fit even at micro-scale.

## Product Direction

**Wedge**: GorillaDesk commission tracker for pest control companies
- Connect to GorillaDesk API (read job completions + revenue per job per tech)
- Owner configures commission rules (% of job revenue, flat rate by service type, bonus thresholds)
- Tech sees real-time earnings in a simple mobile view job-by-job
- Owner dashboard: team leaderboard, per-tech performance trend, projected pay period earnings

**Differentiators vs ShareWillow**:
- GorillaDesk, PestPac, FieldRoutes (not just ST/Jobber/HCP)
- Simpler pricing ($49-79/mo flat vs. ShareWillow's enterprise model)
- Tech-facing mobile app is a recruiting tool ("See what you'd earn at our company")

**Expansion**: Add landscaping crew performance (route efficiency bonuses), HVAC flat-rate commission (% of invoice for each repair type), pool service upsell tracking.
