## One-Line Pitch
A CRM where the core object is the vehicle (not the customer) — purpose-built for auto detailers who service multiple cars per customer, track ceramic coating bay time, and handle mobile route clustering.

## Problem
Auto detailers track customers but not vehicles — yet a single customer brings 3 different cars over a year, each needing its own service history. Generic tools (Square, Jobber) don't understand the detailing-specific workflow: multi-step packages, ceramic coating bay time (2–3 days), on-site payment at job completion, and no-show rates 12–18% without SMS reminders. Mobile detailers need geographic clustering for routing (not generic route optimization). No tool natively handles "per-vehicle service history + package templates + mobile-first invoicing." Multiple 2026 industry comparison articles confirm vehicle-history gap is the #1 missing feature across all tools.

## Market Size
$15B+ US auto detailing industry growing ~6%/year. r/AutoDetailing has 200K+ members. No auto detailing software has ever been listed on AppSumo — first-mover opportunity.
