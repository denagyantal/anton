## One-Line Pitch

Real-time commission tracking for door-to-door sales teams — so reps knocking 10 hours in the Florida sun know exactly what they're earning, and managers don't spend Sundays in spreadsheets.

## Problem

Door-to-door sales operations in pest control, solar installation, home security, and roofing run entirely on spreadsheets. Sales reps work commission-only — they can knock 50 doors a day without any visibility into their earnings until the manager manually calculates payroll.

**The pain is acute**:
- Reps experience "pay anxiety" — 10 hours knocking doors with no real-time feedback on earnings
- Commission calculations are complex (tiered rates, cancellation clawbacks, territory splits, lead-vs-close credits)
- Managers spend Sunday nights in Excel building payroll
- Disputes over commission amounts are common and relationship-destroying
- Leaderboards and gamification (which motivate D2D reps) require real-time data nobody has

**DASHP** (dashp.io) built exactly this for pest control and reached $100K ARR bootstrapped solo. The founder's first sales meeting: the buyer asked "what is your company called and how much does it cost?" — textbook product-market fit signal. This exact problem exists in solar, home security, and roofing where D2D teams are even larger.

## Product Concept

**"CommRoute"** (or "KnockPay" / "RepTrack")

**Core MVP (3-4 weeks)**:
1. **Rep onboarding** — name, territory, commission structure (flat rate per close / tiered / percentage of sale)
2. **Job/sale logging** — rep logs sale from phone (address, service type, amount); manager can approve/reject
3. **Commission calculation** — real-time calculation based on configured structure; handles tiered rates, cancellation clawbacks (sale logged → cancelled within 10 days = clawback)
4. **Rep dashboard** — "My earnings today / this week / this month" = solves pay anxiety
5. **Manager dashboard** — all reps, total commissions due, payout schedule
6. **Payroll export** — CSV for Gusto/ADP/Quickbooks

**Phase 2**:
- GPS check-in (verify rep was at address)
- Leaderboard + gamification (rep ranking, daily standings)
- Daily payout report (text/email to rep at end of day)
- Multi-tier commission (team leads get override on reps they manage)
- API to pull from CRM (if company uses one)

**Pricing**:
- $20/rep/month (manager account free; minimum 5 reps)
- $99 for 1-month trial / $199 LTD for up to 10 reps (one-season option for seasonal businesses)

## Expansion Path (Post-Pest-Control)

The D2D commission pain exists identically in:
1. **Solar installation** — even larger rep counts; commission structures more complex (system size × $/watt)
2. **Home security** (ADT, Vivint dealers) — D2D with recurring contract commissions
3. **Roofing** — post-storm door-knocking with insurance commission structures
4. **Internet/cable** — door-to-door ISP sales reps
5. **Gym memberships** — floor sales teams

Same product, different commission structure templates per industry.

## Key Differentiators
1. **Real-time rep visibility** — solves the day-to-day pay anxiety that kills D2D team morale
2. **Cancellation clawback automation** — the most painful manual calculation (pest control common: customer cancels in first 30 days = commission clawback)
3. **Industry-specific commission templates** — "pest control package" vs "solar $/watt" vs "roofing insurance replacement"
4. **Daily earnings notification** — text/email to rep every evening = retention tool
