## One-Line Pitch
Certified payroll and Davis-Bacon compliance automation for the 1-10 person specialty trade subcontractor — reducing quarterly compliance from 14 hours to 27 minutes, at a price Trayd won't touch.

## Problem
Specialty trade subcontractors (electricians, ironworkers, plumbers, painters, concrete crews) who work on public/government projects face uniquely complex payroll requirements that QuickBooks cannot handle:

- **Davis-Bacon Act**: Federal projects require "prevailing wage" rates; records must prove compliance
- **Certified Payroll (WH-347)**: Weekly certified payroll reports must be submitted to federal/state agencies
- **Union rules**: Multi-rate dispatch (straight time, OT, DT), fringe benefit tracking, union steward hours
- **H-2B workers**: Seasonal visa workers require separate compliance documentation
- **Multi-state work**: Contractors crossing state lines face different prevailing wage rates per jurisdiction

Trayd (YC-backed) built an all-in-one back-office OS for specialty trade subs and reduced payroll processing from 14 hours to 27 minutes — achieving 600% YoY revenue growth. They raised $10M Series A in 3 weeks. The problem is Trayd targets 50+ person specialty trade companies. The 1-10 person sub is completely unserved.

## Product Concept

**Standalone wedge: Certified Payroll Generator (3-4 weeks)**
- Import hours from QuickBooks CSV or manual entry
- Select project (federal/state government contract)
- Apply prevailing wage rates by classification (journeyman, apprentice, foreman)
- Auto-generate WH-347 form (certified payroll report) as PDF
- Submit-ready output for federal contracting officer
- Pricing: $49/mo per project or $199 LTD for compliance templates

**Full product (V2, 3-4 months):**
- Payroll processing: straight time / OT / DT calculation
- Davis-Bacon compliance: prevailing wage rates by job classification + county
- Union fringe benefit tracking
- Multi-state prevailing wage compliance
- Certified payroll submission history and audit trail
- Employee/sub onboarding + I-9 / E-Verify integration

**Pricing:**
- $49/mo (unlimited projects, certified payroll generator only)
- $99/mo (full payroll processing + compliance)
- $199 LTD (compliance templates + WH-347 generator — AppSumo entry)
