## One-Line Pitch

Fleet account CRM for auto detailing businesses serving car dealerships, rental companies, and corporate fleets — one account, 20-100 vehicles, consolidated monthly billing, VIN-tagged service history.

## Problem

Auto detailing businesses that serve fleet clients operate under a fundamentally different model than consumer/retail detailers — yet every software tool in the space is built for the consumer appointment model.

**The fleet detailing workflow**:
- A dealership reconditioning client sends 50-300 units per month
- Each vehicle needs an individual work order (specific services, pre/post photos, damage notes)
- All vehicles belong to ONE billing account — the dealership gets ONE invoice/month
- Service history per VIN matters (paint correction status, ceramic coating applied, previous damage)
- Recurring weekly/monthly schedules for rental companies (Hertz, Enterprise) need predictable scheduling

**What existing tools do**: Every detailing tool (Jobber, OrbisX, QuoteIQ, DetailPro, ManageMart) is built around the customer-appointment model where one customer = one vehicle = one invoice. Fleet billing across 50-300 vehicles under one consolidated invoice requires manual "grouping hacks" — operators export CSVs and manually aggregate in Excel.

**Who has this problem**: Detailing businesses that service dealerships are doing serious B2B recurring revenue. A shop reconditioning 300 units/month at $50/vehicle = $15,000/month from a single fleet account. They need fleet account management, not appointment scheduling.

**Market size**: $4B+ dealership reconditioning market; 60,000+ auto detailing businesses in the US; fleet segment is the highest-revenue niche within detailing.

## Product Concept: "DetailFleet"

**Core MVP (4-5 weeks):**
- **Fleet Account CRM**: one account = one company (dealership, rental car co, corporate fleet); unlimited vehicles per account
- **Multi-vehicle work orders**: check in 20 vehicles in one visit; each vehicle gets its own service record (VIN, make/model, services performed, pre/post photos)
- **Consolidated monthly invoice**: all vehicles serviced in a month → single invoice for the fleet account; line items by vehicle or by service type
- **VIN-tagged service history**: per-vehicle log — when it was detailed, what services, paint correction status, ceramic coating dates, damage notes
- **Before/after photo documentation**: per vehicle, auto-attached to work order and accessible to fleet manager
- **Recurring schedule management**: weekly/monthly fleet runs visible on calendar with fleet account tag

**Pricing:**
- $49/mo — up to 2 fleet accounts (unlimited vehicles per account)
- $79/mo — up to 5 fleet accounts
- $149/mo — unlimited fleet accounts
- LTD: $129 (up to 3 fleet accounts) / $249 (unlimited)

## Key Differentiators

1. **Fleet account model** — one account → many vehicles → one invoice (vs. per-appointment model of all competitors)
2. **VIN service history** — each vehicle tracked across visits; useful for both detailer and fleet manager
3. **Before/after photo documentation** — per VIN, per visit; auto-emailed to fleet manager on completion
4. **Consolidated billing** — one invoice/month per fleet account; line items by vehicle or service type
