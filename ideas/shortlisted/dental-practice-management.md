## One-Line Pitch

Cloud-native dental practice OS for 1–3 chair offices that makes a single unbreakable promise: "Your data is always yours" — no exit fees, no export paywalls — while costing half the price of Dentrix.

## Problem

130,000 single and 2-dentist US practices are trapped in a dysfunctional software landscape:

- **Dentrix (Henry Schein)** ~$500/mo — practices use only 20% of features but pay for 100%; X-rays and imaging sold as separate add-ons; data migration costs are a hidden exit tax
- **Eaglesoft (Patterson)** ~$200/mo — reportedly transitioning to subscription-only, creating anxiety for perpetual license holders; desktop-bound legacy architecture
- **Curve Dental** ~$150/user/mo — 6+ hour downtime incidents documented in 2025–2026; data export requires a support call (effectively held hostage)
- **CareStack** — X-ray integration is a paid add-on; "consistently frustrating" UX reported across clinical workflows
- **Open Dental** — free but requires self-hosting or IT management; not cloud-native; community-based support only

The core pain: 4 disconnected systems (scheduling, billing, X-rays, reminders) with no integration, costing $400–700/mo combined. And when practices want to leave, they face data export ransoms.

## MVP Scope (6–8 weeks)

1. Appointment scheduling with chair/operatory view
2. Patient profiles + treatment notes + basic treatment planning
3. Insurance claim submission (ADA standard paper form PDF at minimum; ERA/EDI in v2)
4. Automated patient reminders (SMS + email)
5. Billing/invoicing with payment collection (Stripe or dental payment partners)
6. X-ray attachment (link/upload existing DEXIS/Carestream files — no proprietary imaging)
7. Data export (CSV/JSON) always available, no friction, prominently displayed in product

## Key Differentiator

**"Your data is always yours"** as explicit product promise. One-click CSV/JSON export of all patient records, clinical notes, and appointment history — available without restriction or support call. No exit fees. This directly addresses the #1 switching barrier documented across all dental practice software reviews.
