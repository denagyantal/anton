# Specialty Auto Enhancement Shop Software — Score: 84/105

**Verdict**: BUILD

## One-Line Pitch
The Urable replacement that PE-owned Fullsteam won't build — affordable multi-service shop management for PPF, tint, ceramic, and wrap shops.

## Problem

**Urable** was the dominant all-in-one CRM for specialty auto enhancement shops (PPF, window tint, ceramic coating, vinyl wrap, detailing). In May 2025, Urable was acquired by **Fullsteam** (a PE-backed software consolidator). PE acquisitions in SaaS historically produce: price increases, reduced support responsiveness, reduced development velocity, and user exodus.

Urable's pricing post-acquisition: $70-220/mo — moving out of reach for 1-5 bay specialty shops.

**What these shops need** (that no single affordable tool provides):
- **Multi-stage job tracking**: deposit → design/template → installation → quality check → delivery/pickup
- **Before/after photo documentation** stored per job (warranty evidence)
- **Warranty certificate auto-generation** (customer name, vehicle, service, warranty period)
- **3D vehicle template** for PPF quoting (front end, full hood, full car packages)
- **Tint film inventory** by shade % + PPF roll tracking
- **Recurring warranty expiration reminders** ("Your 2-year ceramic coating maintenance is due")

**Current alternatives:**
- Tint Wiz — tinting-only, no PPF or ceramic
- DetailPilot ($29-119/mo) — mobile detailing focus, not shop-based enhancement work
- Shopmanager — general, not specialty-specific
- Mobile Tech RX — reconditioning-focused

No single tool covers the full PPF+tint+ceramic+wrap workflow at a reasonable post-Urable price.

## Product Concept

**"ShopShield"** — $49/mo flat for up to 3 service bays; $89/mo for 4-10 bays.

**Core MVP (4-5 weeks):**
1. **Multi-stage job card** — deposit payment → template/material order → installation scheduled → QC checklist → delivery/pickup
2. **Before/after photo documentation** — stored per job for warranty disputes and social media
3. **Warranty certificate auto-generation** — PDF with customer name, vehicle, VIN, service, installation date, warranty terms
4. **Customer vehicle history** — all jobs by vehicle + warranty status
5. **Basic inventory** — PPF roll tracking by type, tint film by shade %
6. **Online booking + deposit collection** — customer books, pays deposit, job created automatically

**Phase 2:**
- 3D vehicle template for PPF quoting (select hood, bumper, mirrors, full front, etc.)
- Recurring maintenance reminders (2-year ceramic = automated rebooking reminder)
- Supplier order management

**LTD Pricing**:
- $149 LTD — up to 3 bays
- $249 LTD — up to 10 bays

## Risks
1. **PE acquisition window closes**: 12-24 months of maximum churn; after that, Urable may stabilize or users settle into alternatives. Mitigation: launch in Q4 2026 to catch the peak churn window.
2. **TAM ceiling**: US auto enhancement market is real but capped at ~15,000-25,000 shops. Mitigation: international expansion (UK, AU, CA) has minimal software options.
3. **3D vehicle template is complex**: Full-featured PPF quoting requires vehicle templates. Mitigation: launch without it (flat-package pricing entry instead) and add in phase 2.
