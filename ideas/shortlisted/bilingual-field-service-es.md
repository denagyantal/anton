## One-Line Pitch

The first field service platform built for the office/crew language split — office staff and Spanish-speaking field crews see the same job record in their preferred language; invoice clients in English, communicate crews in Spanish.

## Problem

~30% of US construction and trades workers are Hispanic (NAHB 2024 data); in Sun Belt metros (TX, FL, AZ, CA, GA) the figure is 40–50%+. The typical small landscaping or cleaning company has:

- **English-speaking owner/office manager** who handles estimates, invoicing, and QuickBooks
- **Spanish-speaking crew** who execute the jobs

Current software assumes everyone speaks English. Spanish-speaking crews:
- Use tools in a language they don't fully read → errors on job notes, time entries, checklists
- Receive job instructions via WhatsApp voice note (in Spanish) because no app delivers instructions in Spanish
- Can't confirm job completion through an app → owner calls/texts each crew lead after every job

Existing tools:
- **ServiceTitan** — Spanish mobile app only at enterprise tier ($300+/tech/month)
- **Jobber** — no Spanish interface
- **Housecall Pro** — no Spanish interface
- **Spanstead** (launched Sept 2026, Leawood KS) — explicitly built for this problem; currently waitlist-only, personal onboarding, tiny team
- **SolvPro** — fully bilingual EN/ES but niche and limited reach

The acquisition opportunity: Spanish-language Facebook groups for trades are completely unserved by any software vendor. "Software para paisajistas" (landscaping software) returns almost nothing relevant.

## Market Evidence

- 30% of US trades workers Hispanic (NAHB 2024 — 9M workers in construction/trades)
- Sun Belt metro areas: landscaping and cleaning crew composition is often >60% Spanish-speaking
- Spanstead launched September 2026 with waitlist — confirming the gap is real and someone is building
- Atlas Unchained analysis: 30% of Sun Belt contractor calls are in Spanish; Voice AI tools handle none of them
- Small landscaping companies (2–10 crew, $200K–$1M revenue) are the primary target — highly underserved by any software

## Key Differentiators

1. **Language split by role** — office staff sees English UI; crew app is in Spanish; same underlying job record
2. **Spanish-language crew job cards** — job instructions, task lists, and photo captions delivered in Spanish to field crew
3. **Bilingual invoice output** — generate invoices in English for homeowners; all internal comms to crew in Spanish
4. **Spanish-language onboarding** — video walkthroughs and support in Spanish (no existing FSM tool does this)
5. **WhatsApp-integrated dispatch** (optional) — job notifications to crew via WhatsApp in Spanish (the channel they already use)

## MVP Features (6–8 Week Build)

1. Bilingual job management (EN admin + ES crew app)
2. Job scheduling (dispatch jobs to Spanish-speaking crews)
3. Spanish-language crew mobile app (accept job, log start/end, take photos)
4. English-facing invoicing (auto-generated from job completion)
5. Owner dashboard in English with crew status in real time

## Revenue Model
