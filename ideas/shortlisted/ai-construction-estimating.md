## One-Line Pitch

Take photos of the job site + describe the scope by voice → get a professional, itemized estimate in 10 minutes instead of 2-8 hours. Built for the solo HVAC tech or electrician who's leaving money on the table because quotes take too long to produce.

## Problem

Contractors in residential trades (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing) spend 2-8 hours on every manual estimate. Current win rates are only 15-25% because by the time the quote arrives, the homeowner has moved on. The math: if a contractor can produce 3× more estimates in the same time, revenue scales without adding headcount.

**The enterprise validation**: Rebar raised $14M, Togal.AI grew +333%, Buildots raised $45M — all targeting large commercial contractors with $200-$500/mo tools. The gap is the solo/small shop (1-5 person operation) who can't justify $200/mo but desperately needs the same productivity gain.

**Multiple YC companies in 2026 all seeing the same gap**:
- Handoff AI (YC 2025): remodeling estimates from photos with localized pricing database
- Scout Out (YC 2026): upload building plans → structured material + labor takeoffs
- CostLogic: browser-based, upload plans → AI measures rooms → generates invoice

All are building horizontal tools for general contractors. None are building **trade-specific** (HVAC load calculation, electrical panel sizing, roofing material selection by product type). Trade-specific accuracy is the moat nobody has claimed.

## Product Concept: "QuoteSnap" (working name)

**Single-trade MVP — start with residential HVAC (8-12 weeks):**

1. **Photo capture**: Contractor takes 5-10 photos of job site from their phone
2. **Voice scope**: 30-second voice memo describing the job ("2-ton AC replacement, 1500 sqft single story, existing ductwork in good shape")
3. **AI processing**: Vision model identifies equipment, space size, access complexity; trade-specific materials database (AHRI-rated HVAC equipment pricing); labor hours by complexity level
4. **Estimate output**: Itemized estimate with good/better/best equipment options, labor breakdown, material list, timeline
5. **Customer proposal**: One-tap to send professional PDF proposal to customer with e-sign
6. **Win tracking**: Customer approves → job created; track win rate over time

**Trade-specific differentiation (HVAC only in V1):**
- Manual J load calculation from room dimensions in photos
- Equipment selection (SEER rating, brand, warranty) pre-populated from national AHRI database
- R-22 refrigerant surcharge automation (EPA phase-out pricing)
- Permitting fees by county/state (pre-loaded)
- Labor hours by job type (new install vs. replacement vs. repair)

**Pricing:**
- $5-10 per estimate (pay-as-you-go, no commitment)
- $49/mo unlimited estimates (power users)
- $79 LTD for first-year AppSumo launch
