## One-Line Pitch

"Landlord OS" for the accidental landlord — 1–10 units, flat $19/mo, no per-unit fees. Online rent collection (2-day ACH), auto-categorized Schedule E expense tracking, photo-documented maintenance requests, and state-specific compliance reminders — the Buildium alternative that doesn't penalize you for growing.

## Problem

The US has 10–15 million small landlords who own 1–10 rental units — most inherited property, bought a duplex to house-hack, or accumulated a small portfolio over years. They're the "accidental landlord" segment: managing rentals is a side operation, not a business.

Their current options are:
- **Free tools** (Innago, TurboTenant free): stay too thin to be useful; no integrated banking, no accounting automation, no compliance reminders
- **Professional PM software** (Buildium, AppFolio): Buildium starts at $62/mo with 6 price increases in 2.5 years since RealPage acquisition; $80→$192 cliff at 41 units; support is a "black hole" for non-premium tiers; built for professional property managers, not accidental landlords
- **Spreadsheets + personal bank accounts**: works until tax season when the accountant explains they've missed $4,200/year in deductions (confirmed by LeaseBase Foundation 2026 audit of 1,200 landlords)

**The result**: small landlords either overpay for Buildium/AppFolio (built for 50+ unit portfolios) or stitch together Excel + Venmo + text messages — and lose $4,200/year in tax deductions they never track.

**Key gap**: Flat pricing (no per-unit penalty), 2-day ACH settlement (vs. TenantCloud's 7-day), and built-in Schedule E export are unbuilt at a price point under $25/mo.

## MVP Feature Set

**Phase 1 (5-6 weeks):**
1. Online rent collection — ACH bank transfer with 2-day settlement (Stripe Treasury / Plaid)
2. Tenant portal — tenants pay online, submit maintenance requests, download receipts
3. Auto-categorized expense tracking — bank feed import → auto-categorize to Schedule E categories (mortgage interest, repairs, insurance, management fees, depreciation bucket)
4. Schedule E export — one-click PDF/CSV for accountant or TurboTax import
5. Photo-documented maintenance requests — tenant submits → landlord tracks → contractor assigned → photo proof of completion
6. Simple dashboard — one screen: rent collected, expenses, net income, overdue tenants, upcoming lease renewals

**Phase 2 (Month 2-3):**
- State-specific compliance calendar (lease renewal deadlines, habitability inspection requirements, rent increase notice periods)
- Tenant screening integration (TransUnion/Equifax credit check)
- DocuSign integration for lease signing

## Risks

1. **ACH rent collection = financial compliance** — Plaid integration, possible money transmitter licensing depending on state; use Stripe Treasury or Dwolla to offload compliance
2. **Innago and TurboTenant have free tiers** — conversion from free to paid is harder than from paid-to-paid; must demonstrate clear ROI (Schedule E export, 2-day ACH, compliance reminders)
3. **Per-state compliance reminders** — lease renewal notice periods, habitability requirements, rent control rules vary by state; building 50-state database adds scope
