## One-Line Pitch
AI-powered front desk for solo and small dental practices — automates insurance verification, appointment scheduling, and patient intake to replace the $40–50K/year front desk role with 40% annual turnover.

## Problem
Dental practices run on manual processes for insurance verification (2–3 hours/day), appointment scheduling, and patient intake. Front desk staff cost $40–50K/year with 40% annual turnover. Enterprise tools (Weave, Lighthouse360) target multi-location DSOs and are priced beyond the 100K+ solo/2-dentist practices. No LTD-priced or affordable entry exists for practices using Open Dental or similar open-source EHR systems. Zirco.ai (HN Show HN, Oct 2026) ran 30+ discovery calls confirming the pain before building — the strongest validation signal this idea has.

## Market Size
100K+ dental practices in the US; ~70% are solo/2-dentist practices. Enterprise validated: Weave/Lighthouse360 multi-billion-dollar incumbents prove at-scale demand. Dental AI receptionist market: $6.26B → $21.15B by 2034.
