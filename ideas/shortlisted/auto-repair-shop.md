## One-Line Pitch

"BayOS" — the only auto repair shop platform with month-to-month pricing, unlimited users, and built-in text-to-pay for 1–3 bay independent shops at $89/mo flat.

## Problem

Independent auto repair shops (160,000+ in the US) are caught between two painful options:

- **Tekmetric** ($179–409/mo + add-ons reaching $1,000+/mo): Annual contracts, slow/clicky UI, no state inspection date tracking, labor guide gaps
- **Shopmonkey** ($125–499/mo + $20/user overages): Proprietary payment processing at higher rates, 30-day cancellation notice required, CRM Essentials is a $315/mo add-on
- **Mitchell1/RO-Writer**: Desktop-era tools with dated UI; "fragmented with multiple windows"
- **Shop4D**: "$1,100/mo + large setup fee + mandatory 1-year contract" + system down daily in first 3 months (BBB documented)
- **ARI** ($40/mo): Most affordable but lacks digital inspections, reliable text-to-pay, and comprehensive labor guide

The gap: a true contract-free, month-to-month shop management platform under $100/mo for 1–3 bay shops that includes digital inspections, text-to-pay, and a reliable labor guide — with flat unlimited-user pricing.

## Product Concept: "BayOS"

**Pricing**: $89/mo flat, unlimited users, month-to-month — no annual contract ever

**Core MVP (6–8 weeks):**
1. **Digital vehicle inspection** — photo + video, templated inspection sheets, branded PDF sent to customer
2. **Labor guide integration** — MOTOR API or ALLDATA for repair time estimates and labor rates
3. **Text-to-pay** — complete RO → send text with payment link → customer pays via Stripe
4. **Customer vehicle history** — VIN-linked service history per vehicle, not just per customer
5. **Parts ordering** — PartsTech integration for parts pricing + ordering from within RO
6. **State inspection date tracking** — simple field + reminder when inspection is due (missing from Tekmetric)

**Phase 2 (MRR upsells):**
- Two-way customer texting (appointment reminders, RO updates)
- QBO/Xero sync for accounting handoff
- Multi-bay scheduler with drag-and-drop
- Canned job recommendations ("customer needs this at their next visit")

**Key Differentiators:**
1. **Month-to-month only** — no annual contracts, ever (addresses #1 switching barrier)
2. **Flat unlimited-user pricing** — no per-tech anxiety when hiring
3. **Built-in text-to-pay** — no third-party integration; works out of the box
4. **State inspection date field** — trivial but missing from Tekmetric; shops track in spreadsheets
