## One-Line Pitch

Mobile inspection checklist + recurring contract billing for commercial cleaning companies (5–50 site portfolios) — the tool ZenMaid ($3M ARR) explicitly doesn't serve, at $99/mo.

## Problem

Commercial cleaning (janitorial, office cleaning, school/hospital cleaning) has fundamentally different software needs than residential maid services:

- **Contract billing vs. per-visit billing** — janitorial companies invoice monthly per-contract, not per-clean; no tool handles this elegantly
- **Per-location quality inspection checklists** — commercial clients require proof of service; cleaners complete a per-room checklist with photos that generates a client-facing completion report after each clean
- **Employee scheduling across fixed locations** — unlike residential routes, commercial cleaners return to the same buildings nightly; scheduling is shift-based, not job-based
- **Supply/chemical inventory tracking** — cleaning chemicals are a meaningful per-location cost; no tool tracks consumption against budget
- **Compliance documentation** — OSHA safety training logs, green cleaning certifications, LEED documentation for managed buildings

Existing tools:
- **ZenMaid ($250K MRR)** — explicitly residential maid services; per-clean workflow, not contract billing
- **Swept** — commercial-focused but users report poor UX and pricing makes small operators feel squeezed
- **Janitorial Manager** — more complete but overpriced for companies with <50 locations
- **Connecteam** — scheduling only, no inspection checklists or contract billing

Result: small commercial cleaning companies (5–20 building portfolios, 5–25 employees) are on spreadsheets + verbal agreements + emailed photos + paper invoices.

## Market Evidence

- ZenMaid confirmed at $3M ARR validates cleaning SaaS works; residential is a proven niche
- Commercial cleaning is a **$70B+ US industry** (2x the size of residential); IBIS World

- Swept and Janitorial Manager exist — proving the commercial niche is a real category — but both have pricing and UX complaints

## Key Differentiators

1. **Mobile per-room inspection checklist** — cleaner completes room-by-room checklist with photos on phone; auto-generates a branded "Cleaning Report" PDF for the client after each clean
2. **Contract billing engine** — monthly contract invoicing (not per-visit); handles price escalators, annual renewals, and scope changes
3. **Proof-of-service documentation** — timestamped photos + checklist completion = protection against "you didn't clean X" disputes
4. **Employee scheduling for fixed-location shifts** — shift-based scheduling (not appointment-based) for cleaners returning to the same buildings nightly
5. **Supply consumption tracking** — log cleaning chemicals used per location; compare against contracted supply budget

## MVP Features (6–8 Week Build)

1. Client/location management (each location has its own inspection template)
2. Mobile inspection app (per-room checklist + photos → branded PDF report emailed to client)
3. Employee shift scheduling
4. Recurring contract billing (monthly invoicing per client)
5. Basic supply tracking
