## One-Line Pitch

Rentman built a $15-20M ARR business serving AV/lighting/staging rental companies — but 50,000+ US party rental companies (tables, chairs, linens, bounce houses, tents) are still on spreadsheets with no equivalent vertical tool.

## Problem

**Party rental companies** — the businesses that deliver tables, chairs, linens, dance floors, bounce houses, tents, and event equipment to weddings, birthday parties, corporate events, and school functions — have a specific operational workflow:

1. **Availability calendar** — Can I commit this set of 200 chairs to Saturday's wedding AND the Friday corporate event?
2. **Delivery route planning** — 3 deliveries on Saturday morning before 11am; who goes where in what order?
3. **Inventory tracking** — Are all 200 chairs back? Are 15 missing? Who had them last?
4. **Damage deposits + invoicing** — Collect deposit, final payment, track damage claims per rental
5. **Contract generation** — Each rental needs a signed rental agreement with damage/cancellation terms

This is distinct from generic field service management (no service calls, no tech dispatch) and distinct from AV event rental (Rentman serves production quality gear, not commodity furniture). **No vertical tool exists for party rental specifically.**

What most operators use today:
- Google Calendar for availability
- Excel for inventory tracking
- Square or Stripe for payments
- DocuSign or paper for contracts
- Their memory for routing

Goodshuffle Pro and RentMy exist but have limited market penetration. The majority of the 50K+ US party rental businesses are unserved.

## Product Concept: "RentalDesk"

**Core MVP (6–8 weeks):**
1. **Inventory catalog** — Each item type (200 chairs, 50 tables, 10 bounce houses, 5 tents) with quantity, description, photos, rental price per day
2. **Availability calendar** — Book-out blocks per inventory item; automatic conflict detection when overlapping events compete for the same items
3. **Quote & contract** — Generate rental quote from item selection + dates; convert to contract with e-signature; deposit collection via Stripe
4. **Delivery route** — Assign delivery jobs to drivers; map view of delivery addresses; estimated driving route
5. **Return tracking** — Mark items returned; flag missing/damaged items; charge damage deposit

**Phase 2:**
- Linen inventory tracking (table sizes, colors, napkin counts)
- Supplier catalog integration (auto-reorder depleted items)
- Customer portal (book online, sign contract, pay deposit, track delivery status)
- Crew mobile app (delivery driver sees their route and items to deliver)
- Review automation (post-event text requesting Google review)

## Competitive Validation Required

Before building, validate:

2. **RentMy** — Pricing and feature set? Why do operators abandon it?
3. **Are operators aware any software exists?** — If not, education is the sales motion (slower)
4. **What's the booking volume per operator?** — If 2-3 events/month, ROI at $99/mo is weak; if 15-20 events/month, strong

## Risks

1. **Goodshuffle Pro and RentMy may already serve this gap** — Need competitive validation before building; they may have better penetration than evidence suggests
2. **Seasonal revenue** — Wedding season (April-October) = most revenue; January-March is slow = higher churn window
3. **Inventory management complexity** — Availability conflict detection for linen/chair quantities across overlapping events is non-trivial engineering
4. **Small average revenue per customer** — At $99/mo, you need 100+ customers for $9.9K MRR; party rental businesses are small; ARA list is the only efficient distribution channel
