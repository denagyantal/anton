## One-Line Pitch
Travel-time-aware scheduling, state-specific document checklists, and automatic fee calculation for mobile notaries — replacing text messages and paper calendars for 200K+ active mobile notaries.

## Problem

Mobile notaries (real estate closings, legal document signing, I-9 verifications) manage their entire business via text message and a paper calendar. The unique challenge: **travel time between appointments is the #1 scheduling challenge**, and no generic tool understands this.

A mobile notary might have three closings in a day across a city — the time between them must account for driving, parking, and meeting setup. A double-booking or late arrival costs the notary their fee ($75-200 per signing) and damages their reputation with the title company or attorney scheduling them.

**Key gaps in existing tools**:
- **Travel-time-aware scheduling**: No generic calendar understands "I can't take 2pm downtown if I have 1:30pm across the city"
- **State-specific document checklists**: What's required for a CA loan signing vs. FL notarization is different; notaries currently memorize or print reference cards
- **Automatic fee calculation**: Fees vary by document type, mileage, notarization type (standard vs. RON); manual calculation takes time and introduces errors
- **Title company booking portal**: Title companies and attorneys want to book notaries online without calling; most notaries have no booking link

**Validation**: One platform serving this exact market reaches $45/mo × 1,100+ mobile notary subscribers = **~$49K MRR**. The market exists and pays.

## Product Concept
**Core features**:
- **Travel-time-aware scheduling**: Integrates with Google Maps; suggests appointment slots based on travel time between jobs; prevents double-booking based on location + travel time
- **State-specific document checklists**: Pre-built signing package checklists for each state; auto-selects required documents by signing type (loan closing, power of attorney, I-9)
- **Automatic fee calculator**: Fees by document type, notarization count, mileage, state; generates professional invoice PDF
- **Customer booking portal**: Public link for title companies/attorneys to book directly; notary sees requests in inbox and confirms
- **RON support**: Remote online notary session management, platform compatibility tracking by state

**Mobile-first**: Most notaries work entirely from their phone between signings
