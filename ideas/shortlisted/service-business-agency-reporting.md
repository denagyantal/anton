## One-Line Pitch

White-label reporting dashboards for marketing agencies and bookkeeping firms that serve boring SMBs (HVAC, restaurants, landlords) — so they can show clients ROI and retain accounts, at $199–$399/agency seat LTD.

## Problem

Two patterns surfaced today create the same opportunity:

**Pattern 1 — The $1M ARR Twitter Signal**: A founder posted on X/Twitter that their reporting software sold to service business agencies (marketing agencies serving home services businesses) had hit $1M ARR at ~$10K/year per agency. The agencies pay because the tool saves hundreds of hours and helps retain clients by making ROI visible. This is a replicable pattern across dozens of agency sub-niches.

**Pattern 2 — Conductor.is ($25K MRR solo)**: A solo founder built a niche accounting integration SaaS — a narrow tool that connects specific accounting platforms for a specific industry. It does $25K MRR, has a $32B company as a customer, and is 100% inbound. The lesson: the narrower the niche, the less competition, the more word-of-mouth, and the higher the willingness to pay.

**The common insight**: Agencies serving boring businesses (plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, restaurants, property management) need to show their clients ROI dashboards. Generic reporting tools (Looker, Tableau, Google Data Studio) require data engineering skills that agency account managers don't have. Purpose-built, industry-specific reporting tools that pull data from the specific platforms these businesses use (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Toast, AppFolio) are a defensible niche with high switching costs.

**The sub-niches are clear:**
- Marketing agencies serving HVAC/plumbing — need to show lead source attribution + ROI from Google LSA, Yelp, and Nextdoor spend
- Bookkeeping firms serving restaurants — need P&L dashboards, food cost tracking, labor %, connected to Toast/Square/Clover data
- Property management consultants — need Schedule E export + maintenance cost per unit reports for portfolio reviews
- Pest control/lawn care agencies — chemical compliance + revenue per route dashboards for operators

## Product Concept: "AgencyLens" (or "ServiceReport")

**Pricing**: $199/mo per agency (10 client accounts included) / $99/mo per 5 additional clients
**LTD**: $299 (up to 20 client accounts, lifetime access to one vertical connector)

**Core MVP — HVAC Marketing Agency Reporting (3–4 weeks):**

1. **ServiceTitan / Jobber connector** — Pull revenue data, job counts, tech efficiency, lead sources from Jobber or ServiceTitan API

2. **Google LSA / Yelp Ad spend import** — Manual CSV upload or API pull of ad spend by channel

3. **ROI dashboard** — Revenue per lead source, cost per customer acquisition, seasonal trends; white-labeled with agency's logo and client's company name

4. **Automated monthly PDF report** — Auto-generated "here's your ROI this month" PDF sent to agency's client (branded as the agency's deliverable)

5. **Agency admin panel** — Manage all clients, enable/disable data sources per client, bulk export

**Phase 2:**
- Add bookkeeping firm sub-niche (Toast/Square → P&L dashboard for restaurants)
- Add property management sub-niche (AppFolio/Buildium → Schedule E dashboard for landlords)
- Slack/email alerts for performance anomalies ("Revenue down 40% vs last month — call your client")
- Client portal login (client can see their own dashboard without asking agency)

## Key Differentiators
1. **Pre-built for one vertical** — Not a generic BI tool; built specifically for HVAC marketing agencies with the specific KPIs they report on (cost per booked call, revenue per lead source, seasonal demand curves)
2. **White-label at base price** — Agency's logo + client's branding on every dashboard; not a feature add-on
3. **No data engineering required** — Pre-built connectors to the 3–5 platforms agencies already use; no SQL, no Zapier chains
4. **Automated monthly PDF** — Agencies deliver reports to clients without manual work; this is the feature that retains clients
