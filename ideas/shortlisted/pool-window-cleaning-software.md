## One-Line Pitch
The ZenMaid formula for pool cleaning, window cleaning, and pressure washing — route-optimized scheduling, recurring billing, and chemical logs for a $79 one-time price, in a market with no dominant vertical SaaS.

## Problem
ZenMaid ($3M/yr bootstrapped) proved that building a dead-simple scheduling tool for ONE specific service vertical (residential cleaning) generates millions in recurring revenue. The model is replicable — but nobody has applied it to adjacent unsexy home service verticals:

- **Pool cleaning**: Weekly chemical maintenance routes, pH/chlorine logs (legally required in some states for commercial pools), equipment service history, seasonal winterization/opening scheduling. GorillaDesk handles it but as an afterthought.
- **Window cleaning**: Ladder-access scheduling, seasonal residential/commercial schedules, deposit requirements, aerial lift certification tracking. No dedicated tool exists.
- **Pressure washing**: Bid calculation by surface area and material, route scheduling for jobs that can't be rescheduled on rain days, before/after photo documentation. Square + phone = current state for most operators.

The shared pain: generic FSM tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro) work but don't understand the specific workflows. LMN and ServiceTitan are wildly overpriced. Nothing exists at $29-79/month that's purpose-built.

## Market Evidence
- ZenMaid: $3M ARR from residential cleaning scheduling, bootstrapped, 11 years — the exact model to replicate
- Pool cleaning: $5B+ US market; ~50,000 pool service companies; GorillaDesk serves the segment but with generic features
- Window cleaning: $3B+ US market; no dedicated software with notable market share
- Pressure washing: $3B+ US market; r/PressureWashingGrowth is an active community

## Product Concept: Pool Service First, Then Adjacent

**Pool cleaning MVP (4-5 weeks):**
- Weekly route scheduling with map view and stop order optimization
- Chemical log per visit (pH, chlorine, alkalinity, algaecide) — legally required for commercial pools
- Equipment service history per pool (pump, filter, heater, salt cell)
- Recurring billing with auto-invoicing after each visit
- Before/after service photos attached to job record
- Client portal showing chemical readings and service history
- Seasonal scheduling (winterization, spring opening as separate service types)

**Expansion to window cleaning:**
- Ladder-access notes per property
- Seasonal schedule management (spring/fall residential, year-round commercial)
- Before/after photo documentation

**Expansion to pressure washing:**
- Surface area + material-based quote calculator
- Rain delay reschedule workflow
- Before/after photo sent automatically to client on job completion

**Pricing:**
- $49/mo (up to 2 users, unlimited stops)
- $79/mo (up to 5 users, multi-truck routing)
- $79 LTD for solo operator (AppSumo)

## Key Differentiators
1. **Chemical log built-in** — not an afterthought; legal compliance for commercial pools
2. **Seasonal service mode** — winter/summer scheduling baked into the workflow
3. **ZenMaid pricing** — $49/mo flat vs GorillaDesk's $49-149+ with features they don't need
4. **Vertical-specific** — purpose-built for one trade, not generic FSM with a pool skin
