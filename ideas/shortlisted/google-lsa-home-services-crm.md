## One-Line Pitch

The cheapest ServiceTitan alternative with real Google LSA attribution — tie your ad spend directly to closed jobs without 12 different spreadsheets, at $29–49/mo for home service businesses.

## Problem

Google Local Service Ads (LSA) is the primary acquisition channel for most home service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, carpet cleaning, roofing. Yet no affordable CRM ties LSA spend directly to closed revenue. ServiceTitan has LSA integration at $500+/mo. Generic CRMs (HubSpot, Zoho) have poor Google Ads API integrations not designed for per-job attribution.

The result: home service business owners are "swapping between 12 different trackers per hour" (direct HN comment) to understand their actual ROAS from LSA spend. They see ad costs in one dashboard, booked jobs in another, completed jobs in a third. No simple, affordable tool connects them.

Crewbird (HN, November 2026) is building exactly this: a CRM built specifically around Google LSA with direct attribution tracking. The founder validated the concept with their dad's carpet cleaning company (approaching 3x ROAS). Crewbird is very early-stage — the category is validated but not yet claimed.

## Product Concept

**"TrackBid"** (or similar) — CRM built around Google LSA for home service businesses.

**Core MVP features (4–6 week build):**
- Google LSA API integration: pull ad spend, impressions, calls
- Job management: create job from LSA lead → track status → close → collect payment
- Attribution dashboard: cost per booked job, cost per completed job, ROAS by job type/season
- Revenue per ad dollar: "Your LSA spend this week = $340, closed revenue = $1,020 (3x ROAS)"
- Simple CRM: customer history, job notes, follow-up reminders
- Optional: Stripe payment collection + QuickBooks sync

**Key differentiator**: The only tool that connects Google LSA spend directly to closed revenue at $29–49/mo. No other tool does this in the sub-$200/mo tier.

## Target Channels

- r/HVAC, r/smallbusiness, r/carpetcleaning

## Risks

1. **Crewbird is building exactly this** — early-stage competitor; validate before building, consider whether to partner or compete
2. **Google API changes** — Google has historically restricted LSA API access; dependency risk
3. **Niche within niche** — only home service businesses who also run Google LSA; smaller TAM than a full CRM
