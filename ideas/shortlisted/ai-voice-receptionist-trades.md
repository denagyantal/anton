## One-Line Pitch
AI phone receptionist for HVAC/plumbing/electrical shops with 1–5 technicians — answers calls 24/7, qualifies callers, and books jobs directly into their existing scheduler — at $99/mo vs. Avoca AI's enterprise pricing.

## Problem
HVAC, plumbing, and electrical businesses miss 30–40% of inbound calls. Each missed call = $200–$800 in lost revenue for a typical service job. The math is immediate: a solo HVAC operator missing 3 calls/week loses $30K–$120K/year to voicemail.

Avoca AI hit a $1B valuation ($125M raised, April 2026) serving 800–1,000+ home service operators — definitively proving the market. But Avoca targets established companies with 5+ technicians at enterprise pricing. The 1–5 tech segment — the vast majority of HVAC/plumbing operators — has no purpose-built affordable option.

Current workaround: voicemail (lose the job), hire a $15/hr part-time receptionist (doesn't scale), or use a generic answering service that can't qualify HVAC callers or book into their scheduling system.

## Market Evidence
- Avoca AI: $1B valuation, $125M raised, 800–1,000+ paying customers (April 2026)

- 100K+ HVAC companies with 1–5 techs; 500K+ plumbing/electrical operators in same tier
- Missed call recovery ROI: even 1 recovered job/week at $300 avg = $1,200/mo vs. $99/mo tool

## Key Differentiators
1. **Price** — $99/mo flat vs. Avoca's enterprise pricing (3–5x more)
2. **Simplicity** — Trained on HVAC/plumbing/electrical FAQ out of box; no custom configuration needed
3. **Scheduling integration** — Books directly into Jobber, Housecall Pro, or ServiceTitan (webhook)
4. **Spanish/English** — Bilingual by default for crew and customer calls
5. **Solo-tech-friendly** — Works even if the owner is the only tech; routes calls when on a job

## Product Concept
**MVP (3–4 weeks):**
- Vapi or Bland.ai powered voice agent
- Pre-trained on HVAC/plumbing FAQ: seasonal tune-up, emergency dispatch, service calls, estimates
- Jobber + Housecall Pro webhook for live booking
- SMS confirmation to customer after booking
- Call transcript + recording sent to owner's email after every call
- Escalation: flags "emergency" calls (no heat in winter, burst pipe) to owner's phone

**Pricing:**
- $99/mo: up to 200 calls/mo; HVAC + plumbing trained
- $149/mo: unlimited calls; add electrical; custom FAQ
- $299–499 LTD: team license (AppSumo launch)
