## One-Line Pitch
Dead-simple pest control software for the 2–10 truck operator: mobile-first routing, recurring treatment reminders, EPA chemical compliance log, and invoicing at $49–79/mo — the GorillaDesk for the long tail.

## Problem
35,000+ pest control companies in the US. Small operators (2–10 trucks) doing $300K–$600K/year in revenue are still running on WhatsApp group chats and spiral notebooks. A pest control operator in Ohio confirmed doing $600K/year, 5 trucks, entirely on WhatsApp.

GorillaDesk validated the category: bootstrapped pest control SaaS, 3,100 customers, $4M ARR, acquired by PestRoutes for ~$300M. However, PestRoutes was then acquired by ServiceTitan — which means enterprise pricing is coming. The long tail of 2–10 truck operators who couldn't afford PestRoutes before will be even more priced out under ServiceTitan. This is the exact same "acquisition creates pricing gap" dynamic as the ServiceTitan → Jobber/HCP story in HVAC.

Unique pest control requirements not served by generic FSM tools:
- **Chemical compliance**: EPA documentation for what was applied, at what concentration, in what locations — required by law
- **Recurring treatment scheduling**: monthly/quarterly/annual cycles with automatic reminder sequences
- **Pest identification library**: linking treatment protocols to specific pest types
- **Service record continuity**: multi-year treatment history tied to property addresses

## Product Concept

**Core MVP (5–7 weeks):**
- **One-screen routing**: day's jobs on a map, tap to navigate, GPS check-in at each property
- **Recurring treatment scheduling**: weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual cycles; auto-reminder to tech 24hr before
- **EPA chemical compliance log**: applied product, EPA registration number, concentration, target pest, application method, date/time — stored per property
- **Service history per property address**: full treatment history tied to address (not customer) = transfers on property sale
- **Automated invoicing**: job complete → invoice auto-generates → SMS to customer
- **Recurring invoice management**: auto-bill on schedule for maintenance contracts

**Pricing:**
- $49/mo — 1–3 techs, up to 200 active properties
- $79/mo — up to 5 techs, up to 500 active properties  
- $119/mo — up to 10 techs, unlimited properties
- LTD: $149 (up to 3 techs) on AppSumo for early customer acquisition

## Key Differentiators
1. **EPA chemical compliance built-in** — not an afterthought; every job requires chemical log entry
2. **Property-address-centric history** — service history tied to address, not customer; transfers on resale
3. **Flat pricing for operator size** — no per-tech overage; $49/mo stays $49 for first 3 techs
4. **Mobile-first** — techs use a phone, not a laptop; full job workflow in 3 taps
