## One-Line Pitch
Pest-specific field service tool with built-in EPA-compliant chemical application logs — the only affordable option between GorillaDesk (too basic) and PestPac (enterprise-priced).

## Problem
1-10 technician pest control operators (the vast majority of 30,000+ pest control businesses in the US) are caught in a gap. GorillaDesk at $49/mo is the only affordable option but lacks chemical usage tracking and has limited route optimization. PestPac and FieldRoutes are enterprise-priced, "overbuilt and overpriced" per operators. No tool owns the middle.

The worst part: chemical application tracking is legally required by EPA and state regulations. Pest operators must maintain compliance logs for every chemical applied (product, concentration, area treated, target pest). Currently done on paper. Getting audited without proper logs = fines and potential license revocation.

Current workaround: paper chemical logs (legally required), GorillaDesk or Jobber for scheduling, separate invoicing. Three disconnected systems plus paper.

## Product Concept

**Core MVP (4-5 weeks):**
1. Chemical application log — record product, EPA registration number, concentration, target pest, area treated, date/tech. State-compliant report generation.
2. Recurring service scheduling — weekly/monthly/quarterly customer visits with auto-reminders
3. Customer portal — service history + technician ETA notifications
4. Invoicing + payment collection — after-service invoice with payment link
5. Basic route optimization — organize daily stops by geography

**Differentiator**: Legal compliance logs built in from day 1. No other affordable tool does this. Marketing hook: "Stop keeping paper chemical logs — one audit can cost more than a year of software."

**Pricing:**
- $69/mo flat for up to 5 technicians (no per-tech pricing)
- $69 LTD for solo operators (AppSumo launch)
- $149/mo for 6-15 technicians

3. **Flat pricing** — no per-tech fees vs PestPac/FieldRoutes enterprise contracts
4. **Simple onboarding** — 30 minutes vs weeks for enterprise tools

3. State chemical compliance requirements vary — need ongoing legal research to keep logs accurate
