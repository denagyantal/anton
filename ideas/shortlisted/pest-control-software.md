# Pest Control Vertical SaaS — Score: 80/105

**Evaluation Date**: 2026-09-24

## One-Line Pitch

Voice-to-work-order field app for pest control techs — log treatments while driving, generate compliance reports automatically, and never miss an upsell opportunity on a recurring route.

## Problem

Pest control operators (technicians and owner-operators) drive between stops, wear gloves, and carry equipment — they cannot safely type work orders while working. The result: techs log jobs at the end of the day from memory, compliance data is incomplete, and upsell opportunities are missed.

Key gaps confirmed by a deep HN thread with active operators:
1. **"Pen and paper still dominates the industry"** — basic digitization is the majority of the opportunity
2. **Voice data entry**: "Techs will often drive and type at the same time" = safety + accuracy problem
3. **Smart upsell prompting**: No tool prompts techs with relevant upsell offers based on customer history or equipment age
4. **Getting data into CRM without physical input** = confirmed as #1 quick win
5. **Commercial food facility compliance reports** — legally required for commercial accounts; generates high-value sticky revenue

## Market Evidence

- HN thread: "There is definitely money in the pest control SaaS business, mine is running at $2M ARR for a few years now" — from a bootstrapped founder who built it
- Pen-and-paper still dominates = massive unrealized digitization opportunity
- Thread poster chose to become a tech-enabled operator rather than sell SaaS because the incumbent software was "decent, cheap, and ubiquitous" — but voice input, auto-scheduling, and smart traps are all unbuilt gaps
- 20,000+ US pest control companies; heavily fragmented market
- Pest control is recession-resistant (homeowners never stop paying for pest treatment)
- GorillaDesk, PestPac, Briostack all have traction = market validated

## Product Concept: "SprayVoice" (or build into SprayRoute)

**Note**: Pest control and lawn treatment chemical tracking have significant overlap. SprayVoice could be a single product serving both markets, differentiated by trade-specific workflow.

**Core differentiation vs. incumbent:**
1. **Voice-to-work-order** — tech speaks treatment while driving; AI transcribes product, rate, pest, notes to work order in real-time (no typing while driving)
2. **Commercial food facility compliance reports** — auto-generated for commercial accounts; creates sticky, high-value relationships with restaurants, food processors, hotels
3. **Upsell prompt engine** — when tech is on-site, app surfaces: "Last visit: customer had rodent droppings near HVAC unit — offer perimeter exclusion service ($X)"

**Pricing**: $79/mo (up to 3 techs) / $129/mo (up to 8 techs) — month-to-month
**LTD**: $99 (1 tech) / $199 (up to 4 techs) on AppSumo
