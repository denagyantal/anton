# Mobile Home Park RUBS Compliance Platform — Score: 87/105

**Verdict**: BUILD
**Tier**: 1 (Strong Opportunity)

## One-Line Pitch
The first affordable MHP management platform with a "RUBS Compliance Shield" — before regulators fine you out of business.

## Problem

Mobile home park operators managing 10-100 lots face a compliance crisis that existing software does not address:

1. **Minnesota** banned RUBS for electricity billing (Jan 2025)
2. **Ohio Supreme Court** ruled submetering companies in manufactured housing communities are public utilities subject to rate oversight (Apr 2026)
3. **California** property management company paid $495,000 settlement for using RUBS to disguise rent increases (Oct 2025)

MHP operators who have used RUBS for years are now exposed to massive legal liability. Yet the only software that exists is:
- **Yardi MH Manager** (launched Dec 2025) — enterprise institutional operators (50+ lots)
- **ManageAmerica** — enterprise only
- **Rent Manager** ($1.50-2.00/unit/mo) — general PM tool, not MHP-specific
- **DoorLoop** — general PM, no RUBS compliance tracking

No affordable cloud SaaS exists at $49-149/mo specifically for the small/mid MHP operator with RUBS compliance baked in.

## Product Concept

**"MHPDesk"** — Flat $49-99/mo for up to 50 lots; $149/mo up to 100 lots.

**Core MVP (4-6 weeks):**
1. **Lot management** — tenant record, lot number, lease dates, contact info
2. **Lot rent billing** — recurring invoices with automatic late fees
3. **RUBS Compliance Mode** — calculates utility allocation using allowable methods per state; generates required tenant disclosures; stores compliance audit trail; alerts when billing practices approach state legal thresholds
4. **State-specific monthly compliance report** — one-click PDF for insurance audits or regulatory inquiries
5. **Tenant communication portal** — work orders, announcements, payment receipts

**Key Differentiator**: "RUBS Compliance Shield" — the only tool that monitors billing for compliance issues before regulators do.

**LTD Pricing**:
- $99 LTD — up to 25 lots
- $299 LTD — up to 100 lots

## Risks
1. **State law complexity**: RUBS rules vary by state and change; requires ongoing legal tracking. Mitigation: start with MN, CA, OH (highest risk states), expand state rules incrementally.
2. **Small market per state**: Individual MHP operators are fragmented; no single platform to reach them all. Mitigation: lean into industry associations + YouTube channels.
3. **Older, non-technical buyers**: Longer decision cycles; need very low-friction onboarding (setup in under 30 minutes, migrate from spreadsheet in one import).
