## One-Line Pitch

The first field service management app built for companies where the office speaks English and the crew speaks Spanish — jobs stop getting "pieced back together at end of day" because the system speaks both languages natively.

## Problem

The US landscaping, lawn care, HVAC, and cleaning industries employ a majority-Hispanic workforce in many regions. The business owner or office manager typically works in English. The field crew communicates in Spanish. Existing FSM tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan) are English-only — not just in the admin UI, but in job assignments, customer communication, and crew notifications.

The result: job assignments get transcribed via phone calls between English-speaking dispatchers and Spanish-speaking crew leads. Job notes from the field are in broken English. Customer-facing communication is in English that the technician who wrote it is not fluent in. At end of day, the dispatcher pieces together what actually happened from WhatsApp voice messages, group texts, and a trip log.

**Spanstead Works** (launched September 24, 2026) is the first mover. The founder has both software engineering and lawn care operations background. The product just launched — meaning the market is forming right now, not years from now.

## Product Concept: "CampoApp" (working name — "campo" = field in Spanish)

**Core MVP (6-8 weeks):**

1. **Bilingual job cards**: Every job has English version (owner/dispatcher sees) + Spanish version (crew sees). Auto-translation layer but with override for trade-specific terminology.
2. **Spanish-first crew mobile app**: Crew gets jobs in Spanish. Status updates (en route, arrived, done) in Spanish. Photo documentation with Spanish captions.
3. **English-facing dashboard**: Owner sees everything in English. Crew communication, job status, GPS locations, notes — all shown in English.
4. **Bilingual customer communication**: Customer portal and notifications in the customer's preferred language (auto-detect).
5. **Crew WhatsApp bridge**: Crew can reply via WhatsApp in Spanish; message auto-translates and logs to job record.
6. **Recurring schedule management**: Weekly/bi-weekly lawn maintenance routes with seasonal adjustments.

**Key differentiations from Spanstead Works:**
- Spanish-first (not English + translation toggle): menus, icons, job terminology designed for Spanish-speaking crews
- WhatsApp integration (crews already use WhatsApp — don't ask them to install another app)
- Invoicing in English for customers, crew pay summaries in Spanish
- Bilingual customer reviews — review request in customer's language, English and Spanish display

**Pricing:**
- $39/mo up to 5 crew members (+ owner)
- $79/mo up to 15 crew members
- $149 LTD (AppSumo launch for up to 8 crew members)
