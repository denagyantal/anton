## One-Line Pitch

Track every chair, compressor, autoclave, and X-ray unit in a dental practice — service intervals, reminders, work history, and one-click compliance report for state board inspections — at $79 one-time vs. industrial CMMS at $10K+/year.

## Problem

Dental offices have $200K–$500K in specialized equipment with zero dedicated maintenance tracking software. The compliance burden is real: state dental boards inspect equipment maintenance records, manufacturers require documented service intervals to maintain warranties, and OSHA/HIPAA compliance audits include equipment logs. Yet the entire workflow is managed with:
- Paper binders or spreadsheets (most common)
- Sticky notes and calendar reminders on personal phones
- Hoping the equipment tech calls when service is due (unreliable)

**The result**: missed service intervals, voided warranties, failed state board inspections, and equipment failures that shut down a practice mid-day.

Dentrix, Eaglesoft, and Open Dental — the three dominant dental practice management platforms — handle patient scheduling and billing but have zero equipment module. Industrial CMMS tools (eMaint, Fiix, Limble) are priced for manufacturing plants at $10K–$50K/year and require IT setup.

**No purpose-built, affordable dental equipment tracker exists.**

## Market Evidence

- 35,000+ dental practices in the US (175,000+ dentists — most run 1-5 chair practices)
- Each practice has 15–40 pieces of tracked equipment (chairs, compressors, autoclaves, X-ray units, lasers, nitrous equipment)
- State dental board compliance mandates documented maintenance records — this is a legal requirement, not a nicety
- Equipment warranties require documented service intervals to remain valid
- 35K practices × $29–49/mo = $12–21M ARR ceiling at 100% penetration; realistically $1–3M ARR at 10–20% penetration

## MVP Feature Set

**Phase 1 (Build in 2-3 weeks):**
1. Equipment registry — add equipment with photo, make/model, serial number, purchase date, warranty expiry
2. Dental-specific asset templates pre-loaded (dental chair, compressor, autoclave, digital X-ray, analog X-ray, ultrasonic scaler, nitrous oxide unit, curing light)
3. Maintenance schedule builder — set service interval per equipment (e.g., autoclave spore test every week, compressor service annually)
4. Email reminder system — sends to practice manager X days before service is due
5. Service log — log service event with tech name, work performed, notes, receipt upload
6. Compliance report export — PDF showing all equipment with service status, overdue items, upcoming due dates (what state board inspectors ask for)

**Phase 2 (Month 2-3):**
- Multi-location support (DSO tier)
- Integration with dental equipment service companies for direct booking
- Mobile photo documentation during service visits
