# HR Document Template Builder

## One-Line Pitch
Drag-drop HR doc builder with smart employee data fields and state compliance inserts — fixes the highest-severity complaint in BambooHR, Rippling, Gusto, Workday, ADP, and UKG simultaneously.

## Problem
Every company needs offer letters, onboarding checklists, performance reviews, and termination notices. And every HRIS tool that generates them is hated. The IH analysis of 39,000+ real software complaints ranked HR document building as the #1 severity pain across the entire dataset at 4.5/5 — confirmed across 6 major HRIS platforms. The current choices are: (1) free Word templates with no logic, (2) your HRIS's broken template engine, or (3) pay $500+/month for an enterprise CLM. The $99–299/month segment is completely wide open.

HR managers at 50–500 person companies spend 2–3 hours per hire customizing templates, merging fields manually, and tracking down signing. Each state has different requirements for what must appear in offer letters, non-competes, and separation agreements — none of the major HRIS tools auto-insert these compliance clauses.

## Target Customer
- HR managers at 50–500 person SMBs
- Companies on BambooHR, Rippling, or Gusto who are frustrated with document templates
- HR teams that currently juggle Word + DocuSign + spreadsheet merge fields
- Companies with regular hiring (10+ hires/year) where template time adds up fast

## Market Evidence
- #1 severity pain (4.5/5) in IH analysis of 39,000+ real software complaints across 6 major vendors
- 6 companies (BambooHR, Rippling, Gusto, Workday, ADP, UKG) all receiving identical complaints
- Upwork: HR document drafting is a recurring hiring category — real money flowing for the manual version
- SHRM: 75%+ of SMBs don't have dedicated legal resources to customize HR docs — they need automation
