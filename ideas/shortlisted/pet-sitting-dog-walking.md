## One-Line Pitch
Clean, crash-free pet sitting and dog walking management for 1–10 sitter operations — GPS check-ins, automated visit report cards, flat pricing with no per-sitter fees — the modern alternative to Time to Pet for independent operators.

## Problem
Time to Pet is the dominant platform for professional pet sitters and dog walkers but has documented recurring complaints: "crashes A LOT," "too expensive when adding additional staff," and feature overload that makes simple scheduling unnecessarily complex.

The core need for a 1–10 sitter operation is simple: client + pet profiles, recurring visit scheduling, GPS-verified check-ins, automated visit report cards (photo + notes sent to owner), and payment collection.

Per-user pricing kicks in when hiring additional walkers, making growth expensive. New entrant Scritches is gaining traction by being "modern, simple, and mobile-friendly... without bloat" — confirming demand for a cleaner alternative exists.

## Market Evidence
- ~300,000 professional pet sitters and dog walkers in the US
- Pet care market: $5B+ and growing (Rover/Wag disruption driving independent operators to seek their own tools)

- Scritches: new entrant positioned as "the modern alternative" — validates the clean-UX gap
- r/PetSitting is an active community with recurring software recommendation threads
- Professional associations: PSI (Pet Sitters International) and NAPPS have active member bases

## Core Feature Set (MVP)
1. **Client + pet profiles**: Owner contact info, pet details (breed, age, vet, special needs, feeding instructions, medications)
2. **Recurring visit scheduling**: Daily, weekly, custom recurring schedules; drag-and-drop calendar
3. **GPS clock-in/out**: Sitter opens app, taps "Start Visit" → GPS-verified; sitter location sent to owner
4. **Automated report cards**: End-of-visit photo upload + notes → auto-sent to owner via SMS/email ("Buddy had a great walk!")
5. **Payment collection**: Card on file; auto-charge after visit or at booking
6. **No per-user pricing**: Flat monthly fee regardless of how many sitters are added
7. **Mobile-first**: Designed for the sitter in the field, not a desk-based owner

## Pricing Model
- **Monthly**: $29/mo (up to 3 staff), $49/mo (unlimited staff, unlimited clients)
- **Annual**: 2 months free
- **LTD**: $79 (up to 3 staff) / $99 (unlimited staff)
- **Key differentiator**: No per-user fees — Time to Pet's per-user pricing is the #1 growth cost complaint
