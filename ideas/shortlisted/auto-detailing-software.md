## One-Line Pitch
CRM for mobile detailers and ceramic coating shops with per-vehicle service history, coating warranty tracking, and B2B fleet account management — built for the detailer who needs to know what was applied to every car they've ever touched.

## Problem
Mobile detailers and ceramic coating shops have a unique need that generic FSM tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro) completely miss: **vehicle-level service history**. When a customer returns after 18 months, the detailer needs to know:
- What ceramic coating was applied (brand, product code, thickness)
- When it was applied and how it's held up
- What paint corrections were done before coating
- What protection film was installed (panels, full car)

Without this, the detailer can't confidently quote a correction job, can't provide warranty service, and looks unprofessional when they don't remember the car they spent 12 hours on.

A second distinct pain: **B2B fleet accounts**. Commercial fleet wash contracts (car dealerships, corporate fleets, rental car companies) are the highest-margin segment for mobile detailers. Managing these requires per-VIN visit history, invoicing per vehicle per visit, and multi-location account management — none of which generic FSM tools handle.

## Key Differentiators
1. **Vehicle-level service history** — every car has its own record: coatings applied, corrections done, products used, before/after photos
2. **Ceramic coating warranty registry** — generate transferable warranty certificates; log warranty claims
3. **Fleet account management** — B2B account with multiple VINs; invoice per vehicle per visit; multi-location management
4. **Before/after photo documentation** — per-vehicle, per-service photo gallery for liability protection
5. **Package/service type pricing** — tiered pricing by service package (basic wash, full detail, PPF, ceramic coating)

## Product Concept
**MVP (2–3 weeks):**
- Customer CRM: name, phone, vehicles (year/make/model/VIN/color)
- Per-vehicle service record: service date, products used, notes, photos
- Scheduling: book a service for a specific vehicle
- Invoice builder: service packages with tiered pricing
- Stripe payment on invoice completion
- Basic warranty certificate generator

**Fleet account module (v2):**
- B2B account: company name, fleet size, billing contact
- Multiple VINs under one account
- Per-VIN visit log + invoicing
- Monthly invoice consolidation

**Pricing:**
- $49/mo: up to 100 active customers; vehicle history; invoicing
- $99/mo: unlimited customers + fleet accounts + warranty registry
- $79 LTD: up to 50 active customers (AppSumo)
