## One-Line Pitch

## Problem

Small healthcare practices (dental, physical therapy, optometry, chiropractic) face mandatory compliance obligations:

- **HIPAA annual risk assessment**: Required by law but done via 30-page paper checklists
- **Staff certifications**: CPR/BLS (expire every 2 years), OSHA bloodborne pathogens (annual), state licensure (varies), dental radiation safety (varies by state)
- **Infection control documentation**: OSHA-required sterilization logs, handwashing protocols, PPE inventory
- **Employee HIPAA acknowledgment**: Staff must sign and acknowledge HIPAA policies annually
- **Equipment maintenance logs**: Autoclave spore test logs, handpiece maintenance records

Every small practice manages this across paper binders, Google Sheets, and personal calendar reminders. When a HIPAA auditor or state inspector arrives, office managers scramble to assemble documentation.

**The cost of non-compliance**:
- HIPAA violations: $100–50,000 per violation
- State dental board sanctions for lapsed staff certifications
- OSHA fines for missing documentation ($15,625 per serious violation)

Enterprise GRC tools (Vanta, Drata) are designed for tech startups targeting SOC2 compliance — not for a solo dentist managing OSHA binders. Price points of $10K–300K per year are laughable for a 2-chair dental office.
