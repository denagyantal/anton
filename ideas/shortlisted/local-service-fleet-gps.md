## One-Line Pitch

GPS fleet management built for local HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, and cleaning fleets — no ELD compliance bloat, no 3-year contracts, $15/vehicle/mo with native Jobber and Housecall Pro integration.

## Problem

Small service businesses with 5-25 vehicles are systematically overcharged by Samsara and Motive — two platforms built for over-the-road commercial trucking that have successfully sold their product to local service fleets who don't need (and are legally exempt from) ELD compliance.

**The contract trap**:
- Every vehicle added to Motive/Samsara starts its own separate 3-year auto-renewing agreement
- A 10-truck HVAC fleet adding 3 trucks in Year 1: each new truck locked for 3 years; staggered contract ends make exit prohibitively expensive
- BBB complaint (Feb 2026): Samsara "refuses to adjust the contract to reflect only the devices we are using" after a fleet downsized 30%
- Early termination on a 10-truck Samsara fleet exiting after 1 year: $9,600+

**The compliance irrelevance**:
- HVAC, plumbing, lawn care, and cleaning fleets operating within 150 air-miles of their home base are legally exempt from ELD requirements
- They're paying for full trucking compliance tooling they legally don't need
- "Using Motive to track a local service fleet is expensive, complicated, and entirely the wrong tool for the job"

**The price gap**:
- Spytec: $8.95/vehicle/mo — basic GPS only, no maintenance scheduling, no digital DVIR, no FSM integration
- Samsara/Motive: $35-50+/vehicle/mo + $99-148 hardware + 3-year minimum
- 10-vehicle fleet: $9,450/year (Samsara) vs $1,611/year (Spytec) — but Spytec has no features
- Nothing at $15-20/vehicle with real service-fleet-specific features

**The FSM integration gap**:
- Local service businesses already run on Jobber or HousecallPro
- Samsara/Motive have no native Jobber/HCP integration — GPS data lives in a separate silo
- A $15/vehicle/mo tool that syncs GPS arrival times with Jobber dispatch boards = a natural co-purchase

## Product Concept: "FleetLocal"

**Core MVP (6-8 weeks):**
- **Real-time GPS tracking**: OBD-II plug ($25 one-time hardware) OR phone-based GPS app (no hardware required initially)
- **Driver arrival accountability**: auto-timestamp when truck arrives at job site; auto-flag late arrivals
- **Maintenance scheduling**: oil change / tire rotation / inspection reminders by mileage or date; push notification to owner
- **Digital DVIR**: simple pre/post-trip inspection from driver's phone; photo of any damage
- **Dispatch notifications**: when tech dispatches, GPS sends arrival time estimate to customer via SMS
- **Jobber + HousecallPro native integration**: GPS arrival data auto-logs to Jobber job; no manual entry

**Pricing:**
- $15/vehicle/mo, month-to-month (no annual lock-in)
- OBD-II hardware: $25 one-time (add-on, not required to start)
- LTD: $99/vehicle lifetime — "track your truck forever for what Samsara charges in 30 days"
