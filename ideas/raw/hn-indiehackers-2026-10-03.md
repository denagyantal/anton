## Zirco.ai — AI Front Desk for Dental Practices

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (beta)
- **Engagement**: 3 comments (low HN traction but technically rich post)
- **Revenue Data**: Beta — 30+ discovery calls done, no MRR disclosed
- **Boring Business Score**: 5 — dental admin is invisible but enormous
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices (30,000+ in the US)
- **Core Value Prop**: Replaces dental front desk staff — handles insurance verification (logs into 10+ carrier portals via Playwright + Availity), answers inbound calls (Vapi voice AI), books appointments into Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft, sends SMS/email reminders. Full front desk workflow, autonomously.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Front desk staff costs $40–50K/year with 40% annual turnover. Insurance verification alone takes 2–3 hrs/day per practice. No clean AI solution existed that integrates with dental PMS software natively. HIPAA compliance is a moat.
- **Our Angle**: Per-seat vs per-call pricing could be a major differentiator. Bundle insurance verification + scheduling as the wedge. Smaller clinics (1–3 chairs) are unserved — large players go enterprise. LTD could work for "insurance verification only" module.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (HIPAA friction makes one-time purchase risky, but a capped-use tier could work)

## Key Themes & Market Signals (2026-10-03)

1. **AI front desk/voice for boring businesses** is the hottest category — dental, HVAC, plumbing. Unit economics are excellent ($300–800 MRR per small business client, 80% margins). Multiple founders validating this independently.

2. **Construction documentation** is having a moment — InspectMind (YC), Opusense (YC), others all launched in 2025–2026. The **home inspector sub-niche** (100K+ sole proprietors) appears unserved by the YC-backed tools.

3. **"Competing with Excel"** remains the dominant signal in boring B2B — BasinCheck vs oil & gas safety Excel sheets, PropertyLedger vs landlord spreadsheets. Any vertical where the competition is Excel is a green field.

4. **Trades don't buy software, they buy booked jobs** — The excavation estimator case study confirms that tools which *directly generate revenue* (online calculators, quote embedders) have near-zero churn. This insight should shape product positioning across all trade verticals.

5. **Small landlord tooling** is resurging — multiple products launching (PropertyLedger, fixRAgent, Rentesy for India). Stessa/Baselane's banking-product model is creating backlash — clean, honest pricing at $19–49/mo is differentiating.

6. **AI Voice Agencies** are becoming productized — the transition from agency to white-label SaaS is the obvious next step. No dominant product there yet for the sub-$1M revenue local business.
