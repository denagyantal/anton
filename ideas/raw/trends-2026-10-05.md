- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.constructionindustry.ai/funding/probook-40m-series-a-home-services-dispatch-ai/ — Probook raises $40M (a16z + Sequoia) for AI dispatch OS for home services
  - https://ai-for-contractors.com/news/netic-ai-23m-series-b-plumbers-roofers/ — Netic AI $23M Series B (Founders Fund) for plumbing/roofing AI ops

  - https://ai-for-contractors.com — Rebar $14M for AI quoting for HVAC/electrical/plumbing
  - https://qubit.capital/blog/rise-vertical-saas-sector-specific-opportunities — $300M+ into contractor AI in Q1 2026 alone
- **Description**: A16z, Sequoia, Founders Fund, and Battery Ventures are all placing large bets on AI-native operating systems for the trades in 2026. The thesis: trades run on dispatch boards, and no existing software was built around dispatch first. Companies like Probook, Netic, and Miter are AI-native (not AI layered on top of legacy FSM), targeting the 75% of contractors with zero dedicated software. The wedge is dispatch; the expansion is everything else: call intake, scheduling, estimating, invoicing, customer follow-up.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, general contracting — shops with 2–50 trucks/technicians
- **Market Timing**: Early → Growing (massive VC validation but 75% of market still unserved)
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook — https://www.constructionindustry.ai/funding/probook-40m-series-a-home-services-dispatch-ai/
  - Netic AI — https://ai-for-contractors.com/news/netic-ai-23m-series-b-plumbers-roofers/
  - Miter — https://www.miter.com/resources/its-time-to-build/
  - ServiceTitan (incumbent, enterprise) — https://www.servicetitan.com/
- **White Space**: Sub-5-truck operations that find Probook/ServiceTitan too expensive. Single-trade focus (e.g., electrical-only or plumbing-only AI dispatch with trade-specific pricing databases). AI dispatch for seasonal businesses (landscaping, snow removal) where demand spikes unpredictably.
- **Speed to Market**: Hard for full platform (4+ months), but a dispatch-only MVP for a single trade vertical in 4–6 weeks is feasible.
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — contractors will pay monthly for mission-critical ops software, but a one-time deal could capture the "just need to get started" segment
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC dispatch software", "plumbing scheduling AI", "ServiceTitan alternative small shop", "field service AI dispatch"
