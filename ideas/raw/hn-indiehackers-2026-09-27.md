## FieldFlow — $5/Month Jobber Alternative for Solo Contractors
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47294092
- **Additional Links**: https://fieldflow-nine.vercel.app/auth
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (March 2026)
- **Engagement**: Solo launch, low comments — still indexing
- **Revenue Data**: $5/month flat fee, no per-user fees. Pre-revenue/very early stage.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Solo trade contractors (carpenter/plumber/electrician/handyman)
- **Core Value Prop**: A $5/month invoicing, scheduling, and client management tool for one-person trade operations. Built by a solo carpenter who was paying $150+/month for Jobber and found it bloated. "I use it myself daily. That's the only quality filter I trust."
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Jobber starts at $39/month; Tradify is $47/user/month. Solo operators (1-person operations) are massively overcharged by tools designed for teams. The market of self-employed tradespeople in the US is enormous — plumbers, electricians, carpenters, handymen — most are still on paper or WhatsApp. The $5 price signals a different willingness-to-pay tier for the smallest operators.
- **Our Angle**: Solo-first trade management at $9/month with offline-capable mobile app. Include quote-to-invoice conversion, client photo storage (before/after job photos), and one-tap Google Review request. The real barrier to solo trades adopting software is not price — it is complexity. A 5-minute setup and phone-native UX wins more than a $5 price tag.
- **LTD Potential**: 4 (low price but massive total addressable market)

## SecurVO — Compliance & Operations Management for Service Businesses
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45906762
- **Additional Links**: https://securvo.com
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (November 2025)
- **Engagement**: Beta stage, 50 testers sought, flat-tier pricing model
- **Revenue Data**: Pre-revenue, beta. Positioning: no per-seat pricing, tier-based with full feature access.
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Property management, facilities management, field services
- **Core Value Prop**: Single platform for recurring tasks (inspections, certifications, maintenance), vendor compliance (COIs, licenses, document expirations), asset maintenance, SOPs with attestation, and incident tracking. Solves the "spreadsheets and $50–200/user/month project management tools that weren't built for compliance work" problem.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Service businesses running 5–50 technicians spend hours manually tracking vendor certificate expirations, inspection schedules, and compliance documents. Existing tools (Jobber, ServiceTitan) handle job dispatch but not compliance/vendor management. Property managers in particular are drowning in COI renewals, annual inspection schedules, and permit renewals — often managed in Google Sheets. The gap is a purpose-built compliance layer that attaches to existing FSM software rather than replacing it.
- **Our Angle**: "Compliance co-pilot" — a dedicated $49/month tool that tracks vendor COI expirations, sends renewal reminders, and generates compliance reports. Positions as add-on to existing FSM software rather than a full replacement. Initial target: property management companies (50+ units) who have the most vendor compliance burden.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 (compliance is recurring by nature — document renewals are ongoing)

## Craftplan — Open-Source ERP for Micro-Bakeries and Small-Batch Manufacturers
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46847690
- **Additional Links**: https://github.com/puemos/craftplan
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (February 2026)
- **Engagement**: 1,000+ GitHub stars, front-page HN, covered by Gigazine
- **Revenue Data**: Open source / self-hosted (no direct revenue). Strong community signal.
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Micro-bakeries, small-batch food producers, artisan D2C manufacturers
- **Core Value Prop**: Production management for small-scale food manufacturers: versioned BOMs with automatic cost rollups, lot traceability, allergen tracking, demand forecasting, purchase orders, order scheduling, invoice generation. Built with Elixir/Phoenix LiveView. The BOM+cost rollup feature is described as "what would have saved hours" for small batch producers pricing their products.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Micro-bakeries, jam producers, cheese makers, candle makers — all small-batch D2C businesses — currently run on spreadsheets. Commercial ERP is vastly overbuilt and overpriced. Craftplan is open-source and self-hosted, which creates an opportunity for a hosted, managed SaaS version of the same product at $29–$79/month. Multiple HN commenters expressed they would pay for a hosted version rather than self-hosting.
- **Our Angle**: Hosted SaaS version of Craftplan-style production management for small-batch food businesses — $49/month, zero setup, handles recipes/BOMs/inventory/orders. Target: cottage food producers, micro-bakeries, small-batch CPG brands. This is an "AppSumo-perfect" product: clear use case, underserved by Shopify/QuickBooks, low ongoing support burden.
- **LTD Potential**: 5 (clear LTD candidate: one-time setup tool that bakers use forever)

## Manufacturing ERP for 3-Year Builder (Carbon/Small Manufacturer)
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44792005
- **Additional Links**: N/A (Carbon Systems)
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (August 2025)
- **Engagement**: Significant thread, 5 live customers at posting
- **Revenue Data**: 5 paying customers (small job shops). No ARR disclosed. ERP consultant commenter mentions the SMB manufacturing ERP market is dominated by Acumatica, Sage, and NetSuite.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Small job shops, contract manufacturers, small-batch custom manufacturers
- **Core Value Prop**: Purpose-built MES/ERP for small manufacturers that does not force them into generic accounting-first workflows. Handles quoting, sales orders, BOMs, shop floor scheduling, work centers, invoicing — but leaves financial reporting to QuickBooks/NetSuite rather than competing with them. The "middle layer" (purchasing, BOMs, invoices, work orders) is standardized; sales and shop floor remain flexible.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Small job shops (5–50 employees) are stuck between: (1) spreadsheets + QuickBooks, which scale poorly, and (2) full ERP systems ($50K+ implementation + $1K+/month licensing). The $200–$500/month MES-only tier that integrates with existing accounting is almost entirely vacant. Multiple comments confirm: "the mid-market manufacturer with 200-ish employees" wants accounting baked in, but the sub-50-employee job shop just needs operations software.
- **Our Angle**: Operations-only MES for small job shops (1–20 employees) at $149/month: quote → work order → shop floor task → invoice. QuickBooks sync built-in from day one. Positions as "the operations layer" that job shops need before they're ready for full ERP. Target: machine shops, fab shops, small electronics assembly, woodworking shops.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 (operational software needs ongoing support, but LTD is viable at $299-$499 for very small shops)

## Boring Local Classifieds Site — $25K MRR (Zero VC Interest)
- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/five-years-in-how-a-boring-local-classifieds-site-quietly-crossed-25k-mrr-4d2395fb81
- **Additional Links**: N/A (no product name disclosed)
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Revenue milestone post (July 2026)
- **Engagement**: Featured IH post with detailed founder breakdown
- **Revenue Data**: $25K+ MRR, 5 years running, solo founder + 1 part-time. Revenue breakdown: ~55% featured listings, ~25% power-seller subscriptions, ~15% escrow fees, ~5% local business directory.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Local classifieds / local service directories (plumbers, tutors, repair shops)
- **Core Value Prop**: Hyper-local classifieds site for a single city/region. Built on a licensed classifieds script base. WhatsApp-first contact flow (vs. in-app messaging). Manual approval queue for listings. Key insight: trust was the bottleneck, not traffic. Escrow for high-value transactions (cars, electronics) unlocked dead categories. The directory line for local businesses ($X/month for a verified profile) is the stickiest and fastest-growing revenue.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The local business directory angle ($5/month from plumbers, tutors, repair shops for a verified profile) is the sleeper revenue stream. This is essentially "Yelp but honest, local, and affordable." Most local businesses hate Yelp/Google review pricing and extortion tactics. A $19/month directory with transparent pricing, no fake review games, and WhatsApp-native lead delivery could undercut Yelp in smaller markets that aren't worth Yelp's attention.
- **Our Angle**: Local business directory for a specific trade (e.g., handymen, cleaners, electricians) in a single city. Charge tradespeople $19/month for a verified listing. Acquire through trade Facebook groups, NextDoor, and local contractor associations. The escrow model for jobs >$500 is a strong trust differentiator.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 (recurring directory subscriptions are the model)

- **Boring Business Score**: 5

A $99/month voice-first field tech app that syncs to any CRM (ServiceMonster, PestRoutes, Jobber) is a real gap.
- **Our Angle**: Voice-first field tech assistant — dictate job notes while driving, auto-populate the CRM, receive upsell prompts based on job type. Works for pest control, HVAC, plumbing, any field tech. White-label for vertical SaaS providers as an add-on module.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 (voice API costs make LTD risky; $49/tech/month recurring is better)

Targets small distributors, job shops, and wholesalers. No replacement required — just a reporting layer they can use on their phone.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 (integration-dependent, but a solid LTD at $199 for the reporting tool alone)

## ServiceTitan / Jobber Alternative Gap — Crew-Sized Field Service (2–10 Techs)

- **Additional Links**: https://projul.com/blog/best-servicetitan-alternatives/
- **Platform**: Industry research / HN synthesis
- **Type**: Market gap analysis
- **Engagement**: N/A
- **Revenue Data**: ServiceTitan: $250–$400/tech/month. Jobber: $39–$199/month (team plans). RazorSync: $40+/month. FieldPulse: competitive pricing. The clear price ceiling pain: ServiceTitan requires $35K–$65K year-one cost for a 15-person shop.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical contractors with 2–15 technicians
- **Core Value Prop** (gap): The 2–10 technician field service company is the most underserved segment. They're too big for solo tools (FieldFlow, solo Jobber) and too price-sensitive for ServiceTitan. Jobber's Grow plan ($199/month) hits the ceiling fast when adding users. The "crew shop" tier (2–10 techs, $100–$200/month total, not per user) is wide open.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Current crew-sized options all charge per-tech: Jobber $39+/tech, Tradify $47/user, ServiceTitan $250+/tech. A flat-fee team plan for 2–10 technicians at $149/month (unlimited techs) would undercut every option by 60–80% for this segment. Must-have features: offline mobile app, GPS dispatch, photo documentation, and a client portal for review requests. The flat-fee team model is the real differentiation.
- **Our Angle**: "Jobber for the rest of us" — $149/month flat fee for unlimited crew members (up to 10 techs). Designed for 2–10 person HVAC, plumbing, electrical shops that find Jobber too expensive once they add a second or third tech. Focus on simplicity: 4-screen mobile flow (job → photos → invoice → review request). Target acquisition: r/HVAC, r/Plumbing, local contractor Facebook groups.
- **LTD Potential**: 4 (strong LTD candidate: one-time $499 for unlimited techs — small shops love lifetime deals)

- **Engagement**: N/A (derived opportunity)

- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Commercial cleaning businesses (janitorial, office cleaning, school/hospital cleaning)
- **Core Value Prop** (gap): Commercial cleaning has fundamentally different software needs than residential maid services: contract billing vs. per-visit billing, compliance tracking (OSHA, green cleaning certifications), employee scheduling across multiple fixed locations vs. flexible residential routes, chemical inventory management, quality inspection checklists. ZenMaid doesn't serve this market. Existing commercial cleaning software (Swept, Janitorial Manager) has poor UX and is overpriced for small operators.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: A $99–$149/month commercial cleaning operations platform focused on: (1) recurring contract billing for 5–50 location clients, (2) per-location quality inspection checklists with photo documentation, (3) supply/chemical inventory tracking, (4) employee scheduling with attendance tracking. ZenMaid's $3M ARR confirms residential cleaning SaaS works — commercial is a larger, more underserved adjacency.
- **Our Angle**: Commercial cleaning operations platform at $99/month. Core differentiator: mobile inspection app where cleaners complete a per-room checklist with photo documentation — auto-generates a client-facing "completion report" after each clean. Positions as professional quality documentation for commercial clients who require audit trails.
