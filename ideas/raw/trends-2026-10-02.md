## Meta-Trends to Watch

1. **Embedded payments as the wedge**: The playbook for boring business SaaS is: own the system of record → layer in payments (2.6-3.5% take rate) → then lending → then AI agents. This is how ServiceTitan ($9B IPO) and CompanyCam ($2B) got there.

2. **AI inbound call handling is the universal entry point**: Every boring business misses calls. AI voice agents trained on vertical-specific scripts are the fastest path to ROI and stickiness for any niche.

3. **Sub-10-employee operators are the white space**: All major funding is going to mid-market and enterprise. The 2-10 person trades shop, the 3-truck carrier, the solo landlord — they're a massive TAM with almost no AI tooling.

4. **Compliance is the new forcing function**: OSHA, rental regulations, EU AI Act, healthcare billing rules — each new regulation forces a wave of boring-business digitization. Find the regulation, build the tracker.

5. **Micro-SaaS viability**: Vertical SaaS is $157B and growing 2-3x faster than horizontal. A solo founder can hit $5K-$30K MRR in these niches with 70%+ margins. The niches are too small for VCs, which means they're available for bootstrappers.
