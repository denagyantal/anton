## Pest Control Scheduling & Routing SaaS

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
- **Additional Links**: https://fromscratch.dev/blog/boring-saas-ideas
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (founder built internal ops tools by going undercover as a pest control technician)
- **Engagement**: 448 upvotes, 182 comments — very strong signal
- **Revenue Data**: No direct revenue disclosed; PestRoutes (market leader) acquired for $300M, signaling massive market
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Pest control (35,000+ US companies)
- **Core Value Prop**: Founder took a job as a technician for 2 months to learn the industry, identified that 60% of market is PE-consolidated but service quality is degrading — independent operators using pen-and-paper
- **Gap/Opportunity**: PE rollups create service degradation, opening door for quality-focused independent operators with better ops software. Key unmet needs: voice-to-CRM data entry (technicians wear gloves, can't type), smart scheduling, upselling prompts, route optimization, EPA/chemical compliance documentation
- **Our Angle**: Lightweight, affordable ($49-99/month) alternative to PestRoutes/ServSuite that serves the independent/owner-operator end. Could start with AI voice input for data entry (glove-friendly), expand to compliance docs
- **LTD Potential**: 3 — recurring compliance and scheduling needs favor subscription, but a limited lifetime deal for smaller operators could drive initial adoption
