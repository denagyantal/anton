## Crewbird — FSM/CRM for Home Services with Google LSA Integration

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418273
- **Additional Links**: https://getcrewbird.com
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Ask HN: What are you working on? (November 2026)
- **Engagement**: Active discussion, multiple people expressing interest in beta/newsletter
- **Revenue Data**: Not disclosed, early-stage; founder's dad carpet cleaning business approaching 3x ROAS
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Home/field service (carpet cleaning, cleaning, HVAC, landscaping etc.)
- **Core Value Prop**: FSM + CRM purpose-built to integrate with Google Local Service Ads — tracks ROAS at the job level, something existing SaaS tools don't do well
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Existing CRMs (ServiceTitan, Jobber) have no native Google LSA API integration. Owner had to track spend in a spreadsheet. The gap between "basic spreadsheet" and "full ERP" is huge and painful — multiple commenters resonated with "soul crushing" experience of juggling 12 trackers. Legacy desktop CRM used by father's business was discontinued, OS-upgrade fragile.
- **Our Angle**: Narrow wedge: Google LSA-native FSM. Acquire small home service businesses that run LSA ads (biggest growth channel for trades). Charge for the ROAS visibility they can't get elsewhere. Add voice AI intake, payroll (CheckHQ), and upsell.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 — recurring model fits better (ongoing LSA spend = ongoing value), but LTD feasible at $299-499

## Prelay — Weather-Based Dispatch for Outdoor Contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (November 2026)
- **Engagement**: 2 points, launched Nov 2, 2026 — very fresh
- **Revenue Data**: Not disclosed, early commercial launch
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Landscaping, roofing, outdoor trades (US)
- **Core Value Prop**: Monitors contractor job schedules vs. weather/soil conditions for 62 activities across 12 trade types. Alerts if job site is unsuitable before crews dispatch. Owner quoted: "I wake up at 4am every day" to check phone weather for 10+ sites.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Current solution is owner checking phone weather app per site — massively manual. Opportunity to extend into payroll integration (auto-track weather cancellations for crew pay calculations), job rescheduling workflows, and integration with existing FSM tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro).
- **Our Angle**: Prelay does weather intelligence but doesn't integrate with FSM. Build a competing product or integration layer that ties weather alerts directly into scheduling/dispatch inside Jobber/ServiceTitan. Or build a focused "weather + dispatch" tool for roofing companies specifically — a narrow, defensible niche.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 — high value as ongoing monitoring service; but could sell a one-time setup + annual subscription hybrid

## Pest Control Vertical SaaS — $2M ARR Incumbent, Gaps Remain

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
- **Additional Links**: https://onhand.pro
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Story post ("I took a technician job to build vertical SaaS for pest control")
- **Engagement**: 448 points, 182 comments — exceptionally high for this niche
- **Revenue Data**: Commenter confirmed pest control SaaS is running at $2M ARR. Founder ultimately chose tech-enabled operator route over SaaS.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Pest control (commercial and residential)
- **Core Value Prop**: The OP explored building SaaS but concluded existing incumbent (PestPac/others) is "decent, cheap, ubiquitous." He pivoted to acquiring a traditional pest control operator and tech-enabling it from within.

## Rudus (YC P26) — AI Estimation for Concrete Subcontractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48374528
- **Additional Links**: https://www.rudus.ai / demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PAMNDRWEdlI
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Launch HN (YC P26, June 2026)
- **Engagement**: 41 points, 14 comments — solid for a niche product
- **Revenue Data**: Not disclosed; YC-funded, enterprise pricing implied
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Concrete subcontractors (construction)
- **Core Value Prop**: AI reads structural PDFs, auto-classifies sheets, extracts all concrete elements, expands into 80-120 priced line items. Cuts takeoff time from days to minutes. Copilot approach (not black box) means estimator stays in control.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The "incumbent software hasn't been updated since 2020" signal is key. Every AI takeoff tool built for GCs — nobody built specifically for concrete subs. - **Our Angle**: Build the equivalent for a different subtrade — masonry, rebar/steel, electrical rough-in, or mechanical (HVAC rough). Same problem: manual PDF takeoffs eating days. Narrower than Rudus = less competition, faster PMF. Target mid-size shops (5-20 estimators) who can't afford enterprise platforms.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 — per-seat enterprise model fits better; but AppSumo deal for single-estimator shops feasible at $299-499
