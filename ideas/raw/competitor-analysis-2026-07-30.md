## Field Service Management: Solo Trades Operators Underserved by Pricing Tiers

### Market Landscape

| Competitor | Price | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|-------|-----------|------------|
| ServiceTitan | $8,000–$15,000+/yr | Deep FSM feature set, industry-specific | Way too complex & expensive for <10 techs; long onboarding; add-on fee creep |
| Jobber | $39–$599/mo (per-user) | Simple UX, good CRM, affordable entry | Per-user pricing becomes painful at 10+ techs; limited route optimization; no native scheduling for recurring jobs |
| Housecall Pro | $59–$329/mo | All-in-one, good mobile app | Mobile bugs/crashes; per-user price creep; lacks depth for specialty trades; routing relies on third-party integrations |
| FieldPulse | $208–$533/mo (annual) | Flat-rate unlimited users | QuickBooks Desktop sync unreliable; service-agreement depth lacking; strains past 30 techs |
| FieldEdge | ~$100–$150/user/mo | Best QuickBooks Desktop real-time sync; mature dispatch | Quote-only pricing (no transparency); expensive |
| Service Fusion | ~$149–$349/mo | Mid-tier, good dispatching | Feature gaps vs. Jobber/HCP at similar price |

### Top Customer Complaints (from reviews)

1. ServiceTitan pricing ($8K–$15K+/yr) eliminates it for solo/1-3 tech shops — [FieldCamp ServiceTitan Review 2026](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/servicetitan/)
2. Jobber's per-user pricing: at 15 technicians, $50/user/mo = $750/mo before add-ons — [Field Service Software Cost Guide](https://fieldserviceguide.com/field-service-software-cost-pricing/)
3. Housecall Pro mobile app bugs and crashes; users revert to desktop — [Housecall Pro Review 2026](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/housecall-pro/)
4. Jobber: "unnecessary complexity when scheduling recurring jobs" and "lack of text box customization" — [GetOneCrew Jobber vs HCP](https://www.getonecrew.com/post/jobber-vs-housecall-pro)
5. FieldPulse QuickBooks Desktop sync is unreliable per multiple G2/Capterra reports — [Field Service Guide FSM Buyer's Guide](https://fieldserviceguide.com/best-field-service-management-software/)
6. Almost every FSM platform charges per user or per technician — cost scales with headcount, not with value — [Field Service Software Cost](https://fieldserviceguide.com/field-service-software-cost-pricing/)

### Identified Gap

- **What's missing**: Flat-rate FSM for 1–10 technician shops (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control) that includes route optimization natively, solid recurring job scheduling, and simple QuickBooks/Xero sync — without per-user pricing punishing growth
- **Who needs it**: Owner-operator trades businesses (HVAC, plumber, electrician) with 1–8 techs; too small for ServiceTitan, but frustrated by Jobber's per-user model at growth stage
- **Why competitors don't do it**: Jobber and HCP grow revenue by charging per seat; moving to flat-rate hurts their unit economics. ServiceTitan targets enterprise.
- **Current workaround**: Jobber or Housecall Pro on the entry plan, export to spreadsheets for routing, Google Maps for navigation, manual QuickBooks entry

### Our Opportunity

- **Product concept**: Flat-rate FSM for small trades shops ($79–$99/mo covers 1–10 techs) with native route optimization, recurring job scheduling, mobile-first job cards, and direct QuickBooks/Xero sync
- **Key differentiator**: Per-shop pricing (not per-seat), so growth doesn't penalize the owner; simpler UX than ServiceTitan; better routing than Jobber
- **LTD price point**: $79 (up to 5 techs) / $99 (unlimited techs)
- **Target channels**: r/HVAC, r/Plumbing, r/electricians, r/smallbusiness, ACCA (Air Conditioning Contractors of America) forums, PHCC forums, Facebook "HVAC Business Owners" group
- **AppSumo potential**: Yes — clear price differentiation story vs. per-user tools; trades are underrepresented on AppSumo (easy to stand out)
