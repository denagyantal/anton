- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Home services (cleaning companies, HVAC, plumbing)
- **Core Value Prop**: AI agents run an entire home services business 24/7 — handles inbound calls/SMS, generates quotes, dispatches workers, runs ads, follows up with leads, customer support. Founder runs 3 cleaning companies in California using this system.

## Faraday — Back Office Automation for Home Services

- **Source**: https://www.faraday.so/ (from HN Who's Hiring — February 2026)
- **Additional Links**: https://hnhiring.com/february-2026
- **Platform**: HN (Hiring signal)
- **Type**: YC-backed startup hiring Senior Full-Stack Engineer at $160K–$200K + equity
- **Engagement**: Hiring signal indicates serious traction
- **Revenue Data**: Not public; identified "$150M in replacement opportunities" for one Southwest HVAC customer using their equipment tracking tool
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Home services — HVAC, plumbers, electricians, water heater installers
- **Core Value Prop**: AI-powered back office for home services contractors. Automates: permit pulling/tracking/inspection scheduling, rebate eligibility calculation + submission, warranty registration + CRM sync, equipment data capture (model, serial, age, warranty dates), upsell opportunity identification.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Faraday targets larger operators ($2M+ revenue). No solution for the 1–3 technician shops that still do permits and rebates manually. Setup fee and integrations with ServiceTitan likely price out small operators.
- **Our Angle**: "Faraday for small HVAC shops" — simplified permit + rebate automation for companies with 1–5 techs. No ServiceTitan required. Works with Google Sheets or basic CRM.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 (complex integrations, not LTD-friendly) — pivot to subscription

- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Pest control companies

- **Type**: Market opportunity identified by multiple IH writers
- **Engagement**: Multiple sources pointing to same gap
- **Revenue Data**: Estimated $10K–$30K MRR potential per analysis
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Small landlords (5–20 units), property management for individuals
- **Core Value Prop**: Landlords managing 5–20 units currently use text messages + spreadsheets. Need: maintenance ticketing, rent reminders, document storage, tenant portal. Buildium/AppFolio target 50+ units with enterprise pricing.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The $10–50/unit/month market for small landlords is fragmented. Gap for a landlord managing 5–20 units that just needs: rent collection, maintenance requests, lease docs, basic accounting.
- **Our Angle**: Landlord Pocket — mobile-first app for self-managing landlords. Rent collection via Stripe, maintenance tickets with photo upload, document storage, tenant messaging. $19/mo for up to 10 units. LTD $149.
- **LTD Potential**: 5

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-profitable-ai-voice-saas-agency-300-800-mrr-per-client-frAbgO1yQMfHOFFtY3gE
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Revenue milestone / how-to post
- **Engagement**: Featured IH post
- **Revenue Data**: $300–$800 MRR per client; ~80% margin; $800–$2K setup fee; upsells to $1,000–$1,100/mo with SMS reminders + email sequences
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices, plumbing/HVAC contractors, hair salons, small law firms, medical clinics, home service providers ($500K–$5M annual revenue)
- **Core Value Prop**: Local service businesses lose $500–$2K per missed call. AI voice agent handles inbound calls 24/7, books appointments, sends reminders. Replaces $30K/year receptionist at $300–$800/month.

- **Our Angle**: Self-serve AI receptionist for HVAC/plumbing companies — 5-minute setup, connects to Google Calendar or Jobber, handles calls/texts, sends appointment confirmations. $149/mo. Much lower friction than agency model.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 (ongoing AI costs, but LTD could work at $299–499 for early adopters)

## HVAC Pricing Transparency & Fraud Prevention Tool

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41828896 (America's new millionaire class: Plumbers and HVAC entrepreneurs)
- **Additional Links**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41832117 (My dad bought a failing HVAC business)
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Discussion thread (high engagement)
- **Engagement**: High (viral HN discussion, widely shared)
- **Revenue Data**: N/A — demand signal, not a product
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Homeowners + HVAC service businesses
- **Core Value Prop**: Widespread consumer frustration with HVAC fraud (capacitor replaced for $500 instead of $100, unnecessary full system replacements). No neutral diagnostic tool exists. Users reference YouTube as their only education source.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Two-sided opportunity: (1) Homeowner app to verify HVAC quotes (enter what was diagnosed, see fair price range) — consumer B2C. (2) Honest HVAC company directory with verified reviews and flat-rate pricing. Neither exists at scale.
- **Our Angle**: HVAC price estimator for homeowners — enter your unit age/brand/issue, get a fair repair cost range with parts breakdown. Monetize via certified HVAC company listings. Free for consumers, $49–99/mo for verified contractor profiles.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 (marketplace model, not LTD-friendly)
