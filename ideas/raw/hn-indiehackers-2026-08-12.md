## FleetFix – Trucking Fleet Management for Small Operators

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46468469
- **Additional Links**: n/a
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: Low (early/small post, Jan 2, 2026)
- **Revenue Data**: Free product (pre-revenue validation play)
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Trucking / small fleet operators
- **Core Value Prop**: Free fleet management tool built specifically for small trucking operators (2–20 trucks) that enterprise platforms like Fleetio or Samsara completely ignore with their pricing and feature complexity. Covers vehicle management (VIN, status tags, mileage), maintenance logs (oil changes, brake inspections, cost tracking), parts tracking, downtime analytics, maintenance calendar, CSV export.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Large fleet platforms (Fleetio, Samsara) are priced and scoped for 100+ truck fleets. The 1–20 truck owner-operator segment is massively underserved — they're running everything on spreadsheets or whiteboards. FleetFix went free but never monetized. Nobody has built a paid, right-sized version for this segment.
- **Our Angle**: Add invoicing, driver scheduling, DOT compliance reminders, and IFTA fuel tax reporting — the actual headaches small trucking operators face beyond just maintenance logs. Charge $49–$99/mo per company. Build trust through a free tier, upsell on compliance features. Owner-operators will gladly pay to avoid DOT violations.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 (trucking operators may prefer subscription, but LTD possible as a one-time lifetime deal at $99–$149)

- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: AV rental, event production, lighting, staging, broadcast, film
- **Core Value Prop**: One-stop operations platform for equipment rental + crew scheduling + quoting + logistics + invoicing for production companies. Started by a founder who ran his own AV rental company at 16 and was frustrated by software built for other industries.

- **Our Angle**: Clone the Rentman model for an untouched adjacent niche — party/event supply rental (tents, tables, linens), medical device rental, or construction tool rental. Same problem (gear + crew + scheduling + invoicing), same buyer profile (small business owner who's frustrated), different TAM. Start with one niche, become the Rentman of that niche.

- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Licensed trades — electricians, HVAC techs, plumbers, cosmetologists, contractors
- **Core Value Prop**: Trades professionals have multi-state license renewal deadlines, continuing education requirements, and bond/insurance renewals they constantly let lapse.
