## Prelay – Weather-Based Dispatch Planning for Outdoor Contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (very recent — 2 days ago)
- **Engagement**: 2 points, early
- **Revenue Data**: Pre-revenue / early launch
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Landscaping, roofing, outdoor construction trades
- **Core Value Prop**: Monitors all scheduled job sites and determines weather suitability for 62 activities across 12 trades using ensemble weather models + custom ground-wetness model. Sends smart alerts before costly weather-related crew callouts.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Owners wake up at 4am to manually check weather for dozens of job sites — if they cancel after 6am, they still have to pay crews. No software focused on weather-triggered scheduling exists at a reasonable price. Interesting debate in thread: should it show raw data or make concrete action recommendations?
- **Our Angle**: Ultra-niche B2B scheduling intelligence. Tight wedge: start with one trade (roofing or landscaping), nail the go/no-go recommendation UX, expand to 12 trades. Could embed into existing FSM tools (ServiceTitan, Jobber) as an add-on. Alternative angle: build the notification-only version as a $29/mo Zapier-like module for any scheduling system.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (narrow feature set now, but could bundle into a broader outdoor contractor OS)
