## CATEGORY 1: Field Service Management (HVAC / Plumbing / Trades)

### Top Competitors & Pricing

**ServiceTitan**
- Pricing: $245–$500/technician/month (3 tiers: Starter, Essentials, The Works)
- Does NOT publish pricing — requires sales demo
- Mandatory 12-month contract
- Implementation fees: $5,000–$50,000+ one-time
- Real-world annual cost for 10-tech HVAC shop: $35,000–$65,000/year
- A documented case: price increase from ~$3K to ~$10K/month in February 2026
- Early termination fee documented at $39,375 (BBB complaint)
- G2 Ease of Setup: 6.8/10 (lowest of all categories; vs. Kickserv 8.9, Workiz 8.7)
- Capterra: 4.3/5 across 336 reviews; Value for Money: 3.8/5
- Sources: https://projul.com/blog/servicetitan-pricing-analysis-2026/, https://www.servicetitanpricing.com/, https://www.getonecrew.com/post/servicetitan-reviews

**Housecall Pro**
- Pricing (standard): Basic $59/mo (1 user), Essentials $149/mo (5 users), MAX $299/mo
- Pricing (HVAC/plumbing-specific, higher tier): Basic $79, Essentials $229–$299 (1 user/team), MAX $349–$649
- Extra users: $100/mo on Essentials, $75/mo on MAX
- Card processing starts at 2.59%
- Real-world cost often exceeds $200/month once needed features are added
- Sources: https://projul.com/blog/housecall-pro-pricing-analysis-2026/, https://schedulingkit.com/pricing-guides/housecall-pro-pricing

**Jobber**
- Pricing (month-to-month): Core $49 (1 user), Connect $139 (5 users), Grow $199 (10 users), Plus $499 (15 users)
- Annual pricing: $24 / $80 / $120 / $320/mo respectively
- Additional users: $29/month on any plan
- 14-day free trial available
- Sources: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/, https://tekpon.com/software/jobber/pricing/

**FieldEdge / Service Fusion / RazorSync**
- Mid-market alternatives; $100–$300/mo range
- Better value for 10–25 truck operations

**Repair-CRM**
- Entry-level at $19/mo single user (as of Aug 2026)
- Includes work orders + QuickBooks Online integration
- Sources: https://www.repair-crm.com/2026/08/30/hvac-software-for-small-business-2026-guide-comparison/

**ServiceTitan complaints (G2/Capterra/user forums):**

**Housecall Pro complaints (Capterra p.13):**

- "mobile app is glitchy"

- Cannot set custom texts outside automated messages

- Lacks video upload (photos only)
- Missing automatic tech time-tracking integration
- Cannot set different schedules for different personnel types
- "The cost — is more than we would like to pay"
- Sales commission reports require manual Excel parsing
- Housecall Pro raises prices based on trade (HVAC/plumbing more expensive than general)

**Jobber complaints (G2/Capterra):**

- Cannot label customers (e.g., "do not service", non-payers)
- No AI-powered dispatch (still manual drag-and-drop)
- No scheduling conflict alerts or automatic double-booking prevention
- No automatic missed call follow-up — still need a human receptionist
- QuickBooks sync errors reported
- Source: https://connecteam.com/reviews/jobber/, https://checkthat.ai/brands/jobber/reviews

### Feature Gaps / Missing Integrations
- **Intelligent dispatching**: No platform in the sub-$300/mo range offers AI-assisted scheduling that considers tech skills, location, and workload
- **Offline mode**: Field technicians in low-signal areas (rural) cannot invoice or update jobs
- **Automated missed call follow-up / AI phone**: All platforms still require human receptionists for inbound calls; customers don't get callbacks — 78% of HVAC contractors have <10 employees and can't afford a receptionist
- **True flat-rate pricing for small shops** (2–5 techs): ServiceTitan is overkill at $1,500–$2,500+/month; Housecall Pro still ends up $200–$600/month with real features; gap exists for $50–$80/month all-in product
- **Integrated payroll / time tracking**: Requires separate tools (Gusto, QuickBooks Payroll)
- **Custom reporting**: All platforms offer limited, non-adaptable reports

### Market Stats
- 78% of HVAC contractors employ fewer than 10 people
- 61% of HVAC firms with 3+ technicians now use FSM software
- Price gap: ServiceTitan at $2,500–$4,000/mo vs. QuoteIQ Max at $699/mo vs. basic tools at $50/mo
- Source: https://www.repair-crm.com/2026/05/27/hidden-costs-of-field-service-software-2026-small-business-guide/

### Opportunity
**"Simple FSM for 1–5 tech trades businesses at $49–$79/month flat"**
- The sweet spot between free/spreadsheets and the $200–$600/month overcomplicated platforms
- Core features: scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, QuickBooks sync, basic customer texting, flat-rate pricebook
- Must work offline on mobile
- Key differentiator: AI-assisted inbound call handling / voicemail-to-job creation
- Viable LTD at $99–$149; AppSumo target market
- Customer acquisition channels: HVAC Facebook groups, r/HVAC, r/Plumbing, r/smallbusiness

## CATEGORY 2: Property Management (Small Landlords)

### Top Competitors & Pricing

**AppFolio**
- Pricing: $1.40/unit/month with $280–$298/month minimum
- 50-unit minimum portfolio — small landlords under 50 units are refused entirely
- Best for: 50–500+ unit professional management companies
- Sources: https://aitoolsbakery.com/blog/appfolio-pricing-2026/, https://leasehub.app/article/appfolio-alternatives-property-managers-2026

**Buildium**
- Essential: $62/month (no minimum unit count)
- Growth: $192/month
- Premium: $400/month
- Per-transaction EFT and eSignature fees push effective cost to $297+/month at scale
- Additional charges for new client leads
- $99 for each bank account linked beyond first
- Best for: property management companies with 50–500 units, 17,000+ management companies use it
- Sources: https://costbench.com/compare/buildium-vs-appfolio/, https://www.clearleaddigital.com/blog/buildium-review

**TurboTenant**
- Free tier: listings, screening, leases, online rent collection, maintenance tracking
- Premium: $149/year ($12.42/month)
- Processing fees for tenant payments described as "hefty"
- Accounting services cost extra (not included in premium)
- Source: https://www.credaily.com/reviews/turbotenant-review/

**Innago**
- Free for landlords (charges tenants fees)
- Rated 4.9/5 on G2; ideal for <50 units, independent landlords
- Source: https://alternativeto.net/software/innago/

**Avail** (owned by Apartments.com)
- Free tier + paid $9/unit/month (Unlimited Plus)
- Source: https://www.avail.com/education/articles/best-free-property-management-software-for-landlords

**Stessa / Rentec Direct**
- Budget-conscious, 1–15 unit owner-operators
- Strong accounting, owner statements, affordable

### Top Customer Complaints

**TurboTenant complaints (Capterra p.2 — direct scrape):**
- "The accounting could be improved along with the reporting" — Angela B.
- "There is no built in accounting function. It is a separate feature" — Eric D.
- Wished for QuickBooks integration or Stessa-like expense reporting
- "The website has become a bit difficult to navigate" — Matt R.
- "Too many glitching between pages upon clicking and loading" — Corrida R.
- Processing fees for online tenant payments described as "hefty" — Nolan W.
- "Limited number of questions you are able to ask on pre-screener" — John I.
- Room-share rentals poorly handled; confusing for tenants
- Desired admin fee capability for landlords
- No Street Easy / Zillow listing integration (Zillow integration is top complaint in Zillow-dominant markets)
- After-hours customer support unavailable
- ACH transfer timing: 7–10 business days — "can complicate monthly cash flow"
- Platform became "slow and froze a lot" during trial — Corrida R.
- Source: https://capterra.com/p/147659/Turbo-Tenant/reviews/?page=2

**General small landlord complaints across platforms:**
- AppFolio/Buildium charge $50–$300+/month plus 2.9% transaction fees for features smaller landlords will never use
- Buildium Essential's $62/month has hidden costs: extra bank account fees, per-transaction fees, lead fees
- ACH payment delays (7–10 days) across virtually all platforms frustrate cash flow management
- No robust QuickBooks / Stessa-style accounting in free tier options
- Maintenance request workflow is weak on lower-tier tools: requests arrive via text/email/voicemail, no centralized tracking
- Lease renewal automation is missing or poor: landlords miss renewal windows, stuck at below-market rent
- No built-in rent increase workflow / rent comps
- Screening criteria customization limited (TransUnion default only on TurboTenant)
- Sources: https://www.helpmanagemyunits.com/turbotenant-review, https://keywise.app/blog/property-management-software-small-landlords

### Feature Gaps / Missing Integrations
- **Real accounting** (P&L, Schedule E tax prep, expense tracking): Available only via Stessa or as add-on; free tiers have none
- **Zillow / Apartments.com syndicated listings**: TurboTenant lacks Zillow; critical in many markets
- **Rent increase workflow + market rent comps**: No platform alerts landlords "your tenant is $200/mo below market" with 1-click renewal letter generation
- **Lease renewal automation**: Proactive alerts 60/90 days out + template-based renewal letters
- **Maintenance vendor network**: Connecting landlords with vetted contractors for repair requests (not just ticket tracking)
- **AI-powered tenant screening**: Beyond basic TransUnion reports; income verification, eviction prediction
- **Cash flow forecasting**: When rent comes in, when bills go out, projected shortfalls
- **Multi-state compliance**: Different lease laws, notice requirements, habitability standards by state — no platform handles this well for DIY landlords

### Market Stats
- 40% of single-family rentals owned by "mom and pop" landlords with average 3 properties each
- Huge underserved segment: 1–20 unit landlords who find AppFolio/Buildium too expensive and free tools too limited
- Source: https://bestutahrealestate.com/news/10-best-property-management-software-platforms-for-2026-picked-by-property-type

### Opportunity
**"Property management for the 1–20 unit landlord: real accounting + smart lease management at $29/month"**
- Core gap: Free tools (TurboTenant, Innago) have no real accounting; paid tools ($62–$298+/month) are overbuilt
- Key differentiator: Built-in accounting (P&L, Schedule E export), rent increase alerts with market comps, automated lease renewal workflows
- Secondary differentiator: Maintenance vendor matching (like a HomeAdvisor lite built in)
- Target: independent landlords with 1–20 units managing their own portfolio
- LTD viable at $79–$129; AppSumo potential; r/Landlord, r/realestateinvesting as channels
- ACH same-day / next-day payouts as competitive advantage over 7-10 day industry standard

## CATEGORY 3: Auto Repair Shop Management

### Top Competitors & Pricing

**Tekmetric**
- Start: $179/mo (annual) / $199/mo (monthly) — unlimited users, unlimited ROs, 70+ integrations
- Grow: $309/mo — adds labor guides, time/job clocks, reporting
- Scale: $409/mo — adds 2-way texting, real-time reporting, employee performance analytics
- Per shop (not per user) pricing — good value vs. competitors
- No free plan or free trial
- Sources: https://costbench.com/software/automotive-shop-software/tekmetric/, https://toolradar.com/tools/tekmetric/pricing

**Shopmonkey**
- Entry: ~$179/mo
- Similar tier structure to Tekmetric
- Strengths: polished UI, fast onboarding, good for staff turnover
- Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/169022/Shopmonkey/

**Mitchell1 (Manager SE)**
- Legacy desktop software; widely used among older shops
- Integrates with Mitchell repair database (labor times, OEM procedures)
- Cloud limitations — dated interface is top complaint
- Source: https://garage360.io/blog/best-auto-repair-shop-software

**Shop-Ware**
- Modern cloud-based; pricing not published
- Strong for multi-location, workflow standardization
- Source: https://www.g2.com/compare/shop-ware-vs-tekmetric

**AutoLeap / Garage360**
- Newer entrants; competitive pricing
- AutoLeap strong on owner visibility and follow-up tools
- Garage360: $179–$199/mo range; good ease-of-use

**Fullbay**
- Heavy-duty / commercial truck repair focus
- Fleet management, commercial repair workflows
- Source: https://www.g2.com/compare/fullbay-vs-tekmetric

### Top Customer Complaints

**Tekmetric complaints (Capterra p.4 — direct scrape):**
- "The schedule is clunky when it comes to moving appointments around" — Joseph S.
- Reporting requires "transfer to an excel spreadsheet in order to print" — Juan B.
- Labor guide "not accurate at all, sometimes off by over 70%" — Evan M.
- "To return a part is a long process, wish there was a shorter way" — Randy J.
- Cannot have "two or more techs on a job inside the repair order" — Isaac N.
- No negative tickets (needed for refunds) — Kenneth G.
- Customer vehicles being deleted removes invoices — Joshua W.
- No video sharing for digital vehicle inspections — Collin E.
- "Hard to navigate" and steep learning curve — multiple reviewers
- Capterra February 2026 review: "Not the worst but just too expensive" — rated 3.0, noted "did the same thing as products half the price"
- Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/190952/Tekmetric/reviews/?page=4

**Shopmonkey complaints (Capterra p.6 — direct scrape):**
- Missing ALLDATA labor time quotes integration
- Missing ECSTUNING parts ordering integration
- Only supports QuickBooks Online (no XERO, no PayPal, no QuickBooks Enterprise)
- No Android app; iPhone app lacks full functionality; "too large to reasonably use on smaller devices"
- Reporting capabilities "could be more robust or in depth"
- No chargeback/dispute support for credit card transactions
- Missing end-of-day cash drawer close-outs
- No mass mail / customer follow-up promotion features
- Limited tractor/trailer/RV repair functionality
- Version 2.0 upgrade caused major problems: loss of QuickBooks sync, "support was pathetic, no training provided"
- Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/169022/Shopmonkey/reviews/?page=6

### Feature Gaps / Missing Integrations
- **Customer follow-up / CRM campaigns**: None of the major platforms have mass marketing / follow-up campaigns (email/text blasts to customers due for oil change, tire rotation, etc.)
- **Multi-tech on single job**: Tekmetric cannot assign 2+ techs to one repair order — workflow gap for larger repairs
- **DVI video sharing**: Digital inspection photos standard; video still not supported in most platforms
- **ALLDATA / Mitchell1 labor guide accuracy**: Labor guide estimates are frequently wrong (Tekmetric flagged at 70% off); shops have to override constantly
- **Android-first mobile**: Shopmonkey iPhone-only; many independent shop owners use Android
- **Parts ordering integration**: No single platform integrates OEM + aftermarket parts ordering (ALLDATA, NAPA, AutoZone, RockAuto) in one workflow
- **Fleet/commercial billing**: Shops doing fleet work need different invoice structure (net-30, PO numbers, consolidated billing) — most platforms miss this
- **QuickBooks Desktop / Enterprise**: Most platforms only support QuickBooks Online; many established shops use Desktop
- **Back-office AP reconciliation**: Vendor invoice vs. parts received matching is still manual across all platforms
- **Multi-location centralized reporting**: Available at $300–$400/mo tier only (Tekmetric Scale, Shop-Ware)

### Market Stats
- Auto repair software market: $556M in 2026, projected $1.13B by 2035
- Independent shops still heavily reliant on legacy Mitchell1 (desktop) — prime migration candidates
- Source: https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6076275/auto-repair-software-market-report

### Opportunity A
**"Customer follow-up & retention add-on for auto repair shops"**
- None of Tekmetric/Shopmonkey/AutoLeap have built-in CRM campaigns
- Oil change reminders, seasonal campaigns, declined service follow-up ("your customer declined brakes 3 months ago")
- Could be standalone SaaS that integrates with existing shop SMS via API
- $39–$79/month add-on; LTD viable; r/MechanicAdvice, r/AutoRepair, independent shop owner FB groups
- Sources: https://shoptechscore.com/shopmonkey-review/, https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-tekmetric-vs-shopmonkey-for-auto-repair-shops-2026

### Opportunity B
**"Affordable all-in-one for independent shops at $99/month"**
- Tekmetric Start is $179–$199/mo; gap exists for <$100/mo with core features
- Target: 1–3 bay independent shops doing <80 ROs/week who don't need Tekmetric's depth
- Must include: QuickBooks Online/Desktop sync, DVI, 2-way texting, parts ordering via 1 integration

### Feature Gaps / Missing Integrations
- **True autopay / automatic recurring billing**: Yardbook is free but requires manual invoice sends; Jobber Basic lacks this; lawn care is the perfect recurring-billing business

- **Chemical tracking / compliance logs**: Pest control and lawn treatment companies need pesticide application records for state compliance — almost no mainstream platform covers this
- **Seasonal pricing / contract management**: Spring cleanups, per-cut pricing, annual contracts — poorly handled in sub-$100 tools
- **Customer-facing booking portal with real-time availability**: Most tools lack this; customers have to call to book
- **QuickBooks Desktop support** at low tiers

**"Weather-smart lawn care scheduling + auto-billing for solo and 2-crew operators at $39/month"**
- Core differentiator: weather API integration auto-reschedules mow jobs when rain forecast; sends customer notification automatically
- True recurring auto-billing (Stripe ACH, not just card)
- Route optimization from day one (not paywalled)
- Target: solo operators and 2-crew lawn/landscaping businesses who currently use Yardbook (free) or are priced out of Jobber

## CROSS-CATEGORY OPPORTUNITY SUMMARY

### Top 3 Opportunities Ranked by Strength of Signal

**#1: Auto Repair Customer Follow-Up / Retention CRM**
- Signal strength: HIGH
- Why: None of the $179–$409/month shop management platforms have built-in marketing campaigns; declined service follow-up is a proven revenue driver; shops actively complain about missing this feature
- Market: ~280,000 independent auto repair shops in the US
- Build: lightweight SaaS that pulls RO data from Tekmetric/Shopmonkey API, sends timed SMS/email campaigns for declined service, oil changes, seasonal promotions
- Price point: $49–$79/month; highly LTD-able

**#2: Lawn Care Scheduling with Weather + Autopay**
- Signal strength: HIGH
- Why: 726,000+ landscaping businesses; market growing at 11.7% CAGR; Yardbook is free but broken; Jobber is too expensive; no one owns the $39/mo "smart" slot
- Key differentiator that no competitor has: weather-based automatic rescheduling
- Adjacent pain: true recurring ACH billing (not just card Stripe)
