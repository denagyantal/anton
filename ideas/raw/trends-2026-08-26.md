## Trades License & Credential Compliance Tracking

- **Category**: Compliance & Regulation Tech
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://licenseroadmap.com/blog/best-contractor-license-tracking-software
  - https://expiryedge.com/compare/best-license-renewal-tracking-software/
  - https://simplecerts.ai/contractor-license-verification
  - https://www.remindax.com/document-tracking/contractor-license
  - https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-best-certification-renewal-software-for-electrical-contractors-2026
- **Description**: Every licensed trade (electrical, HVAC, plumbing) has state-specific renewal cycles, continuing education requirements, and federal certifications (EPA 608 for HVAC). A lapsed license means a contractor can't pull permits or legally work. Most small contractors track this in their head or on a spreadsheet. State databases are fragmented and don't send reminders. Compliance trackers own their niches because encoding each state's renewal rules is tedious — high moat once built.
- **Target Industries**: Electrical contractors, HVAC techs, plumbers, general contractors, roofing companies
- **Market Timing**: Early — solutions exist (SimpleCerts, Remindax, ExpiryEdge) but none have achieved dominance; no clear winner below $50/seat/mo
- **Example Products**:
  - SimpleCerts — https://simplecerts.ai (automated verification for trades)
  - ExpiryEdge — https://expiryedge.com (license renewal tracking)
  - Remindax — https://www.remindax.com (document/license tracking)
  - CrewSense — ties license status to dispatch eligibility (interesting integration pattern)
- **White Space**: An integrated "compliance + dispatch" layer — if a tech's license has lapsed, they can't be dispatched for permitted work. No affordable tool does this out of the box for small contractors. Also: automatic state database polling (vs relying on manual entry).
- **Speed to Market**: 2–3 weeks for MVP (reminder SaaS with state-specific rule library); state data scraping is the hard part
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — $49 LTD per contractor seat is very viable; reoccurring value from automated renewal reminders
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor license tracking software", "HVAC certification renewal reminder", "electrician license expiry tracker", "[state] contractor license renewal"
