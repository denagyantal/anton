## Cross-Cutting Signals

### "AI OS" framing is dominating
Every new entrant is calling their product an "AI Operating System" — Probook, Sophiie, Dalton Mills, Mainteny, Patientdesk, Quinn, Solea, Isaac. This framing signals: unified data layer, agentic workflows, displacing 5–10 point tools. The race is for the system of record.

### $49/month ServiceTitan alternatives
TradeFix ($49/mo for HVAC/plumbing/electrical), Swivl (free core + AI credits), and Billdr ($X/mo for GCs) are all explicitly positioning against ServiceTitan's $2,500/month price point. This is a massive wedge — 90%+ of trades shops are 1–10 trucks and can't justify enterprise pricing.

### WhatsApp/chat-based ERP gaining traction
