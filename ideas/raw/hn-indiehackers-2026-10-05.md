## CraftBoop — Automated Follow-Up Sequences for Service Businesses
- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/craftboop-built-automated-follow-ups-for-service-businesses-just-launched-looking-for-feedback-5aa58e1c39
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Show IH / Launch post
- **Engagement**: Active discussion, multiple substantive comments
- **Revenue Data**: Early stage, $29/mo pricing, 14-day free trial, a handful of early users
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Home service trades — plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, painters, landscapers
- **Core Value Prop**: After each job, sends an automated 5-email sequence: thank-you, review request, check-in, rebooking reminder, referral request. Service businesses do great work but never follow up consistently — reviews don't get requested, repeat bookings are lost.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Product is early with no clear revenue; name ("CraftBoop") is too playful for trust-sensitive service buyers. The underlying problem — follow-up automation tailored to each trade — is very real. Most competitors (Podium, Birdeye) are enterprise-priced. The $29/mo price point is compelling but the niche could be narrowed further (e.g., HVAC-specific with scheduling/rebooking tied into service intervals).
- **Our Angle**: Go niche-first (start with one trade — HVAC or cleaning), integrate directly with Jobber/ServiceTitan triggers, and add voice/SMS follow-up alongside email. Bundle with review management so the value is end-to-end.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — clean, simple product, single pain point, clear ROI to show (reviews + rebooking revenue)

## Summary

- **Total findings**: 14
- **Top opportunities**:
  1. **TradesDoc / Simple PDF Quote Tool for 0-5 Employee Trades** — Directly validated with hundreds of paying users in another market, annual pricing model, 5/5 boring business score. LTD-perfect.
  2. **HVAC Rebate Processing Automation** — WorkHero ($5M seed) and Faraday (300% growth) prove massive unmet demand. Indie wedge: rebate tracking as a standalone $79/mo tool before they commoditize it.
  3. **Cleaning Business Quote-to-Booking Tool** — ZenMaid's $3M/yr revenue proves market. No modern, simple competitor. Cleaning businesses still lack automated quoting with beds/baths/sqft math and instant online booking.
  4. **LandlordDesk — Small Landlord Maintenance Coordination** — Latchel validated the model (YC W19) but it's service-heavy and expensive. Pure-SaaS at $49/mo for 1-20 unit landlords is wide open.
  5. **DentalInbox / Dental Lead Funnel** — Small dental practices lose new patients from missed follow-up. No standalone lightweight tool exists. Clear ROI story, $79/mo price point, AppSumo-friendly.

- **Key themes**:
  - The 0-5 employee trade business segment is chronically underserved: too small for enterprise tools, too tech-averse for mainstream SaaS. Annual/LTD pricing is the right model.
  - Back-office automation for HVAC is attracting VC money (WorkHero $5M, Faraday 300% growth) — indie founders have a narrow window to own a specific workflow (rebates, permits, warranties) before it's commoditized.
  - "Simple over feature-rich" is repeatedly validated as a positioning for trades, but execution requires deeply understanding what "simple" means for each specific trade — not generic simplicity.
  - Cleaning services are a proven vertical (ZenMaid $3M/yr, Onedesk $100K MRR) with room for vertical-specific competitors focusing on modern UX and instant-booking.
  - Customer follow-up automation (reviews, rebooking, referrals) is a universal gap across all service trades — the one who wins picks a specific trade and integrates with the FSM tool that trade already uses.

- **Industries to watch**:
  - HVAC (back office admin, rebate processing — VC-funded market signals)
  - Residential cleaning / maid services (proven SaaS revenue, fragmented tool landscape)
  - Small landlords / property management (Latchel validated the need, no affordable indie option)
  - Dental practices (patient acquisition funnel, scheduling follow-up — no lightweight tools)
  - 0-5 employee trade contractors (landscaping, electrical, plumbing) — annual-priced simple tools
