## Summary of Key Themes / Underserved Industries Identified

**Most validated opportunities with real revenue data:**
1. Cleaning services software (ZenMaid $3M ARR, ConvertLabs $55K MRR peak, BlastoClean new entrant)
2. HVAC/plumbing service management (Breezy $250K ARR in 9 months, Jobber/ServiceTitan prove the market but underserve the small shop)
3. Pest control SaaS (commenter confirms $2M ARR, founder building tech-enabled operator company)
4. Field service AI receptionist (IH case shows 80% margins, $300–800 MRR per client)

**Underserved niches mentioned but no dominant software yet:**
- Concrete subcontractor estimating (Rudus is an early entrant, YC-backed)
- Dental/optometry insurance verification automation
- Google Local Service Ads ↔ FSM integration
- Lawn care chemical application compliance logging
- Small landlord maintenance coordination
- Voice-first field communication for construction (Conkoa early stage)
- Weather-triggered job rescheduling for outdoor contractors (Prelay just launched)

**Price points that work in boring B2B:**
- $29–79/mo for solo/micro businesses (1–3 employees)
- $99–299/mo for small teams (3–15 employees)
- $300–800/mo for AI-enhanced services (AI receptionist, etc.)
- Annual contracts common — LTD potential is real where there's clear one-time value
