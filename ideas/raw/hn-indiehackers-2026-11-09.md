## Prelay – Weather-based work planning for outdoor contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (just posted — 2 days ago)
- **Engagement**: 2 points, low engagement but very fresh launch
- **Revenue Data**: None disclosed — very early
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Landscaping, roofing, outdoor construction trades
- **Core Value Prop**: Monitors a contractor's full job schedule across 62 activity types and 12 trades using ensemble weather models + ground-wetness models. Sends weather-triggered notifications so owners can reschedule early — one owner wakes up at 4am just to check weather for each job site manually.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: No dedicated SaaS addresses weather-based crew dispatch decisions. The only solution today is a phone's weather app. The ground-wetness model (soil conditions) for excavation/landscaping is genuinely differentiated. Founder is debating raw data vs. actionable recommendations — "postpone this job" beats "35% chance of rain."
- **Our Angle**: Narrow to 1-2 specific trades (landscaping, paving, excavation) where weather = money. Integrate directly with Jobber/HouseCall Pro for schedule sync. Bundle with automated customer SMS when rescheduling.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 — weather data model cost may make LTD math tricky; better as low MRR subscription

## Signal Summary — Themes & Opportunities

### Strongest Signals

1. **Construction below Procore** — 1.5M US SMB firms, most with <10 employees, most paying $0 for software. Six specific trades have zero dedicated estimating tools. Daily logs, OSHA checklists, and permit tracking are all clear un-served $29-49/mo product slots.

2. **Field crew voice + communication** — Typing is inaccessible on a job site. Voice-first tools for timekeeping, work order updates, and customer communication are nascent. Conkoa (construction) and several pest control tools are early.

3. **Maintenance plan workflows in service businesses** — Breezy's $250K ARR in 9 months proves that the "boring clipboard ritual" approach wins. HVAC, pest control, and cleaning all have equivalent manual renewal workflows begging to be codified.

4. **Post-job follow-up for trades** — CraftBoop ($29/mo) addresses the universal problem: service businesses do good work but never ask for reviews or rebookings. SMS-first version would outperform email.

5. **Safety compliance for small crews** — BasinCheck ($1.2K MRR at 2 customers, growing fast) proves demand. The $49/mo OSHA checklist for 5-person crews has zero competition in that price band.

### Industries to Watch
- **Concrete subcontracting**: Rudus (YC-backed) confirms estimating is broken. Single-trade takeoff tools for other trades (roofing, HVAC ductwork) remain open.
- **Pest control**: 448-point HN post signals high tech community interest + $2M ARR existing proof in comments.
- **Heavy civil / civil contractors**: FOUNDATION integration layer is a unique opportunity hiding in plain sight.
