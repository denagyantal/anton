## Summary
This scan surfaces strong validation signals across boring-business verticals: HVAC maintenance agreement tracking, solo contractor profitability tools, and micro-rental software all show a consistent pattern — incumbents overpriced for enterprise, small operators stuck on spreadsheets, and a clear $29-49/month gap nobody has filled. ## Solo Contractor Estimate + Profit Tracking Gap

- **Source**: https://www.microgaps.com/blog/saas-niches-nobody-talking-about-2026
- **Additional Links**: https://www.getjobber.com/ (competitor reference)
- **Platform**: Market research synthesis
- **Type**: Gap analysis
- **Revenue Data**: Joist at $8/month (estimates only); Jobber job costing at $39-199/month
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Solo plumbers, electricians, landscapers — 1-3 person operations
- **Core Value Prop**: Write an estimate, then at job completion log actual materials + hours to see real profit vs. estimate. Two screens. The "did I make money on this job?" answer that Joist users currently track in spreadsheets
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Joist has massive market share at $8/month but stops at estimates. Jobber's job costing is buried in a $39-199/month bundle. There is a $15-17/month upsell gap between those two products. Contractors consistently undercharge because they never close the loop on actual vs. estimated cost
- **Our Angle**: Position as "Joist but with a profit score at the end." Build estimate creation (compatible UX) plus a simple job closeout screen. Market in /r/Plumbing, /r/electricians, contractor Facebook groups where this pain is voiced daily
- **LTD Potential**: 5
