They've moved beyond point solutions — the pitch is now: answer calls, book jobs, dispatch, invoice, do follow-ups, all autonomously.   - WorkHero (back-office automation for small HVAC): https://workhero.pro
  - Mainteny (AI ERP for maintenance companies): https://mainteny.com
- **White Space**: HVAC/plumbing/electrical are saturated. White space remains in:
  - Specialty trades: marine services, chimney sweeps, water treatment, well/septic services
  - Pest control (HN thread shows incumbents exist but specific AI-native gap)
  - Multilingual AI receptionists for Latino-owned trades businesses (huge underserved segment)
  - Solo/owner-operator shops (<3 employees) that can't afford $500+/mo enterprise AI

  - Vertical SaaS M&A VC Report 2026 (7.0x median revenue multiple): https://www.saasrise.com/blog/vertical-saas-ma-vc-report-2026
- **Description**: ServiceTitan is too expensive ($300+/mo, complex onboarding) for the 85% of trades shops with under 20 employees. New entrants are attacking from below: WrkOrdr at $20/mo supports 78 professions, WorkQuote was built by a tree service owner who felt the pain. The market is enormous — 80,000 small HVAC alone — and most still use paper + QuickBooks + spreadsheets + a calendar app. The winning formula is mobile-first (technicians don't sit at desks), dead-simple onboarding, and a price point under $50/mo.
- **Target Industries**: Any field service trade: HVAC, tree service, plumbing, cleaning, landscaping, pest control, electrical
- **Market Timing**: Growing (many entrants but no dominant winner below ServiceTitan)
- **Example Products**:
  - WrkOrdr ($20/mo, 78 professions): https://getwrkordr.com
  - WorkQuote (tree service-first): https://workquote.pro
  - FieldServicePro (AI-native): https://fieldservicepro.io
  - JobBer (small biz favorite, more established)
  - Coast (mobile-first team management)
- **White Space**:
  - Single-trade depth vs. multi-trade breadth — a tool built specifically for chimney sweeps or water damage restoration with industry-specific workflows wins against generic FSM
  - Offline-first apps for rural/no-signal crews
  - Integrated rebate/permit submission for HVAC installs (WorkHero found this is a 20+ minute manual task per job)
- **Speed to Market**: 2–4 weeks for a niche-vertical MVP
- **LTD Fit**: High — small trade businesses love lifetime deals; they hate monthly subscriptions
- **Content Strategy**: "field service app for [trade]", "[trade] scheduling software", "affordable ServiceTitan alternative", "invoicing app for contractors"

  - AnswerForce (trades-specific)
  - Loman AI (restaurants)
  - Avoca AI (HVAC/plumbing-specific voice CSR)
- **White Space**:
  - Post-call workflow automation (invoice from voice notes, trigger rebate submission, schedule follow-up)
  - KPI/performance dashboards built for trade business owners (The Nut Report is tackling this)
  - Multilingual voice agents (Spanish-first) for trades in TX, FL, CA, AZ where many contractors are Latino-owned
