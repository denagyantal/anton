## AI Operating System Race for Trades (The Platform Land-Grab)

- **Category**: Vertical AI / SMB Platform Consolidation
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - Probook raises $40M from a16z + Sequoia: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/6/23/3316215/0/en/probook-raises-40m-from-andreessen-horowitz-and-sequoia-to-scale-the-ai-operating-system-for-home-services.html
  - Sophiie AI raises AUD $5M seed, 10–15% MoM growth: https://www.einpresswire.com/article/934145369/sophiie-ai-raises-aud-5-million-seed-round-and-launches-ai-operating-system-for-trades-and-service-businesses
  - Dalton Mills raises $9.2M "Replit for the trades": https://daltonmills.com/resources/fieldnotes/dalton-mills-raises-9m-to-build-the-platform-for-trades
  - Stormy raises $1.7M (YC): https://stormy.ai/blog/stormy-raises-1-7m
  - Mainteny AI-powered OS for maintenance: https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny
  - Stormy on Product Hunt (#1 day rank): https://www.producthunt.com/products/stormy-influencer-marketing-ai-agent
  - Building @probookai thread: https://xitter.cf/georgeprobook
- **Description**: Massive VC conviction around replacing fragmented point solutions (voice agent + outbound + dispatch + invoicing each from different vendors) with a unified AI operating system. Probook's thesis: "operators ended up with five vendors and more overhead." Probook built dispatch first then expanded. Sophiie started as an AI receptionist and evolved to full OS. Multiple $5M–$40M rounds in 2026 alone targeting HVAC, plumbing, electrical as the wedge.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, home services broadly
- **Market Timing**: Growing — still early innings but the land-grab is accelerating
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook (a16z/Sequoia-backed, dispatch-first OS): https://probook.ai
  - Sophiie AI (Australian, expanding to US): https://sophiie.ai
  - Dalton Mills ("diagonal SaaS" / customizable platform): https://daltonmills.com
  - Stormy (YC F25, forklift/HVAC/electrical/plumbing): https://stormy.ai
  - Mainteny (ERP + AI back-office agent): https://mainteny.com
- **White Space**: Nobody has cracked the under-10-truck HVAC or plumbing shop with an affordable, easy-to-onboard OS. Probook serves large PE-backed platforms. Sophiie is mid-market. The solo-operator + 1–5 truck segment is still on paper/ServiceTitan workarounds. Also: multi-trade businesses (HVAC + plumbing combo shops) have no clean platform that handles both trade's compliance requirements simultaneously.
- **Speed to Market**: No — full OS is 6–12 months minimum; but a focused vertical slice (e.g., just dispatch + invoicing for 1–5 truck shops) could be 4–6 weeks MVP.
- **LTD Fit**: Weak — recurring value is core to the pitch; LTD would undervalue it. Monthly subscription ($99–$499/mo) is the right model.
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC dispatch software small business", "plumbing business management software", "field service OS", "replace ServiceTitan small shop"

## Voice-to-Quote / Voice-First Tools for Field Workers

- **Category**: Mobile-First Field Tools / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - SMASH Voice to Invoice (PH launch, 4mo ago): https://www.producthunt.com/products/smash-voice-to-invoice
  - Angebotsmeister — German trades voice quoting (PH, 1mo ago): https://www.producthunt.com/products/angebotsmeister
  - Handoff AI — AI estimating for remodeling/handymen (PH): https://www.producthunt.com/products/handoff-ai
  - Conkoa — voice-first Slack for construction (HN Show HN): https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47325824
  - Feldy (YC F26) — field-first AI with 3D scans, 200+ paying customers: https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SiZ-feldy-field-first-ai-for-home-services-and-contractors/
- **Description**: Field workers lose jobs because quotes take 2–3 days. A contractor can't stop mid-job to type a quote. The voice-first pattern is emerging: speak the job details on-site → AI generates a professional quote with line items and local pricing → customer approves digitally. SMASH ("build and send quotes as fast as you can talk") is live with free tier. Angebotsmeister launching the same workflow for the German trades market. Feldy goes further with 3D LiDAR scans auto-converted to estimates. Conkoa: voice updates sync to Procore.
- **Target Industries**: Remodeling, handymen, general contractors, painters, roofing, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, German/EU trades
- **Market Timing**: Early — multiple indie builders launching, no clear dominant player yet
- **Example Products**:
  - SMASH Voice to Invoice: https://smashinvoices.com
  - Handoff AI: https://www.handoffai.com
  - Angebotsmeister (German): https://angebots-meister.de
  - Feldy (YC F26): https://feldy.ai
  - Conkoa (voice-first construction): https://conkoa.ai
- **White Space**: Voice-to-quote for specialty trades (HVAC quoting requires equipment specs/model numbers, electrical needs load calculations). None of the current tools handle trade-specific pricing logic well. Also: multi-language voice quoting for Spanish-speaking field crews (significant US market).
- **Speed to Market**: Yes — an MVP using Whisper + Claude for voice parsing + existing pricing DB could be built in 2–3 weeks.
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — trades owners would pay $59–$99 LTD for a tool that saves them hours per week on quoting. Already validated by SMASH's free-to-paid model.
- **Content Strategy**: "voice to invoice app contractors", "quote faster HVAC estimate app", "field estimate tool plumber"

## Trades Workforce Tech: Retention, Training & Performance Pay

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Mobile-First Field Tools
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - ShareWillow raises $8.5M: https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/10/sharewillow-home-field-service-technicians-performance-pay-platform-ai-agents-skilled-trades-ryan-shank/
  - Quinn raises $4M from Kalos Ventures (skilled trades workforce OS): https://www.kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn
  - HN thread: I wanted to build vertical SaaS for pest control — workforce retention discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
- **Description**: Jobs for the Future projects 1.7 million skilled trades openings/year through 2035, with only 55 workers trained per 100 needed. The workforce shortage is forcing trades businesses to invest in retention. ShareWillow connects to ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro/Jobber to auto-calculate performance pay, showing techs exactly what each job earned them in real-time ($1,334/mo average increase per tech). Quinn digitizes training/onboarding for the trades — ingests SOPs/manuals and turns them into mobile-first, multi-language interactive training with compliance tracking. Both raised in Q4 2026.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control, roofing, any high-volume home service
- **Market Timing**: Early — this is a newer category, only 2–3 funded players
- **Example Products**:
  - ShareWillow (performance pay, $8.5M seed): https://sharewillow.com
  - Quinn (workforce training OS, $4M seed): (from Kalos Ventures blog)
- **White Space**: Performance pay calculation and real-time tech earnings visibility is solved for ServiceTitan customers by ShareWillow. But the same thing doesn't exist for Jobber-only or smaller FSM platforms. Also: bilingual (English/Spanish) onboarding and certification tracking for immigrant trade workforce — no focused product here.
- **Speed to Market**: Medium — needs FSM integrations (Jobber/HCP APIs are well-documented, 3–4 weeks to build a basic pay dashboard).
- **LTD Fit**: Weak for performance pay (it's a payroll tool, needs to be live). Moderate for training modules ($149–$299 LTD for a training template library).
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC technician retention software", "field service performance pay", "trades employee training app"

  - FieldProMax compliance guide, EPA Section 608 refrigerant tracking requirements: https://www.fieldpromax.com/blog/why-documentation-matters-in-regulated-field-services
  - WebWork CRM compliance module (pesticide, refrigerant, backflow, red tags): https://www.webworkcrm.com/features/compliance/
  - CA CSLB new 2026 rule: all HVAC license holders must carry workers comp even as sole proprietors: https://www.gettradelicense.com/blog/contractor-licensing-changes-2026
  - Utah SB 26 (2026): new pest control permitting requirements, salesperson permits: https://le.utah.gov/Session/2026/bills/enrolled/SB0026.pdf
  - OSHA renews heat emphasis program targeting building services (pest control in scope): https://www.ltkpmp.com/wire/regulatory/
  - AVA Energy RFP: software for heat pump permit streamlining (SB 222 compliance): https://avaenergy.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Permit-Streamlining-RFP-Webinar-Presenation_10.30.26.pdf
  - HN post on building vertical SaaS for pest control: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
- **Description**: Three regulatory forces are converging in 2026: (1) EPA refrigerant regulations expanding (R-410A phasedown, Section 608 tracking now mandatory even for smaller systems), (2) state-level licensing changes (CA mandatory workers comp for HVAC sole proprietors, Utah new pest control permits), (3) OSHA heat emphasis program now targeting pest control/building services. ServiceTitan users are manually building compliance tracking with custom fields — there's no clean purpose-built compliance layer for small trades businesses. Biz-Comply is a focused, early-stage player just for lawn care + expanding to HVAC/pest. WebWork has the most complete compliance module but is a full FSM. White space: a standalone, affordable compliance log that integrates with Jobber/HCP/ServiceTitan.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC (refrigerant), pest control (pesticide logs), lawn care (pesticide), plumbing (backflow), electrical (licensing), all trades (OSHA)
- **Market Timing**: Growing — regulation is tightening in 2026, enforcement is ramping
- **Example Products**:
  - Biz-Comply (early, focused on lawn care/trades): https://biz-comply.com
  - WebWork CRM compliance module: https://www.webworkcrm.com/features/compliance/
  - FieldProMax (broader FSM with compliance tracking): https://fieldpromax.com
- **White Space**: A lightweight "compliance layer" that plugs into existing FSM software (Jobber, HCP) via webhook/integration, auto-generating EPA-required refrigerant logs and pesticide application records from job data — without requiring a full FSM switch. No one is building the integration-first compliance tool.
- **Speed to Market**: Yes — an EPA Section 608 refrigerant log app with Jobber webhook integration could be built in 2–3 weeks.
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — compliance tools are ideal LTD candidates. Businesses pay to avoid fines. $99–$199 LTD for a compliance log tool is very achievable. Biz-Comply model validates this.
- **Content Strategy**: "EPA refrigerant log software HVAC", "pesticide application log app", "HVAC compliance tracking", "pest control compliance software"

The problem: data sits across 3–5 tools (ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, Google LSA, marketing platform, payroll) and nobody has time to reconcile. The Nut Report positions as a "decision tool" on top of Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan — one view, one answer: "are we on track this week?" - **Target Industries**: Any service business running 2–10 trucks — HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, landscaping

- **Example Products**:
  - The Nut Report (pre-launch): (from PH profile)
  - Crewbird (field service CRM + Google LSA): (HN post)

No existing product does this cleanly for the 1–10 truck segment. The integration with Google Local Service Ads specifically (a key acquisition channel for home services) is an underserved wedge.
- **Speed to Market**: Yes — read-only integration with Jobber/HCP APIs + a weekly email report could be built in 2–3 weeks.
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — reporting tools are classic LTD candidates ($49–$99 LTD). Service business owners would buy "set it and forget it" reporting.

## Pest Control: The Next Underserved Vertical SaaS Vertical

- **Category**: Legacy Software Disruption / Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - HN: "I wanted to build vertical SaaS for pest control, so I took a technician job" (448 upvotes, 182 comments): https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
  - Utah SB 26 (2026): new permitting requirements for pest control technicians and salespersons: https://le.utah.gov/Session/2026/bills/enrolled/SB0026.pdf
  - OSHA heat emphasis program now targets pest control (building services): https://www.ltkpmp.com/wire/regulatory/
  - Biz-Comply adding pest control compliance module: https://biz-comply.com/
- **Description**: A founder took a technician job in pest control to validate vertical SaaS demand — the HN post got 448 upvotes, signaling strong community interest. The incumbent VSaaS is "decent, cheap, and ubiquitous" (FieldRoutes/ServiceTitan Pest), which paradoxically creates opportunity for a modern, AI-native alternative targeting the growing compliance burden. 2026 brings multiple new state-level regulations (Utah SB 26, OSHA heat program now explicitly includes pest control in scope). Most small pest control operators (<10 techs) run on PestPac or spreadsheets. The compliance and routing complexity is real.
- **Target Industries**: Pest control operators (PCOs), wildlife control, termite treatment companies
- **Market Timing**: Early — the founder chose to become an operator rather than build SaaS (validation that the SaaS opportunity is nuanced), but compliance forcing functions are increasing
- **Example Products**:
  - Biz-Comply (pest control compliance module coming): https://biz-comply.com
  - WebWork CRM (has pest control compliance built in): https://www.webworkcrm.com
- **White Space**: AI-powered routing optimization + compliance logging for small PCOs (5–20 techs). The routing problem in pest control is different from HVAC (recurring routes, dense neighborhoods, chemical application log requirements). No modern player is nailing all three: routing + compliance + customer communication.
- **Speed to Market**: Medium — requires domain knowledge; compliance logging alone is 2–3 week MVP.
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — compliance tools with LTD pricing ($79–$149) would appeal strongly to small PCOs trying to stay inspection-ready.
- **Content Strategy**: "pest control software small business", "pesticide application log app", "pest control routing software"
