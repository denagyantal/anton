## Cross-Category Patterns

Three complaints appear consistently across all four categories and represent the most validated gaps:

1. **Route optimization / operational core features are gated behind 2–3x price jumps.** Every horizontal platform (Jobber, Housecall Pro) locks the feature that makes it actually useful behind a plan that costs 2–3x the entry price. Vertical entrants that include this at base price win on value perception immediately.

2. **QuickBooks/accounting integration is universally broken or extra-cost.** In all four categories, the #1 or #2 complaint is that syncing to QuickBooks requires a workaround, a third-party tool, or an expensive tier. Clean, native QBO integration is a genuine differentiator in any of these verticals.

3. **No product serves the 1–10 employee operator at $50–100/month with full features.** Enterprise tools ($200–500/mo) overbuild. Free tools underbuild. The $50–100/month bracket with full feature parity is genuinely underserved. This is the natural AppSumo LTD sweet spot: a $79–99 lifetime deal replaces $1,000–5,000/year in subscription fees.
