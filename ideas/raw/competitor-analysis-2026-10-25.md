## Field Service: Flat-Rate Pricebook + Marketing Automation for Solo/Small HVAC Shops

### Market Landscape
| Competitor | Price | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| ServiceTitan | $245–$500/tech/mo + $5k–$50k onboarding | Enterprise dispatching, deep integrations, pricebook | Priced out of solo/2-truck shops, 12-month contracts, complex onboarding |
| Housecall Pro | $65–$249/mo (1-5 users) | Clean UX, payments, customer communication | No flat-rate pricebook, basic reporting, $35/user add-on on MAX plan only |
| Jobber | $39–$199/mo ($29/user overage) | Affordable entry point, solid scheduling | No flat-rate pricebook, no marketing automation, no chemical tracking |
| Workiz | ~$275/mo (5 users) | Built-in phone system, dispatch | Per-minute phone charges, hidden fees, limited pricebook |
| FieldPulse | ~$19k/yr for 5-person team | HVAC-specific workflows | No published pricing, aggressive upsells on add-ons, no flat-rate pricebook |

### Top Customer Complaints (from reviews)
1. **No flat-rate pricebook built in** — HVAC/plumbing shops need pre-set parts+labor bundles; Jobber/Housecall Pro force manual line-item quotes every time ([Capterra - FieldPulse](https://fieldserviceguide.com/fieldpulse/), [ServiceTitan Review](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/servicetitan/))
2. **Offline mobile mode breaks in the field** — inconsistent or nonexistent offline access is the single most cross-platform complaint ([Field Service Software Reviews](https://opssaaslab.com/field-service-management-software-reviews/))
3. **No built-in marketing automation** — no review requests, no email follow-ups, no customer win-back sequences without expensive add-ons ([FieldPulse Review](https://tooleduppro.com/reviews/fieldpulse/))
4. **ServiceTitan too expensive for small shops** — "designed for 20+ technicians with dedicated office staff" — solo operators and 2-truck shops can't justify it ([ServiceTitan Alternatives 2026](https://blog.salescaptain.com/servicetitan-alternatives-for-small-hvac-companies-2026/))
5. **Complex onboarding, slow support** — multiple sources report 4–8 week implementation times and poor post-sale support ([Capterra - ServiceTrade](https://www.capterra.com/p/132690/ServiceTrade-Commercial/reviews/))

### Identified Gap
- **What's missing**: A flat-rate pricebook + built-in marketing automation (review requests, follow-up sequences) at affordable pricing for 1-5 tech HVAC/plumbing/electrical shops
- **Who needs it**: Solo HVAC technician who just started their own shop, or 2-truck plumbing company with no office admin
- **Why competitors don't do it**: ServiceTitan built a pricebook but buried it in enterprise pricing. Housecall Pro/Jobber are general field service tools, not trade-specific.
- **Current workaround**: Manually pricing every job off memory, texting customers manually for reviews, losing repeat business because no follow-up system

### Our Opportunity
- **Product concept**: "TrustBook" — field service app for solo and small-team trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) with built-in flat-rate pricebook, offline-first mobile app, and automated review/follow-up sequences
- **Key differentiator**: Flat-rate pricebook (upload your own bundles or use trade templates) + marketing automation in one tool, no enterprise complexity
- **LTD price point**: $79 (1-2 tech), $149 (up to 5 techs)
- **Target channels**: r/HVAC, r/Plumbing, r/smallbusiness, Facebook Groups "HVAC Talk", "Plumbing Business Owners", trade forums (ACCA members, PHCC network)
- **AppSumo potential**: Yes — strong "boring but essential" category, clear ROI story (one 5-star review = $1,000+ job value), no obvious LTD incumbents

### Identified Gap

- **Current workaround**: Paper log books, Excel spreadsheets, handwritten records — legally risky and time-consuming

### Our Opportunity
- **Product concept**: "GreenLog" — chemical application compliance tracker for lawn care and pest operators: per-application logs with EPA required fields, applicator license management, state-compliant report export (PDF), mobile capture on-site, basic customer history
- **Key differentiator**: First tool built purely around compliance logging (not a scheduling bolt-on), pre-configured for each US state's requirements, exports the exact format inspectors ask for
- **LTD price point**: $59 (1 applicator), $99 (up to 3 applicators/truck routes)
- **Target channels**: r/lawncare, r/landscaping, state pesticide applicator license forums, National Association of Landscape Professionals (NALP) Facebook groups, lawn care business Facebook groups
- **AppSumo potential**: Yes — niche but motivated buyers who face real legal liability, strong "oh shit I need this" hook, no obvious LTD competitor
