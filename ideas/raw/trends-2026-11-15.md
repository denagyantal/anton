## "Vertical Harness" Agents — Done-For-You AI for Local Businesses

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Mobile-first field tools
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://teapawt.org/gregisenberg/status/2111176488162582933 (Greg Isenberg thread on vertical harnesses)
  - https://xitter.cf/starter_story/status/2112958627699192046 (Starter Story: SaaS for electricians $85K AUD/mo)
  - https://www.techtwitter.com/tweet/22fbcd9f-5944-4512-8be3-f749a286877b (Andrew Gazdecki on boring vertical software)
- **Description**: Greg Isenberg's viral thread identifies "vertical harnesses" — agents built for one industry and one workflow (e.g., insurance claims, construction bids) — as one of the highest-signal business models right now. The pattern: take a horizontal AI tool, train it on one industry's vocabulary and workflow, and charge 2–5x the generic price. Done-for-you AI setup (like website agencies in 2010) is a parallel opportunity — local businesses paying someone to configure AI agents for them. The key insight: local businesses have zero technical capacity but strong WTP when you show them a working system saving 10+ hours/week.
- **Target Industries**: Any local/boring business — HVAC, dental, pest control, landscaping, trucking, tutoring agencies
- **Market Timing**: Early (most local businesses have heard of AI but can't implement; installer/integrator market just opening)
- **Example Products**:
  - SayDoo — built inside a real roofing company before selling to others (the "done-for-you" model)
  - Nebula AI — packaged "build an AI for X trade" frameworks
- **White Space**: Franchise-network AI rollouts (a GC deploying AI agents across 50 franchisees); Spanish-language AI setup for Hispanic-owned trade businesses; "AI-in-a-box" for specific trades (roofing-only, pest-control-only)
- **Speed to Market**: 1–2 weeks for a productized setup service; 3–4 weeks for a true SaaS
- **LTD Fit**: Very High — $500–$2,500 one-time setup fee (SayDoo's model) appeals to trades owners who hate monthly bills
- **Content Strategy**: "AI for HVAC companies," "done for you AI setup small business," "AI tools for contractors"
