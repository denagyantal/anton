- **Platform**: Direct product (referenced in search results)
- **Type**: Micro-SaaS product

- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5

- **LTD Potential**: 5/5 — direct ROI product (if it gets you paid faster, it pays for itself immediately). LTD at $59–$99 is a no-brainer for any contractor.
