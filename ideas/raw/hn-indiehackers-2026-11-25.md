## Avenue — Booking Widget for Tradespeople / Mechanics / Electricians
- **Source**: https://howtomakemoney.io/cases/avenue-tom-holliday-tradie-widget-55k/
- **Additional Links**: https://stackstarts.com/avenue-branded-trades-booking-widget/ | https://www.linkedin.com/posts/starterstory123_instead-of-building-another-0-mrr-saas-for-activity-7518744380996767745-TMht | Starter Story podcast (Sep 2026)
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers / Starter Story
- **Type**: Revenue milestone / Case study
- **Engagement**: Widely shared on LinkedIn, Starter Story feature
- **Revenue Data**: AU$85,000/month (~$55K USD) as of Sep 2026; scaled from $597/mo to $63K AUD in ~1 year
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Trades — electricians, mechanics, plumbers, HVAC
- **Core Value Prop**: Embeddable "Book Online" widget for trades websites + Google Business profile booking button. Customer picks service, books time slot, no phone call required. Includes SMS reminders, review requests, two-way SMS, and an AI phone agent (Sophie). 100+ integrations including MechanicDesk.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Trades still rely on phone calls for bookings; majority of website visitors bail before submitting contact forms. First-mover in AU/NZ; US market largely untouched at this price point. Priced at $199–$399/mo — potentially over-priced for solo US tradespeople.
- **Our Angle**: Narrower US-focused version at $49–$99/mo targeting solo trades contractors; lean on Google LSA integration (see Crewbird below) + review automation. Could include AI call answering as a growth tier.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 — recurring model fits better, but an LTD entry-tier could get distribution

## Crewbird — Field Service CRM + Google LSA Integration
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418273
- **Additional Links**: https://getcrewbird.com/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Ask HN — What are you working on? (November 2026)
- **Engagement**: 2 comments, positive response from another field service operator confirming pain
- **Revenue Data**: Not disclosed; founder says dad's carpet cleaning company approaching 3x ROAS
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Field service / home services (carpet cleaning, any Google LSA business)
- **Core Value Prop**: CRM built specifically around Google Local Service Ads — tracks spend, attribution, ROAS. Existing SaaS CRMs have poor Google Ads API integrations. Desktop CRMs being discontinued (old Windows apps).
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Google LSA is a primary acquisition channel for home service businesses, yet no affordable CRM ties LSA spend directly to closed revenue. Huge pain point — responding comment validated "soul crushing to swap between 12 different trackers per hour."
- **Our Angle**: Build the Google LSA ↔ job management bridge as a focused product ($29–49/mo). Expand to other ad channels. Could be the cheapest ServiceTitan alternative with real attribution.
- **LTD Potential**: 4

## Rudus (YC P26) — AI Estimation for Concrete Subcontractors
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48374528
- **Additional Links**: https://www.rudus.ai/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Launch HN (YC P26)
- **Engagement**: 41 points, 14 comments
- **Revenue Data**: YC-funded, not disclosed
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Concrete construction / structural subcontractors
- **Core Value Prop**: AI-powered takeoff and estimation for concrete subs. Reads structural PDFs, identifies every footing/wall/column/slab, produces 80–120 priced line items. Last incumbent software not updated since 2020. Bidding currently takes weeks/months manually.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Every AI takeoff tool was built for general contractors; none for concrete-only. Massive inefficiency — senior estimators spend weeks on bids they lose. Underserved because the niche is too unsexy for most AI builders.

- **LTD Potential**: 2 — high ACV B2B, not LTD-friendly, but signals where construction estimation gaps exist

## Transload (YC P26) — Freight Dimensioning via Existing CCTV
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463273
- **Additional Links**: https://transload.io | https://hn.transload.io
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Launch HN (YC P26)
- **Engagement**: 56 points, 19 comments
- **Revenue Data**: YC-funded; claims ~10% of checked shipments have dimension errors; $50k/site/month in recoverable revenue
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: LTL (Less-than-Truckload) trucking / freight terminals / cross-docking warehouses
- **Core Value Prop**: Measures freight dimensions from existing security cameras using monocular depth estimation AI — no new hardware, no workflow change. Carriers lose revenue when shippers understate dimensions. ~10K relevant cross-docking warehouses in US + Europe.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: LTL pricing is based on dimensions; underbilling is a huge leak. No affordable tool exists. Dedicated dimensioning systems are expensive and disrupt workflow. Existing CCTV is idle.
- **Our Angle**: The "use existing hardware" angle is repeatable — similar approach could apply to warehouse inventory counting, forklift safety compliance, loading dock utilization. Smaller version for regional carriers.
- **LTD Potential**: 1 — enterprise logistics, not LTD territory, but highlights trucking as a gap

## Zirco.ai — AI Front Desk for Dental Practices
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (beta launch)
- **Engagement**: 1 point, 3 comments
- **Revenue Data**: In beta; 30+ dental practices in discovery
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices
- **Core Value Prop**: AI handles full dental front desk — insurance verification via carrier portals (Playwright automation), appointment booking into Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft, inbound calls via Vapi voice AI, SMS reminders. Replaces $40–50K/year staff with 40% annual turnover.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Insurance verification alone takes 2–3 hours/day per front desk. The portal automation (non-API carriers handled via Playwright) is a genuine technical moat. HIPAA-compliant from day one.
- **Our Angle**: Narrower entry point: just the insurance verification workflow as a standalone SaaS at $99–199/mo before attempting to replace the full front desk. Faster to validate, lower sales resistance.
- **LTD Potential**: 3

## KraftunAI — AI Phone Receptionist for Tradespeople
- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-i-built-an-ai-phone-receptionist-for-czech-tradespeople-first-paying-customers-MSythQu01p7HfU7QyKGE

- **Revenue Data**: Early customers; Czech market
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Tradespeople — plumbers, roofers, electricians (Czech Republic → pattern for other markets)
- **Core Value Prop**: AI voice receptionist answers 24/7, books into Google Calendar, sends call summaries. Demo call closes sales — letting prospects call the AI before signing up.

## Prelay — Weather-Based Work Planning for Outdoor Contractors
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (November 2026)
- **Engagement**: 2 points, 0 comments
- **Revenue Data**: Just launched; US-only
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Outdoor contractors — landscaping, roofing (12 trades supported)
- **Core Value Prop**: Monitors contractor's full schedule of job sites and determines weather suitability for 62 activities across 12 trades. Uses ensemble weather models + custom ground-wetness model for soil conditions. Weather-triggered notifications for early rescheduling. Founder notes owner wakes at 4am to check weather app across dozens of sites.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Outdoor contractors check phone weather apps manually per site — no tool connects their job schedule to weather data. Cancellations cost money (workers still get paid after 6am). Early rescheduling saves both.
- **Our Angle**: Add direct integration with scheduling tools (Jobber, HouseCall Pro) so weather-triggered rescheduling is automatic rather than notification-based. Bundle as an add-on ($19/mo) to existing field service tools.
- **LTD Potential**: 3
