## Summary — Top 3 Most Promising Trends for Action

### 1. AI Invoice Auto-Followup for Trades (AR Automation)
**Why:** Narrowest white space with clearest ROI story. Trades businesses lose money daily to unpaid invoices. A standalone tool that plugs into QuickBooks / Xero and runs AI-timed sequences requires no FSM replacement and can be built in 2–3 weeks. The customer pain is acute ("I hate chasing invoices"), measurable (DSO), and daily. LTD pricing works well. AppSumo has no dominant player in this slot. First-mover in the "AR autopilot for trades" brand position wins a tight-knit referral community.

### 2. AI Dispatch / AI Receptionist for Single-Trade Verticals
**Why:** The $40M Probook raise + $5M Sophiie raise in the same quarter signals strong institutional belief in the category. Both target mid-market (10+ trucks). The greenfield is at the micro end: 1–5 technician shops that need dispatch + AI call answering but can't afford enterprise pricing. A $79/month "AI dispatcher + receptionist for pest control" (or HVAC, or pool service) is genuinely unoccupied at the micro-vertical level. Twilio + LLM stack makes this buildable in 3–4 weeks. Revenue compounds via usage (call minutes) and seat expansion.
