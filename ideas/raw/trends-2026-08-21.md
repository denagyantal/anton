- **Speed to Market**: Yes — MVP wrappable in 3–4 weeks using existing voice AI APIs (Retell, Bland, Vapi) + trade-specific prompts and Twilio for call routing
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades are MRR-friendly (recurring monthly subscription), but LTD works well at $199–$399 as a trial conversion tool; AppSumo has precedent with AI call tools
