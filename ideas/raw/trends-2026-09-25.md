## AI-Native Trades Operating Systems (Dispatch as the Brain)

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Trades Going Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.constructionindustry.ai/funding/probook-40m-series-a-home-services-dispatch-ai/ — Probook $40M a16z + Sequoia
  - https://ai-for-contractors.com/news/netic-ai-23m-series-b-plumbers-roofers/ — Netic AI $23M Founders Fund
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny — Mainteny: AI OS for maintenance companies
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/serviceagent — ServiceAgent: AI voice answering for home services
- **Description**: A wave of AI-native (not AI-layered) platforms rebuilding the trades back office from scratch around AI. Probook ($40M from a16z + Sequoia — rare co-lead) built around the dispatch board as the center of every home service business. Netic AI ($23M Founders Fund) trained trade-specific models on plumbing and roofing workflows. Over $300M flowed into contractor-specific AI in Q1 2026 alone. The key insight: 75% of trade contractors use no dedicated software — because existing software was built for SaaS companies, not trades. AI-native tools adapt to the contractor, not vice versa.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing — any skilled trade running 2–30 trucks
- **Market Timing**: Growing (VC is flooding in; product-market fit established; still early for sub-$5M ARR entrants)
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook: https://www.probook.ai — dispatch-first AI OS, $40M raised
  - Netic AI: https://neticai.com — plumbing/roofing AI ops, $23M raised, $199/mo flat
  - Mainteny: https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny — AI back office for maintenance companies
  - ServiceAgent: https://serviceagent.ai — AI call answering for home services, 24/7 booking
- **White Space**: Sub-trade verticals not yet targeted — septic, pest control, pool cleaning, appliance repair. Also: "AI dispatch" for multi-location franchise operators of smaller trades. And bilingual (Spanish-first) AI voice agents for trades with Hispanic workforces.
- **Speed to Market**: 8–12 weeks for a narrow wedge (voice answering or dispatch for 1 specific trade)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — small operators love flat pricing but AI infrastructure cost makes LTD risky; subscription with annual prepay better
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC dispatch software", "AI for plumbing companies", "field service AI", "[trade] scheduling software"

## Construction Back-Office Digitization (Payroll, Accounting, Job Costing)

- **Category**: Paper-to-Digital / Legacy Software Disruption
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/adaptive-raises-30-million-series-b-led-by-tidemark-to-bring-agentic-accounting-to-construction-302881537.html — Adaptive $30M Series B
  - https://www.financialcontent.com/article/gnwcq-2026-9-24-trayd-launches-accounting-becoming-the-single-source-of-financial-truth-for-construction-contractors — Trayd accounting launch (Sept 24, 2026)
- **Description**: Construction companies have run on QuickBooks + spreadsheets + disconnected desktop software for decades. Two separate Series-funded companies launched or raised this week alone: Adaptive ($30M Series B, $57M total, 750+ construction companies, agentic accounting that handles job costing/AP/billing/WIP while human reviews every action) and Trayd (Y Combinator, $15M, cut weekly payroll processing from 14 hours to 27 minutes, just launched accounting module Sept 24). The market: construction finance is uniquely complex — union rules, prevailing wage, certified payroll, multi-state taxes, job costing by project. Generic tools break. Purpose-built wins.
- **Target Industries**: General contractors, specialty trades, residential construction, real estate developers
- **Market Timing**: Growing (multiple funded companies validating simultaneously; legacy tools still dominant)
- **Example Products**:
  - Adaptive: https://www.adaptive.build — agentic accounting for construction, $57M raised
  - Trayd: https://www.trayd.com — payroll + compliance + accounting for construction, YC-backed
- **White Space**: Mid-market GCs ($5M–$50M revenue) who can't afford Adaptive's implementation but are too complex for QuickBooks. Also: specialty trade subcontractors (drywall, roofing, concrete) who have the same job-costing pain but fewer software options than GCs.
- **Speed to Market**: 12–16 weeks for a focused wedge (payroll compliance for one trade type in one state)
- **LTD Fit**: Low — construction finance is mission-critical; operators want SLA guarantees, not one-time deals
- **Content Strategy**: "construction accounting software", "certified payroll software", "job costing for contractors", "prevailing wage compliance"

## Residential Homebuilding Coordination (Trade Contractor ↔ Builder)

- **Category**: Paper-to-Digital / Platform Consolidation
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://getscaffold.com/resources/scaffold-raises-15m-series-seed — Scaffold $15M seed (D.R. Horton + Pulte Homes investors, Sept 15, 2026)
- **Description**: Scaffold raised $15M seed with D.R. Horton and Pulte Homes as investors — the builders themselves are funding the coordination layer. Problem: a trade contractor starts every morning in a dozen different homebuilder portals, manually copying purchase orders and schedule changes. A schedule changes Tuesday; the crew hears Thursday. Scaffold deployed on 200,000+ homes across 30 states, connects to 29 of the top 30 US homebuilder portals, automates PO population, keeps schedules synced, reducing dry runs 50% and PO errors 89% within 60 days. The insight: this is a two-sided market where the demand side (homebuilders) is actively funding the supply-side tool.
- **Target Industries**: Residential construction trade contractors (framing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC rough-in), homebuilder procurement
- **Market Timing**: Growing (Series seed at $15M with marquee investors signals category validation)
- **Example Products**:
  - Scaffold: https://getscaffold.com — AI coordination between builders and trade contractors
- **White Space**: Similar coordination layer for commercial construction (GC ↔ subcontractor scheduling), or the inspection/permitting coordination layer (permit approvals create the same fragmented communication chaos as builder portals).
- **Speed to Market**: 16+ weeks — requires integrations with builder portals; not a quick MVP play
- **LTD Fit**: No — this is infrastructure pricing, not a tool SMB operators pay per-seat for
- **Content Strategy**: "trade contractor scheduling software", "homebuilder subcontractor coordination", "construction schedule software"

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate–Strong
- **Sources**:

  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/handoff-ai — Handoff AI (YC): remodeling estimates with AI

- **Description**: Three separate YC-backed or recently-launched tools targeting the same pain: contractors spend hours manually measuring blueprints and building estimates. All target the "go from blueprint to invoice" workflow. Contractors are still doing this on paper/Excel in 2026. A sub-4-figure job estimate takes 2–4 hours manually; these tools promise 15 minutes.
- **Target Industries**: Residential remodeling, general contractors, specialty trades (roofing, HVAC, plumbing for estimates)
- **Market Timing**: Early–Growing (multiple YC companies validating; incumbents like PlanSwift are dated)
- **Example Products**:
  - CostLogic: https://www.producthunt.com/products/costlogic — AI takeoff to invoice, 2026
  - Handoff AI: https://www.handoffai.com — remodeling estimates, YC 2025
  - Scout Out: https://www.producthunt.com/products/scout-out-2 — AI proposals from plans, YC 2026
- **White Space**: Trade-specific estimating (roofing material takeoff that knows TPO vs. architectural shingle; HVAC load calculation that generates a parts list). The AI generalists are building horizontal; trade-specific accuracy is the differentiator nobody has nailed yet.
- **Speed to Market**: 6–10 weeks for a single-trade estimator (e.g., roofing-only)
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — strong LTD fit; contractors make buying decisions per-project-type; lifetime deal for a specific trade tool resonates
- **Content Strategy**: "roofing estimate software", "HVAC estimation tool", "contractor takeoff software", "[trade] proposal generator"
