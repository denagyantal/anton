  - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48189780 — "I'm building a SaaS with ZERO AI features in 2026" — founder reacting against AI dependency
- **Description**: A counter-trend is emerging: founders and buyers in traditional industries (manufacturing ERP, payroll, logistics, plumbing compliance) are explicitly *avoiding* AI features, positioning "deterministic output" — software that does exactly what you tell it, every time — as a premium quality signal. For compliance-heavy boring businesses, hallucination risk is disqualifying. PaxERP is a clean, fast manufacturing ERP with zero AI that has happy paying customers. HN sentiment in late 2026 is shifting: "In 2024, AI was the value prop. Now it's becoming a liability (compliance risk, hallucinations, unpredictable costs)."
- **Target Industries**: Small manufacturers (custom parts, job shops), payroll processing, compliance tracking, legacy database migration, plumbing compliance
- **Market Timing**: Early counter-trend — most buyers still expect AI checkboxes, but the pendulum is swinging
- **Example Products**:
  - PaxERP — https://paxerp.com (manufacturing ERP, no AI, fast and clean)
  - PostAuth — https://postauth.app (user dashboard, no AI, founder-built)
- **White Space**: Clean, fast, simple vertical ERP for niche manufacturing segments (injection molding shops, custom woodworking, small metal fabricators) that are underserved by both general-purpose ERPs (too complex) and AI tools (too unreliable). Also: compliance tracking software for trades that generates audit-ready PDF reports without AI.
- **Speed to Market**: 6–10 weeks for a niche manufacturing ERP MVP targeting a single segment
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — manufacturing shop owners are pragmatic; a $299–$499 LTD for a clean, offline-capable job tracking + invoicing tool would convert on the right communities

- **Example Products**:
  - UpSmith — (trades workforce AI, $10M Series A)

  - XOi — enterprise field intelligence (too expensive for small shops)
  - Bluon PartsConnect — parts sourcing tool for HVAC techs

- **LTD Fit**: Yes — small shop owners will pay $199 LTD to avoid recurring subscription anxiety; this is a clear AppSumo candidate
