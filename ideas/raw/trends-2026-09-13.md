## AI-Native Dispatch OS for Trades

- **Category**: Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://techfundingnews.com/built-by-a-tradesman-backed-by-a16z-and-sequoia-probook-raises-40m-to-reinvent-dispatch-for-americas-home-service-businesses/
  - https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/probook-40m-home-services-ai-2026
  - https://hvacinsider.com/probook-raises-40m-from-andreessen-horowitz-and-sequoia-to-scale-the-ai-operating-system-for-home-services/
  - https://www.thehardwirenews.com/probook-raises-40-million-ai-dispatch-hvac-contractors/
- **Description**: Probook raised $40M (a16z + Sequoia) to build an AI operating system for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical contractors, anchored around dispatch. The core insight: dispatch is the operational brain of every home service business, yet most vendors built for the top of the funnel (lead gen, booking) and ignored it. Customers report technician-to-dispatcher ratios of 100:1 after adopting Probook.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, home services
- **Market Timing**: Growing — a16z and Sequoia both writing checks to the same unglamorous vertical is a tier-1 signal
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook: https://probook.ai (dispatch-first AI OS)
  - ServiceTitan (dominant enterprise player, $9B valuation)
  - Jobber (SMB breadth play)
  - Housecall Pro (mobile-first SMB — 200K+ professionals)
- **White Space**: Mid-market ($199–$500/mo range) AI dispatch for 2–15 truck shops that can't afford ServiceTitan ($300+/tech/mo). Also: AI for specialty sub-trades (septic, pest, irrigation) that Probook doesn't serve.
- **Speed to Market**: 8–12 weeks for a focused MVP targeting one sub-trade with automated dispatch + call answering
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades owners prefer MRR but would buy a steep LTD to avoid commitment risk; LTD could work as acquisition funnel
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC dispatch software", "plumbing scheduling AI", "contractor dispatch automation", "[trade] operating system"

## Field Estimating via Computer Vision & 3D Scanning

- **Category**: Mobile-First Field Tools / Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SiZ-feldy-field-first-ai-for-home-services-and-contractors/
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/theos-rfm-remote-facility-manager
- **Description**: Feldy (YC W26) uses computer vision and vision-language models to turn jobsite photos, videos, and LiDAR scans into estimates, inspection reports, proposals, and materials lists. 200+ paying customers already. Core problem: estimates in residential home services still take 2–3 days to reach homeowners because reps spend the entire site visit on manual data capture rather than selling. Feldy claims reclaiming $50K–$100K+ in capacity per year for average home-services company. Separately, Theos[RFM] launched a 3D facility management portal for commercial property managers.
- **Target Industries**: Window replacement, painting, roofing, general remodeling, home services broadly; commercial facility management
- **Market Timing**: Early — 200 customers on web, mobile app just launched
- **Example Products**:
  - Feldy: https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SiZ-feldy-field-first-ai-for-home-services-and-contractors/
  - Theos[RFM]: https://www.producthunt.com/products/theos-rfm-remote-facility-manager
- **White Space**: Vertical-specific versions for trades where Feldy doesn't yet have deep models (pool/spa, septic, HVAC diagnostics). "Estimate acceleration" tools for solo contractor who can't afford the full Feldy stack.
- **Speed to Market**: 8–16 weeks (needs CV work) — but a simpler version using photo uploads + GPT-4o vision for specific trade workflows could be faster
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades owners would pay well for a tool that provably speeds up estimates; LTD at $599+ realistic
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor estimating software", "roofing estimate app", "home services field AI", "instant estimate generator"

## Construction Payroll & Union Compliance Automation

- **Category**: Compliance & Regulation Tech / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/construction-tech-automation-trayd-ai-seriesa/
  - https://entrepreneurloop.com/construction-tech-trayd-series-a-y-combinator/
- **Description**: Trayd (YC, $10M Series A, March 2026) automates payroll, HR, compliance, and labor cost tracking for specialty trade contractors (concrete, electricians, plumbers, ironworkers, painters). Reduced payroll processing from 14 hours/week to 27 minutes. 600% YoY revenue growth. Key insight: specialty trade contractors outnumber general contractors 400:1 in the US, yet nearly all construction tech was built for GCs. Union rules, prevailing wage, multi-state taxes make generic payroll (QuickBooks) unworkable. Round closed in 3 weeks.
- **Target Industries**: Specialty trade contractors — concrete, electrical, plumbing, ironwork, painting, fireproofing
- **Market Timing**: Early — Trayd started in NYC/Northeast (highest union density); national expansion just beginning
- **Example Products**:
  - Trayd: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/construction-tech-automation-trayd-ai-seriesa/
  - Procore (GC-focused, not specialty trade)
  - Generic: QuickBooks, ADP (don't handle union/prevailing wage complexity)
- **White Space**: Specialty trade payroll outside NYC (California, Texas, Florida union markets are next). Smaller sub-$5M revenue specialty contractors not yet reached. Multi-state certified payroll reporting for federal/public jobs.
- **Speed to Market**: 12–20 weeks (payroll is compliance-heavy, needs care)
- **LTD Fit**: Weak — payroll is ongoing, MRR model natural; but a flat "setup + 12-month" deal could work
- **Content Strategy**: "union payroll software for contractors", "prevailing wage automation", "specialty trade payroll", "certified payroll software"

## Niche Trade Verticals Skipped by Horizontal Platforms

- **Category**: Legacy Software Disruption / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.startupheist.com/housecall-pro-skipped-septic-thats-a-96k-mrr-opening/
  - https://www.contractormag.com/technology/news/55391529/housecall-pro-launches-trade-specific-software-for-hvac-plumbing-and-electrical-contractors
- **Description**: Housecall Pro launched HVAC, plumbing, and electrical specific packages on July 15, 2026 — deliberately skipping smaller trades. Septic pumping identified as a clear whitespace: 7,717 operators, 3–5 year service cycles (long enough to lose customers without software), state compliance requirements for hauling records, and no leader doing revenue-recovery from dormant customer records. Analysis shows ~$96K MRR potential from 5% of the market at $249/month. Same logic applies to pest control, irrigation, pool/spa, chimney sweep, and other neglected trades.
- **Target Industries**: Septic pumping, pest control, pool/spa service, irrigation, chimney sweep, pressure washing
- **Market Timing**: Growing — platform gap just opened as Housecall Pro explicitly left these trades behind
- **Example Products**:
  - ServiceCore (septic + portable sanitation + dumpster — multi-vertical)
  - SepticCycle ($149/mo flat — closest competitor, but no revenue-recovery motion)
  - PumpDocket ($99–$454/mo — compliance features locked behind expensive tiers)
  - WorkQuote: https://www.producthunt.com/products/workquote-quotes-scheduling-invoices (tree service, solo operators)
- **White Space**: Compliance-native vertical SaaS for individual niche trades (septic, pest control, irrigation) with revenue-recovery motion — mine dormant customer records and convert them to booked recurring jobs. Each trade has its own compliance footprint.
- **Speed to Market**: 6–10 weeks for a focused single-trade MVP
- **LTD Fit**: Strong — small septic/pest/pool operators are exactly the LTD buyer; they want simple and affordable, and LTD eliminates the "another subscription" objection
- **Content Strategy**: "septic pumping software", "pest control scheduling app", "pool service management software", "irrigation contractor CRM"

## "Diagonal SaaS" / Customizable Trade Platforms

- **Category**: SMB Platform Consolidation / Legacy Disruption
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.matchstick.vc/companies/dalton-mills
- **Description**: Dalton Mills (Matchstick Ventures Fund IV) is building a horizontal OS for trades that becomes "the most vertical software any contractor has ever used" via AI-driven workflow customization and an open builder ecosystem. The insight: existing vertical SaaS can't economically support the highly specialized workflows of individual operators (a flooring contractor with 12 crews works very differently from one with 2). AI-assisted development makes hyper-customization economically viable for the first time. Network effect: every new builder/plugin deepens the platform's vertical coverage.
- **Target Industries**: All trades — flooring, roofing, painting, specialty contracting
- **Market Timing**: Early — still in initial funding/buildout phase
- **Example Products**:
  - Dalton Mills (pre-revenue/early stage)
  - Fieldmobi: https://www.producthunt.com/products/fieldmobi (AI-customizable field-focused ERP)
  - ServiceTitan (monolithic incumbent this concept attacks)
- **White Space**: Builder marketplace for trade-specific plugins — if Dalton Mills builds the rails, third-party micro-SaaS builders could create deep modules for individual sub-trades
- **Speed to Market**: Platform/ecosystem play — 6–12 months minimum for meaningful adoption
- **LTD Fit**: Weak for the platform; strong for individual plugins/modules sold through the ecosystem
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor software customization", "trade ERP platform", "field service software builder"
