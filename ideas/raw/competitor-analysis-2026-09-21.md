## 1. Field Service Management (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical): The Solo-Operator Squeeze

### Market Landscape
| Competitor | Price | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|-------|-----------|------------|
| ServiceTitan | $245–$500/tech/mo + $5K–$50K onboarding | Enterprise features, marketing automation | Prohibitively expensive for small shops; aggressive contracts; "way too much for a small family company" |
| Housecall Pro | $79/mo advertised, ~$189/mo real entry | Good UX, brand recognition | Cost-creep from add-ons; no route optimization; Trustpilot 3.2/5; no auto late fees |

### Top Customer Complaints (from reviews)
1. "After sign-up told everything included, then additional fees introduced" — ServiceTitan, Capterra
2. Cost-creep from add-ons is the #1 reason businesses leave Housecall Pro — multiple aggregators
3. Housecall Pro advertised $79/mo but real minimum useful tier is $189/mo — fieldservicecompare.com
4. "Repeated unsolicited sales calls during trial and after subscribing" — Housecall Pro, Trustpilot
5. Solo operators on Jobber pay for 5-seat Connect tier just to get route optimization — tradesly.ai

### Identified Gap
- **What's missing**: Truly solo-first FSM tool — transparent flat pricing, mobile-first, route optimization + QuickBooks sync from day one, no seat-count tiers
- **Who needs it**: 1-truck HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and cleaning owner-operators — explicitly called "consistently underserved by traditional FSM tools" in multiple 2026 analyses
- **Why competitors don't do it**: Revenue optimization — solo operators have lower LTV, platforms deprioritize them and use tiered pricing to upsell features
- **Current workaround**: Legal pads, Google Sheets, or paying for 5-seat Jobber plans they can't fill

### Our Opportunity
- **Product concept**: "SoloTrade" — flat $29/mo field service app: job scheduling, mobile quotes + invoicing, GPS route optimization, QuickBooks/Stripe sync, customer texting — no seat fees, no add-ons, no contracts
- **Key differentiator**: All core features in one price, phone-first UX, never forces upgrade for basic functionality
- **LTD price point**: $79
- **Target channels**: r/HVAC, r/Plumbing, Facebook Groups ("HVAC Techs United"), trade school forums
- **AppSumo potential**: High — clear underserved pain point, low-complexity feature set, viral in trades communities
