## Craftplan — Bakery & Food Production Management

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46847690
- **Additional Links**: https://github.com/puemos/craftplan
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 577 points, 167 comments — #3 most popular Show HN of February 2026
- **Revenue Data**: OSS/free (no direct revenue), but massive signal of unmet demand
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Small food producers — bakeries, artisan food businesses, caterers, craft breweries, meal-prep services
- **Core Value Prop**: Production management for food businesses: recipe BOMs with cost rollups, inventory with lot traceability and allergen tracking, orders, production batch planning, purchasing. Everything spreadsheets do badly.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The tool is open-source and free — meaning no one has successfully monetized this space yet. Comments revealed a huge cohort of small food businesses running entirely on spreadsheets or clunky ERP tools built for other industries. The Craftplan thread triggered a broader conversation about how small businesses are vibe-coding their own ERPs because the market lacks affordable vertical tools. Comments explicitly named bakeries, small-batch food manufacturers, and catering businesses as underserved.
- **Our Angle**: A paid SaaS version of Craftplan — recipe management + cost-of-goods tracking + batch production scheduling + allergen compliance — targeted at small food producers. $49-99/month. The OSS version proves the demand; nobody has put a paywall on it yet. Narrow starting niche: bakeries with 2-20 employees.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — clear AppSumo candidate, food business owners are non-technical and will pay for a polished hosted version vs. self-hosting OSS

## AI Phone Answering for Home Service Contractors (Unanswered Call Crisis)

- **Source**: https://agentzap.ai/blog/home-services-phone-statistics
- **Additional Links**: https://pipelineon.com/blog/ai-tools-for-hvac-plumbing-scheduling/, https://www.whippy.ai/blog/plumbing-answering-service
- **Platform**: HN discussion signal + industry research
- **Type**: Market data / demand signal
- **Engagement**: High — the HVAC/plumbing millionaire class HN thread (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41828896) had 186 points, 260 comments; the missed call statistics are widely cited in 2026
- **Revenue Data**: Existing solutions start at $15/month (AgentZap). Market: $285+ average home service job value; HVAC companies losing $32k-$54k/month in unanswered calls
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control, landscaping — any home service contractor with < 20 employees
- **Core Value Prop**: 62% of calls to small home service businesses go unanswered during business hours. 85% of those callers don't leave voicemail — they call a competitor. 78% of homeowners hire the first contractor who answers. AI voice agent answers 100% of calls, books jobs, and triages emergencies 24/7.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The statistics are staggering: 47% of all service calls come in outside business hours; HVAC calls spike 425% during extreme weather when humans are busiest. The ROI story is trivially obvious to any contractor. Current solutions (AgentZap, Whippy) exist but are not yet dominant — the market is in early adoption (plumbing AI adoption went from 7% in 2024 to 19% in 2026). The segment of HVAC/plumbing contractors with 1-10 employees is massively underserved vs. larger companies using ServiceTitan.
- **Our Angle**: Vertical AI answering agent purpose-built for one trade at a time (e.g., HVAC-only first). Deep integrations with common scheduling tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro). Outcompete generic AI phone tools with trade-specific knowledge (HVAC urgency triage, seasonal pricing awareness, dispatch logic). $99-299/month.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 — recurring service, hard to LTD, but $199 LTD with 1-year cap possible for early-adopter contractors

## FieldLedgr — Free Field Service Software for Tradespeople

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47668698
- **Additional Links**: https://www.fieldledgr.com/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 1 point (very early, April 2026)
- **Revenue Data**: Free product — no monetization yet
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Tradespeople — plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, handymen running solo or small teams
- **Core Value Prop**: Free field service management for tradespeople — scheduling, invoicing, job tracking. The fact that it's free signals the founder is struggling to find price points that stick.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The product exists and someone built it, but the pricing strategy is "free" — meaning there's a market with pain but no dominant paid solution at the micro end. Competitors like Jobber ($49+/month) and Housecall Pro ($65+/month) target established businesses; solopreneurs and 1-3 person trades teams fall through the cracks. There's a gap for a $19-29/month simple job management tool for solo tradespeople.
- **Our Angle**: "Jobber for solo tradespeople" — ultra-simple job quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and customer follow-up. No bloat. Priced for the one-man band. Focus on mobile-first since tradespeople work in the field, not at desks.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5 — classic LTD play: solo operator with recurring need, one-time payment removes friction, AppSumo audience overlaps with small business operators

## Rentman — AV & Event Production Operations Platform ($15M+ ARR)

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/building-a-15m-arr-saas-from-a-gap-he-found-at-his-brick-and-mortar-HFriCBQLHukAmdXVEj1q
- **Additional Links**: https://rentman.io/
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Case study / revenue milestone
- **Engagement**: Featured IH article, April 2026
- **Revenue Data**: $15M–$20M ARR, 70+ countries, 100+ person team, bootstrapped for 8 years before outside capital
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5 — event industry is unglamorous but creative; the operations side is pure logistics
- **Target Industry**: AV rental companies, event production, lighting, staging, broadcast, film — any business that rents out equipment + coordinates crews
- **Core Value Prop**: Operations platform for production companies: equipment rental tracking, crew scheduling, quoting, logistics, invoicing — all in one system. Started because the founder (16-year-old AV rental company owner) couldn't find software that fit, so built his own.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Rentman's success ($15M+ ARR) proves the "equipment rental + crew coordination" model works at scale. But they only serve AV/event. Adjacent niches with the identical workflow problem and no dominant software: party supply rental companies, construction equipment rental (small regional players), medical equipment rental, tool rental shops, staging/tent rental companies. Each of these is a Rentman-sized opportunity.
- **Our Angle**: Clone the Rentman playbook for party supply rental: equipment availability calendar, customer quoting with delivery logistics, damage tracking, driver scheduling. Target the 50,000 party supply rental companies in the US that run on spreadsheets and phone calls. $79-149/month.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — business-critical software with clear ROI, LTD makes sense for the entry tier

- **Revenue Data**: IH article cites dental management software and senior living placement software as examples of boring SaaS printing money
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
