## AI Operating Systems for Trades ("Runs the Business")

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Trades Going Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - Dalton Mills $9.2M raise (June 2026): https://daltonmills.com/resources/fieldnotes/dalton-mills-raises-9m-to-build-the-platform-for-trades
  - SayDoo launch (Nov 6, 2026): https://www.pmmag.com/articles/107849-saydoo-launches-ai-company-for-the-skilled-trades
  - Sophiie AI AUD $5M seed (Aug 2026): https://www.einpresswire.com/article/934145369/sophiie-ai-raises-aud-5-million-seed-round-and-launches-ai-operating-system-for-trades-and-service-businesses
  - Quinn $4M seed (Nov 5, 2026): https://www.kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn
  - Avoca $125M raise, $1B valuation (Apr 2026)
  - Mainteny (Product Hunt 2026): https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny
  - ServiceAgent (Product Hunt 2026): https://www.producthunt.com/products/serviceagent
- **Description**: An entire cohort of well-funded startups (and many bootstrapped ones) is converging on a single idea: replace the trades business owner's "back office" (phone answering, scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, follow-up) with a single AI operating layer. SayDoo launched this week (Nov 6) for roofing/HVAC/plumbing. Sophiie AI raised AUD $5M growing 10-15% MoM serving thousands of businesses. Avoca hit a $1B valuation. The framing has shifted from "field service software" to "AI OS for the trades."
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping, pest control
- **Market Timing**: Growing fast — from seed activity in 2025 to Series A/B in 2026
- **Example Products**:
  - Sophiie AI: https://sophiie.ai — AI OS for trades, AUD $5M seed
  - SayDoo: https://saydoo.com — AI console built inside a roofing company
  - Dalton Mills: https://daltonmills.com — "Replit for the trades"
  - Quinn: mobile-first training + compliance OS for field workers
  - Avoca: voice AI for HVAC/plumbing — $1B valuation
  - Mainteny: https://mainteny.com — ERP + AI back office for maintenance companies
  - ServiceAgent: https://www.producthunt.com/products/serviceagent — AI voice for home services
- **White Space**: Most AI OS products target the mid-market (5+ trucks). The solo or 1-2 person trade shop (plumber, electrician) getting their FIRST digital tool is underserved — the bar is too high. A dead-simple "WhatsApp-first" or SMS-first version of these OS products for the smallest operators remains open.
- **Speed to Market**: MVP in 4-6 weeks using existing voice AI APIs (VAPI, Bland, Retell) + a scheduling layer. Harder to get right operationally.
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades owners are price-sensitive but the ROI is clear (every missed call = $200-500 lost). A LTD for a niche (e.g., roofing-only or HVAC-only) in a geo could work.
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC answering service", "plumber phone answering software", "trade business AI receptionist", "field service booking automation"

## AI Estimating & Quoting for Contractors (WhatsApp → Invoice)

- **Category**: Paper-to-Digital / Mobile-First Field Tools
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - Handoff AI (YC W25): https://www.producthunt.com/products/handoff-ai — AI estimating for remodeling/handyman, thousands of users
  - Nexley (Nov 2026): https://constructionmanagement.co.uk/startup-lowdown-from-a-note-on-whatsapp-to-an-invoice-with-nexley/ — voice note to quote via WhatsApp
  - AirQuote (Apr 2026): https://airquote.co/blog/introducing-airquote — AI quote-to-paid for solo contractors, $12/mo
  - ONEREQ PRO: https://www.onereqpro.com/vs-competitors — all-in-one with AI diagnostics vs. ServiceTitan
- **Description**: Small contractors (roofers, electricians, builders) lose money quoting from memory or on paper at the kitchen table. Multiple new tools now let tradespeople send a voice note or photo and get a professional quote + invoice in seconds. Nexley (UK, launched this spring) uses WhatsApp specifically. Handoff AI (YC) is processing thousands of estimates in the US. AirQuote at $12/mo is explicitly targeting Jobber-to-simple switchers.
- **Target Industries**: Residential construction, remodeling, roofing, general contracting, handymen, electricians, plumbers
- **Market Timing**: Early — many players launching, but no dominant winner yet below Jobber's price point
- **Example Products**:
  - Handoff AI: https://handoff.ai — YC-backed, thousands of users
  - Nexley: UK WhatsApp-to-invoice for builders/tradespeople
  - AirQuote: https://airquote.co — $12/mo, solo contractors
- **White Space**: US WhatsApp penetration is lower — an SMS or iMessage-first version of Nexley for the US market. Also: multi-trade estimating that handles mixed labor + materials with regional pricing databases.
- **Speed to Market**: 2-4 weeks for core quote-to-invoice flow. Regional pricing DB is the hard part.
- **LTD Fit**: Very high — solo contractor pays $59-99 LTD easily if it saves 2 hours/week on paperwork.
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor quoting app", "trade invoice app", "how to quote faster as a contractor", "AI estimate generator for builders"

  - FCC TCPA consent rewrite (effective Nov 25, 2026): texting consent rules changing for HVAC/plumbing/clinics
  - NYS certified payroll digital mandate (Jan 2026): https://cembs.com/new-york-states-2026-certified-payroll-mandate-is-your-firm-ready/
  - UK Making Tax Digital for trades (Apr 2026): https://phpionline.co.uk/opinion/powered-nows-guide-to-making-tax-digital/
  - France e-invoicing mandate for construction (Sept 1, 2026): https://en.batipole.com/Articles/Quotation-and-invoicing-software-in-the-construction-industry%3A-what-will-change-in-2026
  - Roofing compliance (AOB, OSHA, TCPA): https://newagesysit.com/blog/state-contractor-licensing-assignment-of-benefits-statutes-osha-fall-protection-records-and-tcpa-storm-lead-outreach-compliance-rules-for-us-roofing-software/
- **Description**: 2026 is a year of new compliance mandates hitting trades and clinics simultaneously. The EPA AIM Act forced HVAC contractors to track refrigerant usage digitally from January. The FCC's TCPA rewrite goes live November 25 (this month), changing how HVAC/plumbing/clinics must handle customer text consent. New York mandated digital certified payroll for construction. The UK's Making Tax Digital forced self-employed tradespeople to report quarterly digitally. France mandated e-invoicing for construction companies in September. Each mandate creates a forcing function for digitization.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC (EPA refrigerant), roofing (OSHA, AOB), construction (certified payroll, e-invoicing), all trades sending marketing texts (TCPA)
- **Market Timing**: Early-to-growing — mandates just took effect, tools to handle them are sparse
- **Example Products**:
  - Powered Now (UK): trades quoting/invoicing for MTD compliance
  - Brixx Digital compliance tracker: https://brixxdigital.com/tools/compliance-deadlines.html
- **White Space**: A US-focused "compliance calendar + document vault" for trades (HVAC refrigerant logs, OSHA 300A posting reminders, TCPA opt-out tracking, certified payroll) packaged as a simple $29-49/mo app. No one has cleanly bundled this for small trade operators.
- **Speed to Market**: 2-3 weeks for a compliance checklist + document storage MVP; add reminders and auto-filing later.
- **LTD Fit**: High — a one-time tool that "keeps you compliant" is an easy sell at $59-99 LTD.
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC refrigerant log software", "OSHA 300A filing app", "contractor compliance tracker", "TCPA compliance for small business"
