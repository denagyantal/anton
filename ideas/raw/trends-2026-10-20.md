## 1. AI Operating Systems for the Trades ("Replit for Plumbers")

- **Category**: Trades Digital / Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://daltonmills.com/resources/fieldnotes/dalton-mills-raises-9m-to-build-the-platform-for-trades — Dalton Mills raises $9.2M (June 2026)
  - https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SiZ-feldy-field-first-ai-for-home-services-and-contractors/ — Feldy YC launch (Aug 2026), 200+ paying customers
  - https://pitchmap.app/posts/msfk8gzg8bd3702ff1fe6c17 — AI agent platform for HVAC/plumbing/electrical, $38K MRR, raising $1.5M seed
  - https://betakit.com/billdr-relaunches-with-4-4-million-cad-to-build-operating-system-for-construction/ — Billdr relaunches with $4.4M CAD (Jan 2026)
- **Description**: Multiple well-funded startups are positioning as the "operating system" or "Replit" for trades — letting operators build bespoke workflows with AI/no-code rather than forcing their business to conform to generic FSM software. The pitch: every HVAC operator has one workflow their existing software can't handle. Now they build it themselves.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, residential construction, home services
- **Market Timing**: Early — multiple seed rounds but no dominant player yet
- **Example Products**:
  - Dalton Mills (https://daltonmills.com) — $9.2M raised, "agentic, no-code platform for trades"
  - Feldy (https://feldy.co) — YC-backed, field-first AI with 3D scans + estimates
  - Billdr (https://billdr.com) — $4.4M CAD, SMB general contractor operating system
- **White Space**: No dominant winner yet. Most tools still target large contractors (ServiceTitan). The 1–10 truck/van operator is severely underserved. Specific niches: electrical-only, plumbing-only OS tools at accessible price points.
- **Speed to Market**: 4–8 weeks for a focused niche (e.g., "AI admin for electricians under 5 vans")
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades are receptive to lifetime deals if ROI is obvious (time saved on quotes/invoices). Best positioned as monthly with LTD for launch.
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC business software", "field service management for small contractors", "plumbing invoice software", "best software for electricians"

  - Feldy (https://feldy.co) — 3D scan + estimate for any home service
- **White Space**: "Photo to quote in 5 minutes" for trades other than roofing — painting, fence installation, window replacement, concrete work. These tradespeople still walk a job and estimate by memory/spreadsheet.
- **Speed to Market**: 4–6 weeks for a single-trade MVP (e.g., "photo-to-quote for painters")
- **LTD Fit**: High — clear ROI (win more jobs, spend less time estimating). $199–$499 LTD feels right.
