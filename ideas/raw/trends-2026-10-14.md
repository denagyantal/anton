- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades owners want monthly predictability. LTD at $199-$299 for 12 months of credits could work for AppSumo launch.

- **LTD Fit**: Strong — one-time $79-$149 for a compliance tool that protects against $165K fines is an easy sale. AppSumo-ready.
