## FieldFlow — $5/mo Jobber Alternative for Solo Contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47294092
- **Additional Links**: https://fieldflow-nine.vercel.app/auth
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 1 point, 0 comments (March 2026)
- **Revenue Data**: Not disclosed — early MVP, $5/month flat pricing
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Solo contractors — carpenters, plumbers, electricians, HVAC, roofers
- **Core Value Prop**: A solo carpenter built field service software he actually uses. $5/month flat, no per-user fees. Just invoicing, scheduling, client management — the three things contractors actually need.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Built by someone who was paying $150+/month for Jobber and hated it. Jobber starts at $39/mo, Tradify at $47/user. The solo contractor segment (1-2 person shops) is completely underserved — tools are priced and designed for 5-50 employee outfits. $5/mo is probably too cheap to build a business on, but the demand signal is crystal clear. The builder's complaint — "too bloated, too expensive for a one-person operation" — is echoed by virtually every solo tradesperson.
- **Our Angle**: There's a gap between $5 (too cheap, no moat) and $39 (too expensive and complex). A $19-29/mo mobile-first product with instant setup (under 5 minutes), quote → invoice → payment in 3 taps, and SMS customer notifications could own the 1-5 person trades segment that Jobber ignores. No desktop required. No training. Beats FieldFlow on polish and beats Jobber on simplicity + price.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5 — Existing paying Jobber customers are a direct conversion target. Strong AppSumo fit.

## ZenMaid — $3M/Year Scheduling SaaS for Cleaning Services

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/from-a-cleaning-side-hustle-to-a-3m-yr-saas-for-cleaning-services-suhsqkDZB1zIwRmXxrFm
- **Additional Links**: https://get.zenmaid.com/
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Revenue milestone / founder interview
- **Engagement**: 28 upvotes, 10 bookmarks (Feb 2025)
- **Revenue Data**: $250,000/month ($3M ARR). Bootstrapped. Founded 2013. 11 years to build. Healthy growth as a "lifestyle business."
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Residential cleaning / maid services
- **Core Value Prop**: Scheduling software specifically designed for maid services. Founder ran a cleaning business, shut it down, then built software for other cleaning businesses. Deep first-hand industry knowledge = competitive moat.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: ZenMaid is the dominant player for cleaning scheduling but at $3M ARR it may be getting feature-bloated. The "1-3 cleaner" micro-business segment — someone who just started a cleaning side hustle and doesn't want enterprise software — may be underserved. ZenMaid's own success shows the market is real and paying. Adjacent opportunity: cleaning businesses in emerging markets (LATAM, SEA) where ZenMaid hasn't expanded. Or: cleaning business + client communication in one (customers get automatic booking confirmations via WhatsApp, not just email).
- **Our Angle**: Ultra-simple version for nano-cleaners: just route optimization, client communication (WhatsApp/SMS native), and invoicing. Mobile-only. No desktop. Target: cleaning businesses with 1-5 cleaners doing residential work. $19-39/mo.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — Proven market, validated pricing. Good AppSumo candidate. ZenMaid's existence proves customers pay.

## Neuclis — Field Service CRM for HVAC/Plumbing/Electrical/Landscaping

- **Additional Links**: None found — product appears pre-launch
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Discussion / pre-launch
- **Engagement**: Community comment
- **Revenue Data**: Not yet launched — distribution challenge acknowledged
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Home service contractors — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping
- **Core Value Prop**: Solo developer built a field service management CRM covering 8 global markets. Product built, but struggling with distribution/launch.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The builder noted "product is done but hasn't launched yet" with a focus on solving distribution, not the product. ## Small Landlord Software Gap — Property Management for 5-25 Units

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=778170 (Ask HN: Why don't we pay rent online?)
- **Additional Links**: https://www.vibrantsnap.com/blog/micro-saas-ideas-profitable-niches-2026
- **Platform**: HN (Ask HN thread — older but evergreen complaint)
- **Type**: Ask HN / market demand signal
- **Engagement**: Thread with detailed landlord pain point comment
- **Revenue Data**: Vibrantsnap estimates $10k-$30k MRR potential for a well-executed product in this space
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Small landlords / property managers with 5-25 units
- **Core Value Prop**: Small landlords — who represent the vast majority of landlords — use text messages, Excel, and QuickBooks because enterprise tools (Buildium, AppFolio) are built for 500+ unit portfolios and priced accordingly.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: HN commenter (landlord of a student housing co-op) described the problem precisely: "Less is more. Tenant, apartment number, payments, payments owed. Anything more and you'll just confuse us." Current dominant tool (Tenant Pro) described as "crap." Enterprise tools all talk about complex accounting integrations, but small landlords just want: tenant info, maintenance requests, and rent tracking. Vibrantsnap (April 2026) specifically called this out as a $10k-$30k MRR opportunity: "Small landlords (5-20 units) use text messages for maintenance requests, losing track of everything."
- **Our Angle**: Three features only: (1) tenant portal where renters submit maintenance requests with photos, (2) automated rent reminder texts, (3) document storage for leases. $29/mo per landlord. Tenants get a link — no app download required. Onboard in 10 minutes. This beats the big players on simplicity and beats free tools (text messages) on organization. Reach landlords via local real estate investor Facebook groups and REIA meetups.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — Landlords pay for anything that reduces late-night "the toilet is broken" texts. Strong lifetime value, low churn.

## Home Services SaaS for Solo Tradespeople — $500B Underserved Market

- **Source**: https://medium.com/@e2larsen/saas-for-home-services-a-solopreneurs-edge-de6cc095963b (Eddie Larsen, Sep 2025)
- **Additional Links**: https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/technician-scheduling-software, https://capterra.com/field-service-management-software/
- **Platform**: Medium / industry analysis
- **Type**: Market analysis with primary research signals
- **Engagement**: Published article with Reddit/IH quotes
- **Revenue Data**: Market estimated at $500B+ annually in US. Solo contractors already paying $100-200/mo for tools they hate. $39/mo sweet spot for replacement tools. Example: 100 customers × $39/mo = $46,800 ARR attainable in year 1.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, general contractors (1-5 person shops)
- **Core Value Prop**: The home services market is massive but tools are priced and designed for mid-market (10-100 employee companies). Smallest players are underserved.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Five core pain points with zero good lightweight solutions: (1) scheduling chaos and double-bookings, (2) quoting and invoicing still done on Word templates or carbon-copy slips — delays = delayed payments, (3) missed calls = lost jobs, no automated follow-up, (4) dispatch inefficiency — routes not optimized, techs criss-crossing the city, (5) record-keeping buried in file cabinets. One HVAC tech quote on Reddit: *"I can fix a furnace in 20 minutes but spend hours chasing down paperwork. The software we use costs a fortune and feels like it was designed for NASA, not me."* One IH founder quote: *"Every local contractor I talked to was juggling five apps and a wall calendar. Their biggest ask wasn't fancy AI, it was 'just give me something that works and doesn't cost a fortune.'"*
- **Our Angle**: Pick one trade, one city, one pain point. Build "quote-to-invoice for plumbers" or "missed-call lead capture for HVAC." At $39/mo, 250 customers = $117k ARR. Validate by calling local trade shops and offering a free 30-day trial in exchange for feedback. Trade associations and supply houses are excellent distribution partners.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5 — The market clearly pays. Entry via LTD on AppSumo, then convert to monthly. High LTV since these are business-critical tools.

## Appointment Scheduling + Missed-Call Recovery for Trades

- **Source**: https://www.flowjam.com/blog/indie-hackers-saas-ideas-2025-10-you-can-launch-fast (Flowjam, Jun 2026)
- **Additional Links**: https://www.goodcall.com/appointment-scheduling-software/home-service
- **Platform**: Industry research / Indie Hackers ecosystem
- **Type**: Validated niche idea with revenue comparables
- **Engagement**: Published June 2026, market research article
- **Revenue Data**: Flowjam estimates $35k-$450k ARR for "appointment scheduling for a niche (salons/clinics/trades)"
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Trades businesses — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning (1-10 employees)
- **Core Value Prop**: When a contractor is on a job, they miss calls. Every missed call is a lost job. Automated SMS response + basic booking captures leads that would otherwise go to a competitor who answers the phone.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: "Missed calls lead to lost jobs. A lack of automated reminders means more no-shows." This is consistently cited as a top-3 pain point across the home services industry. Large players like ServiceTitan include this but charge $300+/mo. There is no standalone, affordable, trade-specific version. The tool would: (1) automatically reply to missed calls with a text, (2) let customers book a callback slot, (3) send reminder texts to reduce no-shows. $29/mo. No learning curve. SMS-based — no app for the customer.
- **Our Angle**: "Never lose a lead to a missed call" — single-feature product. A/B test conversion on trades Facebook groups and NextDoor ads targeting local service businesses. Can be sold door-to-door to local HVAC and plumbing companies. Extremely simple build.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — Touches revenue directly (recovered leads = recovered income). High conversion on AppSumo if framed as "stop losing $500/missed call." G2 reviews from trades businesses are achievable.

## Quote-to-Invoice for Trades — Paperwork Killer

- **Source**: https://medium.com/@e2larsen/saas-for-home-services-a-solopreneurs-edge-de6cc095963b + HN FieldFlow thread
- **Additional Links**: https://myquoteiq.com/top-10-crms-for-home-service-startups-in-2026/
- **Platform**: Multiple (Medium + HN signal)
- **Type**: Market demand signal / pain point synthesis
- **Engagement**: Consistent across multiple sources
- **Revenue Data**: Contractors already paying $100-200/mo for tools. This problem is worth $39-79/mo standalone.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: All trades contractors — plumbing, electrical, HVAC, carpentry, roofing, painting
- **Core Value Prop**: Convert the take-a-photo-at-the-job-site moment directly into a professional quote, then one-tap into an invoice with online payment collection. Eliminates the Word template workflow entirely.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: "Many providers still use Word templates or hand-written slips. Delays in sending invoices mean delays in getting paid." This is consistent across all research sources as pain point #2 (after scheduling). Existing solutions (Jobber, Tradify, ServiceTitan) include quoting but as part of a full platform at $39-300/mo. A standalone quote → invoice → payment tool at $19/mo is simpler to sell and simpler to build. The friction point: contractors hate typing on phones. The solution: voice-to-line-item (say "replaced water heater $450 labor, $280 parts" → auto-populated quote).
- **Our Angle**: Mobile-first (iOS + Android), voice input for line items, instant PDF quote via text, customer signs digitally, one-tap convert to invoice with Stripe payment link. Integration with QuickBooks for tax time. $29/mo or $19/mo LTD at AppSumo.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5 — Directly solves a cash flow problem. "Get paid faster" = high WTP. Every tradesperson has this problem every single day.
