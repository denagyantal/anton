## [Transload — LTL Freight Dimensioning via Existing CCTV]

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463273
- **Additional Links**: https://transload.io
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Launch HN (YC P26)
- **Engagement**: 56 points, 19 comments (June 2026)
- **Revenue Data**: $50k/site/month in recoverable revenue per customer terminal; ~10,000 relevant LTL cross-dock terminals in US + Europe
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: LTL trucking / freight logistics
- **Core Value Prop**: Automatically measures freight dimensions using security cameras already in the terminal. LTL carriers lose revenue when shippers understate dimensions (10% of shipments had errors for one customer). No workflow changes required.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Transload is YC-backed and technical (monocular 3D CV). Their wedge is revenue recovery for large carriers. Smaller carriers (~$5-50M revenue) have the same problem but can't afford or integrate computer vision. Manual auditing is still done via spot-checks. Simpler adjacent play: freight billing discrepancy flagging tool with evidence capture via existing photos/scans.
- **Our Angle**: "Freight Audit Lite" — a SaaS for small/mid LTL carriers and freight brokers to flag and document dimensional weight discrepancies using photos submitted by dock workers (no CV required). Dispute workflow + customer billing correction. $99-299/mo per terminal.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 (per-terminal enterprise pricing; LTD not natural fit)

- **Engagement**: 111 upvotes, 15+ comments
- **Revenue Data**: $15–20M ARR; bootstrapped 8 years; 70+ countries; now funded and targeting US market
- **Boring Business Score**: 4

- **Core Value Prop**: End-to-end ops platform for equipment rental and production companies — gear availability, crew scheduling, quoting, logistics, invoicing. Cloud-first, replaced spreadsheets and on-prem tools.

## [Trades License & Credential Renewal Tracker]

- **Source**: https://sumotrends.com/ideas/boring-saas-niches-printing-money-2026/
- **Additional Links**: https://redwerk.com/blog/micro-saas-ideas-that-print-money/, https://www.microgaps.com/blog/boring-micro-saas-ideas-that-make-money
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers / research discussions (2026)
- **Type**: Recurring opportunity signal (multiple sources independently identify this gap)
- **Revenue Data**: Estimated $7k–$18k MRR for a single-state app; scales with state coverage
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Licensed trades — electricians, HVAC technicians, plumbers, cosmetologists, general contractors
- **Core Value Prop**: Trades professionals must renew licenses on state-specific schedules with continuing education (CE) requirements that vary by state, license type, and specialty. A lapsed license = no work + potential fines. Existing tools are generic reminder apps that don't know state rules.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: No purpose-built, state-aware license renewal tracker exists for trades. The problem is painful (livelihood at stake), recurring (annual/biennial), and has zero sexy competitors targeting it. Multi-tech companies (10+ licensed employees) have even more pain — tracking expiries across teams in spreadsheets is fragile.
- **Our Angle**: Build a state-specific CE and renewal database for electricians (start with top 10 US states). Product: (1) automated renewal reminders by SMS/email, (2) CE course hour tracker, (3) team dashboard for companies managing multiple licensed techs, (4) direct links to state renewal portals. $15–35/mo per user. Strong AppSumo / LTD story: "never miss a license renewal again."
- **LTD Potential**: 5

## [Independent Freight Dispatcher Software]

- **Source**: https://sumotrends.com/ideas/boring-saas-niches-printing-money-2026/
- **Additional Links**: https://medium.com/write-a-catalyst/6-boring-micro-saas-niches-that-could-quietly-make-5-000-month-87b80da395db
- **Platform**: Research / discussion (multiple indie hacker sources, 2026)
- **Type**: Opportunity signal
- **Revenue Data**: Industry estimated $5k–$15k MRR for purpose-built tool; large market (tens of thousands of independent dispatchers in US)
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Independent freight dispatching (self-employed brokers who coordinate between owner-operators/small trucking fleets and shippers)
- **Core Value Prop**: Independent freight dispatchers manage 3–20 trucks, negotiate load rates, handle paperwork (rate confirmations, BOLs, check calls), and invoice carriers for dispatch fees — almost entirely via Excel, WhatsApp, and email. No purpose-built tool exists for this segment.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Large TMS platforms ($500–2000+/mo) are built for fleets of 50+ trucks. Owner-operator fleets and small carriers are completely underserved. The independent dispatcher sits between them with no software at all. Strong existing community (large Facebook groups, YouTube channels) for customer acquisition.
- **Our Angle**: CRM + load board integration + paperwork generator for independent freight dispatchers. Track carriers, loads, rates, invoices, and check calls in one place. $49–99/mo. Distribution via Facebook Groups (e.g., "Freight Dispatching" groups with 100k+ members) and YouTube content. LTD viable via AppSumo.
- **LTD Potential**: 4
