## Field Manual Copilot for Sub-20-Tech Shops

- **Category**: Legacy Software Disruption + Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.startupheist.com/the-field-manual-copilot-79k-mrr-underneath-servicetitan/ (full analysis, May 2026)
  - https://blog.salescaptain.com/servicetitan-alternatives-for-small-hvac-companies-2026/
  - https://www.ventoxy.com/blog/servicetitan-alternatives-small-shops
  - https://fieldtics.com/blog/servicetitan-alternatives
  - https://softwarefortrades.com/servicetitan-alternatives/ (8 cheaper alternatives)
- **Description**: There are ~60,000 HVAC/plumbing shops with under 5 employees in the US. ServiceTitan costs $245–$398/tech/month with $5K–$50K implementation — too expensive and complex. These shops live in a "messy middle": paper manuals, PDF folders on a desktop, texting photos to the boss. A mobile RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) tool that lets a field tech snap a photo of equipment, pull the manual, summarize relevant pages, draft parts notes, and generate invoice language — without replacing their existing FSM — addresses a real workflow gap.
- **Target Industries**: Residential HVAC, plumbing, boiler/hydronic heating, electrical (small shops)
- **Market Timing**: Early — XOi owns the enterprise lane, ServiceTitan is moving up-market, leaving small shops unserved
- **Example Products**:
  - XOi (enterprise field intelligence — not competing in this niche)
  - Bluon PartsConnect (https://www.bluon.com) — HVAC parts sourcing, launched April 2026, integrates with Housecall Pro
  - Mainteny (https://mainteny.com) — AI operating system for maintenance companies, PH launch 2026
- **White Space**: A $29–$79/month mobile app for the 2-tech plumbing shop that: (1) identifies equipment from a photo, (2) surfaces the right manual pages, (3) creates a structured job note. No migration required. Narrower than XOi, simpler than ServiceTitan.
- **Speed to Market**: 6–10 weeks. RAG over equipment manual PDFs + equipment photo ID (Vision API) + job note template. Backend: Supabase + pgvector. Mobile: React Native.
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — small shop owners love one-time purchases. $79–$149 LTD with feature tiers per trade type.
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC manual app for small shops", "plumbing equipment lookup tool", "field technician job notes app"
