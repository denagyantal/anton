## Zirco.ai — AI Front Desk Employee for Dental Practices

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Additional Links**: https://zircoai.vercel.app/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (beta launch)
- **Engagement**: Low upvotes (early stage), 3 comments
- **Revenue Data**: Pre-revenue, in beta. 30+ dental practices surveyed during discovery.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices
- **Core Value Prop**: Replaces the dental front desk — insurance verification (Playwright automation + Availity API), inbound call handling (voice AI via Vapi), appointment booking into Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft, and SMS/email reminders. Full HIPAA compliance.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Front desk staff cost $40-50K/yr with 40% annual turnover. Insurance verification alone takes 2-3 hours/day manually — this is the clearest ROI unlock. No dominant AI-native solution owns this space yet.
- **Our Angle**: Narrow to just the insurance verification + appointment booking piece first (vs. full front desk replacement). Faster time-to-value, easier demo, lower trust barrier. Price at $299-499/mo per practice.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (healthcare requires trust-building; subscription model with multi-year contracts is more natural)
