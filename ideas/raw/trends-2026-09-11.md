Avoca hit $1B valuation at $125M raised (Kleiner Perkins + General Catalyst) building AI voice agents that answer calls, schedule jobs, and dispatch — for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical. These aren't AI features bolted onto existing software; they're AI-native platforms where the AI IS the dispatch. Industry runs on 20-year-old software or manual processes; 3.5M trade contractors in the US, only ~25% using any field service software.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing contractors (1–200 trucks)
- **Market Timing**: Growing fast — $300M+ into contractor AI in Q1 2026 alone
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook — $40M, dispatch AI: https://probook.com
  - Avoca — $125M/$1B valuation, voice AI agents: https://avoca.ai

Sub-$200/month AI dispatch for the one-truck operator is wide open. Also: specific trade verticals beyond HVAC/plumbing/electrical (septic, pest control, window cleaning) are completely untouched by these funded players.
- **Speed to Market**: MVP in 4–8 weeks for a niche sub-vertical using existing LLM/voice APIs
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — these businesses often prefer monthly but upfront cash offer works for $99–$149 LTD tiers
