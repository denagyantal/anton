## Field-First AI Estimating (Visual Capture → Instant Quote)

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Paper-to-Digital Transition
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SiZ-feldy-field-first-ai-for-home-services-and-contractors — Feldy YC launch (Aug 2026)
  - https://ai-for-contractors.com/news/netic-ai-23m-series-b-plumbers-roofers/ — Netic AI satellite estimating for roofers
  - https://www.openpr.com/news/4629974/equipt-ai-launches-next-gen-field-service-management-software — Equipt.ai launch (Sept 14 2026)
- **Description**: The single biggest operational bottleneck in residential home services in 2026 is estimate speed — window contractors, painters, and similar trades still take 2-3 days to send quotes after a site visit. Feldy (YC, 200+ paying customers) tackles this with 3D building scans, computer vision, and vision-language models to turn a contractor's site walk into an instant estimate. The math: 600M estimates/year × 3.5 hours each = 2.1 billion labor hours; Feldy estimates $27-37B in productive capacity currently locked in low-value measuring/documenting work. Netic AI uses satellite imagery for preliminary roofing estimates before anyone climbs a ladder. Equipt.ai launched Sept 14 2026 with a next-gen FSM emphasizing real-time job tracking and mobile accessibility.
- **Target Industries**: Window installation, roofing, painting, siding, fencing, landscaping, any trade with in-person scope assessment
- **Market Timing**: Early — Feldy is the clearest signal, 200 customers but just launching mobile app
- **Example Products**:
  - Feldy — https://feldy.ai — 3D scan + AI estimate (YC, 200+ customers Aug 2026)
  - Netic AI — satellite roof estimating included in their AI platform
  - Equipt.ai — https://equipt.ai — new FSM launch Sept 14 2026
  - WorkQuote — https://www.producthunt.com/products/workquote-quotes-scheduling-invoices — mobile-first estimating for tree service
- **White Space**: Single-trade depth (e.g., AI estimating ONLY for window contractors, vs. broad field service). Integration with Google Street View / property databases for remote estimates before site visit. Offline-capable mobile app for areas without signal. Voice-driven capture ("walk through the house talking, get your estimate").
- **Speed to Market**: Moderate — basic AI estimate generator (photo + GPT-4o Vision → itemized quote) is buildable in 2-4 weeks; 3D scan capability is 3-6 months.
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — trade owners would absolutely pay $299-$599 one-time for a tool that saves them 2 hours per estimate. Strong AppSumo fit.
- **Content Strategy**: "window estimate software", "roofing estimate app", "how to speed up contractor quotes", "AI estimate generator for contractors", "[trade] quote software"

## AI Call Answering for Trades / After-Hours Lead Capture

- **Category**: Vertical AI / SMB Platform Feature
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://ai-for-contractors.com/news/netic-ai-23m-series-b-plumbers-roofers/ — Netic AI phone handling analysis
  - https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/podium-launches-industrys-first-ai-operating-system-for-home-services-302862084.html — Podium: Carlsbad Heating & Cooling "has every call covered after hours without adding staff"
  - https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/probook-40m-home-services-ai-2026 — Probook: AI answering incoming calls
- **Description**: Missed calls are the single highest-ROI loss event for trade businesses — a missed plumbing emergency call is typically $500-$2,000 gone to a competitor. Every major AI platform for trades now emphasizes AI call answering as a flagship feature, but they bundle it into full platforms at $199-$300+/month. The underlying problem: plumbers, HVAC techs, and electricians are often on-site and can't answer their phone, especially nights/weekends. Trade-specific AI phone agents trained on the terminology and triage logic for each trade (vs generic AI receptionist tools) is an underserved standalone product.
- **Target Industries**: Plumbing (highest urgency = highest ROI), HVAC, electrical, roofing, any home service with emergency call volume
- **Market Timing**: Growing — all major trade AI platforms include this, but standalone affordable versions are rare
- **Example Products**:
  - Netic AI — AI phone handling as part of platform ($199/mo)
  - Probook — AI call answering as part of their OS
  - Podium — includes after-hours AI call coverage
  - Generic: Goodcall, Smith.ai (not trade-specific)
- **White Space**: Standalone affordable trade-specific AI phone agent ($49-$99/mo) that doesn't require full FSM migration. Pre-trained on plumbing/HVAC/electrical triage scripts. Integrates with existing Google calendar or simple scheduling. Small trade shops (1-3 person operations) who will never pay $199/mo for a full platform.
- **Speed to Market**: Fast — can build on top of existing AI voice API providers (Bland.ai, Retell.ai, Vapi) in 2-4 weeks. Trade-specific training data and prompt engineering is the differentiator.
- **LTD Fit**: Strong — $299-$499 LTD for "never miss a plumbing call again" has obvious AppSumo appeal.
- **Content Strategy**: "AI answering service for plumbers", "after hours answering service HVAC", "missed call recovery for contractors", "virtual receptionist for small contractors"

- **Market Timing**: Early — HomeBreeze is years-old but the category hasn't broken out
- **Example Products**:

  - TaskRabbit (handyman marketplace)

- **Speed to Market**: Slow for marketplace; moderate for embedded pricing widget SaaS.
- **LTD Fit**: Low for marketplace; moderate for SaaS pricing widget.

## Key Meta-Trends to Watch

1. **$300M+ into contractor AI in Q1 2026** — this category is becoming institutional. The window for bootstrapped tools serving small contractors (1-15 trucks) who can't afford enterprise platforms is now, before VCs fund more Series B/C players.

2. **ServiceTitan went public at $9B (2025)** — validates the TAM; AI-native competitors targeting the sub-10-truck long tail ServiceTitan ignores.

3. **"AI-native" as the wedge** — every new launch positions itself as "built for AI first, not AI added on." Netic AI explicitly built trade-specific training data. This is the winning message vs legacy tools.

4. **YC backing home services** — Feldy's YC launch (Aug 2026, 200+ customers) signals YC is actively interested in this space. Watch YC W27 batch for more trades/field service companies.

5. **UK compliance pressure** — UK landlord licensing expansion and building safety regulations are forcing small landlords to digitize. UK-specific property management software with compliance calendars is an underexplored US-founder gap.
