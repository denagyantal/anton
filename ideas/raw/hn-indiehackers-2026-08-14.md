## Mobile Field Inspection Tool for Construction / Field Service

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-analyzed-39-000-software-complaints-the-best-micro-saas-gaps-are-all-in-boring-industries-801c41685b
- **Additional Links**:
  - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49156683 (HN "Who Is Hiring" Aug 2026 — multiple construction field software companies hiring)
  - https://bigideasdb.com/boring-industries-begging-for-micro-saas
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Data analysis / market research post (July 26, 2026)
- **Engagement**: 50+ upvotes, 21 bookmarks
- **Revenue Data**: Target price ~$29/user/month for subcontractors with 5–20 field workers
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Construction, field service, trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control, inspection firms)
- **Core Value Prop**: Desktop-first FSM platforms have terrible mobile functionality. Field workers can't submit inspection reports from the job site — they either wait to get back to the office or fill out paper forms. Analysis of 39,000+ negative reviews found this complaint at 4.0/5 severity across 5 major platforms.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Narrow build — photo uploads with GPS tagging, form-based inspections, instant sync to office. No Gantt charts, no invoicing, no bloat. Existing platforms (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Workiz) are desktop-first and their mobile apps are afterthoughts. Severity 4.0/5, 5 companies all getting the same complaints.
- **Our Angle**: Pure mobile-first approach targeted at small field service subcontractors (5–20 workers) who can't afford or learn enterprise FSM. Onboard in minutes, not days. First inspection submitted from a job site in under 60 seconds.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — field service businesses respond well to one-time tools; recurring inspection needs justify subscription but LTD could work for entry

- **Revenue Data**: Target $149–299/month per firm. HN founders (LayerNext.ai) pivoted from enterprise CFO software to small business bookkeeping after discovering they couldn't even close their own books using QuickBooks.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Small accounting firms (1–10 CPAs), bookkeeping services, small businesses doing their own books
- **Core Value Prop**: QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Gusto all have different APIs, formats, and sync schedules and none of them are incentivized to make cross-platform syncing work. Accounting firms burn 3–5 hours/week on CSV reconciliation. Gap severity: 4.0/5 across 8 companies.

- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 — accounting tools are typically subscription; LTD possible for entry-level tier
