- **Description**: The EPA AIM Act's new Subpart C rules took effect January 1, 2026, expanding HFC refrigerant tracking requirements. The HVAC service industry (hundreds of thousands of technicians and small shops) is suddenly required to document every refrigerant transaction digitally. The existing compliance software market is thin: RefriTrak and a handful of enterprise-focused tools like Fexa exist, but there's almost no mobile-first, affordable solution targeting the small HVAC shop with 2-10 technicians. This was already identified as an MVP idea (see PRD in _bmad-output) — the market signal is now confirmed by the January 2026 rule enforcement date.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC companies, refrigeration maintenance firms, facility management teams, commercial real estate operators
- **Market Timing**: Early (regulation just took effect; compliance software adoption wave is 6-18 months behind)
- **Example Products**:
  - RefriTrak — https://www.refritrak.com (compliance tracking, primarily enterprise)
  - Fexa — https://fexa.io (facility management compliance platform)
  - ServiceChannel — https://servicechannel.com (enterprise facility management)
- **White Space**: Mobile-first refrigerant log app targeting 2-10 person HVAC shops. Simple: technician scans cylinder, logs usage, auto-calculates leak rate, generates EPA-required reports. No enterprise contracts, no $500/month pricing.
- **Speed to Market**: 2-4 weeks MVP (simple log + report generation)
- **LTD Fit**: Very high — HVAC shop owners will pay $99 once to avoid $60k/day fines
- **Content Strategy**: "EPA refrigerant compliance software", "AIM Act tracking app for HVAC", "Subpart C compliance tool", "HFC leak rate tracking"
