  - https://www.globenewswire.com/news-releases/2026/6/23/3316215/0/en/probook-raises-40m-from-andreessen-horowitz-and-sequoia-to-scale-the-ai-operating-system-for-home-services.html ($40M raise)

  - https://www.fmj.co.uk/elyos-ai-raises-a-13m-series-a-to-build-ai-agents-for-the-trades/ (Elyos AI $13M Series A, Jan 2026)
  - https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/driive-raises-pre-seed-round-to-build-the-ai-scheduling-platform-for-home-service-trades-302766196.html (Driive pre-seed, May 2026)
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/stormy-influencer-marketing-ai-agent (Stormy, YC, #1 PH day)
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny (Mainteny, AI back-office for maintenance)
  - https://www.pmmag.com/articles/107849-saydoo-launches-ai-company-for-the-skilled-trades (SayDoo launch, Nov 2026)
- **Description**: The home services trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing) are receiving a wave of AI-first operating systems — not add-on AI features bolted onto legacy FSMs. The key insight resonating with top-tier VCs (a16z, Sequoia): win the hardest, most central decision first (dispatch), then expand outward. Probook booked 2,542 jobs in one month with zero human intervention for a 260-tech customer. Del-Air went from 22 dispatchers to 10. Dalton Mills pitches as "Replit for the trades." Multiple YC companies (Stormy, Elyos, Driive) attacking the same problem from different angles.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, general home services
- **Market Timing**: Growing (VC dollars flooding in; flagship products already at scale with large operators)
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook (probook.ai) — dispatch-first, $40M a16z/Sequoia
  - Dalton Mills (daltonmills.com) — "platform the trades build on," $9.2M
  - Elyos AI (elyosai.com) — YC, $13M Series A, UK-based
  - Driive / Dot (getdriive.com) — AI booking agent, pre-seed
  - Stormy (stormy.ai) — YC, all-in-one field service AI, #1 PH day
  - Mainteny (mainteny.com) — AI back-office OS for maintenance companies
  - SayDoo (saydoo.com) — built inside a roofing company, Nov 2026 launch
- **White Space**: Mid-market independent shops (5–50 techs) are underserved — the well-funded players are going upmarket to PE-backed rollups. A lighter-weight, faster-to-deploy version optimized for indie shops without an enterprise sales motion could own this segment. Also: multi-trade all-in-one for shops doing HVAC + plumbing + electrical together.
- **Speed to Market**: 4–8 weeks for core MVP (dispatch + booking AI + text communication)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades owners are cost-conscious but ROI is obvious; a one-time deal with per-job or per-tech add-ons could work
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC dispatch software," "AI booking for plumbers," "home service scheduling automation," "field service AI," "dispatcher software small business"

## Skilled Trades Workforce Training & Compliance Tech

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Compliance & Regulation Tech
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn (Quinn $4M seed, Nov 2026)
  - https://skillmaker.ai/tech-company-oversubscribed-seed-round-closed/ (Skillmaker $4.5M, Jun 2026)
  - https://turbineworkforce.com/ (Turbine Workforce — apprenticeship OS)
  - https://getnexus.pro/ (Nexus — OSHA compliance for abatement/demolition)
  - https://provasafety.app/ (Prova — safety management for construction)
- **Description**: The skilled trades face a structural workforce crisis — technicians are aging out faster than they're being replaced, and onboarding new hires takes years on legacy training methods. A new category of AI-native workforce operating systems is emerging: ingest existing SOPs/manuals/videos, convert to mobile-first interactive training, track compliance, and manage apprenticeship programs. Quinn (funded Nov 5, 2026 — just 5 days ago) explicitly positions as "the OS for skilled trades employers." Skillmaker uses XR/smart glasses for hands-on simulation. Turbine manages apprenticeship compliance (RAPIDS/PIRL reporting) and OJT tracking.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing, automotive, manufacturing, pest control
- **Market Timing**: Early (structural problem is well-understood but software solutions just arriving)
- **Example Products**:
  - Quinn — AI workforce OS for skilled trades (kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn)
  - Skillmaker (skillmaker.ai) — XR + AI training, NAPA partnership, $4.5M seed
  - Turbine Workforce (turbineworkforce.com) — apprenticeship compliance OS
  - Nexus (getnexus.pro) — OSHA compliance for abatement/demolition contractors
  - Prova (provasafety.app) — safety management, construction/general industry
- **White Space**: Most solutions target employers with 20+ techs. A solo/small-shop product (<10 employees) that handles OSHA cert tracking, state license renewal alerts, and simple onboarding checklists could serve the long tail. Also: multi-language (Spanish-first) training for trades with large immigrant workforces.
- **Speed to Market**: 3–4 weeks for MVP (cert tracking + expiration alerts + simple training modules)
- **LTD Fit**: Strong — compliance anxiety is real; a one-time tool that keeps certifications organized and sends renewal alerts would sell well on AppSumo
- **Content Strategy**: "OSHA certification tracking software," "trades apprenticeship management," "technician onboarding software," "trade license renewal tracker," "[trade] training compliance"

- **Sources**:

  - https://www.dentaleconomics.com/practice/practice-management-software/article/55416933/can-agentic-ai-solve-dental-revenue-cycle-management-challenges (Dental Economics, Nov 2026)

  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/frontdesk-ai (Frontdesk AI — AI COO for practices)
- **Description**: Dental practice management software (Dentrix, Eaglesoft) is 20+ years old, on-prem, and deeply painful. AI-native challengers are moving from narrow point solutions (AI perio charting, AI scheduling) to full practice operating systems. Oryx Dental grew 402% 2023–2026 building the "Toast for dentistry." Dental RCM is a particularly hot sub-category — insurance claim rejection rates are high (dental has more complex coding than medical), and AI agents that auto-correct and resubmit rejections are showing 90–95% automated remittance matching. 70% of new Oryx customers sign up for their AI plan.
- **Target Industries**: Dental practices (solo, group, DSOs)
- **Market Timing**: Growing (AI features now expected; legacy incumbents being displaced)
- **Example Products**:
  - Oryx Dental (oryxdental.com) — full AI-native DPMS, bootstrapped, 2,000 practices
  - InsideDesk (insidedesk.com) — dental RCM for DSOs, $12.6M
  - Patientdesk AI (YC W26) — AI-native dental clinic OS
  - Zentist/Caviar — agentic claims review, 15 rejection types automated
- **White Space**: Solo dentist practices (1–3 chairs) — most AI dental tools are priced/structured for DSOs or groups. A $99/month AI dental assistant that handles just claim submission + patient reminders + schedule optimization for indie practices is wide open. Also: multi-language patient communication (Spanish, Mandarin, Portuguese) for practices in immigrant-dense markets.
- **Speed to Market**: 6–8 weeks for narrow MVP (AI claim rejection triage + resubmission workflow)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — dentists are high-income but risk-averse; prefer MRR but would try LTD for a narrow tool
- **Content Strategy**: "dental billing software," "dental practice management AI," "dental claims software," "dental RCM automation," "dentrix alternative"

## PE Rollup Software Consolidation (Multi-Location Home Services)

- **Category**: SMB Platform Consolidation / Franchise & Multi-Location Tools
- **Signal Strength**: Strong (structural, not cyclical)
- **Sources**:
  - https://capitalandclarity.substack.com/p/pe-rollups-are-acquiring-their-own (PE acquiring their own tech stacks, Mar 2026)
  - https://vistapointadvisors.com/news/fsm-software-ma-what-the-roll-up-wave-means-for-founders (FSM M&A analysis)
  - https://cdn.hl.com/pdf/2026/field-and-frontline-operations-management-software-mar2026.pdf (Houlihan Lokey field ops report, Mar 2026)
  - https://www.capstonepartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Capstone-Partners_Residential-Services_MA-Coverage-Report_October-2026.pdf (Capstone Partners residential services M&A, Oct 2026)
  - https://www.servicetitan.com/press/servicetitan-expands-into-enterprise-pest-control-lawn-care-and-pool-spa-markets (ServiceTitan Property Care, Oct 2026)
- **Description**: PE-backed home services rollups (buying up 10–100 local HVAC/plumbing/pest shops) now account for 60.5% of all residential services M&A. These platforms need standardized software across dozens of acquired brands without disrupting field workers. The trend: sponsors are now acquiring or building their own tech stacks to own the data layer. ServiceTitan expanded into pest/lawn/pool in Oct 2026. Probook's highest-value customer segment is explicitly PE-backed rollups. The FSM vendors that win multi-location rollup contracts become defensible and high-retention. Houlihan Lokey's investment bank report confirms sustained deal flow.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control, landscaping, pool & spa — specifically multi-location operators
- **Market Timing**: Growing/Maturing (the rollup wave is 5+ years old but software specifically for multi-brand ops is still early)
- **Example Products**:
  - ServiceTitan (servicetitan.com) — dominant platform, now on Nasdaq, expanding to property care
  - Probook (probook.ai) — targeting PE rollups specifically
  - Dalton Mills — "Replit for the trades," multi-location focus
- **White Space**: "Roll-up in a box" — a lightweight multi-location dashboard that sits on top of disparate FSMs (some locations on ServiceTitan, some on Jobber, some on spreadsheets) and normalizes KPIs without forcing migration. PE operators consistently mention that forcing system consolidation across acquisitions destroys value. A reporting/analytics layer that works with what shops already have is a real gap.
- **Speed to Market**: 6–8 weeks for normalized reporting MVP across 3–4 major FSM integrations
- **LTD Fit**: Low — PE operators buy on ROI and contracts; MRR model with per-location pricing
- **Content Strategy**: "multi-location home service software," "HVAC company rollup software," "home services KPI dashboard," "field service multi-branch management"

## Done-for-You AI Agent Setup for Local Businesses

- **Category**: Vertical AI / SMB Platform Consolidation
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://teapawt.org/gregisenberg/status/2111176488162582933 (Greg Isenberg — "done for you agent setup" trend, 2026)
  - https://unrollnow.com/status/2092665799332745220 (Greg Isenberg — "boring business + agents" thread, Aug 2026)
  - https://www.explained.consulting/ (Explained Consulting — fully managed AI for home services/SMBs)
  - https://aiagentsetup.co/ (AI Agent Setup Co. — white-glove AI setup for local businesses)
  - https://www.unrollnow.com/status/2104058582362661064 (HouseHackerJon — plumbing company owner building/open-sourcing AI automations)
- **Description**: A new services category is emerging: done-for-you AI setup for local businesses that want AI automation but can't/won't configure software. The pattern mirrors the early days of web design agencies — "we build it, we run it, you don't touch it." Greg Isenberg identified this as one of the most asymmetric opportunities: looks like an agency from the outside, runs like software on the inside. Priced $1,500–$4,000 setup + $500–$2,000/month managed. The open-source movement (HouseHackerJon releasing 9 automations for plumbers/HVAC) is feeding the ecosystem and creating playbooks others can sell as services.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, dental, legal, roofing, cleaning — any local service business
- **Market Timing**: Early (services model; will likely productize into SaaS over 2–3 years)
- **Example Products**:
  - Explained Consulting (explained.consulting) — fully managed AI for home services
  - AI Agent Setup Co. (aiagentsetup.co) — white-glove setup + ongoing management
- **White Space**: A productized "AI starter pack" for one specific trade vertical (e.g., HVAC only) with a fixed price, 48-hour setup, and clear ROI metrics. Sold as a subscription service with an affiliate channel through industry associations and trade shows. The productization of what today is custom agency work.
- **Speed to Market**: 2–3 weeks to build the productized template; then it's a sales/ops motion
- **LTD Fit**: Very Strong — local businesses are skeptical of ongoing fees; a one-time "set up my AI" product at $499–$999 would sell extremely well
- **Content Strategy**: "AI for HVAC companies," "AI automation for plumbing business," "AI receptionist for contractors," "missed call text back for trades"
