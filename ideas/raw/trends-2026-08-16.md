## Summary

**Top 3 Highest-Opportunity Trends:**

1. **Voice AI Answering for Trades and Home Services** — Avoca's $1B valuation proves the market, but the opportunity for smaller/cheaper solutions targeting the 1-10 tech contractor is wide open; immediate ROI story and low technical barrier make this the fastest path to $10K MRR.

2. **Refrigerant Compliance Tech for HVAC (AIM Act)** — Hard regulatory deadline (January 2026) creates mandatory software adoption with no dominant SMB solution yet; compliance-driven buying eliminates the need for a sales pitch and makes LTD sales straightforward.

3. **ServiceTitan Exodus — Affordable Field Service Management** — A confirmed wave of departures from overpriced FSM platforms is generating active purchase intent from contractors who are searching for alternatives right now; a niche-specific (one trade vertical) flat-rate FSM can capture this demand with relatively low technical complexity.
