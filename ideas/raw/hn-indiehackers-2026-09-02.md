## Documentorium — Document Engine for Tradespeople

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47540841
- **Additional Links**: https://documentorium.com
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Ask HN (response in tradespeople thread)
- **Engagement**: Thread with direct founder outreach to trades community
- **Revenue Data**: "Hundreds of paying users, almost all renewed their yearly" in a similar foreign market
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Trades (0–5 employee contractors, electricians, plumbers, HVAC)
- **Core Value Prop**: Simple document engine for creating estimates, quotes, and contracts — specifically for tradespeople who do it by hand or with clunky templates. Free without account; paid tier unlocks prefill, history, etc.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: No-BS, affordable doc tool for micro trade shops. The founder validated it first in another country (Eastern Europe), found hundreds of paying users, and is now moving to the English-speaking trades market. Existing tools (ServiceTitan, Jobber) are overpriced and over-featured for a 1-2 person shop.
- **Our Angle**: Bundle simple quote/estimate/contract generation with SMS delivery and basic job tracking — priced at $29–49/month flat, no per-user fees. AppSumo LTD potential is high: these users want to pay once and be done.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5

## Rudus — AI Estimating for Concrete Subcontractors (YC P26)

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48374528
- **Additional Links**: https://www.rudus.ai/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Launch HN
- **Engagement**: 41 points, 14 comments
- **Revenue Data**: YC-backed, early customers (live in production with paying concrete estimators)
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Concrete subcontractors (construction trades)
- **Core Value Prop**: Automates the concrete takeoff/estimation process — reads structural PDF blueprints, identifies every concrete element (footings, walls, columns, slabs), and generates 80–120 line-item cost estimates that previously took days by hand. The incumbent software hasn't been updated since 2020. Built for concrete subs only — not GCs, not generic.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Every other AI takeoff tool treats concrete as one checkbox. Estimating for concrete specifically requires specialized CV models that general tools don't have. The market: concrete subcontractors are on every commercial job site; most are 5–50 person shops drowning in manual Excel work.
- **Our Angle**: Rather than AI takeoff (capital intensive, complex CV), focus on a simpler adjacent problem: **concrete subcontractor proposal + invoice + change order automation**. Estimators have the numbers — they need to turn those numbers into professional client proposals and track payment. $99/month, works alongside their existing spreadsheet workflow.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (enterprise-ish pricing likely better)

- **Revenue Data**: 7-figure ARR (confirmed profitable); 100% uptime since 2016; sub-60-second support SLA
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Tax preparers and CPA firms using specific software (Drake, Lacerte, UltraTax, QuickBooks Desktop)
- **Core Value Prop**: Vertical cloud hosting specifically for tax software — handles IRS compliance (PTIN/WISP), knows Drake Tax crashes during tax season, staffed with tax software specialists not generic IT help. Serves 1,000 firms out of 90,000 possible = 0.4% market share.
