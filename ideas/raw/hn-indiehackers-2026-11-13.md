# HN & Indie Hackers Scan — 2026-11-13

## Summary

The dominant themes this cycle are (1) vertical field-service SaaS for sub-20-employee trades shops being radically underserved by both enterprise tools (too expensive/complex) and generic SaaS (not workflow-specific enough), and (2) AI-augmented back-office automation for boring service businesses — HVAC, pest control, cleaning, landscaping, property management — generating real investor interest and bootstrapped revenue. Multiple data points confirm $3M–$79K+ MRR is achievable in these niches with tiny teams. The clearest gap is the mid-market between "pen and paper" operators and enterprise FSM players like ServiceTitan.

## Pest Control Vertical SaaS (onhand.pro / HN thread)
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN / Discussion (448 upvotes, 182 comments — extremely high engagement)
- **Revenue Data**: One commenter disclosed $2M ARR for a pest control SaaS running "a few years." Market described as "$50K MRR achievable even with antiquated operators."
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Pest control / field service
- **Core Value Prop**: The original poster (tezclarke) went undercover as a pest control technician to understand the workflow before building vertical SaaS. Key pain: scheduling, CRM data entry done while driving, no digital job records.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Existing VSaaS (vertical SaaS) in pest control is described as "decent, cheap, ubiquitous" but has no AI layer. Manual data entry into CRM while driving is a safety hazard and accuracy problem. Smart traps/stations and upsell automation are unaddressed. The OP concluded the SaaS route is saturated and went the tech-enabled operator route instead — meaning the pure-SaaS wedge remains open.
- **Our Angle**: Voice-first CRM data entry for pest control technicians (dictate job notes hands-free while driving), combined with smart upsell triggers based on service history. A $79–$149/month tool that sits under existing VSaaS as a companion app. No need to replace ServicePro or PestRoutes — just add the AI layer.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (recurring value from ongoing AI usage; LTD possible at $299–$499)

## Crewbird — Field Service CRM + Google LSA Integration
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418273
- **Additional Links**: https://getcrewbird.com/
- **Platform**: HN (Ask HN: What are you working on? November 2026)
- **Type**: Builder showcase in monthly thread
- **Engagement**: Strong thread engagement — multiple people asking for access, newsletter signups, and two independent builders saying "I'm building this too"
- **Revenue Data**: Early stage, dad's carpet cleaning company at 3x ROAS proof-of-concept
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Carpet cleaning / field service SMBs using Google Local Service Ads
- **Core Value Prop**: FSM + CRM with direct Google LSA API integration to track ad spend ROI per job. Existing SaaS CRMs have poor Google Ads integration; the Google LSA dashboard itself lacks useful attribution.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The comment thread reveals a massive unmet need: "the space between a full blown ERP and an Excel spreadsheet is abysmal." Multiple people validated they need something simple that handles fleet, field crew, procurement, and AR/AP without 12 different tools. The Google LSA integration angle is a unique differentiator — no major FSM player has cracked this.
- **Our Angle**: Build the simplest possible FSM + CRM with Google LSA as the killer feature. Target cleaning, landscaping, and HVAC companies spending $1K+/month on Google LSAs who can't measure ROI. Charge $99–$199/month. The LSA integration alone justifies switching.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5

- **LTD Potential**: 3/5

## Field Manual Copilot for Small Plumbing/HVAC/Boiler Shops
- **Source**: https://www.startupheist.com/the-field-manual-copilot-79k-mrr-underneath-servicetitan/
- **Platform**: Independent research / market analysis (Startup Heist)
- **Type**: Opportunity analysis with revenue model ($79K MRR projection)
- **Revenue Data**: Model projects 300 shops at $79/month = $24K MRR; 1,000 shops = $79K MRR. No live product yet.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Small plumbing, HVAC, boiler, electrical shops (sub-20 technicians)
- **Core Value Prop**: Mobile RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) app — technician photographs equipment or enters model number, AI retrieves the manual, summarizes relevant pages, drafts parts notes and invoice language. Replaces the technician texting photos to the owner and calling distributors.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: ServiceTitan is priced out of reach for small shops. XOi covers enterprise field intelligence. 60,000+ sub-5-employee shops have no tool. The specific wedge: field technicians need to identify old/unfamiliar equipment quickly without calling the office. A photo-to-manual tool solves a daily pain point with zero workflow change required.
- **Our Angle**: Build the MVP as described: photo → model number → manual retrieval → invoice language. Price at $29–$79/month per shop. Use boiler and hydronics as a cold-weather wedge. The moat is the manual + parts database quality over time.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 (clear one-time value for shop owner; $299 LTD tier)

## Pierline — Vertical SaaS + Payments for Mobile Dog Groomers ($1M ARR)
- **Source**: https://www.thestackstories.com/founder-stories/bootstrapped-saas-to-1m-arr-in-fintech-embedded-payments
- **Platform**: The Stack Stories (indie founder publication)
- **Type**: Revenue milestone ($1M ARR, bootstrapped)
- **Revenue Data**: $1M ARR (March 2026) — ~50% SaaS ($69/month flat) + 50% payment spread (0.4% on top of Stripe). 1,140 customers, NRR 118%, monthly churn dropped from 4.1% to 1.2% after QuickBooks sync launch.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Mobile pet grooming (14,000 US operators)
- **Core Value Prop**: Route + payment app for mobile groomers. Schedule appointments, card-on-file payments at the truck, automated rebooking texts. The QuickBooks sync was the single feature that turned it into a "system of record" and crushed churn.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The playbook is directly replicable in adjacent mobile service verticals: mobile car detailing (~50,000 US operators), mobile pressure washing, mobile dog washing. Each has 10,000–50,000 small operators with no purpose-built software, paying by check or Venmo.
- **Our Angle**: Clone the Pierline model for mobile car detailing or mobile pressure washing. Same stack (Next.js, Postgres, Stripe Connect, Twilio), same embedded-payment revenue model. The QuickBooks sync is table stakes on day one given Pierline proved it's the churn killer. Launch at trade shows.
- **LTD Potential**: 2/5 (payment spread model requires subscription, not LTD — but high LTV)

## Avenue — Online Booking + AI Phone Agent for Trades (AU$85K MRR)
- **Source**: https://howtomakemoney.io/cases/avenue-tom-holliday-tradie-widget-55k/
- **Additional Links**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47138087 (Conduit AI — similar concept on HN)

- **Revenue Data**: AU$85,000/month (~US$55K) by September 2026. Standard $199/month, Premium $395/month, AI agent $399/month. 100% bootstrapped. Customer acquisition was door-to-door with iPad demos.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Auto mechanics, electricians, joiners, equipment hire (Australia) — directly translatable to US trades
- **Core Value Prop**: "Book Online" button on Google Business profile + website widget + SMS reminders + automatic Google review requests + two-way SMS + AI phone agent (Sophie). Makes it easy for customers of trades businesses to book without calling.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The US market equivalent is wide open. The HN thread for Conduit AI (similar concept) got minimal traction, suggesting the category hasn't been cracked in the US yet at scale. Avenue's door-to-door sales motion (walk into workshops with an iPad demo) is replicable anywhere.
- **Our Angle**: US-focused Avenue clone targeting independent auto repair shops, HVAC companies, and plumbers. Lead with the Google Business "Book Online" button as the hook (zero workflow change for the customer), then upsell SMS reminders and AI phone agent. $149–$299/month. The revenue data from Australia is proof this works.

## Bilingual SOP System for Trade Crews ($49.5K MRR model)
- **Source**: https://www.startupheist.com/the-99-qr-sticker-bilingual-sops-for-small-trade-crews/
- **Platform**: Startup Heist (opportunity analysis)
- **Type**: Market opportunity analysis with revenue model
- **Revenue Data**: Model: 500 customers at $99/month = $49,500 MRR. No live product confirmed.
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Construction, landscaping, roofing, any trade with English/Spanish mixed crews (803,000 US construction establishments)
- **Core Value Prop**: Voice-first SOP builder — owner speaks into phone for 3 minutes, AI transcribes + structures into bilingual (English/Spanish) checklist, publishes as a mobile page behind a QR code on the equipment. Crew scans QR, sees the procedure. No app install, no login.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Trainual ($249/month) is built for office teams. No product exists specifically for the owner-operator who has 5–30 mixed-language field workers and needs to document how to operate each piece of equipment. Zero documentation tooling is funded for this segment.
- **Our Angle**: Build the QR sticker SOP tool. Voice-to-SOP with English/Spanish output, $99/month per company (no per-seat), QR codes per equipment piece. Market through landscaping and roofing Facebook groups. The LTD angle: $299 lifetime for up to 50 SOPs — extremely sellable on AppSumo.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5

The same equipment-centric documentation workflow, branded for commercial refrigeration. Restaurants are penalized by health inspectors for missing maintenance records — compliance urgency = high willingness to pay. $149–$249/month per location.

## Latchel (YC W19) — Rental Property Maintenance Coordination
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=19370333
- **Additional Links**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35430744 (2023 update thread)
- **Platform**: HN Launch HN
- **Type**: YC launch + 2023 update
- **Engagement**: YC-backed, active since 2019, still growing in 2023
- **Revenue Data**: Not disclosed, but scaled to "hundreds of thousands of renters" on platform
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Property management / rental maintenance coordination
- **Core Value Prop**: 24/7 emergency maintenance answering + dispatching for property managers. $25 + $1/unit/month for emergency coordination; $25 + $10/unit for all maintenance. Combines human ops + software. Moves maintenance scheduling from days/weeks to minutes.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Latchel targets third-party property managers at the SMB level. Their constraint: low-margin property managers can't afford premium service. The unaddressed gap is single-family rental operators (the majority of US rentals) who have 1–10 properties and no maintenance coordination at all. A $29/month self-serve tool that handles tenant maintenance requests (AI triage, contractor dispatch, photo documentation) for the small landlord is completely unbuilt.
- **Our Angle**: Build "Latchel Lite" — a self-serve maintenance request portal for small landlords (1–10 units). Tenant submits request via SMS or web link, AI categorizes urgency, landlord approves/dispatches from phone, contractor gets job details automatically. $29–$49/month per 10 units. Huge LTD potential for landlords who hate recurring fees.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5
