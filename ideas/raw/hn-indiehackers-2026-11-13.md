# HN & Indie Hackers Scan — 2026-11-13

## Summary

The dominant themes this cycle are (1) vertical field-service SaaS for sub-20-employee trades shops being radically underserved by both enterprise tools (too expensive/complex) and generic SaaS (not workflow-specific enough), and (2) AI-augmented back-office automation for boring service businesses — HVAC, pest control, cleaning, landscaping, property management — generating real investor interest and bootstrapped revenue. Multiple data points confirm $3M–$79K+ MRR is achievable in these niches with tiny teams. The clearest gap is the mid-market between "pen and paper" operators and enterprise FSM players like ServiceTitan.

## Pest Control Vertical SaaS (onhand.pro / HN thread)
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN / Discussion
- **Engagement**: 448 upvotes, 182 comments — extremely high engagement
- **Revenue Data**: One commenter disclosed $2M ARR for a pest control SaaS running "a few years." Market described as "$50K MRR achievable even with antiquated operators."
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Pest control / field service
- **Core Value Prop**: The original poster went undercover as a pest control technician to understand the workflow before building. Key pain: scheduling, CRM data entry done while driving, no digital job records.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Existing VSaaS in pest control is "decent, cheap, ubiquitous" but has no AI layer. Manual data entry into CRM while driving is a safety hazard. Smart traps/stations and upsell automation are unaddressed. The OP concluded SaaS is saturated and went the tech-enabled operator route — meaning the pure-SaaS AI wedge remains open.
- **Our Angle**: Voice-first CRM data entry for pest control technicians (dictate job notes hands-free while driving), combined with smart upsell triggers based on service history. A $79–$149/month tool that sits under existing VSaaS as a companion app.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5

## Crewbird — Field Service CRM + Google LSA Integration
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418273
- **Additional Links**: https://getcrewbird.com/
- **Platform**: HN (Ask HN: What are you working on? November 2026)
- **Type**: Builder showcase in monthly thread
- **Engagement**: Strong thread validation — multiple people asking for access, newsletter signups, two independent builders saying "I'm building this too"
- **Revenue Data**: Early stage, proof-of-concept (dad's carpet cleaning company at 3x ROAS)
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Carpet cleaning / field service SMBs using Google Local Service Ads
- **Core Value Prop**: FSM + CRM with direct Google LSA API integration to track ad spend ROI per job. Existing SaaS CRMs have poor Google Ads integration; the LSA dashboard itself lacks useful attribution.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The comment thread reveals massive unmet need: "the space between a full blown ERP and an Excel spreadsheet is abysmal." Multiple commenters validated they need something simple that handles fleet, field crew, and AR/AP without 12 different tools. The Google LSA integration angle is a unique differentiator — no major FSM player has cracked this.
- **Our Angle**: Build the simplest possible FSM + CRM with Google LSA as the killer feature. Target cleaning/landscaping/HVAC companies spending $1K+/month on LSAs who can't measure ROI. Charge $99–$199/month.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5

## WorkHero — AI Back Office for Small HVAC Contractors
- **Source**: https://www.workhero.pro/blog/workhero-raises-5m-seed-round
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: HN hiring threads + press
- **Type**: Funded startup — $5M seed (Navitas Capital, ex-ServiceTitan angel)
- **Revenue Data**: Pre-revenue at seed; investor conviction validates the market
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: HVAC contractors under 20 employees
- **Core Value Prop**: AI agents + human office managers handle permits, rebates, warranties, pricebook creation, billing, collections — integrating with Jobber, HouseCall Pro, QuickBooks, ServiceTitan. No new software to learn.

- **Our Angle**: Pure-SaaS rebate + warranty + permit tracker for small HVAC shops at $49–$79/month. WorkHero validates the customer; we undercut on price by removing the human layer.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5

## Field Manual Copilot for Small Plumbing/HVAC/Boiler Shops
- **Source**: https://www.startupheist.com/the-field-manual-copilot-79k-mrr-underneath-servicetitan/
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Startup Heist (opportunity research publication)
- **Type**: Opportunity analysis with revenue model
- **Revenue Data**: Model projects 300 shops at $79/month = $24K MRR; 1,000 shops = $79K MRR. No live product confirmed yet.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Small plumbing, HVAC, boiler, electrical shops (sub-20 technicians)
- **Core Value Prop**: Mobile RAG app — technician photographs equipment or enters model number, AI retrieves the manual, summarizes relevant pages, drafts parts notes and invoice language. Replaces the technician texting photos to the owner and calling distributors.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: 60,000+ sub-5-employee shops have no tool for this. The specific wedge: field technicians need to identify old/unfamiliar equipment quickly. A photo-to-manual tool solves a daily pain with zero workflow change.
- **Our Angle**: Build the MVP: photo → model number → manual retrieval → invoice language. $29–$79/month per shop. Use boiler/hydronics as cold-weather wedge state by state.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5

## Pierline — Vertical SaaS + Payments for Mobile Dog Groomers ($1M ARR)
- **Source**: https://www.thestackstories.com/founder-stories/bootstrapped-saas-to-1m-arr-in-fintech-embedded-payments
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: The Stack Stories (indie founder publication)
- **Type**: Revenue milestone ($1M ARR, bootstrapped)
- **Revenue Data**: $1M ARR (March 2026). ~50% SaaS ($69/month) + 50% payment spread (0.4% on Stripe). 1,140 customers, NRR 118%, monthly churn dropped from 4.1% to 1.2% after QuickBooks sync.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Mobile pet grooming (14,000 US operators)
- **Core Value Prop**: Route + payment app. Schedule appointments, card-on-file at the truck, automated rebooking texts. QuickBooks sync was the churn-killing feature — made it the system of record.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The playbook is directly replicable in adjacent mobile service verticals: mobile car detailing (~50,000 US operators), mobile pressure washing, mobile windshield repair. Same structure: 10,000–50,000 small operators, paying by check or Venmo, no purpose-built software.
- **Our Angle**: Clone the Pierline model for mobile car detailing or mobile pressure washing. Same stack, same embedded-payment revenue model, QuickBooks sync on day one.
- **LTD Potential**: 2/5 (payment spread model requires subscription — but high LTV)

## Avenue — Online Booking + AI Phone Agent for Trades (AU$85K MRR)
- **Source**: https://howtomakemoney.io/cases/avenue-tom-holliday-tradie-widget-55k/
- **Additional Links**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47138087 (Conduit AI — similar concept)
- **Platform**: Starter Story / press
- **Type**: Revenue milestone (bootstrapped, ~1 year to AU$85K/month)
- **Revenue Data**: AU$85,000/month (~US$55K) September 2026. Standard $199/month, Premium $395/month, AI agent $399/month. 100% bootstrapped. Customer acquisition was door-to-door with iPad demos.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Auto mechanics, electricians, joiners, equipment hire (Australia) — directly translatable to US trades
- **Core Value Prop**: "Book Online" button on Google Business profile + website widget + SMS reminders + automatic Google review requests + two-way SMS + AI phone agent. Makes it easy for trade business customers to book without calling.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The US market equivalent is wide open. The HN Conduit AI thread (similar concept) got minimal traction, suggesting it hasn't been cracked in the US at scale. Avenue's door-to-door sales motion is replicable anywhere.
- **Our Angle**: US-focused Avenue clone targeting independent auto repair shops, HVAC companies, and plumbers. Lead with the Google Business "Book Online" button as hook, then upsell SMS reminders and AI phone agent. $149–$299/month. Australia proved the model.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5

## Bilingual SOP System for Trade Crews ($49.5K MRR modeled)

- **Additional Links**: N/A

- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5

- **Target Industry**: Property management / rental maintenance coordination
- **Core Value Prop**: 24/7 emergency maintenance answering + dispatching. $25 + $1/unit/month emergency; $25 + $10/unit all maintenance. Combines human ops + software. Moves scheduling from days/weeks to minutes.

## Key Takeaways

- Three findings have the strongest combination of live revenue validation + clear gap: **Pest control AI layer** (448-vote HN thread + $2M ARR comp), **Avenue US equivalent** (AU$85K MRR proven in Australia), and **Latchel Lite for small landlords** (YC-validated market, self-serve tier unbuilt).
- The **bilingual SOP system** scores 5/5 on LTD Potential — extremely sellable on AppSumo, zero competition in the segment, voice-first simplicity.
- **Pierline** provides the clearest blueprint for a replicable vertical payments + scheduling play in adjacent mobile service verticals (car detailing, pressure washing).
- WorkHero's $5M seed raise is the strongest investor signal that small HVAC back-office automation has a large market; the pure-SaaS version of their offering remains unbuilt at accessible price points.
