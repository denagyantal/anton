- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.expirationreminder.com/blog/top-expiration-tracking-tools-compliance
  - https://expiryedge.com/compare/best-license-renewal-tracking-software/
  - https://licenseroadmap.com/blog/best-contractor-license-tracking-software

  - https://www.mycarrollcountynews.com/online_features/press_releases/article_45ab0151-9188-5ee0-b0fa-adf12b58e710.html
- **Description**: Licensed trades (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, general contractors) and their employees must track licenses and continuing education across all 50 states, each with different renewal windows, CE requirements, and forms. A missed renewal = can't legally work = lost revenue. Micro SaaS estimate: $7K–$18K MRR from a low-code app with state rules database and automated reminders. The market has some players (Expiration Reminder, ExpiryEdge, Nationwide Contractor Licensing) but no dominant purpose-built solution for the trades specifically.
- **Target Industries**: Licensed trades (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, roofing), construction GCs, pest control, home inspection
- **Market Timing**: Early-Growing — awareness is rising, no dominant player yet
- **Example Products**:
  - Expiration Reminder: https://www.expirationreminder.com/
  - ExpiryEdge: https://expiryedge.com/
  - Briely.ai: https://briely.ai/
  - Nationwide Contractor Licensing: https://www.mycarrollcountynews.com/online_features/press_releases/article_45ab0151-9188-5ee0-b0fa-adf12b58e710.html
- **White Space**: None of the existing tools have deep state-by-state CE/license rules baked in — they're generic expiration trackers. A trades-specific tool with: (a) pre-loaded state rules by trade type, (b) CE course recommendations/links, (c) team dashboard for multi-crew operations, (d) insurance certificate tracking in same dashboard — would be a clear winner.
- **Speed to Market**: 2–3 weeks for a focused MVP (license + CE tracking with email reminders, state rules for top 10 states)
- **LTD Fit**: Excellent — "set it and forget it" compliance tool is a natural LTD product. $59–$99 one-time for solo contractors, $149+ for team plans
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor license renewal reminder", "HVAC CE tracking software", "electrical license expiration tracker", "[state] contractor license requirements"

## Restaurant Financial Reconciliation & Multi-Platform Accounting

- **Category**: Paper-to-Digital / Legacy Software Disruption
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.deliverguard.io/research/restaurant-software-pain-points-2026
  - https://www.nory.ai/blog/buyers-guide-to-restaurant-tech-2026
  - https://www.fooddocs.com/post/restaurant-management-software
- **Description**: Restaurants in 2026 run on 4–7 disconnected systems: POS (e.g., Square, Toast), delivery platforms (DoorDash, Uber Eats, GrubHub), payment processor, accounting software (QuickBooks/Xero), and bank. None were designed to talk to each other. The result: money leaks between systems due to mismatched settlement timelines, commission clawbacks, and manual reconciliation. Operators consistently rate "integration ecosystem" as their #1 pain point. This is a real, recurring, money-adjacent problem for every multi-channel restaurant.
- **Target Industries**: Independent restaurants, QSR franchises, ghost kitchens, multi-location food chains
- **Market Timing**: Growing — delivery platform proliferation has made the reconciliation problem worse since 2023
- **Example Products**:
  - DeliverGuard: https://www.deliverguard.io/ (delivery reconciliation)
  - Nory: https://www.nory.ai/ (AI restaurant ops)
  - MarketMan (inventory/cost management)
- **White Space**: Automated daily P&L reconciliation pulling from POS + delivery + bank — one dashboard showing "here's where your $3,247 went today." Most tools solve one piece; nobody owns the unified financial picture for independent restaurants at an affordable price ($49–$99/mo).
- **Speed to Market**: 4–6 weeks for MVP with Toast + Square + DoorDash + Uber Eats integrations
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — SaaS subscription ($49–$79/mo) may be a stronger play, but LTD launch to validate demand makes sense ($199–$299)
- **Content Strategy**: "restaurant delivery reconciliation", "Toast QuickBooks integration", "DoorDash accounting software", "restaurant daily P&L dashboard"
