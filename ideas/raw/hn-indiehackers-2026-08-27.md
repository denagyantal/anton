## Craftplan – Micro-ERP for Small-Scale Manufacturers (Bakeries, Breweries, Soap Makers)
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46847690
- **Additional Links**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46869383 | https://puemos.github.io/craftplan
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: Top 10 highest-rated Show HN for the week of Feb 7 2026; 22 points on secondary submission
- **Revenue Data**: Free / open-source (AGPLv3); not yet monetized — clear gap for a paid hosted tier
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Small-batch manufacturing — bakeries, breweries, soap makers, candle makers, food producers
- **Core Value Prop**: Recipes with versioned BOMs + automatic cost rollups, lot-traceable inventory, allergen flags, demand forecasting, order scheduling, production batches, purchase orders, basic CRM. Self-hostable via Docker.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Existing ERP software (QuickBooks, Odoo, generic inventory tools) is either too expensive or too generic for tiny manufacturers. The creator built it free because nothing affordable existed. No paid SaaS player owns this sub-segment.
- **Our Angle**: Launch as a $39–$79/month hosted SaaS of Craftplan (or a clean-room equivalent). Add food-safety compliance exports (FDA lot traceability, allergen reports) and direct Shopify/Etsy order sync. Market through r/ArtisanFoods, food entrepreneur Facebook groups, and craft brewery associations. LTD on AppSumo at $99–$149 to build initial base.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5

- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: Show HN discussion; product battle-tested at large wind/solar plants
- **Revenue Data**: Free tier; paid plans exist but limited public data
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Facilities management — office buildings, factories, restaurants, solar/wind plants, any asset-heavy operation

## Hemut – AI Operating System for Trucking Back Office
- **Source**: https://yespress.io/hemut-yc-x25
- **Additional Links**: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hemut | https://fondo.com/blog/hemut-launches
- **Platform**: HN / YC (YC Spring 2025)
- **Type**: YC launch + press coverage
- **Engagement**: 500 carriers on platform; ~$1M booked revenue; team grew 4 → 20; $1.8M seed at ~$30M valuation
- **Revenue Data**: ~$1M booked revenue; approaching PMF in mid-market trucking
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Trucking / freight — mid-sized carriers and freight brokers, 10–100 trucks
- **Core Value Prop**: AI-native TMS that replaces the dispatcher back office: AI voice agents for check calls and booking, document parsing (rate cons, BOLs, invoices), load sourcing, dispatch, real-time tracking, accounting — all in one platform replacing legacy TMS + spreadsheets + phone.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: $875B US trucking market running on 2004-era software. Dispatchers manually retype the same load data into 3 systems. Mid-market fleets (dozens of trucks) can't afford custom tooling but are too complex for generic tools. Hemut is attacking this — but regional niches (refrigerated freight, flatbed, specific lanes) remain open for specialized competitors.
- **Our Angle**: Rather than full TMS (Hemut's territory), build a specific module: "AI freight invoice audit" (3–5% error rate on a $900B market = billions in billing errors). A standalone tool that audits carrier invoices against rate confirmations, flags discrepancies, and generates dispute documentation. $99–$299/month for freight brokers handling 50–500 loads/week. Strong LTD potential.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5
