## 1. Prelay — Weather-Based Work Planning for Outdoor Contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 2 points, recent
- **Revenue Data**: Pre-revenue, just launched
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Landscaping, roofing, outdoor contracting (12 trades covered)
- **Core Value Prop**: Monitors entire job schedule and determines weather suitability for 62 outdoor activities; custom ground-wetness model for soil conditions. Sends rescheduling alerts before the owner has to decide at 4am.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Current practice: owners check a phone weather app manually for every job site at 4am. If crews aren't told before 6am, the business pays them anyway even if they can't work. No existing tool links weather data to job schedules and trades-specific activities.
- **Our Angle**: Build into a lightweight field service scheduler add-on — weather suitability is the hook, but the real product is weather-triggered crew dispatch notifications and calendar reschedule workflows.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — outdoor contractors have simple, recurring pain; high LTD willingness

## 3. Zirco.ai — AI Employee for Dental Front Desk Operations

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN / product launch
- **Engagement**: 1 point, 3 comments
- **Revenue Data**: Beta stage, 30+ dental practices interviewed
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices
- **Core Value Prop**: AI handles full dental front desk — insurance verification (logs into 10+ carrier portals via Playwright), appointment booking into Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft, SMS/email reminders, inbound call handling via voice AI (Vapi.ai).
