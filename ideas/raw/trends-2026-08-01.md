## Trade License & Credential Compliance Tracking

- **Category**: Compliance & Regulation Tech
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://ustechautomations.com/resources/blog/automate-best-certification-renewal-software-for-electrical-contractors-2026
  - https://licenseroadmap.com/blog/best-contractor-license-tracking-software

  - https://www.servicetitan.com/licensing/hvac/new-york
  - https://www.workyard.com/construction-management/hvac-license
- **Description**: Trade contractors (HVAC, electrical, plumbing) face a compliance nightmare: multi-state licenses, OSHA certifications, arc flash cards, manufacturer certs — all expiring on different cycles, all tracked in spreadsheets or not at all. License lapse fines average $2,500–$8,000 per jurisdiction. Some platforms (CrewSense, Competency Manager) address this but the market is fragmented and many tools are clunky enterprise plays. The real gap is a lightweight tool for 5–50 employee trade companies that ties license status directly to dispatch eligibility.
- **Target Industries**: Electrical contractors, HVAC, plumbing, general contractors, roofing
- **Market Timing**: Early (compliance pain is high; software solutions are scattered and overpriced)
- **Example Products**:
  - Competency Manager — (niche enterprise tool)
  - CrewSense — https://crewsense.com (scheduling + credentialing, trades-focused)
  - LicenseRoadmap — https://licenseroadmap.com (tracking tool)
- **White Space**: Dead-simple license tracker: employee → certifications → expiry dates → automated alerts at 90/60/30/7 days → block dispatch if lapsed. No enterprise bloat. Targeted at 5–50 employee trade businesses who can't afford ServiceTitan.
- **Speed to Market**: 2–3 weeks for MVP (CRUD + alert emails/SMS)
- **LTD Fit**: Strong — compliance tools are "must-have" purchases; $99–$199 LTD is a no-brainer vs. $2,500 fine
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor license tracking software", "HVAC license renewal tracker", "employee certification management trades"
