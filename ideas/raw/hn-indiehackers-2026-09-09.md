## Automated Follow-Up System for Service Businesses (CraftBoop)

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/craftboop-built-automated-follow-ups-for-service-businesses-just-launched-looking-for-feedback-5aa58e1c39
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Launch post / feedback request
- **Engagement**: Posted April 2026, community discussion
- **Revenue Data**: $29/mo, 14-day free trial (no CC), early users only — just launched
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Trades (plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, painters, landscapers)
- **Core Value Prop**: After each job, sends automated 5-email sequence — thank you, review request, check-in, rebooking reminder, referral ask. Add the customer once, tool handles the rest.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Service businesses do great work but NEVER follow up. No Google review asks, no rebooking reminders, no referral requests. All manual or never happens. Problem explicitly validated by founder doing direct customer interviews.
- **Our Angle**: CraftBoop is email-only and just launched. Add SMS, WhatsApp, automate Google Review requests with direct links (review generation is a premium service at $200-500/mo elsewhere), and integrate with existing job management tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro). Could also add a "no-show charge" reminder flow.
- **LTD Potential**: 4 — simple enough to explain, service businesses buy LTDs on AppSumo all the time

## AI Voice Receptionist for Local Service Businesses

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/built-an-ai-voice-receptionist-for-my-dads-dental-clinic-40-more-bookings-80e2c4a830
- **Additional Links**:
  - https://www.indiehackers.com/post/vocea-ai-voice-assistant-for-service-providers-e071f2997f
  - https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-profitable-ai-voice-saas-agency-300-800-mrr-per-client-frAbgO1yQMfHAzWbL5fvQJ
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Launch + revenue milestone + agency model post
- **Engagement**: Multiple posts, 9/12 validated businesses said yes in survey
- **Revenue Data**: $300-800 MRR per client (agency model); dental clinic saw 40% more bookings
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Dental offices, HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, law firms, salons
- **Core Value Prop**: 40% of calls to local service businesses come after-hours and mostly don't call back — each missed call is $3-5k in lost revenue for a dental clinic. AI answers 24/7, books appointments, handles common questions.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Multiple builders validating this same model (AnveVoice, Vocea, Callin.io-based agencies) — market is getting validated fast. Current tools are generic; no one has built deeply vertical for a single trade (e.g., "HVAC-specific AI receptionist" that knows how to triage service calls, schedule urgency, handle warranty questions).
- **Our Angle**: Go deep on one vertical (HVAC or dental). Build a specialized AI that knows the industry vocabulary, triage logic, booking flows, and pricing structures. Charge $400-600/mo with setup fee. Can white-label to MSPs who serve these industries.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 — recurring nature and per-call costs make LTD tricky, but possible with usage caps

## Vertical Cloud Hosting for Regulated Professions

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-we-built-a-profitable-paas-by-serving-only-0-4-of-us-businesses-4927bc5f68
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Case study — 10 years, 0.4% market share, profitable
- **Engagement**: High (featured interview)
- **Revenue Data**: Profitable bootstrapped cloud platform (Verito Technologies), 1,000 customers from 90,000 target (tax/accounting firms), strong retention, year-over-year growth
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Tax preparers, CPA firms, accounting offices → extendable to dental, law, medical
- **Core Value Prop**: Cloud hosting purpose-built for tax software (Drake, UltraTax CS) with IRS-compliant security, sub-60-second support during tax season, built-in WISP compliance. Generic providers can't touch this.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: After 10 years they still have 0.4% penetration. The model works but is hard to replicate for existing accounting software cloud hosting. HOWEVER — the pattern applies to many other professional verticals that use specialized software: dental (Eaglesoft, Dentrix), veterinary (AVImark, Cornerstone), funeral homes (FrontRunner), auto repair (Mitchell1, Shop-Ware). Each of those verticals has ~100k+ small businesses, specific software, compliance requirements, and almost no specialized cloud hosting.
- **Our Angle**: Clone the Verito model for dental office cloud hosting (HIPAA-compliant, Dentrix/Eaglesoft optimized, dental-support-team-native). $200-400/mo per practice. 60k dental practices in the US alone.
- **LTD Potential**: 1 — infrastructure SaaS doesn't fit LTD model

## Bakery / Small-Batch Food Production Management (Craftplan)

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46847690
- **Additional Links**: https://github.com/puemos/craftplan
- **Platform**: Hacker News
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 577 points, 167 comments — #3 Show HN of February 2026
- **Revenue Data**: Open source, no revenue — built for founder's wife's bakery
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Artisan bakeries, food production, catering, meal prep businesses
- **Core Value Prop**: Recipe management (versioned BOMs with cost rollups), inventory (lot traceability, demand forecasting, allergen tracking), orders, production batch planning, purchasing — all for small food production businesses.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: 577 HN points is a MASSIVE signal. The comments showed overwhelming demand — bakers, caterers, meal-prep founders all saying "I need this." Existing tools (MarketMan, BlueCart) are expensive ($300-500/mo) and built for restaurants, not production businesses.
- **Our Angle**: Take Craftplan's open-source core, add a SaaS wrapper with hosted deployment, add customer-facing order portal, Shopify/Etsy integration for cottage food businesses, and compliance-focused features (nutrition labels, allergen declarations). Price at $49-79/mo. "The software your Craftplan can't host itself."
- **LTD Potential**: 5 — cottage food / small bakery community LOVES LTD deals, highly price-sensitive, high trust through AppSumo

## Service Business Payment & No-Show Prevention (HandyPay)

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/the-boring-way-we-reached-1k-mrr-in-less-than-60-days-61739ba3d9
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Revenue milestone
- **Engagement**: Community post
- **Revenue Data**: $1k MRR in under 60 days — spas, salons, service businesses
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Spas, salons, service businesses (beauty, wellness, trades)
- **Core Value Prop**: Collect deposits upfront, reduce no-shows, get paid fast. Founder literally walked into service businesses and set them up on the spot.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: HandyPay is early and narrow (payment collection). But the no-show problem extends everywhere in service businesses — repair shops, HVAC tune-ups, dog grooming, dental hygiene appointments. The founder's go-to-market (direct, in-person, WhatsApp) shows that these businesses need human-touch sales AND a simple product. - **Target Industry**: HVAC, law firms, roofing, veterinary, any local service business
- **Core Value Prop**: Autonomous pipeline: finds leads via Google Places, scores them on 4 pillars (reputation, lead capture, after-hours coverage, website health), generates personalized cold pitch with their specific grade and estimated annual revenue leakage, takes payment.

## Small Farm / Pasture Livestock Management (PastureEgg)

- **Additional Links**: https://www.pasturegg.com
- **Platform**: Hacker News
- **Type**: Side project mention in Ask HN thread
- **Engagement**: Community responses positive ("Vertical SaaS for poultry producers not something I expected!")
- **Revenue Data**: Pre-revenue, solo founder, built for personal use first
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Pasture poultry farms, small livestock operations, small-scale agriculture
- **Core Value Prop**: Track flocks, manage records, organize labor for pasture poultry producers — replacing Google Sheets that are easy to forget to update.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Almost no software serves small-scale poultry and livestock operations (under 10k birds/head). The existing players (FarmersEdge, Granular) target large commodity operations.
