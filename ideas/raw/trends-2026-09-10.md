## Vertical SaaS Market Context (2026)

- **Overall funding:** $11.8B raised in vertical SaaS through July 2026 (954 rounds), down 25% from 2025 — but boring-industry verticals are the standout performers
- **Highest-signal niche pattern of 2026:** "boring industry" vertical SaaS — HVAC management, pest control scheduling, roofing PM, field service automation — consistently low competition + high willingness to pay
- **Why boring beats glamorous:** No alternative to switch to → better Net Revenue Retention. Trades owners pay and stay.
- **The winning formula (per SMB vertical SaaS playbook):** Own the SMB's system of record → layer embedded payments (2.6–3.5% take) → then lending → then AI agents
- **ServiceTitan validation:** IPO'd on NASDAQ with $772M implied ARR, 24% YoY growth, ~10,000 customers paying ~$78K/year. Shows the ceiling for vertical SaaS in boring industries.
