## FieldFlow — $5/mo Field Service Tool for Solo Contractors
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47294092
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Hacker News
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 1 point (very new, submitted ~3 months ago)
- **Revenue Data**: None disclosed — priced at $5/month flat, no per-user fees
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Residential trades — carpentry, plumbing, electrical, HVAC (solo operators)
- **Core Value Prop**: Built by a solo carpenter who was paying $150+/month for Jobber and hated the bloat. Offers invoicing, scheduling, client management for $5/month flat — forever. "I use it myself daily. That's the only quality filter I trust."

- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — this is an AppSumo natural; solo tradespeople buy LTDs, field service is sticky

## FleetFix — Free Trucking Fleet Management for Small Fleets
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46468469
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Hacker News
- **Type**: Show HN (January 2, 2026)
- **Engagement**: Low upvotes — early launch
- **Revenue Data**: Free tier currently — monetization model not disclosed
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Small fleet trucking / logistics (under 20 trucks)
- **Core Value Prop**: "Built specifically for smaller fleet operations that are underserved by large fleet systems like Fleetio or Samsara." Features: vehicle management, maintenance logs, parts tracking, downtime analytics, maintenance calendar, CSV export. Targets owner-operators and small regional trucking companies priced out of Fleetio ($4-10/vehicle/month × 100+ vehicles = $400-1,000+/month minimum).
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Fleetio and Samsara are enterprise. A 5-truck owner-operator has very different needs: maintenance reminders, insurance documentation, driver assignment, DOT compliance logs. No dominant indie tool exists at the $29-79/month range for <20 vehicles.
- **Our Angle**: Add DOT compliance checklists, driver HOS (hours-of-service) logging, IFTA fuel tax tracking — the things small trucking companies actually get fined for. This is a recurring compliance-driven subscription that churn-proofs itself (fear of DOT audit).
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 — trucking LTDs are harder to sell; monthly recurring is more natural here

## Indie Hackers: 39,000 Software Complaint Analysis — 6 Boring Industry Gaps
- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-analyzed-39-000-software-complaints-the-best-micro-saas-gaps-are-all-in-boring-industries-801c41685b
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Data-driven research post (July 2026)
- **Engagement**: High — prominent featured post
- **Revenue Data**: N/A (research, not product)
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Multiple boring verticals
- **Core Value Prop**: Author analyzed 39,000+ negative reviews from Capterra, G2, Reddit, and Upwork. Filtered by: severity 3.5+/5, 3+ companies reporting same issue, no focused micro-SaaS solving it below $500/month enterprise. Six industries survived:

  1. **Construction/field service** — "Poor mobile functionality" (4.0/5 severity, 5 companies). Build: photo uploads with GPS tagging, form-based inspections, instant sync to office. $29/user/month to 5-20 field worker subcontractors.
  2. **HR/payroll** — "Inefficient template building" (4.5/5 severity, 6 companies). Build: HR template builder with drag-and-drop, smart auto-fill, state compliance inserts. $99-299/month.
  3. **Legal/contract** — "Time-consuming contract drafting" (Upwork frequency 6). Build: clause library + assembly for solo attorneys and small firms. $79-149/month/attorney.
  4. **Logistics/inventory** — "Slow data loading" (4.5/5 severity). Build: lightweight fast inventory lookup with barcode scan. $199/month per location.
  5. **Education/training** — "High learning curve" for LMS (4.0/5 severity). Build: upload-video-add-quiz-share-link simplicity. $49-99/month.
  6. **Accounting** — "Integration challenges" (4.0/5 severity). Build: Zapier for accounting workflows with real data validation. $149-299/month per firm.

- **Gap/Opportunity**: Construction/field service mobile gap (#1) is most actionable for a bootstrapped founder — direct customer access via trade shows, LinkedIn, Reddit. The ROI pitch: field service manager saves 5 hrs/week at $75/hr = $1,500/month recovered; charging $149/month is a rounding error.
- **Our Angle**: Mobile field reporting app with GPS photo capture — $29/user/month. Target subcontractors with 5-20 workers. Vertical: start with home inspection firms or HVAC companies doing installations.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5

## Key Takeaways

**1. The trades/field service market is venture-validated in 2026.** Avoca ($125M at $1B, April 2026) and Probook ($40M from a16z/Sequoia, June 2026) both raised this year for HVAC/plumbing/electrical software. This is the single strongest market validation signal — VC money flows to where the pain is. The bootstrappable opportunity is the 1-5 person shop that Avoca and Probook explicitly do not serve.

**2. Pricing gap is enormous.** ServiceTitan: $125+/user/month. Jobber: $39+/user/month. Tradify: $47+/user/month. The $5-39/month tier is wide open, validated by FieldFlow's existence and the IH 39,000 complaints analysis showing "poor mobile functionality" as the #1 field service pain point.

**3. Cleaning companies + lawn care are the most LTD-friendly.** Recurring, predictable revenue model (weekly/biweekly service) = perfect for SaaS. Both communities are active on Facebook Groups and Reddit. AppSumo has proven dental and ops buyers; cleaning/lawn are direct adjacencies.

**4. The "Ask HN: What Industries Are Underserved?" thread (192 points, 169 comments)** from 2020 remains one of the best demand signal documents in the boring SaaS space. The top-mentioned industries are still largely unaddressed at the indie hacker price point 6 years later.

**5. BuildOps' story** — founded 2018, VCs laughed, now $100M ARR — is the playbook. Commercial contracting was the proof. Residential trades and sub-10-employee shops are the 2026 version of that same opportunity.
