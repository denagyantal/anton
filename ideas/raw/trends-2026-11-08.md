## Highest Opportunity Ideas for Solo Builders / Small Teams

1. **AI Voice Receptionist for a Single Trade (niche)** — HVAC-only or pest control-only, regional model, $199-$399/mo. Bootstrappable. Proven: existing bootstrap doing $979K TTM.

2. **Voice-to-Quote Mobile App for Contractors** — Talk your estimate on-site → pulls your rates → sends professional proposal instantly. SMASH is early. Market wide open for trade-specific versions.

3. **Chemical Compliance Tracking for Lawn Care / Pest Control** — Most states require application logs; most shops use paper. EarthaPro includes it but it's buried. A standalone compliance SaaS could be a $29-$59/mo wedge into a bigger platform.

4. **Technician Performance Pay Templates for Small Shops** — ShareWillow is enterprise-ish (connects to ServiceTitan). There's a gap for a simpler tool at $49-$99/mo that helps a 3-5 tech shop set up incentive pay without expensive integrations.

5. **Pricebook Standardization Tool for PE-Acquired Home Service Companies** — ServiceTitan consultants charge $5K-$20K to standardize pricebooks post-acquisition. A productized version of that could be $500-$2,000/mo for PE firms with 2-10 portfolio companies.
