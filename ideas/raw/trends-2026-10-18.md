## AI Voice Agents for Trades (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing)

- **Category**: Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.idlen.io/news/avoca-ai-1-billion-valuation-kleiner-perkins-services-economy-voice-agents-april-2026/
  - https://aiautomationglobal.com/blog/avoca-ai-voice-agent-trades-unicorn-2026
  - https://contractortoolstack.com/software/avoca-ai/
  - https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/avoca-ai/
  - https://serviceagent.ai/blogs/avoca-ai-pricing/
- **Description**: Avoca AI raised $125M at a $1B valuation (April 2026) to automate phone handling for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, and pest control — answering inbound calls 24/7, qualifying leads, and booking appointments directly into scheduling software. This is the most heavily funded sub-segment in boring-business SaaS this year. The U.S. HVAC market alone is worth $50B projected to hit $75B by 2032. Competing voice AI platforms for trades include Sameday, Rosie, Dialzara, My AI Front Desk, and Smith.ai.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, lawn care
- **Market Timing**: Growing (recent unicorn signals category formation, but <5% penetration among SMBs)
- **Example Products**:
  - Avoca AI — https://avoca.ai ($1B valuation, ~$1,000-$3,500/mo)
  - Sameday AI — competitor for HVAC booking
  - My AI Front Desk — https://www.myaifrontdesk.com (10,000+ businesses, free plan)
  - Rosie AI, Dialzara — lower-priced alternatives
- **White Space**: Sub-vertical specialization is still wide open. Avoca serves 800-1,000 operators at $1K-$3.5K/mo — tens of thousands of smaller shops (1-3 techs) need a $100-$300/mo version. Also: outbound AI follow-up sequences for estimates/quotes not yet commoditized.
- **Speed to Market**: 2-4 weeks for a focused inbound booking agent using voice AI APIs (Bland, Retell, Vapi). Differentiate on vertical-specific scripts and CRM integrations.
- **LTD Fit**: Marginal — services businesses prefer monthly subscriptions tied to usage. But a capped LTD at $300-$500 for 1-tech shops could convert well on AppSumo.
- **Content Strategy**: "AI receptionist for HVAC companies", "after hours answering service plumbing", "HVAC call answering software", "[trade] booking automation"

Solea AI is purpose-built for pest control with 5+ techs. WAIve AI is route-optimization + AI D2D sales for pest control. Vertical SaaS now commands a 7.0x median M&A revenue multiple (2026 report).
- **Target Industries**: Pest control, lawn care, pool service, tree service, HVAC (small shops)
- **Market Timing**: Growing (ServiceTitan IPO validated; SMB segment still open)
- **Example Products**:
  - ServiceTitan — https://servicetitan.com (enterprise, NASDAQ: TTAN)
  - Housecall Pro — https://housecallpro.com (just launched trade-specific packages 2026)
  - FieldCamp — https://fieldcamp.ai (AI-native, multi-vertical)

- **LTD Fit**: Yes — trades owners respond well to LTDs for software they'll use daily. $99-$199 LTD for single-technician tier.
