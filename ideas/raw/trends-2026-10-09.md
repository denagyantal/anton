## Construction Workforce & Field AI — Miter's "Frankenstack" Play

- **Category**: Legacy Software Disruption / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/miter-the-ai-platform-for-construction-raises-40-million-for-the-built-world-302893778.html
  - https://www.miter.com/resources/its-time-to-build/
  - https://getsaasbrief.com/story/sirex-vertical-saas-contractor-erp-launch
- **Description**: Miter raised $40M Series B (Battery Ventures, Bessemer, Coatue) to kill the construction "Frankenstack" — the cobbled-together set of 1980s tools, spreadsheets, and paper that contractors use for payroll, HR, field ops, and job costing. 2 in every 100 American construction workers now get paid through Miter. Key insight: construction payroll is uniquely complex — prevailing wage, certified payroll, multi-state crews, union rules, OSHA recordkeeping. Modern software hadn't solved it. Miter also launched AI for safety reports (from photos/voice), AP automation, and job cost intelligence. Sirex (India) is running the same playbook for SMB contractors in emerging markets — same paper-to-digital gap but even less penetration.
- **Target Industries**: General contractors, specialty contractors (electrical, flooring, mechanical), construction enterprises, SMB builders
- **Market Timing**: Growing (Miter tripled customers in 18 months)
- **Example Products**:
  - Miter — https://miter.com ($78M raised, 2,000+ contractors)
  - Sirex — https://sirex.in (India, mobile-first, launched Aug 2026)
  - Procore — https://procore.com (large enterprise incumbent)
- **White Space**: Miter covers payroll/HR/workforce well. Gaps: **small specialty contractors** (under 20 workers) that can't afford Miter's tiers; **equipment rental tracking + billing** for SMB contractors; **subcontractor payment automation** (sub-specific version of accounts payable). Emerging market angle: construction workforce management for Southeast Asia, Africa where paper is still 100%.
- **Speed to Market**: Slow for full platform (compliance complexity); moderate for a narrow wedge (e.g., just certified payroll or just equipment tracking)
- **LTD Fit**: Weak — construction software buyers expect SaaS subscriptions, not one-time deals
- **Content Strategy**: "construction payroll software", "certified payroll software", "prevailing wage software", "construction job costing"

- **Market Timing**: Growing (OSHA enforcement increasing; new regs = new software demand)
- **Example Products**:
  - Field1st — https://field1st.com (AI safety, field-heavy industries)

A simple mobile app for a 5-15 person roofing or electrical crew (daily safety checklist, incident reporting, training log) at $50-100/month or LTD. Also: **Restaurant health inspection prep software** (food safety compliance + mock audit checklists) — every restaurant chain faces repeated health department inspections but has no systematic prep tool.

- **LTD Fit**: Strong for small contractors — they know a single OSHA fine > 10 years of software costs; clear ROI framing
- **Content Strategy**: "OSHA compliance software small business", "construction safety app", "field safety checklist app", "health inspection prep software"
