## CraftBoop — Automated Follow-Ups for Service Businesses

- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5

## Signal Summary

**Strongest opportunities from this scan:**

1. **AI back-office for skilled trades** (WorkHero model for non-HVAC trades) — massive demand signal, validated by active hiring
2. **HVAC Voice Receptionist in a Box** — proven unit economics ($179K MRR in segment), product-ize what agencies build manually
3. **Post-job customer lifecycle for home services** — CraftBoop is early, gap exists for integrated review + follow-up + rebooking
4. **Permit tracker for solo contractors** — IH analysis called it explicitly; zero indie competitors; $19-29/mo recurring
5. **ZenMaid clone for adjacent verticals** — pool cleaning, window cleaning, pressure washing have no equivalent vertical SaaS

**Macro theme emerging across HN + IH:**
Vertical AI in "boring" industries (trades, construction, local services) is the dominant opportunity theme in late 2026. YC P26 is funding concrete-specific AI. IH data shows vertical AI cleaning at $130K MRR and AI receptionist at $179K MRR. The pattern: take a workflow that takes hours of manual work, build AI that does it in minutes, charge $50-300/mo, target businesses with 1-20 employees who can't afford full-time staff.
