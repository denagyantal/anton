## Vertical Pest Control SaaS / Tech-Enabled Operator (OnHand.pro)
- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47509571
- **Additional Links**: https://onhand.pro
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN / founder story (448 points, 182 comments)
- **Engagement**: 448 upvotes — one of the highest-engagement boring-business posts in 2026
- **Revenue Data**: A commenter mentions running pest control SaaS at $2M ARR. Founder is acquiring a small residential operator to tech-enable it.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Pest control / field services
- **Core Value Prop**: Founder took an actual technician job at a large pest control company (passed training in 13 days using a custom GPT he built) to get real domain knowledge before building. Concluded the incumbent VSaaS (FieldRoutes/ServiceTitan) is "decent, cheap, and ubiquitous" — so instead of selling SaaS, plans to acquire an operator and tech-enable it from within (smart traps, hands-free CRM data entry, AI upselling, route optimization).
- **Gap/Opportunity**: HVAC contractors pay ~$600 per closed lead from online ads and close only 1-in-4 to 1-in-5. Lead generation for trades is broken. Also: pest control companies pay techs piecemeal, rely on paper routes, and have no good AI-assisted scheduling or voice-to-CRM data entry. A commenter noted a pest control SaaS can do $50K MRR from "antiquated operators."
- **Our Angle**: The "AI training GPT" side project (no reliable quiz/prep tool exists online for pest control licensing) is an interesting micro-SaaS. Bigger angle: a voice-to-CRM agent that lets techs dictate job notes while driving — confirmed pain point from the thread. Also: route optimization + smart scheduling ("right tech for this job") for small-to-mid pest/HVAC operators. Pest control is 60% consolidated — the independent 40% are the SAM.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (recurring SaaS possible, LTD harder — operators want ongoing support)

- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN / beta launch

That 2-3 hours/day saved is immediately measurable ROI. Could target Open Dental (open-source) users as a segment.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — dental practices are exactly the AppSumo/LTD buyer profile: small businesses, tech-skeptical but desperate for cost savings, willing to pay upfront to replace a recurring staff cost.

## CraftBoop — Automated Follow-Ups for Service Businesses
- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/craftboop-built-automated-follow-ups-for-service-businesses-just-launched-looking-for-feedback-5aa58e1c39
- **Additional Links**: https://www.craftboop.com
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Launch post
- **Engagement**: Active comments thread
- **Revenue Data**: Just launched, $29/mo pricing, early users
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Home service businesses — plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, painters, landscapers
- **Core Value Prop**: After each job, sends an automated 5-email sequence: thank you, Google review request, check-in, rebooking reminder, referral request. Service businesses do great work but never follow up systematically — they lose reviews, rebooking, and referrals constantly.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Google reviews are the lifeblood of local service businesses but getting them manually is inconsistency at best. The rebooking + referral loop (plumbers don't need weekly service, but homeowners need plumbers 2-3 times a decade — referrals are how they grow) is almost entirely untapped. No good SMS-first version exists. Most competitors are generic email tools.
- **Our Angle**: Add SMS-first sequences (service businesses and their customers communicate via text, not email). Add a "negative review interception" step — if customer sentiment seems unhappy, route to a private feedback form before asking for a public Google review. Bundle with a simple "text to request a Google review" QR code for in-person use. LTD at $59-79 is very natural here.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5 — exact AppSumo profile. Simple, clear ROI, one-time setup, recurring value.
