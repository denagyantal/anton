## Craftplan — Bakery Production Management Tool

- **Source:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46847690
- **Additional Links:** https://github.com/puemos/craftplan
- **Platform:** Hacker News
- **Type:** Show HN
- **Engagement:** 577 points, 167 comments — #3 Show HN for February 2026
- **Revenue Data:** N/A (open-source, built for personal use first)
- **Boring Business Score:** 4/5
- **Target Industry:** Food production / bakeries / small food manufacturers
- **Core Value Prop:** Production management tool for artisan bakeries — recipes, batch planning, ingredient tracking, production scheduling. Built by a developer for his wife's bakery because nothing affordable existed.
- **Gap/Opportunity:** The high engagement (577 pts) signals huge latent demand for simple, affordable production management in food businesses. Existing solutions (DEAR, Katana, MRPeasy) are overkill and expensive. Small bakeries, coffee roasters, jam makers, candle shops, soap makers all share this pain. No focused indie solution exists.
- **Our Angle:** A focused, simple, affordable SaaS (not open source) for small food/craft producers. Tiered by production volume. LTD-friendly. Could expand to kombucha breweries, hot sauce makers, small batch spirits — all use the same recipe/batch/COGS tracking logic.
- **LTD Potential:** 4/5 — clear, tangible ROI (reduce waste, track COGS), $59-100 LTD is a no-brainer vs. paying $200+/mo for Katana.

## CrewChief.cc — AI Automotive Diagnostics App

- **Source:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49233423 (comment from user "meandave" in "Ask HN: What are you working on? August 2026")
- **Additional Links:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crewchief-auto/id6760673109 — https://crewchief.cc
- **Platform:** Hacker News
- **Type:** Discussion / project in progress
- **Engagement:** Part of 1180-comment "What are you working on?" thread; multiple high-quality replies
- **Revenue Data:** Not disclosed — seeking non-friend users, focused on marketing next
- **Boring Business Score:** 4/5
- **Target Industry:** Auto repair / vehicle owners / DIY mechanics / right-to-repair
- **Core Value Prop:** iPhone app combining OBD2 Bluetooth scan + AI diagnostic report. Pulls VIN info, DMV records, DTC codes, repair cost estimates, and generates make/model-specific diagnostic reports (e.g., E46 P0171 in context of known failure patterns). Built by an ex-mechanic.
- **Gap/Opportunity:** Strong HN thread discussion about audio-based diagnostics as next step ("I would pay to have this kind of guidance in a phone app" — commenter). Gap: integration with shop booking, mobile mechanic dispatch, or fleet management. Also no Android app yet, and no commercial/fleet version. The auto repair market ($280B/yr) massively underserved by affordable tech.
- **Our Angle:** Expand from consumer app to B2B: fleet managers, mobile mechanics, or indie auto shops using AI diagnostics as a lead-gen/upsell tool. Audio diagnostics (identify sounds) is an untapped feature mentioned multiple times with enthusiasm.
- **LTD Potential:** 3/5 — strong for consumer/prosumer tier, harder to structure for fleets.

## AI Voice Receptionist for Trades (Validated Business Model)

- **Source:** https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-profitable-ai-voice-saas-agency-300-800-mrr-per-client-frAbgO1yQMfHOFFtY3gE
- **Additional Links:** https://www.trillet.ai/blogs/best-ai-answering-service-for-trades-2026 — https://www.gosameday.com/resources — https://growth100x.com/insights/best-ai-voice-agents-hvac-plumbing-2026/
- **Platform:** Indie Hackers
- **Type:** Revenue milestone / playbook post (January 2026)
- **Engagement:** Featured post; high interest from comments
- **Revenue Data:** $300-800 MRR per client, ~80% margin. Setup fee $800-2K. Existing players: Sameday, Goodcall, Avoca AI, Smith.ai. HVAC contractors lose avg $45,600/yr to missed calls; plumbers up to $125,000/yr.
- **Boring Business Score:** 5/5
- **Target Industry:** HVAC, plumbing, electrical, dental, cleaning, home services, law firms
- **Core Value Prop:** AI answers 100% of inbound calls 24/7, books appointments, triages emergencies, captures leads — replacing a $30K+/yr human receptionist at $400/mo.
- **Gap/Opportunity:** Market is real but crowded at the platform level (Sameday, Goodcall). The gap is *vertical-specific* AI receptionists with deep integrations into trades-specific CRMs (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro). A white-label solution with pre-built workflows for specific trades (HVAC scheduling logic, emergency dispatch triage, permit-aware scheduling) could carve a defensible niche vs. generic voice AI.
- **Our Angle:** Build an HVAC-specific or plumbing-specific voice + SMS intake system that understands trade-specific workflows (seasonal demand, emergency vs. routine, parts/inventory implications). Integrate directly with Jobber/ServiceTitan. Charge per trade company, not per call.
- **LTD Potential:** 3/5 — hard to do LTD for usage-based calls, but a seat-based or "per location" model works for an LTD campaign.

## PastureEgg.com — Farm Management SaaS for Poultry Producers

- **Additional Links:** https://www.pasturegg.com
- **Platform:** Hacker News

- **Engagement:** Positive community response; "Vertical SaaS for poultry producers is not something I was expecting to see :) awesome, best of luck!" — HN commenter
- **Revenue Data:** Goal is revenue in 2026; founder using it personally as sole user currently
- **Boring Business Score:** 5/5
- **Target Industry:** Pasture-raised poultry farms (laying hens, broilers)
- **Core Value Prop:** Farm management SaaS replacing Google Sheets for small pasture poultry farms — tracking hen counts, batch production records, broiler cycles, work scheduling.
- **Gap/Opportunity:** Ag-tech for micro/small farms is massively underserved. Existing tools (FarmQA, AgriWebb) target large commercial operations. Small pasture farmers (the "sweaty startup" of farming) have zero affordable software. Same pattern applies to: egg CSA subscriptions, small vegetable farms, specialty mushroom growers, small-scale aquaponics.
- **Our Angle:** A lightweight, affordable ($19-49/mo or LTD) farm management tool targeting the growing "regenerative agriculture" / small farm movement. Batch tracking, mortality logs, production forecasting. Market channel: direct to farmers through online farming communities (r/homesteading, pastured poultry Facebook groups).
- **LTD Potential:** 4/5 — farmers hate subscriptions, LTD fits perfectly; community-driven distribution.

- **Platform:** Hacker News (hiring signal)
- **Type:** Who is Hiring signal
- **Engagement:** Growing company, active hiring
- **Revenue Data:** Paying customers confirmed; specifics not disclosed
- **Boring Business Score:** 5/5
- **Target Industry:** Construction / real estate / contractors / insurance / lending
- **Core Value Prop:** Aggregates and normalizes building permit data, contractor license data, and parcel data from 20,000+ US jurisdictions (most of which have no online portal). Sells as an intelligence layer to companies that need to find contractors, predict construction activity, or underwrite risk.
- **Gap/Opportunity:** The "data as a product" angle for the built world is validated — Shovels is building it, customers are paying. Gap for indie builders: vertical-specific apps *on top of* this data. Example: a contractor lead-gen tool that surfaces homeowners who just pulled permits for roofing → sells to roofing contractors. Or a permit-based insurance risk tool for smaller markets.
- **Our Angle:** Build a niche permit-intelligence tool for one vertical (e.g., solar installers, roofing companies, window/door replacement contractors). Sell it as a lead-gen subscription: "here are 50 homeowners in your ZIP who pulled a permit for work adjacent to what you do." $99-299/mo per contractor.
- **LTD Potential:** 3/5 — data-subscription tools are harder to LTD, but a "credits" model could work.

## HarGharPG — PG / Shared Accommodation Management (India)

- **Source:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46716567
- **Additional Links:** https://www.hargharpg.com
- **Platform:** Hacker News
- **Type:** Show HN (February 2026)
- **Engagement:** 1 point, 2 comments — low HN engagement but signals the niche
- **Revenue Data:** Not disclosed
- **Boring Business Score:** 5/5
- **Target Industry:** PG (paying guest / shared accommodation) operators in India
- **Core Value Prop:** Software for PG owners in India to manage tenant onboarding, rent payments, room occupancy, and day-to-day operations.

- **Additional Links:** https://sawdust.diy
- **Platform:** Hacker News
- **Type:** Show HN / discussion (August 2026)
- **Engagement:** Top comment in 1180-comment thread — sustained high-quality discussion, multiple follow-up threads
- **Revenue Data:** Early stage; not yet monetized
- **Boring Business Score:** 3/5
- **Target Industry:** Woodworkers / carpenters / furniture makers / cabinet shops
- **Core Value Prop:** Carpentry-native CAD simulator — work with real wood dimensions (not abstract 3D shapes), specify shop operations (cuts, joints, finishes), get a bill of materials and cut plan with real SKUs for lumber yards. MCP integration for AI agents. AR preview.
- **Gap/Opportunity:** The HN enthusiasm validates demand for trade-specific software that "speaks the language" of the craft. - **Our Angle:** Build a B2B version for cabinet shops / custom furniture makers: quote generation from a floor plan, cut list optimization for sheet goods, integration with CNC cutters. Cabinet shops are a $17B industry in the US with almost no modern software.
- **LTD Potential:** 3/5 — consumer woodworkers are hobby buyers; B2B cabinet shops need MRR for ongoing support.
