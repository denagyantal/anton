Restoration/emergency services (highest urgency = highest willingness to pay). Multi-location rollups that need centralized AI dispatch across brands.

- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — contractors prefer monthly predictable cost, but LTD possible at $500–$1,000 for the right niche
