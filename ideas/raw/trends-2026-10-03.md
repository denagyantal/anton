- **Speed to Market**: Moderate — RAG on equipment manuals + photo recognition is 4–6 weeks for MVP; consider starting with one trade (boilers in Northeast US)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — small shop owners would buy $149–$249 LTD for a tool that prevents embarrassing field moments
