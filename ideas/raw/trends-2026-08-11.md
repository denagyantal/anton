4. **Trend Seeker's #1 niche (Aug 6, 2026)**: "Missed-call recovery for service businesses" — 82 signals, 4 source types, 0 mapped direct products. Highest current signal-to-competition ratio in the database.

5. **Vertical SaaS funding**: $11.8B raised in 2026 YTD (down 25% vs 2025), but "boring industry field service" identified as top opportunity alongside dental/healthcare sub-specialties.
