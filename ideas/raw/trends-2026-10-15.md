## Making Tax Digital — UK Contractor Compliance Gap

- **Category**: Compliance & Regulation Tech
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://fvaccounts.com/making-tax-digital-for-contractors-2026-compliance-guide/ — MTD contractor compliance guide
  - https://hmrccountancy.co.uk/making_tax_digital/ — Sole trader MTD guide
  - https://www.constructionmagazine.uk/2026/06/mtd-software-construction-sole-traders.html — Construction industry MTD software
  - https://blog.voxamtd.com/blog/sole-trader-tax-compliance-making-tax-digital-2026 — MTD 2026 compliance walkthrough
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Making_Tax_Digital — MTD overview
- **Description**: From 6 April 2026, UK sole traders and landlords with qualifying income above £50,000 must keep digital records and submit quarterly digital updates to HMRC via approved software. Threshold drops to £30,000 in 2027 and £20,000 in 2028 — meaning millions more are pulled in each year. The rules ban manual copy-paste; data must flow between systems digitally. Construction and trades sole traders (plumbers, electricians, decorators) are the largest affected segment and the least digitally native. Most existing compliant software (Sage, QuickBooks, Xero) is overkill for a sole-trader tradesperson.
- **Target Industries**: UK-based sole traders in trades (plumbing, electrical, painting, carpentry), landlords, freelancers, consultants
- **Market Timing**: Early → Growing (mandate just kicked in April 2026; 2027 expansion incoming)
- **Example Products**:
  - Sage MTD (https://www.sage.com/en-gb/making-tax-digital/sole-trader/) — free basic tier
  - Clear Books (https://www.clearbooks.co.uk/free-mtd-software/) — free MTD tier
  - VoxaMTD (https://voxamtd.com) — early-stage niche MTD tool
- **White Space**: A dead-simple MTD app purpose-built for tradespeople — not accountants, not generalists. One-tap expense capture (photo receipt → category), quarterly submission with one click, built-in CIS (Construction Industry Scheme) support, no accountancy jargon. Existing tools are repurposed bookkeeping platforms. Nobody owns "MTD for plumbers" as a brand.
- **Speed to Market**: Yes — HMRC has an open API; an MVP bridging app could be live in 3–4 weeks
- **LTD Fit**: Strong — tradespeople hate subscriptions; a one-time "lifetime compliance tool" at £79–£149 would convert well on AppSumo (UK audience)
- **Content Strategy**: "making tax digital for sole traders", "MTD software for tradespeople", "MTD for plumbers 2026", "CIS digital records software"

  - Buildertrend — job costing for larger GCs, too complex for small trades

AI: suggest materials list and hours based on similar past jobs. No project management, no CRM — just profit clarity.
- **Speed to Market**: Yes — 3–4 weeks for quote creation + job closeout + margin comparison
- **LTD Fit**: Strong — trades owners actively look for "stop losing money on jobs"; LTD at $149–$299 on AppSumo would land
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor profit tracking software", "job costing app for contractors", "estimate vs actual for trades", "how to know if a job is profitable"
