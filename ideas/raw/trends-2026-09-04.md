- **Description**: The core pain: QuickBooks doesn't do job costing for contractors. Jonas Construction Software handles this for mid-large contractors. Holdings is doing AI bookkeeping for independent contractors with auto-categorization. But there's a gap for small contractors (1-5 employees) who need job-cost accounting, WIP reporting, and profit-per-job tracking without paying Jonas/Foundation Construction prices ($5K+/year). The winning product here bundles: bank feed + job costing + invoice generation + simple P&L per job.
- **Target Industries**: General contractors, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping (1-10 employees)
- **Market Timing**: Early — no clear winner at the sub-$100/month price point for job-cost-aware bookkeeping
- **Example Products**:
  - Holdings (AI bookkeeping for contractors): https://getholdings.com/resources/guides/best-accounting-software-contractors
  - Jonas Construction Software (enterprise): https://myofficeops.com/resources/best-bookkeeping-software-for-contractors/
  - Wave (free, no job costing): https://www.waveapps.com
  - QuickBooks (generic, inadequate job costing for trades)
- **White Space**: A "QuickBooks for trades" that's built from scratch around profit-per-job, not around general ledger accounting. Bonus: AI that automatically tags expenses to jobs based on receipt photos. Should cost $29-49/month or offer a compelling LTD.
- **Speed to Market**: 6-8 weeks for MVP (bank sync + job tags + invoice + per-job P&L). Can build on Plaid for bank feeds, Stripe for invoicing.
- **LTD Fit**: High — trades owners hate recurring software costs; clear financial ROI ("see exactly where you're losing money on jobs")
- **Content Strategy**: "job costing software for contractors", "profit per job tracking", "contractor bookkeeping app", "QuickBooks alternative for contractors"
