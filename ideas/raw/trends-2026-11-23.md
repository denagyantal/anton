- **Category**: Vertical AI / SMB Platform Consolidation
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - Broccoli raises $52M (Khosla): https://www.newswire.com/news/broccoli-raises-52m-led-by-khosla-ventures-to-help-home-services-businesses
  - Drillbit raises $4.4M (YC S24): https://dealroom.co/news/172736-drillbit-raises-4-4m-to-put-ai-office-workers-inside-home-services-firms/

  - WorkHero raises $5M (HVAC focused): https://www.workhero.pro/blog/workhero-raises-5m-seed-round
  - Zoop (beta, free): https://zoop.pro/
- **Description**: The home services AI wave has moved beyond voice bots answering calls. 2026 is seeing a wave of funding for companies that own the full job lifecycle — lead intake → dispatch → quoting → invoicing → payment collection — with AI handling administrative work autonomously. Broccoli ($52M, 1,500+ businesses, 10M+ conversations) is the standout. Drillbit (Mason AI agent) reports $95M GMV across 90,000+ opportunities with 42% MoM growth. WorkHero combines AI agents with human "Senior Office Managers" for a human-in-the-loop service model.

- **Market Timing**: Growing (crowding fast — Avoca at $1B valuation in April 2026, ServiceTitan adding own AI agents)
- **Example Products**:
  - Broccoli: https://broccoliai.com
  - Drillbit (Mason): https://drillbit.ai
  - Probook: https://probook.com
  - WorkHero: https://workhero.pro
  - Zoop: https://zoop.pro
  - ServiceAgent: https://www.producthunt.com/products/serviceagent
  - Rezora IO (YC 2026, $23K MRR): https://rezora.io
- **White Space**: Niche sub-trade verticals (pool service, chimney, pest control) that the well-funded generalists haven't reached yet. Also multi-location franchise operators who need cross-brand dispatch consolidation — only 4 out of ~900 home service franchise brands mandate an FSM platform (FranCloud 2026 data).
- **Speed to Market**: 4–8 weeks for a niche sub-trade version; competing with well-funded players in HVAC/plumbing requires significant differentiation
- **LTD Fit**: Low — recurring value is the pitch; LTD doesn't align with AI usage costs
- **Content Strategy**: "[trade] scheduling software", "AI receptionist for contractors", "HVAC back office automation", "automated dispatch for plumbers"

- **Market Timing**: Early-to-Growing — incumbents are $15K+ implementations, new entrants are sub-$500/mo with same-day setup
- **Example Products**:
  - tab32 Alpine: https://tab32.com
  - Ayla Dental: https://tryayla.com
  - Dozorvexa Dental: https://www.dozorvexa.com

Dental recall and reactivation AI that plugs into existing software via API rather than requiring a full migration.

- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — dental practices buy software carefully but $500–$1,000 LTD for an AI add-on module is plausible

## PE Roll-Up Tech: Franchise & Multi-Location Field Service

- **Category**: Franchise & Multi-Location Tools / SMB Platform Consolidation
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - FranCloud 2026 data: https://www.francloud.com/resources/home-services-software-boom
  - Granular blog on PE consolidation: https://www.granular.to/blog/field-service-software-consolidation
  - Houlihan Lokey March 2026 report: https://cdn.hl.com/pdf/2026/field-and-frontline-operations-management-software-mar2026.pdf
- **Description**: 14 PE firms are actively rolling up home-service trades into multi-brand super-platforms. 24,584 combined locations across top 5 commercial cleaning brands (more than McDonald's + Subway) — but only 4 out of ~900 home service franchise brands mandate a field-service platform. 48 brands mandate QuickBooks for accounting only. This creates a massive gap: franchise HQs can't see real-time operational data across all locations. One HQ software decision turns into a system-wide mandate instantly — the sales motion is to close the PE platform owner, not individual franchisees.
- **Target Industries**: Commercial cleaning, home services, pest control, HVAC franchises, multi-location electrical/plumbing
- **Market Timing**: Growing — PE consolidation accelerating in 2026, software vendors are lagging behind
- **Example Products**:
  - ServiceTitan (enterprise, expensive): https://www.servicetitan.com
  - Home Service One (multi-brand built-in): https://homeserviceone.io
  - FranCloud (franchise data): https://www.francloud.com
- **White Space**: Franchise operations dashboard that works *on top of* existing field service tools (FieldRoutes, Jobber, Housecall Pro) rather than replacing them — a reporting/compliance layer for the franchisor HQ that doesn't require franchisees to switch platforms.
- **Speed to Market**: Read-only dashboard MVP in 4–6 weeks; write-back integration adds 8+ weeks
- **LTD Fit**: Low — franchise HQ buyers want subscriptions with SLAs
- **Content Strategy**: "franchise field service software", "multi-location HVAC management", "home service franchise operations dashboard"
