## Finding 4: CraftBoop — Automated Follow-Up Sequences for Service Businesses

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/craftboop-built-automated-follow-ups-for-service-businesses-just-launched-looking-for-feedback-5aa58e1c39
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Launch post / feedback request
- **Engagement**: Early stage post
- **Revenue Data**: $29/mo pricing, few early users
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, painters, landscapers
- **Core Value Prop**: After each job, sends 5-email automated sequence: thank-you, Google review request, check-in, rebooking reminder, referral ask. Add customer once, it handles the rest.

- **Our Angle**: Expand to SMS + WhatsApp follow-ups (higher open rates than email for blue-collar customers). Add Google My Business review management. Bundle with simple job tracking. Target: one-person trade shops at $19–29/mo — under ServiceTitan radar.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 (simple, clearly bounded scope, AppSumo-friendly)

## New Raw Ideas (for Evaluator)

### Idea A: HVAC Maintenance Agreement Tracker ("ServiceStack Lite")
Target the 118,000 HVAC contractors managing maintenance agreements in spreadsheets. Focus only on agreement lifecycle: create → schedule → remind → renew → invoice. Nothing else. ServiceTitan does this but charges $245–$500/tech/month. We charge $29–49/month flat. LTD launch on AppSumo to acquire first 500 customers.

### Idea B: BatchBook — Production & Compliance for Micro Food Producers
Recipe costing with allergen tracking, batch production scheduling, order management. Target cottage food businesses (35+ US states have cottage food laws), food trucks, micro-bakeries. Open-source Craftplan validated the workflow — nobody is selling it as accessible SaaS. $39/mo or $299/lifetime.

### Idea C: TradeDoc — Quote/Estimate/Contract PDF Tool for 0–5 Employee Trades
Single-purpose document tool: create estimates, quotes, and service contracts as PDFs. Prefill from customer database. Send via email or WhatsApp. Sign digitally. Collect payment link. $99/year flat. No per-user fees. Validates from Documentorium EU success + direct HN founder statement of hundreds of yearly paying users.

### Idea D: STR Turnover Tracker with AI Damage Assessment
For 1–20 listing short-term rental operators. Cleaner submits photos → AI checks for damage → triggers guest deposit claim pre-fill → notifies host only when problem found. Integrates with Airbnb/VRBO. Differentiator: automated damage evidence chain for dispute resolution. $9/listing/month.

### Idea E: Trade Technician Certification Prep ("TradePass")
Exam prep and study tool for pest control, HVAC, electrical, and plumbing license exams. Flashcards, practice tests, video explainers. Validated gap: no reliable test/quiz prep tools exist for most trade license exams (per HN discussion). B2C ($29/one-time per exam prep) + B2B (companies pay for onboarding new hires). LTD viable.
