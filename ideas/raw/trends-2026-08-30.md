## AI Receptionists Purpose-Built for Trades & Home Services

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Mobile-First Field Tools
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.vellum.ai/blog/best-ai-receptionist-for-small-business
  - https://upfirst.ai/blog/best-ai-answering-services
  - https://www.getaira.io/blog/best-ai-receptionist
  - https://trillet.ai/blogs/top-10-ai-receptionists-for-small-business-2026
- **Description**: Virtual receptionist market hit $4.64B in 2026, projected to $10.85B by 2035 (9.8% CAGR). Trades businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) lose jobs every time a call goes to voicemail. Human answering services cost $235–$289+/month (Ruby, AnswerConnect). New AI-first players (Rosie, Dialzara, AIRA) are entering at $24–$49/month specifically for home services. Rosie is specifically trades-targeted. Most are still horizontal — huge white space for a trade-vertical AI receptionist with FSM integration (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan).

- **Market Timing**: Growing — early players exist but trade-specific integration is a gap
- **Example Products**:
  - Rosie (https://www.rosie.ai) — budget trade-focused AI answering, ~$49/mo
  - Dialzara (https://dialzara.com) — $29/mo AI receptionist
  - AIRA (https://www.getaira.io) — $24.95/mo, general SMB
  - Smith.ai — hybrid AI+human, $95/mo+
  - Ruby (legacy human) — $235/mo+
- **White Space**: Trade-specific AI receptionist that auto-books jobs in Jobber/HCP/ServiceTitan, reads the price book for basic estimates, and routes emergency calls differently than routine scheduling. No player owns this integration layer yet.
- **Speed to Market**: 2–4 weeks for MVP (Twilio + LLM + Jobber API). Differentiation through trade-specific conversation flows.
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — solo plumbers/electricians would absolutely pay $199 once vs. $235/month for Ruby. Strong LTD candidate.
- **Content Strategy**: "AI answering service for plumbers," "never miss a service call," "HVAC phone answering software," "virtual receptionist for contractors"

Physical therapy, dental, and home health are particularly underserved with affordable options (enterprise solutions like Availity are cost-prohibitive for small practices).

- **Market Timing**: Growing — regulatory deadlines create urgency now
- **Example Products**:
  - Prosper AI (https://www.getprosper.ai) — automated prior auth
  - Insight Health AI (https://www.insighthealth.ai) — specialty clinic focus

- **White Space**: Affordable prior auth automation for solo/small specialty clinics (PT, chiro, OT) under $200/month. Most solutions price at enterprise SaaS rates. Small practices (1-5 providers) have exactly the same pain but no affordable solution.
- **Speed to Market**: 4–6 weeks (AI document parsing + payer form API or RPA + clinic onboarding). Regulatory complexity is the moat.
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — healthcare buyers prefer monthly subscription due to compliance liability, but a one-time setup fee + low MRR could work.
