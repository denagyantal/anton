## Summary

This scan focused on "boring business" verticals — trades, field service, cleaning, logistics, pest control, HVAC, property management, trucking — looking for validated demand signals, revenue milestones, and product gaps across HN and Indie Hackers.

**Key themes:**
1. **The ServiceTitan pricing vacuum is real and massive.** A 2-tech HVAC/plumbing shop pays $8K-$18K/year for ServiceTitan vs. $1.3K-$2.2K for Jobber. ~100,000 small field-service shops cannot justify enterprise FSM pricing — a focused micro-vertical tool at $29-49/month is a direct wedge.
2. **Cleaning/maid SaaS is proven at scale.** ZenMaid is $3M/yr bootstrapped ($250K/month). A new competitor BlastoClean launched Oct 1, 2026 with a fresh angle (task-time-based pricing). The category has real paying customers.
3. **AI receptionist/booking for home services is the hottest adjacent signal.** FounderSignal data: AI receptionist/booking tools at $179K MRR aggregate. Contractors lose $45K-$120K/year to missed calls. EliseAI is at $2.2B valuation for the property management slice alone.
4. **HVAC maintenance agreement tracking is an unbuilt $29/month product.** 118,000 HVAC businesses in the US; most track maintenance contracts in paper files. ServiceTitan doesn't serve this segment; no dedicated tool exists under $100/month.
5. **Freight/logistics AI has clear ROI.** Logistics invoice error rate 3-5% across a $900B market. HappyRobot hit eight-figure revenue on logistics communication automation. Freight audit automation is sellable on direct ROI.
6. **Solo contractor estimate + profit tracking has an identified $15-17/month gap.** Joist handles estimates at $8/month but stops there. Jobber adds job costing only on $39-199/month plans. No standalone profitability tracking tool exists for solo plumbers/electricians/landscapers.
7. **Niche CRM for specific boring verticals compounds to $11M ARR.** Recruit CRM (recruitment agencies) bootstrapped to $11M ARR at 40% YoY growth with 40% free cash flow margins. The pattern: outdated incumbent + modern UX + deep niche focus.

**Top opportunities for building:**
- HVAC maintenance agreement tracker ($29-49/month, 118K US businesses)
- Solo contractor estimate + profit tracker ($15-17/month, wedge above Joist)
- Micro-rental booking for solo operators ($15/month kayaks/bounce houses/party gear)
- AI missed-call / booking for home service businesses
- Freight invoice audit automation (strong ROI story)
