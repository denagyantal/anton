## AI Operating Systems for the Trades (Dispatch-First Platforms)

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Trades Going Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.constructionindustry.ai/funding/probook-40m-series-a-home-services-dispatch-ai/
  - https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/04/29/avoca-closes-125m-at-1b-valuation-to-power-americas-services-economy-with-ai/
  - https://www.idlen.io/news/avoca-ai-1-billion-valuation-kleiner-perkins-services-economy-voice-agents-april-2026/
  - https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/avoca-voice-ai-trades-unicorn-2026/
  - https://www.lvlup.vc/post/why-we-backed-plmbr-ai-agents-and-the-contractor-back-office
- **Description**: The biggest story in boring-business software in 2026 is the race to build the "AI operating system" for trades. Probook raised $40M (a16z + Sequoia co-led — rare) to own the dispatch board for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical shops. Avoca hit $1B valuation on $125M in funding (Kleiner → Meritech/General Catalyst) for voice AI that answers every call, books jobs, and runs re-engagement campaigns. PLMBR (backed by LvlUp Labs) pitches AI agents that replace back-office hires for small contractors at $10.5K–$25K/yr ACV. These are not feature additions — they are full platform plays competing with the cost of hiring an office manager.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, junk removal, moving services
- **Market Timing**: Growing fast — institutional VC is flooding in, but SMB segment (1–10 technicians) still mostly untouched by quality software
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook: https://www.constructionindustry.ai/funding/probook-40m-series-a-home-services-dispatch-ai/
  - Avoca AI: https://avoca.ai (voice agents, $1B valuation)
  - PLMBR: https://www.lvlup.vc/post/why-we-backed-plmbr-ai-agents-and-the-contractor-back-office
  - Stormy: https://www.producthunt.com/products/stormy-influencer-marketing-ai-agent (AI agent for field service shops — calls, quotes, scheduling, invoicing)
  - Mainteny: https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny (AI OS for maintenance companies, launched 2026)
- **White Space**: Probook and Avoca target mid-market ($1M–$50M revenue shops). The 1–5 technician owner-operator is still underserved. A lighter-weight, $99–$299/mo tool focused purely on after-hours call capture + job booking for solo operators is unbuilt. Also: non-English-speaking trade operators (Hispanic-owned contracting is a massive underserved segment).
- **Speed to Market**: 4–6 weeks for a focused MVP (AI voice booking + SMS follow-up + simple calendar)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trades owners want to try before committing, but $299–$499 LTD for "never miss a call again" is highly sellable on AppSumo
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC answering service software", "plumbing scheduling software", "contractor after-hours call answering", "AI receptionist for small HVAC company"

## Vertical AI Voice Agents Reaching Unicorn Status

- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/avoca-voice-ai-trades-unicorn-2026/
  - https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166287-missed-calls-lost-jobs-why-hvac-companies-are-adopting-voice-ai-agents

Avoca alone is on track to book $1B in jobs through its platform in 2026. The category is now spawning many competitors: CloudTalk, Synthflow, Bland AI, Vapi, Retell AI, Newo, IVAI — all targeting the same vertical. Key differentiator is trade-specific knowledge (service types, urgency triage, technician availability) vs generic voice AI.

- **Market Timing**: Early-to-Growing — Avoca proved the model, now it's a race for vertical specialists
- **Example Products**:
  - Avoca AI: https://avoca.ai
  - Newo AI: https://newo.ai/hvac-plumbing-ai-receptionist/
  - IVAI (HVAC): https://getivai.com/hvac
  - Bizrnr: https://bizrnr.com/industries/hvac-plumbing ($99/mo AI answering)
  - CallMissed: https://www.callmissed.com/blog/ai-voice-agent-home-services-2026-plumbing
- **White Space**: Dental, optometry, veterinary practices — same "missed call = lost patient" problem, but AI voice for healthcare is still fragmented and expensive. Also: Spanish-language voice agents for Hispanic-owned service businesses.
- **Speed to Market**: 3–4 weeks (voice AI infrastructure is commodity now via Vapi/Retell; vertical customization is the differentiator)
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — "never miss a customer call again" resonates strongly with $299–$499 LTD on AppSumo
- **Content Strategy**: "AI answering service for HVAC", "plumbing after hours call answering", "dental office AI receptionist", "AI phone answering small business"

## Contractor Back-Office AI Agents (Replacing Admin Hires)

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.lvlup.vc/post/why-we-backed-plmbr-ai-agents-and-the-contractor-back-office
  - https://getsaasbrief.com/story/sirex-vertical-saas-contractor-erp-launch
  - https://relayfi.com/blog/9-game-changing-ai-tools-contractors-scale-2026/
  - http://distributionstrategy.com/2026/09/contractors-ramp-up-ai-use-but-profit-gains-lag/
- **Description**: The key insight driving this trend: small contractors don't need better organization software — they need software that *does the work*. PLMBR's pricing ($10.5K–$25K/yr) signals they're selling against a hire, not against a SaaS subscription. AI agents handle proposals, customer comms + follow-up, scheduling, invoicing, and project coordination. 69% of plumbing contractors and 64% of electricians now use AI in some form (2026), but only 38% report measurable business impact — indicating massive white space for tools that actually take work off the owner's plate rather than just organizing it.
- **Target Industries**: General contractors, specialty contractors (plumbing, electrical, HVAC remodelers), construction subcontractors
- **Market Timing**: Early — PLMBR has 5 customers at $73K ARR 4 months post-launch; the category is just beginning
- **Example Products**:
  - PLMBR: https://www.lvlup.vc/post/why-we-backed-plmbr-ai-agents-and-the-contractor-back-office
  - Sirex (India): https://getsaasbrief.com/story/sirex-vertical-saas-contractor-erp-launch
- **White Space**: The $150–$400/mo price point (vs. PLMBR's $10K+/yr) for micro-contractors (1–3 person shops) running under $300K revenue. Most tools assume the owner has time to learn software; AI agents that work from a WhatsApp conversation or text thread would be transformative.
- **Speed to Market**: 6–8 weeks (LLM + workflow automation + basic CRM)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — annual contract model makes more sense, but a $299 LTD "AI estimating assistant" for contractors would sell on AppSumo
- **Content Strategy**: "AI estimating software contractors", "contractor back office software", "proposal automation small business contractor", "AI job costing"

- **Signal Strength**: Moderate

- **Market Timing**: Early — category barely exists yet
- **Example Products**:
  - The Nut Report: https://www.producthunt.com/p/introduce-yourself/hey-product-hunt-i-m-chad-founder-of-the-nut-report-here-s-why-i-built-it
- **White Space**: Financial forecasting / hiring decision layer sitting on top of existing FSM platforms (Jobber, ServiceTitan, HCP). Nobody has built "tell me if I can afford to hire my next tech" as a standalone product with clean UX.
- **Speed to Market**: 3–4 weeks for a read-only analytics layer on Jobber/HCP APIs
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — $99–$199 LTD for a simple financial clarity tool would sell to service business owners who "just want to know the number"
- **Content Strategy**: "service business financial dashboard", "can I afford to hire a technician", "HVAC business profitability calculator", "Jobber analytics"

## SMB Compliance & License Tracking for Trades

- **Category**: Compliance & Regulation Tech
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://redwerk.com/blog/micro-saas-ideas-that-print-money/ (insurance agency license tracking as micro SaaS model)
  - https://www.safeboxtech.com/blogs/compliance-in-2026-what-smbs-need-to-know-about-new-regulations/
  - https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/regtech-market (RegTech $20B → $116B by 2036)
  - https://cynomi.com/learn/compliance-management-software-solutions/
- **Description**: New 2026 regulations are pushing digital record-keeping requirements onto SMBs. Trade contractors face a web of compliance requirements: license renewals, continuing education credits, insurance certificates, chemical usage logs (pest control), OSHA training records, and more. Most shops track this in a spreadsheet or not at all. The micro SaaS pattern validated for insurance agencies (license + CE tracking dashboard) applies directly to HVAC, electrical, and plumbing contractors who need to track: state licenses, EPA certifications, OSHA 10/30 cards, vehicle inspections, and insurance certificates across a team. RegTech market growing at 19.2% CAGR to $116B by 2036.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC contractors, electrical contractors, plumbing contractors, pest control (EPA licensing), CDL trucking (DOT compliance)
- **Market Timing**: Early — no dominant player owns this for sub-50-person trade shops
- **Example Products**:
  - Generic compliance platforms (Cynomi, Drata) — not trade-specific
  - No known trade-specific license tracker at SMB price point
- **White Space**: A "contractor compliance dashboard" — tracking employee licenses, certifications, insurance certs, and renewal deadlines with automated reminders. $49–$99/mo for a 5–20 person shop. Nobody has built the simple version.
- **Speed to Market**: 3–4 weeks (CRUD app with reminders + document upload)
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — "never let a license lapse again" is a $199–$299 LTD offer that resonates with trade business owners
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor license renewal tracking software", "HVAC EPA certification tracker", "trade business compliance software", "employee certification tracking construction"
