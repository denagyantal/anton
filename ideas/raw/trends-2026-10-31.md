- **Sources**:

  - Elyos AI $13M Series A (YC, UK): https://www.uktech.news/ai/ai-platform-for-the-trades-raises-10m-series-a-round-20260119
  - UpSmith $10M Series A (ServiceTitan partner): https://www.newswire.com/news/upsmith-raises-10-million-series-a-to-power-the-people-who-power-22870667
  - Feldy (YC W26): https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SiZ-feldy-field-first-ai-for-home-services-and-contractors
  - $1.5M seed raise for HVAC/plumbing AI agents at $38K MRR: https://pitchmap.app/posts/msfk8gzg8bd3702ff1fe6c17
- **Description**: The defining theme of H2 2026 is "AI operating system for trades." A wave of well-funded startups is replacing the patchwork of point solutions (voice agent here, CRM there, scheduling elsewhere) with a single AI-native layer that runs intake, dispatch, scheduling, invoicing, and customer comms end-to-end. The common framing: legacy field service software (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro) is the system of record but doesn't *act* — these new OS players sit on top and automate the execution layer.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, drain cleaning, pest control, home services broadly
- **Market Timing**: Growing fast — Series A checks from a16z and Sequoia signal this is no longer early
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook (probook.ai) — dispatch-first AI OS, $40M a16z/Sequoia
  - Sophiie AI (sophiie.ai) — AI receptionist → full OS, thousands of AU/NZ/UK customers
  - Elyos AI — voice agents for plumbers/electricians/HVAC, YC + Blackbird
  - UpSmith — AI profit engine (follow-up, estimates, memberships) on top of ServiceTitan
  - Mainteny (mainteny.com) — "AI COO" for maintenance companies
  - Frontdesk AI (myaifrontdesk.com) — 10,000+ businesses, AI front office with sequences
- **White Space**: The market is getting crowded at the large/multi-location end (PE-backed platforms). Under-served: solo operators and 2-5 tech shops who can't afford $500+/month enterprise OS tools. Affordable tier AI OS for the single-truck operator is still wide open.
- **Speed to Market**: Hard to replicate the full OS in 2-4 weeks, but a narrow slice (e.g., AI follow-up on open estimates only) is buildable fast. The integration layer (ServiceTitan/Jobber API) is the moat.
- **LTD Fit**: Possible but difficult — these are deeply workflow-integrated tools. LTD more viable for point-solution modules than full OS. Monthly SaaS at $99-$299/mo is the natural fit.
- **Content Strategy**: "AI for HVAC business", "ServiceTitan alternatives", "home service AI answering service", "trades business automation"

## Compliance Automation for Trades (COIs, SWMS, Safety Docs)

- **Category**: Compliance & Regulation Tech
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate-Strong
- **Sources**:
  - Gabrio (AI-drafted estimates + COIs + safety docs, $100/mo/shop): https://gabrio.ai/
  - ComplAI (dual-code compliance for field engineers): https://getcompl.ai/
  - ComplyFlow (contractor compliance with AI review agents): https://www.complyflow.com/solutions/ai
  - BuilderPal (California CSLB-compliant CRM for contractors): https://www.mybuilderpal.com/
  - FieldForge (compliance packs built into every job, SWMS/SDS/toolbox talks): https://fieldforge.com.au/
  - Workvoice (PPM compliance, ISO/NFPA/CEN-ready audit trails): https://www.workvoice.co/
  - Morrow Systems Group (agentic AI for licensed trades compliance): https://morrowsystemsgroup.com/
- **Description**: Tradespeople face mounting compliance requirements: OSHA paperwork, state contractor licensing (CSLB etc.), certificates of insurance per job, Safe Work Method Statements (Australia), chemical SDS records, toolbox talks, EPA/PHMSA for some trades. Most shops handle this manually or forget it entirely until an audit. A new category of tools automates the paper trail: AI reads drawings/regs/policies and drafts the required docs; a human reviews and signs off; the system keeps an append-only audit log.
- **Target Industries**: Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, general contracting, pest control (chemical compliance), facilities/PPM, construction subcontractors
- **Market Timing**: Early-to-growing. Regulation is increasing (California CSLB enforcement, OSHA expansion), creating forced adoption.
- **Example Products**:
  - Gabrio (gabrio.ai) — estimates + COIs + safety docs, $100/mo per shop, human-in-loop
  - ComplAI (getcompl.ai) — dual-code compliance (industry codes + company standards) for field engineers
  - BuilderPal (mybuilderpal.com) — CA contractor CRM with 6 AI agents including compliance advisor
  - FieldForge — SWMS/SDS/toolbox talks bundled into every job automatically (AU market)
  - ComplyFlow — AI agents score contractor-submitted safety docs against your criteria
- **White Space**: Most compliance tools are either enterprise (expensive, complex) or AU-specific. A US-focused lightweight compliance tool specifically for solo/small contractor shops — covering COIs, OSHA toolbox talks, and state license tracking in one mobile app — is not well served. Also: no one has cracked pest control chemical compliance (APVMA-equivalent for the US market = EPA records).
- **Speed to Market**: Medium — compliance is a sensitive domain, needs careful doc handling. A narrow slice (COI tracking + reminder per job) is buildable fast. Full AI-drafted docs takes longer.
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — compliance tools need ongoing regulatory updates, making pure LTD hard. A "compliance starter pack" LTD with annual for updates is viable.
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor COI tracking", "OSHA compliance app for contractors", "SWMS app for tradespeople", "California contractor compliance software"

## Technician Retention & Performance Pay Platforms

- **Category**: SMB Platform Consolidation / Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - ShareWillow $8.5M seed (Lerer Hippeau, techs earn $1,334/mo more): https://alleywatch.com/2026/10/sharewillow-home-field-service-technicians-performance-pay-platform-ai-agents-skilled-trades-ryan-shank/
  - UpSmith $10M Series A: https://www.newswire.com/news/upsmith-raises-10-million-series-a-to-power-the-people-who-power-22870667
  - 2026 skilled trades workforce outlook: https://peaktechnical.com/2026-skilled-trades-workforce-outlook/
  - Technician shortage index 2026: https://exoserva.com/blog/technician-shortage-index-2026
  - ShareWillow data: 3M+ workers in HVAC/plumbing/electrical alone, 1.7M trades openings/year through 2035
- **Description**: The skilled trades labor shortage is structural and worsening. McKinsey estimates 20 job openings for every new trades worker through 2032. 68% of technicians are over age 45. Replacing a tech costs $15,000-$35,000. The result: retention software is suddenly a hot category. ShareWillow raised $8.5M specifically to build the "performance pay platform" — connecting to ServiceTitan/Jobber/Housecall Pro, calculating pay per job in real time, showing techs their earnings as they work. The AI layer ("Willow") will brief techs before calls and coach them through the week.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, drain cleaning, any home services business with field technicians
- **Market Timing**: Growing — the labor shortage drives urgency. PE-backed multi-location platforms are the immediate buyers; independent shops are the long tail.
- **Example Products**:
  - ShareWillow (sharewillow.com) — performance pay + AI coaching for techs, $2M paid out in Sept 2026 alone
  - UpSmith — AI profit engine (includes tech coaching, job coaching angles)
- **White Space**: ShareWillow owns the performance pay angle. Gaps remain in: (a) recruiting software specifically for trades businesses (not generic job boards), (b) apprenticeship/training tracking for growing shops, (c) tech scorecards and coaching without needing ServiceTitan-tier infrastructure.
- **Speed to Market**: Medium — needs FSM integrations (ServiceTitan/Jobber APIs). A lighter version using Zapier + webhooks could be faster. Core logic (calculate tech pay per job, show them a dashboard) is an achievable 4-week MVP.
- **LTD Fit**: Low for the full platform (owners need ongoing data updates). High for specific modules like a standalone "tech scorecard" or "performance pay calculator" template tool.
- **Content Strategy**: "how to retain HVAC technicians", "performance pay for service technicians", "tech turnover cost calculator", "Jobber technician dashboard"
