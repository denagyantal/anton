  - https://getivai.com/hvac — IVA HVAC voice agent (24/7 booking & dispatch)
  - https://superdupr.com/blog/ai-for-hvac-plumbing-electrical — AI automation overview for trades
  - https://www.saasrise.com/news/housecall-pro-adds-96k-mrr-with-tradespecific-saas-for-hvac-plumbing-and-electrical-a5f4591c-b035-4228-b070-69e732790528 — Housecall Pro trade-specific SaaS adding $96K MRR
- **Description**: Roughly 60% of calls to small trade businesses go unanswered; 80% of those callers will not leave a voicemail — they call the next company. Median ticket sizes for HVAC/plumbing range $350–$2,200, making each missed call a four-figure loss. Avoca (voice AI for field-service trades) hit a $1B valuation and 8-figure ARR in April 2026 on $125M in funding. A whole second tier of competitors (CloudTalk, Synthflow, Vapi, Retell, Smith.ai) is commoditizing the underlying voice layer.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, general contracting
- **Market Timing**: Growing — Avoca as category leader, secondary players still cheap to beat
- **Example Products**: Avoca AI (https://avoca.ai), CloudTalk (https://cloudtalk.io), Synthflow (https://synthflow.ai), Retell AI (https://retellai.com), Smith.ai (https://smith.ai), IVA (https://getivai.com)
- **White Space**: Vertical-specific voice AI trained on the specific trade vocabulary + dispatch workflows for pest control, pool service, and appliance repair — all smaller than HVAC but still underserved. Also: voice AI that integrates with sub-$50/mo scheduling tools (not just ServiceTitan/HCP).
- **Speed to Market**: MVP 2–4 weeks using existing voice AI APIs (Vapi/Retell) with trade-specific prompt tuning
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trade business owners understand the ROI easily (cost of one missed job pays for the tool), but prefer monthly billing with a clear ROI calculator. LTD possible as a hook.
- **Content Strategy**: "answering service for HVAC," "plumbing answering service AI," "never miss a call HVAC," "pest control phone answering"

  - https://brothersautomate.com/blog/ai-bookkeeping-for-small-business/ — AI bookkeeping for small business
  - https://www.mbcconsultant.com/blog/bookkeeping-automation-2026-guide-to-ai-tools-benefits-cost — Bookkeeping automation guide 2026

  - https://www.intuit.com/blog/innovative-thinking/best-ai-accounting-software-tools/ — Intuit on AI accounting tools
- **Description**: AI can automate 80–90% of routine bookkeeping (transaction entry, categorization, reconciliation, invoice processing). Yet only 29% of small businesses use AI for bookkeeping — despite it being the single most automatable function. Trade businesses (HVAC, plumbing, landscaping) are particularly underserved: they have job-based revenue, material costs, subcontractor payments, and equipment depreciation that generic tools handle poorly. QuickBooks/Xero are general-purpose; no dominant player has built bookkeeping specifically for a single trade.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest control, cleaning businesses
- **Market Timing**: Early-Growing — AI tools exist but vertical-specific ones don't

- **White Space**: Bookkeeping tool purpose-built for a single trade vertical — e.g., "HVAC Bookkeeper" that understands equipment installs vs. service calls, tracks technician labor costs per job, auto-categorizes vendor invoices, and integrates directly with ServiceTitan/HCP job data. Competitors are horizontal; vertical player has a clear differentiation story.
- **Speed to Market**: 3–4 weeks for MVP (job-cost tracking + bank feed AI categorization)
- **LTD Fit**: Strong — trade business owners hate paying accountants; one-time payment for automated books is a compelling LTD pitch
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC bookkeeping software," "bookkeeping for plumbers," "QuickBooks alternative contractors," "job costing software trades"
