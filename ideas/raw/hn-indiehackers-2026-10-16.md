## Zirco.ai – AI Employee for Dental Front Desk Operations

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Additional Links**: https://zircoai.vercel.app/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (Ask-HN style intro post)
- **Engagement**: 3 comments, early stage
- **Revenue Data**: Beta stage, 30+ dental practices discovered, no MRR yet
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices
- **Core Value Prop**: Automates the full dental front desk workflow — insurance verification (Playwright-based portal automation), inbound scheduling calls (Vapi voice AI), appointment booking into Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft, SMS/email reminders

- **Our Angle**: Similar concept applied to dental billing (not just scheduling) — or clone for veterinary practices/optometry, which have similar front-desk insurance burdens without Zirco's current focus.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 (HIPAA/healthcare makes LTD tricky; high value per customer pushes toward MRR anyway)

## CraftBoop – Automated Follow-Up Sequences for Service Businesses

- **Revenue Data**: Just launched April 2026, $29/mo pricing, few early users, growing
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Home service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, painters, landscapers)
- **Core Value Prop**: After each job, sends a 5-email automated sequence to the customer: thank you → review request → check-in → rebooking reminder → referral request

- **Engagement**: N/A
- **Revenue Data**: Joist (estimates only) at $8/mo has large user base. Jobber starts at $39-199/mo. Gap between them is validated.
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Solo plumbers, electricians, landscapers (1-3 person operations)
- **Core Value Prop**: Estimate creation (Joist UX) + job closeout screen where contractor logs actual materials + hours to see profit vs. estimate. Simple two-feature product.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Joist users have no way to track if their estimates are profitable. That's a $15-17/month upsell for a standalone product — or a wedge to build a full lightweight FSM tool. Joist's market size proves demand for cheap estimate tools.

## Ask HN Discussion — Why Is There No Uber for Plumbing/HVAC?

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45828971
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Discussion thread
- **Engagement**: High (extensive comments, multiple perspectives)
- **Revenue Data**: N/A
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Plumbing, HVAC, skilled trades
- **Core Value Prop**: Market structure discussion — why marketplace models don't work in skilled trades, what would actually work
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Key insight from thread: the problem isn't a marketplace (Uber model fails here). The real gap is:
  1. **Instant quoting for common, known-scope jobs** (replace a water heater, tune up HVAC) — no skill needed, scope is defined, only price matters
  2. **Maintenance agreement management** — repeat revenue, but terrible software
  3. **Post-job review/rebooking automation** — tradespeople don't ask; they lose repeat customers
  Thread also validates high VC-avoidance: "there's no reason for a plumber to sign up for a platform that takes a cut and will say if you're bad" — direct-to-SMB SaaS avoids this.
- **Our Angle**: The comments explicitly identify what software can solve vs. what it can't. Instant-quote wizards for defined scope jobs (replace X, install Y) are the software wedge that doesn't require marketplace trust. Validates InstaBid Solo's angle.
- **LTD Potential**: N/A (this is market research, not a product)
