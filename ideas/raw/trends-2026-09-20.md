## Embedded Finance in Trades Software (Payments + Lending)

- **Category**: SMB Platform Consolidation / Legacy Disruption
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://startupfundraising.com/smb-vertical-saas-fundraising — "Own the SMB's system of record, layer embedded payments (2.6–3.5% take), then lending (Toast Capital, Shopify Capital, Square Loans model), then AI agents on top"
  - https://saaslatestnews.com/vertical-saas-startups-2026/ — Embedded payments as key differentiator for vertical SaaS in 2026
  - https://ai-for-contractors.com/news/netic-ai-23m-series-b-plumbers-roofers/ — Netic AI pricing at $199/month flat; embedded payments is the upsell path
  - https://www.constructionindustry.ai/funding/probook-40m-series-a-home-services-dispatch-ai/ — Probook's thesis: own dispatch → own the rest of the business
- **Description**: The playbook is now established: win a boring business's system of record (scheduling, dispatch, invoicing), then add embedded payments (take 2.6–3.5% of every invoice processed), then offer business lending ("you processed $50K last month; want a $20K advance?"). Toast did it for restaurants, Shopify for e-commerce, Square for retail. The trades are next. No dominant player has yet layered all three for HVAC/plumbing/electrical at the SMB level. The payments take alone at scale turns a $100/month software subscription into $500–$2,000/month per customer.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning services, pest control — any trade business processing >$10K/month in invoices
- **Market Timing**: Early (infrastructure exists; no dominant trades-focused player has fully executed the stack yet below ServiceTitan scale)
- **Example Products**:
  - Toast (restaurant analogue) — most successful execution of this model
  - Probook — https://www.constructionindustry.ai/funding/probook-40m-series-a-home-services-dispatch-ai/ (pursuing the model)
  - Docyt — AI bookkeeping + multi-location ops for SMBs
- **White Space**: A simple "collect payment on-site via Stripe" layer bolted onto any existing field service tool — most Jobber users still send paper invoices or email PDFs. The company that owns the moment of payment owns the relationship.
- **Speed to Market**: Medium (payments integration is well-understood via Stripe; the hard part is building the system-of-record that justifies the transaction)
- **LTD Fit**: Low (the payments model requires ongoing transaction volume; LTD doesn't fit)
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor payment software", "HVAC payment processing", "collect payment on job site app"
