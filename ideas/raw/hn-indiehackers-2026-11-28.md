## 1. Avenue — Online Booking Widget for Tradespeople

- **Source**: https://www.starterstory.com/stories/avenue-tom-holliday (Starter Story interview, Sep 2026)
- **Additional Links**: https://howtomakemoney.io/cases/avenue-tom-holliday-tradie-widget-55k/
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers / Starter Story
- **Type**: Revenue milestone case study
- **Engagement**: Widely shared; viral LinkedIn post from Starter Story
- **Revenue Data**: AU$85,000/month (~US$55K MRR) as of Sep 2026; launched Feb 2025; bootstrapped; costs ~$4K/mo Twilio + $500 Vapi + $150 AWS
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Trades — electricians, mechanics, plumbers, joiners, equipment hire
- **Core Value Prop**: A "Book Online" button on Google Business Profile + website widget so customers can book without calling. Tradespeople miss calls because they're physically on the job. Avenue captures those leads 24/7 with SMS confirmations, review requests, two-way SMS, and an AI phone agent.
- **Pricing**: Standard $199/mo, Premium $395/mo, AI Agent add-on $399/mo
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Still AU-focused; no dominant equivalent in the US/UK/EU market. Incumbents (Jobber, ServiceTitan) are full platforms, not lightweight entry-point widgets. No one owns the "Google Business Profile booking button for trades" wedge in most markets.
- **Our Angle**: Build the US equivalent of Avenue — hyper-focused on the Google LSA + Google Business booking entry point for HVAC/plumbing/electricians. Start with one trade vertical (HVAC) and one geo. Sell door-to-door or via cold outreach using ROI-based demos.
- **LTD Potential**: 2/5 (subscription model works better; $199-$399/mo is proven)

## Summary

### Top 5 Opportunities Ranked by Potential

**1. Field Manual Copilot / HVAC RAG Tool (Score: 5/5)**
The clearest technical opportunity with a validated market. ServiceTitan proves the demand; XOi proves the enterprise tier is funded. The 60,000 sub-5-tech HVAC/plumbing shops in the US that are too small for ServiceTitan but need field intelligence represent a wide-open $79/mo SaaS. Mobile PWA, photo-to-manual workflow, bootstrappable. High LTD appeal at $299-$499 for solo techs who hate subscriptions. The Startup Heist analysis lays out a credible path to $79K MRR.

**2. Avenue-Clone: Google LSA + Booking Widget for US Trades (Score: 5/5)**
Avenue is doing $55K USD MRR in Australia from a standing start in 18 months, selling door-to-door, with no coding background. The US market is 20x larger. No equivalent product dominates the "Google Business Profile booking button for trades" wedge in the US. The moat is integrations (Google LSA, SMS, AI phone agent) + trusted local brand. Defensible pricing at $199-$395/mo. This is the strongest direct "copy in a new market" play in this scan.

**3. Pest Control Vertical SaaS / AI Receptionist (Score: 4.5/5)**
The 448-point HN thread is the best validation signal in this scan — it proves intense founder interest and market awareness. A commenter confirmed $2M ARR is achievable. The pest control market is 60% consolidated but 40% remains fragmented indie operators. The right product is NOT a full FSM clone (incumbents exist) but a narrow AI receptionist + upsell automation layer ON TOP of existing workflow. The missed-call problem is identical to Avenue's — pest control techs are on routes, not at desks. Pricing: $99-$199/mo.

**4. Instant Online Estimator (Operator-Clone) for Pest Control / Property Maintenance (Score: 4/5)**
Operator reached $15K MRR in 1 year with a single-trade (HVAC) instant estimate widget. The same mechanism applies to any trade where the customer wants a price before calling. Pest control, pressure washing, landscaping, and property maintenance are each underserved by this model. The key differentiator: build trade-specific pricing logic (e.g., square footage × treatment type × frequency for pest control) that makes the estimate actually useful, not just a lead capture form. Wedge price: $79-$149/mo.

**5. Dental Front Desk Automation / Insurance Verification (Score: 4/5)**
Zirco.ai (HN, Mar 2026) identified the right problem with solid technical depth: 2-3 hours/day of manual insurance verification, $40-50K/year front desk staff turning over at 40%. The moat is integrations (Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft) + carrier portal automation (Availity + Playwright). The founder validated with 30+ discovery interviews. The wedge: sell insurance verification-only first at $299/mo, expand to full front desk later. The compliance complexity (HIPAA, BAA agreements, audit logging) keeps copycats out. Similar to Conductor — ugly legacy system = great defensible market.
