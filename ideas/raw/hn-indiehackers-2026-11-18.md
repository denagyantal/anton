## Prelay – Weather-based Work Planning for Outdoor Contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 2 points, fresh (2 days ago)
- **Revenue Data**: None disclosed — early launch
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Landscaping, roofing, paving, outdoor construction
- **Core Value Prop**: Monitors contractor job schedules for weather suitability across 62 activities / 12 trades. Sends weather-triggered alerts so owners can reschedule before paying crews who can't work (one landscaper said he wakes at 4am every day to check weather for every job site).
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Owner built ensemble weather models + ground-wetness model for soil conditions — far beyond a standard weather app. Existing tools: none that do this for trades scheduling. The key UX question they're debating: raw data ("35% chance of rain") vs actionable recommendation ("consider postponing this job") — raw → recommendation is an unsolved design problem.
- **Our Angle**: They're early and un-monetized. A tighter integration with FSM tools (Jobber, ServiceTitan) + opinionated scheduling recommendations (not just data) would differentiate. Could be an add-on feature for an FSM or a standalone vertical tool for roofing/landscaping.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 — weather APIs have ongoing costs; $49-79 LTD could work for small contractors

## AI Voice Receptionist for Trades (Conduit AI / Dolfyn / Rainslice)

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47138087 (Conduit AI)
- **Additional Links**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48580402 (Dolfyn AI agents for contractors), https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48769010 (Rainslice — autonomous home services)
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN / product posts
- **Engagement**: Multiple independent products launching in this space — strong market signal
- **Revenue Data**: IH post shows $300-800 MRR per client using white-label stack (Callin.io + n8n + Cal.com) — 80% margins
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: HVAC, plumbing, dental, salons, home cleaning, any appointment-based service business
- **Core Value Prop**: AI voice agent answers missed calls, books appointments, captures lead info. 80% of callers won't leave voicemail — they call a competitor. Replaces $30-40K/year receptionist.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Lots of early products here (Conduit AI, Dolfyn, Rainslice, Zirco for dental). The crowded entry point is "answer missed calls." Dolfyn's insight is what happens *after* — connecting the agent to CRM, dispatch, lead tracker automatically. Rainslice is running entire cleaning companies autonomously. Full "back-office as a service" is the defensible moat.
- **Our Angle**: Most competitors stop at call answering. Build for a single vertical (e.g., HVAC only) with deep integrations to their existing FSM tools + automated dispatch + follow-up. Voice → schedule → dispatch → invoice as a single flow.
- **LTD Potential**: 2 — MRR model is natural fit given ongoing AI costs; LTD less suitable

## TechSplit – Technician Commission & Payout Calculation

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/TechSplit
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Product page / early stage
- **Engagement**: Early — still in customer discovery
- **Revenue Data**: None disclosed — actively talking to customers
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Mobile grooming, home inspection, lawn care, pool service, mobile detailing
- **Core Value Prop**: Log each job once; tool calculates technician commission, tips, tiered rates, split pools, and pays out automatically for any date range. Removes the weekly Excel nightmare for service business owners with field crews.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Every multi-tech service business (mobile grooming, lawn care, pool service) manually calculates payouts weekly. No dedicated tool exists — it's always a spreadsheet or QuickBooks hack. TechSplit is early and unproven. The problem is universal across field service.

Key insight: technicians check their own payouts on mobile → build a tech-facing app + owner dashboard. Payment processing (take a cut of payouts) is the revenue model beyond SaaS.

## Tamper-Proof Work Verification (Cryptographic Before/After Photos)

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47086864
- **Additional Links**: yourbeforeafterwork.netlify.app
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: 4 points — small but interesting niche concept
- **Revenue Data**: None — builder is selling full IP/source code
- **Boring Business Score**: 4
- **Target Industry**: Contractors, cleaners, delivery, property management
- **Core Value Prop**: Mobile app generating cryptographically verifiable proof of completed work: before/after photos hashed with SHA-256, Ed25519 signature, RFC 3161 timestamp, GPS coordinates. Any tampering invalidates the signature. Client-side verification, no backend trust required.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Property managers disputing cleaning quality, contractors fighting payment disputes — these happen constantly. The pain is real. The cryptographic approach is clever but possibly over-engineered for the market. A simpler "tamper-evident job completion report" that's legally admissible could be the wedge.
- **Our Angle**: Simplify to: "Job Proof" — take before/after photos, they get automatically timestamped + GPS-tagged + signed, one-click PDF report emailed to customer. Target cleaning companies and property managers first (high dispute frequency). Key feature: customer signs off on the PDF report digitally = payment trigger.
- **LTD Potential**: 5 — perfect LTD: one-time tool, clear value, no ongoing costs beyond storage
