Existing tools charge $35-60/truck/month making a 5-truck fleet pay $175-300/mo. An owner-operator-first design with zero professional installation and 30-min setup is underserved.
- **Speed to Market**: 4-6 weeks for an MVP (ELD hardware integration APIs exist via Geotab/Samsara SDK; IFTA is a known formula)
