## Crewbird — Field Service CRM with Google LSA Integration

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418273
- **Additional Links**: https://getcrewbird.com
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Ask HN — What are you working on? (November 2026)
- **Engagement**: Active thread, multiple commenters expressing strong interest
- **Revenue Data**: "quickly approaching 3x ROAS" for founder's dad's carpet cleaning company; still early commercial stage
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Home services / carpet cleaning / field service businesses
- **Core Value Prop**: FSM + CRM for service businesses built with deep Google Local Service Ads (LSA) integration, tracks every dollar to determine ROAS. Founded after founder's dad's Windows CRM was discontinued and Google LSA dashboard proved inadequate for attribution.

- **Our Angle**: Thin, opinionated FSM + CRM focused exclusively on the Google LSA→job attribution loop. Skip the bloat — just bookings, dispatch, revenue tracking tied to ad spend. First mover on LSA-native field service.

## Prelay — Weather-Based Work Planning for Outdoor Contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (November 2026)
- **Engagement**: 2 points, 0 comments (very fresh launch)
- **Revenue Data**: None yet — just launched
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Landscaping, roofing, outdoor trades
- **Core Value Prop**: Monitors contractor job schedules against site-specific weather forecasts for 62 activities across 12 trades. Auto-notifies when to reschedule before the 6am "pay them anyway" threshold. Ground-wetness model for soil conditions.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Contractor owners wake up at 4am checking weather apps for each job site manually. No existing tool maps scheduled jobs + per-site weather + trade-specific suitability thresholds. Generic weather apps can't tell a roofer vs. a landscaper whether to send crews.

Lean into actionable decisions rather than just data. Integrate with popular scheduling tools (Jobber, ServiceTitan) for schedule-aware weather alerts.

## Cross-Cutting Theme: "Boring Business" Software Gaps Still Wide Open

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-analyzed-39-000-software-complaints-the-best-micro-saas-gaps-are-all-in-boring-industries-801c41685b
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Research / data analysis post (July 2026)
- **Engagement**: Active IH post from Om Patel
- **Revenue Data**: N/A — research findings
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Construction/field service, payroll, HR, accounting, manufacturing
- **Core Value Prop**: Analysis of 39,000+ software complaints filtered to: severity >3.5/5, 3+ companies reporting same issue, no focused micro-SaaS already solving it. Six surviving categories:
  1. **Construction/field service mobile** — 4.0/5 severity, 5 companies: photo uploads + GPS tagging + form inspections, instant office sync. ~$29/user/month to 5-20 person subcontractor teams.
  2. Payroll admin, HR, accounting also surfaced (less relevant to boring trades)
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The key insight: "If your tool saves a field service manager 5 hours/week at $75/hour, that's $1,500/month recovered. Charging $149/month is a rounding error." Low-churn because switching cost is fear of getting it wrong, not money. The filter methodology itself is worth running against fresh complaint data.
- **Our Angle**: The mobile-first field inspection tool for 5-20 person subcontractor crews is a direct, validated opportunity: photo + GPS + form + sync, $29/user/month, no Gantt charts or invoicing. Classic "half of ServiceTitan at a tenth of the price" play.
