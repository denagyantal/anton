## AI Voice Receptionists for Home Services Trades

- **Category**: Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Xi8-rezora-io-the-self-improving-ai-receptionist-actually-built-for-nontechnical-users (YC launch, Nov 2026)
  - https://www.newswire.com/news/broccoli-raises-52m-led-by-khosla-ventures-to-help-home-services-businesses (Broccoli $52M Series A+B, Oct 2026)
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/serviceagent (ServiceAgent PH launch, 2025, #6 day rank)
  - https://tradesusecases.com/use-cases/tuck-howell-broccoli-ai-after-hours-overflow-calls-plumbing-hvac/ (case study: 2,254 calls handled, $405K revenue booked)
  - https://agentmoneyboard.com/e/quickvoc/ (QuickVOC: $817/mo MRR, launched Nov 2026)
- **Description**: A dense cluster of AI voice agent companies specifically trained on home-service industry data (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical) are hitting the market simultaneously. These are not generic AI call handlers — they understand job types, emergency triage, seasonal demand, and booking flows. The core problem: 27% of home-service calls go unanswered, a receptionist costs $40K/yr, and the owner is on-site 60+ hours/week. Broccoli ($52M, 1,500+ businesses, 10M+ conversations handled) proves VC and enterprise adoption. Rezora IO (YC W26, $23K MRR, 20% WoW growth) proves indie/self-serve traction. Multiple players are validating the same market simultaneously.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, general home services
- **Market Timing**: Growing — VC money + YC + indie builder activity all at once
- **Example Products**:
  - Broccoli: https://www.newswire.com/news/broccoli-raises-52m-led-by-khosla-ventures-to-help-home-services-businesses
  - Rezora IO: https://rezora.io/ — $589/mo, YC, 40+ businesses, $23K MRR
  - ServiceAgent: https://serviceagent.ai/ — 100+ hrs/mo saved, industry-trained
  - QuickVOC: https://agentmoneyboard.com/e/quickvoc/ — $817 MRR, early-stage indie
- **White Space**: Niche sub-verticals within home services still underserved — pool & spa, chimney sweep, appliance repair. Also: bilingual (Spanish/English) AI voice agents for immigrant-owned trade businesses. Outbound follow-up and re-engagement AI (quotes sent, never answered) rather than just inbound answering.
- **Speed to Market**: 2-4 weeks for a narrowly scoped niche (e.g., pool service only), using existing voice AI platforms
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — service businesses will buy LTD if sub-$500 and proven. Higher ticket subscriptions ($300-600/mo) are already working.
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC answering service", "missed call HVAC", "AI receptionist for plumbers", "answering service for contractors"

## AI-Native Operating Systems for Trade Contractors

- **Category**: SMB Platform Consolidation + Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://daltonmills.com/resources/fieldnotes/dalton-mills-raises-9m-to-build-the-platform-for-trades (Dalton Mills $9.2M raise, Jun 2026)
  - https://www.kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn (Quinn $4M seed, Nov 2026)
  - https://biltix.ai/blog/introducing-biltix (Biltix launch, Oct 2026 — MEP trade contractors)
  - https://linkedin.com/company/clevra-ai (Clevra.ai — agentic back office for 50+ trades, SF, 2026)
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny (Mainteny PH launch — AI operating system for maintenance companies)
- **Description**: The next generation of trades software is moving beyond "FSM + invoicing" toward full AI operating systems that run entire business functions autonomously. Dalton Mills frames itself as "Replit for the trades" — letting owners build bespoke software without code. Clevra goes further: AI agents proactively run the front desk, follow up on quotes, dispatch, invoice, and collect — without the owner prompting each action. Biltix targets MEP (mechanical/electrical/plumbing) sub-contractors specifically, with AI that reads drawings and runs takeoff. The common thread: replacing the patchwork of 6-10 disconnected tools with one agentic system that acts, not just stores.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, MEP sub-contractors, roofing, general home services, maintenance companies
- **Market Timing**: Early-to-Growing — multiple well-funded entrants, but no clear winner yet
- **Example Products**:
  - Dalton Mills: https://daltonmills.com/ — $9.2M seed, "Replit for trades"
  - Clevra: https://clevra.ai/ — agentic AI, free to start, 50+ trades
  - Biltix: https://biltix.ai/ — MEP-specific, AI reads drawings (BRIX), early-access pricing
  - Mainteny: https://mainteny.com/ — AI back office for maintenance companies
- **White Space**: Mid-market trades (5-50 employees) who have outgrown Jobber but can't afford ServiceTitan. Specialty sub-verticals: plumbing-specific or electrical-specific AI OS rather than "all trades." Integration-layer tools connecting existing FSM software to AI agents (rather than replacing everything).
- **Speed to Market**: 4-8 weeks for a narrow vertical with AI agents layered onto existing FSM data
- **LTD Fit**: Low for full platform; High for specific AI agent modules (e.g., "quote follow-up AI agent" as a standalone add-on)
- **Content Strategy**: "field service management software", "HVAC business software", "replace ServiceTitan", "trade contractor software", "AI for contractors"

Three distinct new entrants are attacking it from different angles in 2026: tab32 Alpine (17 AI agents covering scheduling, recall, insurance, billing at $95/mo) is positioning as cheap + AI-complete; Isaac PracticeOS (AI-native built on a clinical AI model that scored 100% on USMLE) is positioning as "the system that thinks" at $299/mo, backed by 8K dentists who funded the company themselves; Kairos Health (YC) targets the front desk specifically. The incumbent stack costs $1,500-2,000/mo combined. All three are targeting independent practices who can't leverage enterprise contracts.
- **Target Industries**: Independent dental practices, new practice owners, dental DSOs (smaller ones)
- **Market Timing**: Early — VC and YC attention just arriving, legacy software (Eaglesoft, Dentrix) still dominant
- **Example Products**:
  - tab32 Alpine: https://tab32.com/ — $95/mo, 17 AI agents, 14-day free trial, launched Nov 2026

- **White Space**: Chiropractic, optometry, veterinary — same paper-heavy workflows, legacy software, independent practice model. Dental specialty practices (orthodontics, oral surgery) with specific workflow needs. Insurance appeals automation as a standalone tool (not full PMS replacement).
- **Speed to Market**: 6-12 weeks for a narrowly scoped dental add-on (e.g., insurance appeal drafter, recall automation only)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — dentists earn well and pay for tools, but prefer subscriptions. LTD for a specific module (not full PMS) is viable at $299-499.

## Skilled Trades Workforce Training & Compliance Tech

- **Category**: Vertical AI + Compliance & Regulation Tech
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn (Quinn $4M seed, Nov 2026 — AI workforce OS for skilled trades)
  - https://crewrise.io/ (Crew Rise — voice-first AI mentor for electrical apprentices, NEC-aligned, DOL compliance)
  - https://ironpathpro.com/ (IronPath — training and certification tracking for specialty trade crews)
- **Description**: The skilled trades are facing an acute workforce crisis: the existing workforce is aging out faster than apprentices are replacing them. Simultaneously, federal apprenticeship compliance (DOL Circular 2026-01) is tightening requirements for training documentation. Quinn ($4M seed) ingests a company's own SOPs, manuals, and field notes and converts them into mobile-first interactive training with compliance tracking and multi-language delivery. Crew Rise takes a novel approach: a voice-first AI mentor that an electrical apprentice talks to via a single earbud, hands-free on the job, with automatic sponsor dashboard logging for DOL compliance. This is a category that existing L&D platforms (Coursera, Lessonly) completely ignore.
- **Target Industries**: Electrical contractors, HVAC companies, plumbing shops, construction — any trades business with apprenticeship programs or multi-tech crews
- **Market Timing**: Early — regulatory tailwind (DOL compliance), workforce crisis driving urgency
- **Example Products**:
  - Quinn: https://www.kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn — $4M seed, Kalos Ventures
  - Crew Rise: https://crewrise.io/ — voice-first AI tutor, NEC-aligned, DOL circular compliant
  - IronPath: https://ironpathpro.com/ — certification tracking for specialty trades
- **White Space**: Plumbing and HVAC apprenticeship AI mentors (Crew Rise only covers electrical). Multilingual training (Spanish-first) for trades companies with predominantly Spanish-speaking crews. Certification renewal reminders and auto-filing as a standalone SaaS.
- **Speed to Market**: 4-8 weeks for a certification tracking / compliance reminder tool. Voice AI tutor takes longer (trade-specific knowledge base required).
- **LTD Fit**: High — trades business owners are skeptical of recurring SaaS; a one-time compliance tool they can trust is very appealing. $149-299 LTD viable.
- **Content Strategy**: "apprenticeship training software", "OSHA compliance tracking trades", "electrical apprentice training app", "DOL apprenticeship compliance"
