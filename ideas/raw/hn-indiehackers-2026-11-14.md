## Key Market Observations

**Dominant themes this scan:**

1. **Google LSA integration gap**: Local service businesses are spending heavily on Google Local Service Ads but no FSM/CRM tool is built around LSA as a primary channel. Huge opportunity for a purpose-built tool.

2. **Voice AI for trades**: Multiple products (Conkoa, Clawbolt, Zirco) attacking the same problem — trades workers can't type on job sites. Voice-first field data entry is validated and early. The first product to nail the UX wins a large market.

3. **Legacy software replacement wave**: Desktop CRMs being discontinued (Windows EOL, Intuit killing old versions) is forcing thousands of small service businesses onto new tools right now. Active pull vs. push.

4. **Follow-up automation for local services**: CraftBoop, HandyPay, and others confirm that local service businesses are terrible at follow-up and will pay to automate it. Review requests + rebooking reminders is the most monetizable sequence.

5. **Boring SaaS infrastructure**: Conductor ($46K MRR) proves that "modern API for a legacy system" is a viable, inbound-only business in unglamorous verticals. Many other legacy platforms have the same gap.
