  - Stormy (YC 2025, #1 PH day rank): https://www.producthunt.com/products/stormy-influencer-marketing-ai-agent
  - ServiceAgent (AI voice for home services, 2025): https://www.producthunt.com/products/serviceagent
  - Mainteny (AI OS for maintenance cos, 2026): https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny

- **Description**: Multiple well-funded startups are racing to become the "AI operating system" for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and roofing companies. The pattern is consistent — AI-native dispatch, intake, scheduling, invoicing, and customer messaging replacing fragmented point solutions. Probook alone ($40M a16z/Sequoia) processes 74% of home service calls without human intervention. The first wave of AI in trades was point solutions (one for voice, one for outbound); the second wave consolidates everything. Even bootstrapped operators are winning: one HVAC/plumber AI voice startup hit $979K TTM revenue, $360K profit, 85% growth.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, drain cleaning, solar
- **Market Timing**: Growing (VC is pouring in, but most SMBs still not using AI)
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook: https://probook.ai (dispatch-first AI OS, a16z/Sequoia)
  - Sophiie AI: https://sophiie.ai (Australian, 10-15% MoM growth, expanding to US)
  - UpSmith: https://upsmith.com (200+ operators, ServiceTitan certified partner)
  - Stormy: https://stormy.ai (YC-backed, field service ERP + AI agents)
  - ServiceAgent: https://serviceagent.ai (AI voice for HVAC/plumbing/solar)
  - SayDoo: https://saydoo.com (built inside a real roofing company)
- **White Space**: Still significant gaps in: (1) AI for small shops (1-5 techs) — most funded players target mid-to-large operators; (2) vertical-specific AI (pest control, cleaning services) rather than generic "home services"; (3) AI that connects technicians in the field (not just the office)
- **Speed to Market**: 4-8 weeks for a focused MVP (voice booking + scheduling for one vertical)
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — service businesses pay monthly but would pay $299-499 LTD for core features if proven
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC scheduling software", "plumbing dispatch software", "AI for home service businesses", "field service automation", "ServiceTitan alternative"

## Trades Workforce Training & Compliance Tech

- **Category**: Compliance & Regulation Tech / Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - Quinn $4M seed (Nov 2026, Kalos Ventures): https://www.kalosventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-quinn
  - Skillmaker $4.5M oversubscribed seed (Jun 2026): https://skillmaker.ai/tech-company-oversubscribed-seed-round-closed/
  - Navigate.AI $25M (May 2026, Opendoor co-founder): https://www.housingwire.com/articles/a-start-up-with-a-deep-tech-pedigree-launches-ai-to-train-the-trades/
  - CertChain £750K (UK construction compliance): https://haatch.com/latest-news/haatch-backs-certchain-in-750k-round-to-transform-construction-workforce-skills-compliance
  - Trayd $10M Series A (Mar 2026, YC-backed, $15M total): https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/03/trayd-construction-payroll-workforce-management-platform-funding-specialty-trade-contractors-anna-berger/
  - Dili $15M Series A (Aug 2026, Khosla Ventures): https://www.techcompanynews.com/dili-raises-15m-in-series-a-funding-round/
- **Description**: 1.7 million skilled trades openings per year through 2035, with only 55 workers trained per 100 needed. This labor crisis is creating an urgent market for: (1) AI-powered training platforms purpose-built for trades (not retrofitted corporate L&D); (2) compliance automation as Davis-Bacon Act updates and IRA federal project requirements create new paper-trail obligations; (3) workforce management tools with skills passports and certification tracking. Trayd reduced payroll processing from 14 hrs/week to 27 minutes for specialty contractors. Dili is protecting $1B+ in tax credits across 700 federal projects.
- **Target Industries**: Construction (specialty trades), HVAC, plumbing, automotive repair, electrical, infrastructure/utilities
- **Market Timing**: Early-to-Growing (regulatory tailwinds from 2023 Davis-Bacon updates + IRA creating urgency)
- **Example Products**:
  - Quinn: https://quinntrades.com (mobile-first training OS, $4M seed)
  - Skillmaker: https://skillmaker.ai (XR + AI training, NAPA partnership, $4.5M seed)
  - Navigate.AI: https://navigate.ai ($25M, Meta glasses + phone video for construction training)
  - CertChain: https://certchain.co.uk (skills passport + compliance for UK construction)
  - Trayd: https://trayd.com (payroll + compliance for specialty trade contractors, $10M Series A)
  - Dili: https://dili.ai (federal project compliance automation, $15M from Khosla)
- **White Space**: Affordable compliance + training tools for small trade shops (under 20 employees) — most funded solutions target mid-to-large contractors. Specific gaps: (1) OSHA compliance tracking + e-learning for HVAC/plumbing SMBs; (2) multi-language training (many trade workforces are Spanish-speaking); (3) lightweight "skills passport" for individual technicians to own and carry
- **Speed to Market**: 4-6 weeks MVP for compliance checklist + certification tracking; training content harder
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — training platforms work better as subscriptions, but compliance tools could do LTD
- **Content Strategy**: "trade apprenticeship software", "construction compliance automation", "OSHA training app for contractors", "prevailing wage software", "skilled trades workforce management"

## Voice-First Estimation & Invoicing for Trades On-Site

- **Category**: Mobile-First Field Tools / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate-Strong
- **Sources**:
  - SMASH Voice to Invoice (2026, PH #49): https://www.producthunt.com/products/smash-voice-to-invoice
  - Handoff AI (AI estimating for remodeling/handyman): https://www.producthunt.com/products/handoff-ai
  - Feldy (YC launch, Aug 2026, 200+ paying customers): https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SiZ-feldy-field-first-ai-for-home-services-and-contractors/
  - Angebotsmeister (German trades, voice → quote → invoice, Oct 2026): https://www.producthunt.com/products/angebotsmeister
  - Jon ONeill (plumbing co. owner, open-sourced 9 AI automations): https://www.unrollnow.com/status/2099323544366916046
  - Tutti (ERP for contractors via WhatsApp/chat, $1,202 MRR, 35 subs): https://trustmrr.com/startup/tutti
- **Description**: Tradespeople lose jobs because estimates take 2-3 days to arrive. The fix is voice-first tools that convert on-site job walkthrough audio/video into structured estimates in under a minute. Feldy uses computer vision + LiDAR to scan job sites and auto-generate estimates. SMASH uses voice to build quotes from the contractor's own rate card on-site. Angebotsmeister is doing this specifically for German trades (localized, voice-first, no typing). Tutti is doing ERP entirely through WhatsApp chat for Spanish-speaking contractors. The category is very early with fragmented players — no dominant tool yet for small trade shops.
- **Target Industries**: Remodeling, painting, roofing, landscaping, HVAC install, plumbing, electrical, cleaning
- **Market Timing**: Early (problem is well understood, solutions just emerging in 2025-2026)
- **Example Products**:
  - SMASH: https://smashinvoices.com (voice → quote → invoice, mobile + Chrome extension)
  - Handoff AI: https://handoff.ai (AI estimating for remodeling/handyman, real-time local pricing)
  - Feldy: https://feldy.ai (3D scans + AI estimates + field docs, 200+ paying customers)
  - Angebotsmeister: https://angebots-meister.de (German trades, voice → quote)
  - Tutti: https://trustmrr.com/startup/tutti (WhatsApp ERP for trade contractors, Spain/Mexico/Argentina)
- **White Space**: Voice-to-quote specifically for HVAC service calls (diagnostic + repair estimates), Spanish-language version for US trade contractors, integration with ServiceTitan/Jobber/Housecall Pro to push estimates directly
- **Speed to Market**: 2-4 weeks for voice-to-quote MVP using Whisper + LLM + PDF generation
- **LTD Fit**: High — contractors hate recurring fees and would pay $99-199 LTD for a tool they use daily
- **Content Strategy**: "contractor estimate app", "voice to invoice app", "quick quote tool for contractors", "on-site estimating software", "construction quote generator"

## Technician Retention & Performance Pay Tools

- **Signal Strength**: Moderate-Strong
- **Sources**:
  - ShareWillow $8.5M seed (Oct 2026): https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/10/sharewillow-home-field-service-technicians-performance-pay-platform-ai-agents-skilled-trades-ryan-shank/
  - The Nut Report (decision tool for service business owners on HCP/Jobber/ServiceTitan): https://www.producthunt.com/p/introduce-yourself/hey-product-hunt-i-m-chad-founder-of-the-nut-report-here-s-why-i-built-it
- **Description**: 1.7 million skilled trade openings per year through 2035, only 55 workers trained per 100 needed. Most AI for trades targets the office — but technician retention is the actual bottleneck. ShareWillow (8x revenue growth in 18 months, $8.5M seed) connects to ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro/Jobber and turns job data into real-time performance pay plans. Average tech on a plan earns $1,334 more/month. $2M in performance pay processed in September 2026 alone. The adjacent opportunity: decision-support dashboards for service business owners who run on HCP/Jobber/ServiceTitan but can't quickly see if they're on track (The Nut Report concept).
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, drain cleaning, roofing, pest control — any home service with field technicians
- **Market Timing**: Early (ShareWillow is the pioneer; most shops still calculating performance pay manually or not at all)
- **Example Products**:
  - ShareWillow: https://sharewillow.com (performance pay + AI coaching for technicians, $8.5M)
  - The Nut Report: (pre-launch, targeting HCP/Jobber/ServiceTitan users)
- **White Space**: (1) Lightweight performance pay tool for small shops (1-10 techs) that don't need ShareWillow's enterprise features; (2) technician-facing mobile app showing real-time earnings per job (white-label for shop owners); (3) "job coaching" before service call — briefing techs on customer history, upsell opportunities, and expected issues before they arrive
- **Speed to Market**: 4-6 weeks MVP for a ServiceTitan/Jobber webhook → performance pay calculator
- **LTD Fit**: Low — this is clearly a per-tech subscription business; technician retention ROI is high
- **Content Strategy**: "technician performance pay", "field service employee retention", "HVAC technician compensation", "ServiceTitan performance tracking"

## "Diagonal SaaS" — Custom Software Platforms for Niche Trade Verticals

- **Category**: Legacy Software Disruption / SMB Platform Consolidation
- **Signal Strength**: Moderate
- **Sources**:
  - Dalton Mills $9.2M raise + Matchstick VC thesis: https://www.matchstick.vc/companies/dalton-mills
  - Billdr $4.4M CAD for GC operating system (Jan 2026): https://betakit.com/billdr-relaunches-with-4-4-million-cad-to-build-operating-system-for-construction/
  - Fieldmobi (AI-customizable field ERP, PH 2025): https://www.producthunt.com/products/fieldmobi
  - Greenleaf PM replacing Salesforce with Claude-built custom app: https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/property-managers-claude-replace-pm-software-2026/
  - Jon ONeill plumbing company AI automation thread: https://unrollnow.com/status/2087635639701573962
- **Description**: The "diagonal SaaS" thesis (coined by Matchstick VC) holds that AI enables custom software for niche/long-tail industries that existing vertical SaaS can't economically serve. Dalton Mills is building a horizontal OS for trades (CRM, quoting, dispatch, invoicing) where each contractor builds their own vertical customizations on top — "Replit for trades." Meanwhile, mid-market businesses are already doing this: a property management firm built their own CRM with Claude/Replit for $300/mo (replacing $100K Salesforce). A plumbing company owner automated work order intake across 6 pieces of software with AI agents in one weekend. Billdr is doing it for general contractors ($4.4M seed). The implication: industries with niche workflows (window contractors, specialty glazing, irrigation, pest control sub-niches) are ready for lightweight custom ops tools.
- **Target Industries**: Any specialty trade sub-niche: window/glazing contractors, irrigation, pest control, specialty commercial HVAC, concrete flatwork, industrial cleaning
- **Market Timing**: Early (AI development tools just crossed the threshold where this is feasible at small scale)
- **Example Products**:
  - Dalton Mills: https://daltonmills.com (Replit for trades, $9.2M)
  - Billdr: https://billdr.pro (vertical SaaS OS for small-to-mid GCs, $4.4M CAD seed)
  - Fieldmobi: https://fieldmobi.com (AI-customizable field ERP)
- **White Space**: Individual vertical sub-niches where no modern software exists — examples identified by research: window/glazing contractors (xTriam serves them on Salesforce, very expensive), irrigation companies, specialty commercial maintenance, concrete flatwork contractors
- **Speed to Market**: The play here is picking one hyper-specific niche and building in 2-4 weeks, not building a platform
- **LTD Fit**: High for focused point solutions within a niche ($299-599 LTD very viable)
- **Content Strategy**: "[niche] software", "[niche] business management", "[niche] estimating software" — go very specific

## AI Voice Agents for Small Service Businesses (Bootstrapped Opportunity)

- **Category**: Vertical AI / Mobile-First Field Tools
- **Signal Strength**: Strong (validated with real revenue)
- **Sources**:
  - Andrew Gazdecki tweet about bootstrapped AI voice agent: $979K TTM revenue, $360K profit, 85% growth, now on acquire.com: https://xitter.cf/agazdecki/status/2114778346089283919
  - Phena (UK AI receptionist for small businesses, £29/mo): https://getphena.com/
  - ServiceAgent PH launch (HVAC/plumbing/solar, 2025): https://www.producthunt.com/products/serviceagent
  - Frontdesk AI PH (10K+ businesses): https://www.producthunt.com/products/frontdesk-ai
  - Jon ONeill using Muse agent to run plumbing company from bed at 6am: https://twiscan.com/en/x/HouseHackerJon/2102036626478420315
  - SignalLEMO (AI outreach for field service contractors): https://www.producthunt.com/products/signallemo-ai-outreach-made-simple
- **Description**: The clearest validated bootstrapped opportunity in this whole space: AI voice agents answering calls for plumbers and HVAC companies. One bootstrapped startup (not named, on acquire.com) built this and hit $979K TTM revenue, $360K annual profit, 85% growth — all from answering calls, booking appointments, and chasing overdue payments. 40% of appointments booked after hours. The moat is industry-specific training: an AI that knows what an "emergency call" vs "routine maintenance" means for HVAC, that understands technician availability, that integrates with ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro. Phena (UK, £29/mo) is doing this for all small businesses. The white space is industry-specific voice agents for specific trades (pest control, window cleaning, electrical — not just HVAC/plumbing).
- **Target Industries**: Any trade business that gets phone calls: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control, window cleaning, cleaning services, landscaping, locksmith
- **Market Timing**: Growing (early entrants have proven the business model, most of the market still untapped)
- **Example Products**:
  - ServiceAgent: https://serviceagent.ai
  - Phena: https://getphena.com
  - Frontdesk AI: https://myaifrontdesk.com
  - Bootstrapped unnamed: on acquire.com (search for HVAC/plumbing AI voice)
- **White Space**: (1) Pest control-specific AI voice agent (highly seasonal, appointment-driven, specific terminology); (2) window cleaning/cleaning services AI scheduler; (3) locksmith dispatch AI (emergency/non-emergency routing is critical); (4) bundling AI voice + AI quote follow-up for a single vertical
- **Speed to Market**: 2-3 weeks MVP using existing voice AI infra (VAPI, Retell, Twilio + LLM)
- **LTD Fit**: Low for end users (they'd rather pay monthly); but re-seller/agency model could work with LTD
- **Content Strategy**: "AI receptionist for [trade]", "missed call solution for HVAC", "appointment booking AI for plumbers", "answering service for contractors"
