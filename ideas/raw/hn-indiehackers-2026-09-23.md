## Zirco.ai – AI Front Desk Employee for Dental Practices

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: ~1 upvote, 3 comments (early, beta stage)
- **Revenue Data**: None — currently in beta, 30+ discovery interviews completed
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices
- **Core Value Prop**: Automates the entire dental front desk workflow: voice AI for inbound calls (Vapi), insurance benefit verification via carrier APIs + Playwright automation, direct appointment booking into Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft, and SMS/email reminders. One front desk employee costs $40–50K/yr with 40% annual turnover — this replaces that.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Insurance verification is a 2–3 hr/day manual process. No indie solution addresses this end-to-end. Existing solutions are enterprise-priced. The HIPAA + multi-tenant complexity creates a real moat. Comment thread noted the website was initially down — product is rough, market is real.
- **Our Angle**: Rather than full AI employee (heavy), target just the insurance verification pain point as a standalone tool at $99–199/mo per practice — drastically simpler to build, still saves 2+ hrs/day. Or focus on automated recall/reminder campaigns as a lighter wedge (no HIPAA exposure).
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 — HIPAA compliance adds friction for LTD model, but the problem is so valuable that a lifetime deal is feasible for a focused sub-feature.

- **Platform**: HN
