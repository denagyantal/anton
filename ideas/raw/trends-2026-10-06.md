  - https://www.saasmag.com/vertical-ai-agents-eating-horizontal-saas/

Probook raised $40M (a16z + Sequoia co-signing) by building an AI OS that automates call intake, scheduling, dispatch, customer messaging, and follow-up. Avoca hit a $1B valuation on $125M funding doing AI voice agents for the same trades. Both pitch the same insight: the dispatch board is the brain, and no prior software was built around it — they were built around the technician or the invoice.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, home services, private-equity-backed trades roll-ups
- **Market Timing**: Growing — institutional money is arriving (a16z, Sequoia, Kleiner, Meritech, General Catalyst), but the vast majority of trade shops are still undigitized
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook: https://www.probook.ai (dispatch-first AI OS, $40M Series A)
  - Avoca: https://www.avoca.ai ($125M, $1B valuation, voice AI for HVAC/plumbing/roofing)
  - Mainteny: https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny (AI OS for maintenance companies, PH launch 2026)
  - Housecall Pro trade-specific packages: https://www.contractormag.com/technology/news/55391529
- **White Space**: Smaller, sub-$5M ARR trade shops can't afford Probook/Avoca enterprise contracts. A lighter-weight, $99-$299/mo AI dispatch assistant targeting 1-10 truck shops is largely unserved. Also: specialty trades (septic, fire suppression, commercial refrigeration) are ignored by every major player.
- **Speed to Market**: 4-8 weeks for an MVP AI phone agent + basic scheduling; harder to build a full dispatch OS
- **LTD Fit**: Moderate — trade shop owners appreciate upfront pricing; LTD viable for the voice/receptionist layer, less so for the full OS
- **Content Strategy**: "HVAC dispatch software", "plumbing scheduling AI", "AI answering service for contractors", "home service business software"

## Meta-Trend: Vertical AI Economics Are Now Viable at the SMB Layer

- **Category**: Market Structure / Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.saasmag.com/vertical-ai-agents-eating-horizontal-saas/
  - https://insights.euclid.vc/p/the-vertical-report-2026-full-version
  - https://techaisle.com/blog/668-2026-smb-top-10-business-issues-tech-priorities-agentic-ai-shift
- **Description**: The Euclid Vertical Report 2026 and SaaSMag confirm the structural shift: vertical AI agents now achieve 2.3x higher ROI than horizontal tools, with 71% value retention at 6 months vs. 32% for generic LLM tools. The critical unlock for boring-business SaaS: fragmented SMB markets previously too small for traditional SaaS are now economically viable for vertical AI because the pricing changes — you sell against a labor cost ($50K/yr office manager), not against another software tool ($99/mo). Industries with high labor costs, compliance requirements, and measurable outcomes are the frontier. The defensible moat is permissioned access to industry-specific data (HVAC job histories, vet clinical records, construction labor data).
- **Implication for builders**: If you can access one boring industry's data and automate one expensive job function (dispatcher, estimator, office manager, compliance officer), the economics work even at 50-100 customers.
