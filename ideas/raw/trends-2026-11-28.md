## Summary

The boring-business SaaS wave is deepening in two directions simultaneously: massive VC money is consolidating around "AI operating systems" for home services trades ($40M–$125M rounds), while a parallel micro-SaaS layer is exploding with solo founders building hyper-vertical tools at $29–$99/month that replace manual admin for plumbers, electricians, contractors, dental practices, and funeral homes. The most underexplored white space remains specialty verticals one layer below trades — funeral homes, independent veterinary practices, independent pharmacies, and specialty sub-trades (septic, grease trap, generator maintenance) — where legacy software is 20+ years old and there's almost no VC attention yet.
