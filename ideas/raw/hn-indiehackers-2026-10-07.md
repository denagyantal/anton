## Zirco.ai — AI Front Desk Employee for Dental Practices

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Additional Links**: https://zircoai.vercel.app/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: Early-stage (3 comments), but rich founder post with deep technical detail
- **Revenue Data**: Beta, 30+ dental discovery calls completed, not yet disclosed
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices
- **Core Value Prop**: AI handles the full front desk workflow end-to-end — inbound call answering (voice AI via Vapi), insurance verification across 10+ carrier portals (Playwright automation + Availity), direct appointment booking into Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft, SMS/email reminders. Replaces a $40-50K/year front desk employee who churns at 40% annually.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Insurance verification alone wastes 2-3 hours/day per practice. Competitors (Weave, Swell) address communication only; no one owns the full workflow loop including insurance verification. HIPAA compliance and multi-tenant row-level security are non-trivial barriers to entry.
- **Our Angle**: Could position as "insurance verification as a service" first — the most painful specific step — before expanding to full front desk replacement. Or build the same workflow for other high-churn, high-admin healthcare verticals (optometry, chiropractic, physical therapy).
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 — healthcare compliance adds friction, but very high willingness to pay; practices currently spending $40-50K/year on human labor would pay $5K-15K/year easily

- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Small trucking, HVAC/plumbing fleets, landscaping companies, delivery businesses with 5-25 vehicles
- **Core Value Prop**: Fleet inspection checklists, maintenance scheduling/reminders, compliance paperwork (DOT for commercial vehicles), driver DVIRs, spare parts tracking. All the things a $2M/year trucking company needs but doesn't need a $500/month enterprise platform for.

- **LTD Potential**: 4/5 — strong LTD case, easy G2/Trustpilot review collection from happy small operators

## Recurring Themes & Underserved Market Signals

### Industries with broken software that emerged from research:
1. **Dental front office** — insurance verification is deeply painful; existing AI tools are point solutions
2. **Small trades (0-5 employees)** — quote/invoice generation is either manual or overpriced
3. **Small fleet operators (5-25 vehicles)** — compliance, maintenance, inspections; no good middle tier
4. **Small-batch manufacturers** — spreadsheet-based production planning; ERP is overkill
5. **Door-to-door field sales** (pest control, solar, roofing) — commission anxiety with zero tooling
6. **Wholesale/specialty distributors** — running 30-year-old TUI systems

### Pricing patterns that work in boring B2B:
- Flat annual fee (no per-seat, no per-job) — trades love this
- LTD with clear usage ceiling — AppSumo-ready
- Usage-based for data products — aligned with value
- "Sub-$500/month beats everyone above" positioning — consistent theme

### Acquisition channels that work:
- Facebook Groups for specific trades
- Reddit communities (r/HVAC, r/pestcontrol, r/smallbusiness)
- Trade association forums and newsletters
- Word-of-mouth within tight professional communities (Verito's entire growth was CPA-to-CPA)
- SEO on long-tail industry terms (low volume, high intent)
