## 2. AI Dispatch & Operating System for Home Services (The Platform Play)

- **Category**: SMB Platform Consolidation / Vertical AI
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://a16z.com/announcement/investing-in-probook/ (a16z thesis: dispatch is the brain, not voice/chat)
  - https://xitter.cf/dhaber/status/2069417665764978725 (Probook announcement thread)
  - https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/6/23/3316215/0/en/probook-raises-40m-from-andreessen-horowitz-and-sequoia-to-scale-the-ai-operating-system-for-home-services.html
  - https://mainteny.com (Mainteny — AI operating system for maintenance companies)
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/mainteny
  - https://daltonmills.com/resources/fieldnotes/dalton-mills-raises-9m-to-build-the-platform-for-trades (Dalton Mills $9.2M — "Replit for the trades")
- **Description**: a16z's thesis: "In vertical AI, you do not win by adding another tool at the edge. You win by owning the hardest, most central decision in the business." For home services, that's dispatch. Probook built dispatch first, then expanded to intake, messaging, outbound — all sharing one context layer. Summers Plumbing booked 2,542 jobs in its first month with zero human intervention. Del-Air doubled productivity from 10 to 22 techs per dispatcher. The pattern: replace the patchwork of 5 point solutions with one AI OS that owns the full job lifecycle.
- **Target Industries**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing (larger shops, 5-50 technicians)
- **Market Timing**: Growing — the VC money is here but most operators haven't switched yet
- **Example Products**:
  - Probook: https://probook.ai ($40M)
  - Stormy: https://stormy.ai (YC, field service AI OS)
  - ContractorHUB: https://contractorhub.com (roofing/paving, operator-led round)
  - Mainteny: https://mainteny.com (AI OS for maintenance companies)
  - Dalton Mills: https://daltonmills.com (no-code OS for trades, $9.2M seed)
- **White Space**: The enterprise play (Probook, Avoca) is locked up. The SMB opportunity — shops with 2-10 technicians who can't afford $5K/month platforms — is wide open. A simpler "AI office manager" at $299-$499/month that handles booking, follow-up, and basic dispatch for a 3-truck HVAC shop has no strong incumbent.
- **Speed to Market**: 4-8 weeks for MVP (use existing FSM integrations via ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro APIs)
- **LTD Fit**: Low — high operational complexity and ongoing AI costs make LTD unsuitable
- **Content Strategy**: "AI for HVAC dispatching", "field service management software small business", "home services AI operating system", "replace ServiceTitan alternative"
