## 1. AI Front Desk for Dental Practices (Insurance Verification + Scheduling)

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47385090
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN
- **Engagement**: Low upvotes (early-stage post), 3 comments — the lack of engagement reflects HN's bias, not the idea's merit
- **Revenue Data**: Beta; 30+ dental practices interviewed during discovery
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Dental practices
- **Core Value Prop**: Automates the full dental front desk workflow — insurance benefit verification via 10+ carrier portals (browser automation for portals without APIs), inbound call handling with voice AI, appointment booking into existing software (Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft), SMS/email reminders. A single front desk employee costs $40–50K/year with 40% annual turnover.

- **Our Angle**: Narrower initial scope — insurance verification automation only — as a standalone point solution priced at $299–599/month. One-shot problem that immediately saves hours daily. Expand to scheduling after nailing verification.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (HIPAA compliance makes pure LTD tricky, but dental associations + AppSumo alternative marketplaces possible)

## 2. Fresco — AI Copilot for Construction Superintendents (YC F24)

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42204939
- **Additional Links**: https://fresco-ai.com
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Launch HN
- **Engagement**: Launch HN post, YC-backed
- **Revenue Data**: $1,000/month per site, discounts for multi-month/multi-site. Active in market with real customers.
- **Boring Business Score**: 4/5
- **Target Industry**: Construction (site superintendents)
- **Core Value Prop**: Supers take 30–200 photos/day for liability/documentation. Fresco lets them record short videos/voiceover on site walks → AI generates notes, punch lists, daily logs, assigns crew action items via text. Routes to Procore/ACC with one click.

- **LTD Potential**: 3/5

## 3. Trades Capacity & AI Receptionist Problem — Validated Pain in HVAC/Plumbing

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47493339
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Discussion thread
- **Engagement**: Substantial thread discussing trades not answering phones
- **Revenue Data**: Multiple commenters confirm the dynamic — every trade shop in their area is "too busy," nobody picks up
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, appliance repair
- **Core Value Prop**: Trades shops miss calls not because they lack demand but because they're capacity-constrained. The real product need: **triage and qualify inbound calls** — not just answer them. AI that captures lead details, schedules non-urgent jobs (remodels, upgrades), and sends an instant "we got your request" SMS boosts revenue without overwhelming the shop.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Key insight from the thread: "A founder assumes every missed call is lost revenue, but for capacity-constrained shops, a missed call is just triage they did not have to do." The winning product isn't a voice bot — it's a **smart job intake + triage system** that filters emergency vs. planned work.
- **Our Angle**: Job intake form + SMS confirmation + simple scheduling queue. Not trying to be a phone bot. $49–99/month for solo operators. Integrates with Jobber/Housecall Pro.
- **LTD Potential**: 4/5

## 10. Legacy System Modernization for Wholesale/Distribution (TUI Replacement)

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45823234
- **Additional Links**: N/A
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Ask HN
- **Engagement**: Large thread with many confirming still running on 1993-era TUI systems (AS/400, Unix boxes)
- **Revenue Data**: Companies running on these systems for 20–30 years; deeply embedded
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Wholesale distribution, light manufacturing, retail (small regional operators)
- **Core Value Prop**: Many small-to-mid wholesale distributors still run inventory, ordering, invoicing, kit management on terminal-based software from the 80s/90s. Not because they're happy — because migration risk is terrifying and modern software doesn't fit their workflows.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: "Boring industries punish bad software immediately" — this is the extreme case. No one is building AI-native replacement tooling that actually respects TUI workflows (fast keyboard navigation, structured data entry, batch operations). Opportunity to build a "modern TUI": fast, keyboard-first, no animations, just the data.
- **Our Angle**: Start with one specific workflow module — e.g., **order entry + inventory for small wholesale distributors** — that can run alongside the legacy system and eventually replace it. Focus on importers (CSV/Excel from old system), keyboard navigation, zero onboarding friction. $99–299/month.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (complex implementation, harder for LTD model)
