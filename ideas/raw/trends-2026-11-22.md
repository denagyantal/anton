  - Loady.ai — voice AI, beta $10/mo
  - Zen Trucking (zenteck.ai)

- **Speed to Market**: 4-6 weeks for a load-ranking tool wrapping DAT/Truckstop APIs; voice integration adds 2-3 more weeks
- **LTD Fit**: Low — truckers pay per load or monthly; LTD unusual in this market
- **Content Strategy**: "AI dispatcher for small trucking fleet", "owner operator load board software", "freight dispatch automation"
