- **White Space**: Fire safety inspection companies (required quarterly/annual inspections by law, still done on paper clipboards). Elevator inspection companies. Restaurant equipment service techs (FDA log requirements on paper). These are compliance-driven paper workflows with mandatory digital upgrade paths.
- **Speed to Market**: 3-5 weeks for a mobile-first digital form + report generation MVP for a single industry
- **LTD Fit**: Very strong — compliance-driven businesses value certainty; a one-time $99 LTD for "never lose a paper inspection form again" is a slam dunk
