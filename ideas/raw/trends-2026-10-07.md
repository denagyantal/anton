PLMBR is charging contractors $10.5K–$25K/year for AI agents that actually do proposals, scheduling, invoicing, and follow-up — pricing against a hire, not against software.

## 3. Construction Back Office Disruption — Killing the Frankenstack

- **Category**: Legacy Software Disruption / Paper-to-Digital
- **Signal Strength**: Strong
- **Sources**:
  - https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/miter-the-ai-platform-for-construction-raises-40-million-for-the-built-world-302893778.html — Miter $40M Series B (Sep 30, 2026)
  - https://completeaitraining.com/news/trayd-adds-general-ledger-to-its-payroll-platform-for/ — Trayd launches GL module (Sep 25, 2026)
  - https://www.miter.com/resources/its-time-to-build/ — Miter founder blog on Frankenstack
  - https://www.workyard.com/compare/certified-payroll-software — certified payroll landscape
  - https://www.lumberfi.com/blog/best-construction-payroll-software-in-2026-a-contractors-comparison-guide — Lumber AI payroll
- **Description**: Contractors are running their businesses on a "Frankenstack" — Sage/Foundation/Timberline accounting (some 30+ years old), separate time tracking, separate HR, separate job costing — none of which talk to each other. Miter ($78M total, tripled customer count in 18 months) is replacing this with an AI-native platform. Trayd just launched a full general ledger embedded in their payroll system, targeting the exact accounting platforms contractors have run on for decades. Two of every 100 American construction workers now get paid through Miter. The problem is acute for specialty contractors (union, prevailing wage, multi-state) where payroll complexity is extreme.
- **Target Industries**: General contractors, specialty trade contractors (electrical, plumbing, HVAC subs), union shops, prevailing wage projects
- **Market Timing**: Growing rapidly — multiple well-funded players, massive legacy installed base to displace
- **Example Products**:
  - Miter (miter.com) — AI workforce OS for construction, $78M raised (Battery, Bessemer, Coatue)
  - Trayd (traydapp.com) — payroll + GL for specialty contractors, $15M raised (YC, White Star)
  - Lumber (lumberfi.com) — AI construction payroll with prevailing wage automation
  - Adaptive — $30M Series B, construction finance intelligence (integrates with legacy ledgers)
  - eBacon — prevailing wage compliance specialist
- **White Space**: The SMB end ($1M–$10M revenue specialty contractor) is underserved — Miter and Trayd are moving upmarket. A lighter-weight, affordable, all-in-one for sub-10-employee specialty contractors could capture the long tail. Also: compliance tooling specifically for new infrastructure bill prevailing wage requirements.
- **Speed to Market**: Hard at full scope, but a narrow wedge (e.g., certified payroll report generator as standalone SaaS) could be MVP-ready in 2-4 weeks.
- **LTD Fit**: No — construction payroll is a mission-critical monthly service. Contractors won't buy LTD for something touching payroll.
- **Content Strategy**: "construction payroll software", "certified payroll software", "prevailing wage software", "ServiceTitan alternative construction", "Sage 300 alternative construction"

  - Avoca — voice AI for trades, $1B valuation
  - Miter (miter.com) — construction workforce AI, $78M raised
  - Probook — home services dispatch AI, $40M raised
- **White Space**: Industries that *haven't yet* had a well-funded vertical AI entrant: funeral homes, auto repair (beyond Mitchell/CCC), dry cleaning/laundry, self-storage operators, parking lot management.
- **Speed to Market**: Moderate — pick a narrow workflow in an underserved vertical; build AI-native from day 1.
- **LTD Fit**: Depends on ACV — low-ACV verticals (sub-$200/mo) are LTD candidates if there's a clear productivity win.
