## Prelay – Weather-Based Work Planning for Outdoor Contractors

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341158
- **Additional Links**: https://prelay.lwnn.news
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Show HN (very recent — "2 days ago")

- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5

- **Gap/Opportunity**: The founder's core UX question — raw data vs. actionable recommendation — is unresolved. - **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (small-to-medium TAM but very tangible ROI — avoiding paying idle crews)

## Key Trends Observed

1. **AI voice agents for local service businesses** — Multiple products validating this market (Conduit AI, Dolfyn, Zirco.ai). The winning approach is vertical-specific scripts + system integrations, not generic voice AI.

2. **Trade-specific instant quoting** — Contractors lose jobs to whoever quotes fastest. InstaBid, Avenue ($50K MRR AUD), and similar tools are growing by embedding quote capture on contractor websites.

3. **Job profitability analytics** — Cleaning/trades businesses track revenue but not profit-per-job. BlastoClean and others are approaching this. The founder who can deliver "here's your actual margin by job type" will win.

4. **Physical direct mail as go-to-market** — The manufacturing warehouse podcast episode is a signal: offline buyers in boring industries are reachable via mail, not SEO. Under-explored channel for new SaaS launches in trades/manufacturing.

5. **Small landlord / independent property management** — LeaseBase, Latchel, and others are validating the gap between consumer spreadsheets and enterprise property management platforms. Tax prep + document management is the highest-stickiness use case.

6. **Pest control, HVAC, dental still wide open** — DASHP proved pest control D2D management at $100K ARR. Breezy proved HVAC at $250K ARR in 9 months. The pattern: pick one specific workflow in an unsexy vertical, solve it completely, grow from there.
