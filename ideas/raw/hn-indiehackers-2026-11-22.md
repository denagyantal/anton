## Crewbird — FSM + CRM with Native Google Local Service Ads Integration

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418273
- **Additional Links**: https://getcrewbird.com/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Ask HN / What are you working on (November 2026)
- **Engagement**: Active discussion, strong validation comments ("the space between full blown ERP and spreadsheet is abysmal")
- **Revenue Data**: Dad's carpet cleaning business approaching 3x ROAS — early stage, no MRR disclosed
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Home services / field service (carpet cleaning, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing etc.)
- **Core Value Prop**: FSM + CRM with direct Google Local Service Ads (LSA) API integration — tracks every dollar of ad spend, attributable ROAS. Existing SaaS CRMs have no decent LSA integrations and the Google LSA dashboard itself is poor.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Google LSA is a primary acquisition channel for home service businesses but no CRM properly integrates with it. Founder built this after finding his dad's Windows desktop CRM was about to break. Comments show deep validation: fleet + field crew management + procurement + AR/AP all scattered across 12 trackers.
- **Our Angle**: First FSM purpose-built around Google LSA ROAS tracking. Add AI call attribution (LSA call tracking → job outcome) and automated review requesting post-job. Price at $99-199/mo targeting 1-20 employee service businesses.
- **LTD Potential**: 3 — operational software, but LTD could work as a launch offer at $299-499 for first year of features
