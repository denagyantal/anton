## Key Market Insights

**Market Validation (from HN/IH discussions):**
- ServiceTitan's pricing ($300–700/tech/month) leaves a massive gap for sub-$100/month tools serving solo to 10-tech shops
- Offshore competition is minimal because trades software requires local market knowledge (regulations, equipment brands, association relationships)
- Rob Walling confirms TinySeed is betting on boring niches for 7-figure ARR companies
- Multiple $5M+ seed rounds in 2025 for HVAC/trades AI validates the market without creating direct indie competition

**Best GTM Patterns Found:**
1. **Association newsletter sponsorships** — CAC as low as $280 (funeral home case)
2. **Distribution equity deal** — trade equity for day-one customer base (Amazon Launch Fast case)
3. **Facebook group presence** — Pest control, landscaping, HVAC operators all have active FB groups

**Biggest Open Gaps (no funded competitor):**
- Pest control: chemical compliance + routing + AI call answering
- Roofing: AI estimating from aerial/photo + storm damage workflow
- Veterinary clinics: compliance + appointment + boarding management (like Auxpanel for dental)
- Small aquaculture / specialty farms: record-keeping for regulatory compliance
- Chiropractic / occupational therapy: prior-auth automation (copy of the $41k MRR PT agent)

**Pricing Benchmarks:**
- Pest control / landscaping shops: $29–$199/month is the sweet spot (below Jobber at $39–$599)
- Fleet management: $25–$80/vehicle/month
- Compliance tools (dental/medical): $300–$500/location/month
- AI call answering add-on: $99–$199/month
