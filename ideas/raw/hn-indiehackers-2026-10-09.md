## Mobile Field Reporting App for Construction/Subcontractors

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-analyzed-39-000-software-complaints-the-best-micro-saas-gaps-are-all-in-boring-industries-801c41685b
- **Additional Links**: https://bigideasdb.com/boring-industries-begging-for-micro-saas
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Data analysis / gap research (Jul 2026)
- **Engagement**: High-quality primary research post (39,000+ complaints from Capterra, G2, Reddit, Upwork)
- **Revenue Data**: Suggested price $29/user/month; comparable tools at $500+/month enterprise only
- **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Construction, field service, subcontractors (5-20 field workers)
- **Core Value Prop**: Mobile-first field reporting — GPS-tagged photo uploads, form-based inspections, instant office sync. Desktop-first incumbents have mobile apps as afterthoughts; field workers can't submit inspection reports from job sites.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: Severity 4.0/5 from complaint analysis. 5 distinct vendors with the same complaint. No focused micro-SaaS exists — only enterprise platforms at $500+/month. No Gantt charts, no invoicing bloat needed — pure field-to-office reporting.
- **Our Angle**: Narrow build: photo + GPS tagging, form inspections, instant sync. Mobile-first, not desktop-first with a mobile skin. $29/user/month to 5-20 person subcontractor crews. LTD at ~$299/user or flat team rate.
- **LTD Potential**: 4

- **Core Value Prop**: "No BS" tool for creating quotes, estimates, and contracts as clean PDFs. - **Boring Business Score**: 5
- **Target Industry**: Small warehouses, local distributors, supply houses (plumbing/electrical/HVAC distributors)

- **Gap/Opportunity**: Severity 4.5/5 in the complaint dataset — second highest overall. 5 distinct legacy WMS vendors with the same complaint. No fast overlay tool exists. The narrow build: a fast search/scan layer that sits in front of existing systems, doesn't replace them.
