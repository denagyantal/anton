- **Market Timing**: Early — enforcement urgency building, SMB tooling gap remains wide open
- **Example Products**:
  - Legalithm (https://www.legalithm.com) — free through ~April 2028, EU-native, self-serve
  - Drata (~$15K–$60K/year) — enterprise GRC, not SMB
  - Scrut Automation (~$15K/year on AWS Marketplace)
- **White Space**: Self-serve EU AI Act compliance checklist + documentation generator for SMBs under 50 employees at $49–$99/month. Automate the Article 4 training log, AI inventory, and risk classification in a simple dashboard. No-code, no legal jargon.
- **Speed to Market**: 2–4 weeks for a focused compliance checklist SaaS using Claude/GPT to power the risk classification logic
- **LTD Fit**: Yes — compliance is a one-time pain; EU founders would pay $300–$500 LTD to check a box and get a PDF certificate
- **Content Strategy**: "EU AI Act compliance for startups", "EU AI Act SME checklist", "AI Act Article 4 training documentation"
