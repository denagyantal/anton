## Key Market Signals Summary

### Biggest trends from today's research:

1. **AI search is replacing Google for local business discovery** — 6% → 45% in 12 months. Trades (HVAC, roofing) are the most invisible in AI search results despite having the highest-value service tickets. AI GEO tools for local trades = immediate opportunity.

2. **Home services automation validated** — AI receptionist/booking tools already at $179K MRR (FounderSignal). Autonomous home service operations (Show HN, 3 live companies). This is moving fast.

3. **Construction/trades quoting pain is large and proven** — Rebar ($14M), Togal.AI (333% growth), Buildots ($45M). SMB tier is completely unserved below $300/month.

4. **39K+ complaint dataset confirms 6 boring industries with no focused solutions** — Construction mobile, HR templates, legal drafting, logistics speed, accounting integrations, and education LMS. All severity 4.0–4.5/5 with fewer than 8 competitors.

5. **Rob Walling (Tiny Seed) explicitly names** home improvement contractor SaaS, CAD add-ons, and electrical commissioning as paths to 7-figure bootstrapped ARR.

6. **"Plumbers tell other plumbers"** — QuoteSmith for UK tradespeople mentioned in Feb 2026 IH post as a word-of-mouth growth model. Trades communities are tight; one happy customer drives referrals naturally.
