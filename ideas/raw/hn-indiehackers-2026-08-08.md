## Key Themes Across All Findings

1. **Compliance creates defensibility**: Pest control (EPA records), dental (OSHA/HIPAA), funeral homes (death certificates), trucking (FMCSA) — any product with compliance automation has extreme stickiness and pricing power.

2. **Legacy UI = indie opportunity**: In every boring niche, the incumbent has a product that "looks dated" but works. Modern UX + modern API + honest pricing undercuts them reliably.

3. **Per-seat pricing is the enemy of small trades**: Jobber, Housecall Pro, and others all charge per technician. A flat-per-business model at $79-$149/mo wins against them for 1-10 tech operations.

4. **PE acquisition of boring businesses is accelerating**: HVAC, pest control, landscaping, and funeral homes are all consolidating under PE. PE buyers want software-enabled operations — this drives willingness to pay and creates natural B2B sales channels.

5. **SMS-first beats app-first for field workers**: Workers don't download apps. Scheduling confirmations via text, clock-in via SMS, customer reminders via text all have higher adoption than app-based equivalents.
