## WorkHero — AI Back Office for HVAC / Skilled Trades

- **Source**: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49524167 (HN Hiring, September 2026)
- **Additional Links**: https://www.workhero.pro/ | https://builtforward.simplecast.com/episodes/workhero-the-ai-back-office-for-hvac-w-furman-haynes | https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/markets-news/ACCESS%20Newswire/35698951/workhero-raises-5m-seed-round-to-advance-its-ai-powered-back-office-platform-for-small-hvac-contractors/
- **Platform**: HN
- **Type**: Who is Hiring / Company signal
- **Engagement**: Actively hiring Senior SWE, AI Automation Engineer, Senior PM (multiple open roles)
- **Revenue Data**: VC-backed, $5M seed round raised; not indie
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: HVAC / Skilled Trades (plumbing, electrical next)
- **Core Value Prop**: Pairs dedicated human office managers with AI agents to handle the back-office work that crushes small HVAC contractors — rebates, permits, equipment registrations, billing, collections, warranty registration, pricebooks. Small contractors spend their nights on this admin work.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: WorkHero is VC-backed and targets small-to-mid HVAC shops. The model (human + AI hybrid) is expensive to run at scale. An indie/affordable version focused on one specific back-office workflow (e.g., rebate management, permit filing, or pricebook automation) could undercut at a lower price point for the smallest shops (1-5 techs) who can't afford $500+/mo. The pain is proven — $5M was raised to solve it.
- **Our Angle**: Vertical SaaS micro-tool targeting the most painful single workflow (likely: rebate + warranty registration automation) for tiny HVAC shops at $49-99/mo vs. WorkHero's enterprise pricing. Or clone the model for an adjacent trade (landscaping, electrical, pest control) that WorkHero hasn't touched yet.
- **LTD Potential**: 3/5 (B2B trade software, LTD is a harder sell but $297-497 one-time could work for AppSumo)

## Breezy — Purpose-Built HVAC Field Service Platform

- **Source**: https://getbreezyapp.com/ | https://getbreezyapp.com/faq/how-is-breezy-different-from-all-the-other-crms-or-hvac-platforms (Referenced in HN hiring threads 2025-2026)

- **Engagement**: Referenced across multiple HN hiring threads as "replacing ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro"
- **Revenue Data**: $3M seed (August 2023), ~7 employees, growing
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Residential HVAC
- **Core Value Prop**: Full-featured FSM (scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, payments, memberships, technician management) plus AI agents for missed calls, website visitors, and booking. Built specifically for HVAC — not a generic FSM adapted for HVAC.

## CraftBoop — Automated Post-Job Follow-Ups for Service Businesses

- **Source**: https://www.indiehackers.com/post/craftboop-built-automated-follow-ups-for-service-businesses-just-launched-looking-for-feedback-5aa58e1c39 (IH, April 2026)
- **Additional Links**: https://www.craftboop.com
- **Platform**: Indie Hackers
- **Type**: Product launch / feedback request
- **Engagement**: Community discussion, feedback thread
- **Revenue Data**: $29/mo, 14-day free trial, early-stage (few users)
- **Boring Business Score**: 5/5
- **Target Industry**: Service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, cleaners, painters, landscapers)
- **Core Value Prop**: After each job, sends an automated 5-email sequence to the customer: thank you → Google review request → check-in → rebooking reminder → referral ask. One-time customer setup, fully automated thereafter.
- **Gap/Opportunity**: The pain is real — service businesses are terrible at follow-up. But email-only in 2026 feels weak. Service business owners communicate via SMS/text. A SMS-first version with review request automation (Google + Facebook) timed after job completion, tied into existing scheduling tools (Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro) via webhook/Zapier, at $39-59/mo could be meaningfully better.
- **Our Angle**: SMS-first version of CraftBoop, integrated with Jobber/Housecall Pro, with review gating (only send 5-star requests to happy customers). Add a "no-show fee reminder" module and upsell seasonal service promotions.
- **LTD Potential**: 5/5 (exactly the right product for AppSumo — simple, clear ROI, service business audience)
