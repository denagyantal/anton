- **LTD Fit**: High for specialized plugins solving a specific pain (e.g., "septic compliance trip ticket generator" as a Dalton Mills plugin)

- **Market Timing**: Growing (Trayd is early-stage national expansion; most of the US is still untouched)
- **Example Products**:
